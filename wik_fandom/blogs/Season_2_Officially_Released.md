**Jian Lai Season 2 Officially Released — New Journey Begins!**![Season 2 preview.jpg](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/sword-of-coming/images/9/9b/Season_2_preview.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20251225083442)
After months of excitement and anticipation, Season 2 of Jian Lai has officially premiered! The second season of this popular Chinese donghua adaptation of Feng Huo Xi Zhu Hou’s Jian Lai novel was released on December 25, 2025, exclusively on Tencent Video.  

This new season continues the story from the conclusion of Season 1, following Chen Ping-an and his companions as they embark on a long and perilous journey south. Season 2 focuses heavily on Chen Ping-an’s travels with Li Bao-ping and others as they head toward the Daxiu cliffside academy, facing dangers such as the Dali cavalry’s attacks, demon spirits, and old adversaries along the way.  

In the lead-up to the premiere, the official final trailer showcased intense action moments, including Chen Ping-an’s sword qi against a giant serpent and strategic confrontations, reinforcing the season’s themes of growth, challenge, and martial spirit.  

The full season consists of 26 episodes and is being released weekly on Tencent Video. Leading up to launch, the series broke records with over 800 million reservations on the platform, indicating overwhelming fan enthusiasm and making it one of the most anticipated donghua releases of 2025.   

Fans can now tune in to witness new character developments, expanded world-building, and more breathtaking cultivation battles as Jian Lai continues to unfold its epic tale of swordplay, honor, and personal transformation.
