# Daoism

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Daoism)

---
Daoism in Eastern Treasured Vial Continent flourishes as a prominent force, rivaling Confucianism in influence and shaping the world's power dynamics. The story depict a vibrant and multifaceted Daoist tradition, encompassing diverse schools of thought, powerful figures, and a deep-rooted presence in the lives of cultivators and ordinary people.

**Daoism's Ascendancy:**

- **Popularity and Recognition:** Daoist figures like True Lords and Heavenly Masters hold immense sway in Eastern Treasured Vial Continent, enjoying widespread recognition and reverence. This stands in stark contrast to Buddhism's declining influence.
- **Strength in the Town:** Daoism is described as the strongest force in the small town, further emphasizing its dominance and active presence. This local dominance reflects a broader trend across the continent.

**Diversity and Complexity:**

Daoism is not a monolithic entity but encompasses a vast array of schools, practices, and beliefs. The sources highlight this inherent diversity:

- **Numerous Branches and Schools:** Ning Yao, knowledgeable about Daoist traditions, mentions the existence of four Ancestral Temples - above, below, south, and north. This suggests a wide-reaching network of Daoist institutions and lineages, contributing to the tradition's vastness and complexity.
- **Varied Practices and Teachings:** Daoist practices range from talismanic sword crafting, a revered art form, to Observing the Sea of Clouds, a unique cultivation technique employed by Dawn Cloud Mountain. This diversity highlights the adaptability and multifaceted nature of Daoism, accommodating various approaches to spiritual development and attaining Dao.

**Key Concepts and Principles:**

Several core concepts shape Daoist thought and practice in Eastern Treasured Vial Continent:

- **Pursuit of Tranquility and Harmony with Nature:** Daoists seek to align themselves with the natural order, emphasizing balance and inner peace. Cultivating a tranquil mind and harmonizing with the Dao are essential aspects of Daoist practice.
- **Attaining Immortality:** Daoist teachings offer paths to transcending mortality and achieving immortality. This pursuit of longevity and spiritual ascension motivates many cultivators and shapes their actions.
- **Internal Demons and Self-Cultivation:** Daoism recognizes the concept of "internal demons," psychological and emotional obstacles that hinder spiritual progress. Overcoming these internal challenges through self-cultivation and moral refinement is essential for attaining Dao.
- **The "Teaching" as a Broad Concept:** Daoism, while referred to as a "teaching," is not a rigid or singular entity. Ning Yao emphasizes that the breadth of Daoism far exceeds expectations, suggesting that it encompasses a vast spectrum of beliefs, practices, and lineages.

**Notable Daoist Figures and Institutions:**

The sources introduce several prominent Daoist figures and institutions that play crucial roles in shaping the world:

- **True Lords:** Powerful Daoist figures who wield significant influence in the empire. They contribute to the empire's stability and prosperity, often holding high positions in government or military. Fu Nanhua, a character seeking to become a True Lord, embodies the ambition and power associated with this title.
- **Heavenly Masters:** Highly respected figures within Daoism, known for their mastery of Daoist arts and teachings. They often lead sects, guide disciples, and command respect throughout Eastern Treasured Vial Continent. The Heavenly Master Residence on Mount Longhu is a testament to their power and influence.
- **Divine Edict Sect:** A unique Daoist sect with chaotic inheritances and lineages from all three teachings, making it a complex and somewhat unorthodox entity. He Xiaoliang, a disciple of Divine Edict Sect, showcases the sect's unique blend of Daoist practices and principles.
- **Old Dragon City:** A powerful city in the southernmost region of the continent, associated with Daoist traditions and influential True Lords. Fu Nanhua hails from Old Dragon City, emphasizing the city's role as a center of Daoist power and ambition.

Daoism's Profound Influence:

Daoism, with its diverse schools, powerful figures, and emphasis on attaining Dao, profoundly impacts Eastern Treasured Vial Continent.

- **The pursuit of immortality and spiritual ascension motivates countless cultivators, shaping their actions and contributing to the world's dynamic power struggles.**
- **Daoist principles influence governance, societal structures, and the lives of individuals across the continent.**

The story reveals a rich and multifaceted Daoist tradition, playing a central role in shaping the destinies of characters and the unfolding events of the story.
