> **Spoiler Warning:** This article contains spoilers from the *Sword of Coming* (*Jian Lai* / *Unsheathed*) novel and donghua.

**Pei Qian** is [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md)'s first disciple, his "opening disciple". She is a street urchin from the capital of the Southern Garden Nation in the Lotus Flower Blessed Land, who was dragged out of that world by its old Daoist master and left in Chen Ping'an's care. At first greedy, deceitful and cruel, a girl Chen Ping'an genuinely detested, she gradually grows into a fiercely loyal, comedic and big-hearted young martial artist who idolises her master.

She has mysterious eyes containing the light of the sun and moon, an extraordinary talent for martial arts, and a love of money, treasures, sunflower seeds and her "self-created" Deranged Demon Sword Techniques. After the death of her martial arts teacher [Cui Cheng](Cui_Cheng.md), she inherits half of his martial fortune.

## Appearance

Pei Qian is a skinny, heavily tanned girl, described as being as thin as a bamboo pole when Chen Ping'an meets her. For years she barely grows taller, to the point that Li Baoping asks why she isn't growing. Her most striking feature is her eyes, which Zhong Kui compares to the sun rising over the eastern seas and the moon over the western mountains. She is almost always seen with her bamboo hiking pole, a small bamboo bookcase, a bamboo sword and saber made by Chen Ping'an hanging at her hip, and a Treasure Pagoda Demon-Suppressing Talisman stuck to her forehead.

## Personality

When she first leaves the blessed land, Pei Qian is lazy, greedy, a habitual liar and a petty thief. Having survived alone on the streets, she treats a hostile world with hostility in return, resents anything good she cannot have, and would rather destroy it than leave it to someone else. She is extremely skilled at reading people, and she casually compares killing people to eating rice.

Under Chen Ping'an's patient but strict guidance, especially the daily transcriptions he makes her write, she slowly changes. She remains mischievous, boastful, theatrical and obsessed with money. She calls her master "Father" in private and "Master" in public, flatters shamelessly, invents stories about being a lost princess, and loves novels about the martial world. But she becomes sincere, protective of her friends and deeply devoted to Chen Ping'an. By the end of Volume 8, she no longer needs her eyes to read people's hearts.

## Background

Pei Qian grew up alone and impoverished in the capital of the Southern Garden Nation. She occasionally sneaked out of the city to look at a small, overgrown grave mound. She first appears unnamed as a skinny little girl sitting on a stool during the great battles that shook the capital.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> She steals and sells books, and eventually lives in the courtyard of the boy [Cao Qinglang](Cao_Qinglang.md), where Chen Ping'an is staying.

When the master of the Lotus Flower Blessed Land, an old Daoist priest, plucks the sun from the sky in front of her, her eyes are seared with pain. Looking at her, he recognises her identity: she is a "Go piece" secretly planted in his blessed land by the old scholar ([Scholarly Sage](Scholarly_Sage.md)), who had won from him a token and a promise to protect whoever held it.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> When Chen Ping'an leaves the blessed land, the old Daoist reaches into the air and drags Pei Qian out after him, leaving her face-down on the road.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

## Plot

### Journey Through Parasol Leaf Continent

On the road north, Pei Qian complains constantly, lies, tries to wheedle money out of Chen Ping'an, and kills butterflies and insects when he is not looking. Chen Ping'an genuinely detests her and refuses to teach her even his walking meditation.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> When she foolishly stares at a ghostly wedding procession, he protects her, and when they must escape through a dangerous mountain, she runs several kilometres with talismans in both hands. Her stamina surprises Chen Ping'an, and the translator notes that her name is a pun on "losing money".<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

At Fox Town, she lies about borrowing money and stealing a kite, and accuses Chen Ping'an of preferring Cao Qinglang to her. Zhong Kui first notices her remarkable eyes.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> Later, at the Burial River's water residence, a water goddess offers her a treasure in exchange for the book Chen Ping'an makes her read. Pei Qian silently refuses to give it up. Chen Ping'an tells her it is fine, and she bursts into tears, apologising to him on his back as they leave.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> From then on, she gradually changes.

Chen Ping'an's four companions from the blessed land, [Zhu Lian](Zhu_Lian.md), [Wei Xian](Wei_Xian.md), [Lu Baixiang](Lu_Baixiang.md) and [Sui Youbian](Sui_Youbian.md), join them. Pei Qian spends years watching them, learning Go rules, music and cooking from them, and pestering them for martial arts lessons.

### Old Dragon City and Beyond

At [Old Dragon City](Old_Dragon_City.md), Chen Ping'an apologises to her for wrongly blaming her in an earlier quarrel over a pancake. Pei Qian is moved and tells him she no longer wants to explore the world now that she has met him.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> He makes her write transcriptions every day, and she slowly comes to do them willingly. He also teaches her A'Liang's Eighteen Stops breathing technique, which she picks up with astonishing speed.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

She meets [Cui Dongshan](Cui_Dongshan.md), whom she nicknames the "big white goose" and grudgingly accepts as her "senior brother", then travels with Chen Ping'an through the Azure Phoenix Nation. At Mountain Cliff Academy, she befriends [Li Huai](Li_Huai.md), who believes her lost-princess stories and loses to her in a treasure showdown. She also comes to worship [Li Baoping](Li_Baoping.md), whose mountains of transcriptions humble her. When she finally gets her hands on her own savings, she spends them on a present for her master.

### Downtrodden Mountain and Dragon Riding Alley

Chen Ping'an leaves her at [Downtrodden Mountain](Downtrodden_Mountain.md) with Zhu Lian while he goes to Bamboo Scroll Lake. She spends years running the shops on Dragon Riding Alley, scaring customers by putting her head on the counter, and avoiding Cui Cheng, who believes Chen Ping'an is wasting her talent. When Chen Ping'an becomes the strongest fifth-tier martial artist and receives a jewel of martial fortune, he gives it straight to Pei Qian, and it vanishes into her hands.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup>

Chen Ping'an enrols her in the private school in the small town, which she resists with every delaying tactic she can bargain for.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> While he is away on [Northern Complete Reed Continent](Northern_Complete_Reed_Continent.md), she takes charge of the little water monster [Zhou Mili](Zhou_Mili.md), whom Chen Ping'an had sent to Downtrodden Mountain, and makes her a loyal follower and "right guardian".<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup>

### Cui Cheng's Disciple

After Zhu Lian takes her back to the Southern Garden Nation, Pei Qian climbs to the second floor of the bamboo building for the first time and asks Cui Cheng to teach her. He asks why, and she replies that there is one person she absolutely cannot lose to. That person is Cao Qinglang. Cui Cheng accepts her as his direct disciple.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup> She leaves the private school and endures beatings as savage as her master's, being carried down to medicinal baths by [Chen Nuanshu](Chen_Nuanshu.md) and [Zhou Mili](Zhou_Mili.md), then insisting on writing her transcriptions anyway. Eventually, she lands a punch on Cui Cheng that makes him dodge.

Before his death, Cui Cheng takes her on a long journey through the Lotus Root Blessed Land, encouraging her to face her fears and telling her that the Bodhisattvas did hear her prayers, by sending Chen Ping'an to her. When she finds him dead in the Heart Manifestation Temple, her Deity Drumming fist intent stirs his one last time. Half of his martial fortune is left for her, and she accepts it with trembling hands.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup> She refuses to leave the blessed land until Chen Ping'an returns, then weeps in his arms.

### Later Events

Pei Qian reaches the fourth tier, though Cui Cheng had complained that her progress was like a crawling tortoise. Cui Dongshan worries that her rapid breakthroughs and inherited fortune draw attention, and Wei Bo hides her from prying eyes.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup> Chen Ping'an promises that once she reaches her fifth-tier bottleneck, she may travel on her own, with the little donkey he promised her, and decide whether to take Li Huai along. She visits Mountain Cliff Academy with him, where Li Baoping hands her the title of alliance leader of the martial arts world, to Li Huai's horror.

## Abilities

- **Eyes of the sun and moon:** Ever since the old Daoist plucked the sun from the sky in front of her, her eyes have held the light of the sun and moon. She can see spirits invisible to others, stare at the sun without pain, and read people's hearts. At times her eyes cause her agony.
- **Martial arts:** She has extraordinary aptitude. Zhu Lian considers her talent beyond the Five Elements, and she learns techniques simply by watching. As of ch. 567 she is a fourth-tier pure martial artist, trained in Cui Cheng's Deity Drumming Technique, and she holds half of his martial fortune as well as the jewel of martial fortune Chen Ping'an gave her.
- **Eighteen Stops:** A'Liang's breathing technique, which she learned quickly.
- **Deranged Demon Sword Techniques:** her "self-created" and wildly flailing sword style, performed with her hiking pole.

## Possessions

- **Hiking pole**: her most cherished item, which she lets no one touch.
- **Bamboo sword and saber**: made for her by Chen Ping'an.
- **Little bamboo bookcase.**
- **Treasure Pagoda Demon-Suppressing Talisman**: which she keeps stuck to her forehead.
- **Nine-grid treasure box**: gifted by the Yao Clan's Yao Jinzhi, filled with a wooden ganoderma, rare coins, a Daoist badge and an osmanthus branch.
- **Embroidered pouch**: from Lady Gui of Old Dragon City, used as her money pouch.

## Trivia

- 裴钱 (Péi Qián) is a homonym of 赔钱 (péi qián), meaning "losing money", which is ironic given her love of it.
- She detested Go because players take turns instead of placing several stones at once.
