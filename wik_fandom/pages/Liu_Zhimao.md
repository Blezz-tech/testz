**Liu Zhimao**, known as the **River Severing True Lord**, is a vagrant cultivator and the master of Cyan Gorge Island in Bamboo Scroll Lake. He first turns up in [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md) disguised as a storyteller, takes [Gu Can](Gu_Can.md) as his final disciple, and quietly works against [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md). Years later, with Gu Can and his flood dragon beside him, he rises to rule Bamboo Scroll Lake, loses everything to Liu Laocheng, and climbs back to become one of the few vagrant cultivators of the Upper Five Tiers on [Eastern Treasured Vial Continent](Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent.md).

## Appearance

Liu Zhimao is an old man with a beard. In the small town he poses as a wandering storyteller carrying a plain white bowl that can pour out more water than a whole vat can hold.

## Personality

Most people in Bamboo Scroll Lake see Liu Zhimao as a quiet man who always wears a smile. Those close to him know a crueler side, and the late master of Heavenly Governess Island summed him up as "a phony of a True Lord, a smiling Buddha with a blade of slaughter concealed up his sleeve".<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> He is patient, calculating and quick to bow to anyone stronger, and he despises the "official immortals" of established sects. He calls vagrant cultivators like himself wild dogs who have to scrap for every meal.

He is also hard to rattle. Locked in a water dungeon with his acupoints sealed, he laughed at his jailer and lectured him on how much tougher a vagrant cultivator's mind is than an official immortal's.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

## Background

Liu Zhimao practices a heretical cultivation technique, and his title of True Lord was never granted by any empire. He started out as a mere Sea Observation Tier vagrant cultivator, and over a century of fighting and dealing he built Cyan Gorge Island into a real power, though his reputation stayed poor and his friendships shallow.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> His oldest follower, Zhang Ye, was with him from the days when the two of them were young and their first island was sunk to the bottom of the lake.

## Plot

### Jewel Small World

Disguised as a storyteller, Liu Zhimao found that Gu Can had received a locust leaf with ancestral blessings and that the boy's aptitude was too heavy for his secret technique to lift, so he decided to take him as a disciple. When [Cai Jinjian](Cai_Jinjian.md) of Dawn Cloud Mountain came for Gu Can, he pulled rank by naming her sect master and sent her away. He then had Gu Can's mother tell him everything about Chen Ping'an, and from it he worked out that the boy's father had not died in an accident.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

To secure Gu Can and the catfish, he meddled three times on Clay Vase Alley at the cost of several decades of cultivation. He made Cai Jinjian step in the dog dung, delayed Chen Ping'an's warning by a split second so she would take it as spite, and planted a false sense of enlightenment in her.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup> [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md) later told Chen Ping'an that the storyteller was Liu Zhimao and that he was the real cause of the trouble with Cai Jinjian.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> When a divination backfired and made him spit blood, Qi Jingchun caught him in a Halted Realm. Liu Zhimao, though a Daoist of an unorthodox school, bowed as deeply as he could in the Confucian manner, begged for mercy and left town at once with Gu Can and his mother.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> That brief divination of the town's formation cost him several decades of his lifespan.

### Master of Cyan Gorge Island

Back at Bamboo Scroll Lake, one of his guest elders wanted Gu Can's mother as a mistress, and Liu Zhimao neither agreed nor refused. When Gu Can let his flood dragon eat the senior brother who had lured him away, Liu Zhimao's face darkened, then he laughed and patted the boy on the head. He did nothing even after his first disciple was killed, killed every assassin who came for Gu Can, and then led Gu Can and the dragon to wipe out the sect that had sent them.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

At a banquet he declared that only Gu Can could inherit his true teachings and become the next island master.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup> With the flood dragon now at the Nascent Tier, Cyan Gorge Island cut off the lineages of its eleven subsidiary islands and swept across the lake, and Liu Zhimao was hailed as the ruler of its cultivation world. Observers noted that he himself was stuck at the Nascent Tier and owed much of his rise to Gu Can and the dragon.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup>

### Chen Ping'an at Bamboo Scroll Lake

When Chen Ping'an arrived, he sent Liu Zhimao a jade tablet to hold until he left. Liu Zhimao first took it for a fake, but once he confirmed it was real, he was badly shaken.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> He ordered Tian Hujun to keep the island's cultivators in line during Chen Ping'an's stay, the first time she had ever seen him show restraint.

In private, Chen Ping'an accused him of confusing Cai Jinjian with a secret technique and of planting the words "seek death with resolve" in Chen Ping'an's own heart, so that he would go after Cai Jinjian and Fu Nanhua and die. Liu Zhimao also let Chen Ping'an hear, through his bowl of water, a conversation he had with Gu Can's mother, and privately regretted not taking her by force when she first arrived.

After Liu Laocheng beat Gu Can and the flood dragon nearly to death, Liu Zhimao's hold on the lake began to slip. He drank with Chen Ping'an several times, and offered to repay him handsomely if he could obtain a Peace and Safety Tablet for him.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup> Chen Ping'an made him lift the secret restrictions he had placed on Gu Can's mother, and the two became uneasy allies alongside Tan Yuanyi, a spy of the Great Li Empire. In two separate deals, Liu Zhimao gave Chen Ping'an a walnut bracelet and a high-grade secret technique.

### Imprisonment

Liu Laocheng then attacked Cyan Gorge Island. After Liu Zhimao lost the fight and tried to flee, a group of hidden cultivators trapped him with a formation of immortal treasures, and he was dragged off to the water dungeon under Palace Willow Island. Zhang Ye slipped away to tell Chen Ping'an.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup> Someone even used Liu Zhimao's own flying sword to send Chen Ping'an a warning.

Zhou Fenglu of the Jade Tablet Sect came to his cell three times to press him into service. Liu Zhimao told him that serving the Great Li Empire would make him a free-roaming dog, which beat being caged, and laughed in his face. Then Jiang Shangzhen arrived with Liu Laocheng and offered him a place as a guest elder of the Jade Tablet Sect. He accepted, joined the sect's new lower sect, the True Realm Sect, and in time regained Cyan Gorge Island.

### Upper Five Tiers

Before going into seclusion, Liu Zhimao visited Gu Can. He told him he had asked Jiang Shangzhen to give Cyan Gorge Island and White Fish Island to Gu Can if he broke through, handed him the River Severing Sutra, his most prized possession for centuries, and drank with him through the night. He told Gu Can that a man must seek joy while he is still young.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup>

He then broke through to the Unpolished Jade Tier, becoming the second vagrant cultivator of the Upper Five Tiers on the continent after Liu Laocheng.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup> Downtrodden Mountain pointedly sent no congratulations. Later he rebuilt his residence on Cyan Gorge Island and admitted to Li Fuqu over tea that even Liu Laocheng was afraid, and that the Great Li Empire could make use of all of them.

## Abilities

- **Cultivation:** Unpolished Jade Tier of the Upper Five Tiers, previously a Nascent Tier cultivator a step away from breaking through.
- **Heretical techniques:** He practices the heretical method recorded in the *River Severing Sutra*.
- **Divination and mind techniques:** He reads fate from the lines of his palm, confuses people's minds and can plant thoughts in a person's heart.
- **Water bowl:** His white bowl holds far more water than it should, and he uses its surface to replay what was said elsewhere.

## Trivia

- The early translated chapters call Bamboo Scroll Lake "Lake Shujian".
- He longed to reach heights where he could drag the third-generation disciples of the Buddha, the Dao Ancestor and the Confucian Hierarch into a realm of his own.
- He recognized Chen Ping'an's wine gourd as a premium Sword Nurturing Gourd and confirmed it to Tian Hujun in secret.
- He later gave his most treasured scripture, the *River Severing Sutra*, to Gu Can.
- He later secured Cyan Gorge Island for Gu Can's future.
- He worked with Gu Can's mother to raise Gu Can and kept her under secret restrictions until Chen Ping'an made him remove them.
- Although Chen Ping'an saw him as an enemy from the small town, he became an uneasy ally at Bamboo Scroll Lake. Chen Ping'an once told him they were half alike, friends or foes aside.
- He paid a skilled master to secretly refine the skin the Gu Can's little catfish shed on reaching the Nascent Tier into the dark green robe Gu Can wears.
- His walnut bracelet, an old bracelet with eight remaining beads carved with rain gods and thunder gods, was later given to Chen Ping'an.
