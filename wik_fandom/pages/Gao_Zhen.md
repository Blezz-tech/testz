**Gao Xuan** is a prince of the imperial Gao Clan of [Great Sui Nation](Great_Sui_Nation.md). As a boy, he entered Jewel Small World, deep in the territory of his nation's enemy, the Great Li Empire, escorted by the old eunuch Wu Yue. There he introduced himself as **"Gao Zhen"**, the name of one of his brothers, and claimed two of the small world's greatest fated opportunities: the golden carp and the Dragon King Basket.

He later becomes a friend of [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md), a student of [Mountain Cliff Academy](Mountain_Cliff_Academy.md) and close friend of [Yu Lu](Yu_Lu.md). After the treaty between the two empires, he is sent to Forest Deer Academy in Dragon Spring Prefecture as a royal hostage.

*Note: the name "Gao Zhen" in Volume 1 refers to Gao Xuan. The real Gao Zhen, his brother, is a separate, timid prince who never visits the small town.*

## Appearance

Gao Xuan is a handsome boy who first appears in brocade robes, dressed in a colourful and extravagant manner. Chen Ping'an counts him among the good-looking youths of his age he has met, alongside Song Jixin, Zhao Yao and Lin Shouyi.

## Personality

Gao Xuan is friendly, bold and carefree on the surface, but quick to anger when crossed, as when his own servant defies him. He is courageous enough to have travelled into the heart of an enemy nation as a child, which Chen Ping'an considers far more impressive than Song Jixin's later visit to Great Sui. He tells Yu Lu that with women he always tries to show his best side, while with men he can reveal his true self. As a hostage, he outwardly seems calm and carefree, though [Dong Shuijing](Dong_Shuijing.md) and Xu Ruo guess at the disheartenment beneath. He is sincere and respectful towards scholars.

## Plot

### Jewel Small World

Gao Xuan first appears outside the courtyards of [Clay Vase Alley](Small_Town.md#clay-vase-alley) with an elderly servant, and offers to buy [Song Jixin](Song_Jixin.md)'s maidservant [Zhi Gui](Zhi_Gui.md).<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> In an alley, he examines an antique green jade seal carved with coiling dragons and crouching tigers, secretly hoping that a passing young woman with a veiled hat and saber, [Ning Yao](Ning_Yao.md), will try to take it from him.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

When his servant, the old eunuch Wu Yue, suddenly tries to kill the young woman, Gao Xuan introduces himself to her as "Gao Zhen from Yiyang County of Great Sui" and offers compensation. When Wu Yue defies him and pursues her anyway, Gao Xuan is livid, calling Wu Yue an "old, castrated donkey" and shouting that he has committed a crime punishable by death.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

Gao Xuan and Wu Yue intercept [Li Er](Li_Er.md) as he is about to sell the golden carp in the Dragon King Basket to Chen Ping'an, and buy both themselves. Zheng Dafeng scolds Li Er for wasting the basket on "that small prince from Great Sui".<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> Gao Xuan gives Chen Ping'an a pouch of gold essence copper coins as thanks for the opportunity.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

### The Name Gao Zhen

Years later, the emperor of Great Sui reveals that Gao Xuan used his brother Gao Zhen's name in Jewel Small World. The girl Wu Yue nearly killed later descended from the heavens with several sword immortals from another continent to seek vengeance against Gao Zhen. She realised she had the wrong person and apologised, but the timid Gao Zhen was deeply traumatised. Wu Yue, meanwhile, judges that Gao Xuan reaped "two enormous pieces of fortune" on the trip, and the emperor concludes that "Xuan'er is more fortunate than I am".<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

### Friend of Chen Ping'an

At the end of Chen Ping'an's journey to Great Sui, Gao Xuan arrives with an enormous escort and introduces himself under his real name. He brushes off Wu Yue's concerns, saying a name is meant to be called, and personally escorts the group to the capital.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> He later catches up with Chen Ping'an outside the capital, insists that they are now friends, slaps palms with him, and gives him a carriage stocked with fine wine, pills and makeup.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> Chen Ping'an later realises why Gao Xuan seemed so apologetic about the golden carp: it was originally meant for him.

### Mountain Cliff Academy

Gao Xuan often visits Mountain Cliff Academy, accompanying Li Huai and Yu Lu to the city during the Lantern Festival. When Great Li and Great Sui form an alliance, he meets Song Jixin again. Recalling that he once offered to buy Zhi Gui, he asks if the price of 10,000 taels of gold still stands, and tells Li Baoping that he is now good friends with Chen Ping'an.

The emperor of Great Sui formally enrols him in the academy. He becomes Yu Lu's roommate and inseparable friend, fishing with him at the lake. Yu Lu eventually confides that he is the former crown prince of the Lu Empire and a seventh-tier martial artist, and Gao Xuan is proud to have chosen such a friend.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

### Royal Hostage

Under the treaty between Great Li and Great Sui, Gao Xuan is sent to the Great Li Empire as a royal hostage, accompanied by an eleventh-tier gatekeeper and the old ancestor of the Geyang Gao Clan. He studies at Forest Deer Academy on Cloud Drape Mountain.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> To his surprise, no one there treats him as important. The old ancestor explains why, and Gao Xuan understands at once. In his free time, he wanders the mountains of Dragon Spring Prefecture and the streets of the small town with his bookcase, sometimes visiting Li Er's empty old house.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup>

Calling himself a wanderer seeking knowledge, he befriends Dong Shuijing over wontons and rice wine, enjoying a friendship free from transactions. Dong Shuijing hands him his shop keys so he can climb the mountain to watch the sunrise.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup> Wei Bo counts him among Chen Ping'an's allies in the Great Li Empire.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup>

When Mao Xiaodong leads Mountain Cliff Academy's students on an excursion, the white-haired scholars of Great Sui are finally able to see him. When they bow to him in tears, Gao Xuan, who had not felt especially wronged by his life as a hostage, also tears up, and bows to them in return as a junior Confucian disciple.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup>

## Possessions

- **Golden carp**: one of the five greatest fated opportunities of Jewel Small World.
- **Dragon King Basket**: the fish basket that held the golden carp, caught by Li Er.
- **Green jade seal**: an antique seal carved with coiling dragons and crouching tigers.

## Trivia

- He once offered to buy Zhi Gui from Song Jixin, who named a price of 10,000 taels of gold.
- Zheng Dafeng compares Ma Kuxuan's white cat to Gao Xuan's golden carp in significance.
