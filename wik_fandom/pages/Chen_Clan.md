# Chen Clan

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Chen_Clan)

---
> **Spoiler Warning:** This article contains spoilers from the *Sword of Coming* (*Jian Lai* / *Unsheathed*) novel and donghua.

The **Chen Clan** is not a single family but several unrelated clans that share the surname Chen. They range from the impoverished line of [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md) in the small town of [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md) to the Yingyin Chen Clan of Southern Whirl Continent, the only clan in the world honored as "Pure Confucian", and the clan of the old great sword immortal [Chen Qingdu](Chen_Qingdu.md) at the [Sword Qi Great Wall](Sword_Qi_Great_Wall.md).

## Chen Clan of the small town

According to [Old Man Yao](Old_Man_Yao.md), the small town once had two branches of the Chen Clan. One moved away long ago. The other, the branch Chen Ping'an belongs to, flourished at some point, but so long ago that no one could say whether it was five hundred, eight hundred or a thousand years. It then split into several sub-branches and slowly dwindled. Many of its graves were forgotten, and some of the mountains where they lay were sealed off by the kiln supervision officials. Old Man Yao said that even immortals could not find the branch's ancestral burial ground. For the past few centuries it had produced no one of note, and its only pride was that it had not become servants of the Four Families and Ten Clans.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>

Most of the other Chen descendants in the town did become servants, many of them for generations. The steward of the kiln supervision office noted that the Song and Zhao clans treat their Chen servants almost as family, and that their head stewards come from the clan. He remembered only one member who had stayed independent, the son of a highly skilled potter who had been praised by two kiln supervision officials and died young.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> That son is Chen Ping'an.

The town's records for the past thousand years only cover this remaining branch. Chen Dui told Chen Songfeng that it shares an ancestor with his Dragon Tail County Chen Clan. Ruan Xiu later told Chen Ping'an that he and Chen Songfeng are distant relatives from several centuries back.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

## Dragon Tail County Chen Clan

The Chen Clan of Dragon Tail County, also called the Dragon Tail Creek Chen Clan, is a powerful and wealthy clan with more than a thousand years of history. Its eldest grandson Chen Songfeng describes it as a clan of scholars rather than cultivators. Its ancestral rules forbid it from taking part in other sects' and clans' conflicts. It knows of the Yingyin Chen Clan only through a distant relationship.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

The clan later set up a private school in the small town. It is free to everyone, even the children of Lu Empire prisoners and refugees.

## Yingyin Chen Clan

The Yingyin Chen Clan is also known as the Pure Confucian Chen Clan. Chen Songfeng explains that it is the branch that left the small town long ago and moved to another continent, where it became known as an "archway that assembles the characteristics of all teachings". The steward also recalled that when that branch left, it assigned graveyard attendants to tend its graves. Those attendants were [Liu Xianyang](Liu_Xianyang.md)'s ancestors.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

The clan was once the smallest of eight branches of the Yimen Chen Clan on the Middle Earth Divine Continent, and it moved to Southern Whirl Continent to escape conflict. After its ancestor appeared, carrying a sun and a moon on his shoulders, the clan was transformed. A Confucian school and an academy were built on its land, and the road to its gates is lined with archways honoring its descendants. It is the only clan granted the title "Pure Confucian". Its descendants were proud enough not to feel ashamed when their patriarch announced that the sun on his shoulder had been borrowed by someone for a hundred years.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

Its clan leader is Chen Chun'an, who guards Southern Whirl Continent.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> He once came to the Sword Qi Great Wall, where Chen Qingdu counted him among the most interesting outsiders he had met.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> Chen Dui, a direct lineal descendant, came to the small town to worship her ancestors and took Liu Xianyang to Southern Whirl Continent. The agreement was that he would return to Ruan Qiong after twenty years.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup> The clan also used its influence behind the scenes to help Ruan Qiong become overseer of Jewel Small World after Qi Jingchun.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup>

## Chen Clan of the Sword Qi Great Wall

The Chen Clan is one of the great clans in the city north of the Sword Qi Great Wall, and its old ancestor is Chen Qingdu. He never left the wall, even for the weddings of his clan's top prodigies or the funerals of its fallen sword immortals.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> Like the Dong Clan, its power rests on generations of sword immortals, and its sword technique strengthens the bones.

Its current leader, Chen Xi, is an old sword immortal who engraved the character "Chen" on the wall.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup> Even so, he still feels like a child in front of Chen Qingdu, so the clan's members are the most reluctant of all the clans to visit the city wall.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup> After the commotion at Mirage Market, Chen Qingdu summoned the clan leader and wounded him with a single sword strike.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup> Chen Sanqiu is a young master of the clan. Chen Xi once asked Chen Qingdu in private whether Chen Sanqiu could leave with a Confucian sage to study in the Majestic World.

The clan controls the wall's sword market.

## Other Chen Clans

- A Chen Clan village in a province of the [Azure Phoenix Nation](Azure_Phoenix_Nation.md) produces many armed escorts. Its leader, known as Chen's Archway, is so well known that his name helps his clansmen find work.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup>

## Trivia

- The accounts of how the town's Chen branches relate to the Yingyin Chen Clan do not fully agree. Chen Songfeng says Chen Dui's clan is the branch that left the town. Ruan Xiu says her clan is unrelated to the town's Chen Clans even a thousand or two thousand years back. Ruan Qiong also tells an old Pure Confucian scholar that the two town branches have nothing to do with his clan.
- That old scholar was saddened by the state of the town's Chen descendants. In Southern Whirl Continent, he said, no Chen with a name in the genealogy would ever become a servant, however poor.
- Cao Xi suspected that either the Dragon Tail Creek Chen Clan had tampered with its records, or one of its ancestors had been so fortunate that he used up his descendants' luck for dozens of generations.
- Old Man Yao suggested that the fortune of Chen Ping'an's branch may have been taken by the other branch.
