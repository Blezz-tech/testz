## Appearance

Xu Yuanxia is described as a **tall and sturdy** man with a **big beard**. He has a **rough** and **unrefined** appearance. He has a **muscular** build and is often seen with a **saber**. He also is noted to have an **unruly and untamed appearance**.

Xu Yuanxia is frequently depicted wearing **simple attire**, and he is also seen wearing **armor** on occasion. His clothing is sometimes described as **old** or **worn**. He is said to have **sideburns like halberds**, and he has a **dark and weathered** complexion. His face is sometimes compared to that of a **furious war deity**. He is also described as having **thick eyebrows** and a **wide mouth**.

Despite his imposing appearance, Xu Yuanxia is not necessarily intimidating, and is seen as a **hero** by some. He is also portrayed as having a **strong and healthy** physique. His overall appearance is that of a **rugged and powerful martial artist**.

## Personality

Xu Yuanxia is portrayed as a **bold and straightforward** individual with a **strong sense of justice**. He is **unreserved and outspoken**, and does not hesitate to voice his opinions. Despite his rough exterior, he is **kind-hearted and compassionate**, and is willing to help others in need. He is also described as being **loyal** and **protective** of his friends.

Xu Yuanxia is **passionate about martial arts** and is proud of his abilities. He can be **boastful and arrogant** at times, and he enjoys telling stories about his past exploits. He is also depicted as being **somewhat naive** and **easily impressed**, and is easily swayed by flattery. While he can be **quick to anger**, he is also quick to forgive. He is also **shown to be quite perceptive** of others. He is **fond of drinking alcohol** and enjoys the company of his friends.

Xu Yuanxia is also portrayed as being **somewhat sentimental and nostalgic**, often thinking about his past and his family. He has a **strong sense of responsibility**, and he feels a duty to protect the innocent and fight against evil forces. Though he is **not particularly interested in romantic relationships**, he admits to having liked a woman before leaving home. In addition, it is mentioned that he is **"not a stingy person"** and also **"not good with words,"** and that he has a tendency to **overthink things**.

## Background

Xu Yuanxia's background is that of a **soldier** who served in the border army, where he fought in many battles. He is also described as a **fifth-tier martial artist**. Although he is a skilled martial artist, he comes from a **highly educated family**, which is considered a prominent clan in his county. Despite his family background, he chose to join the army, surprising many people.

Xu Yuanxia left the army to travel the world, but he continued to encounter death and suffering, which caused him some distress. He is said to have some knowledge of **battle formations**. He is also an **earth immortal**. He has a connection to the **Azure Phoenix Nation**, and was involved in bringing an ash urn back to a person's family. He also attended the Liberation Rite of Water and Land and the Heavenly Worship Ceremony with Zhang Shanfeng.

Xu Yuanxia is said to be from a **well-off family** and had a marriage arranged for him before he left home, and he admits to liking the woman he was meant to marry. He is also described as being familiar with the local customs and traditions of the places he visits. He has traveled to the **Azure Phoenix Nation** and the **Great Li Empire**. It is also mentioned that he has been wandering around for more than ten years. While he is a skilled martial artist, he is also described as someone who is **"not a stingy person"** and **"not good with words,"** and that he has a tendency to **overthink things** [previous conversation].
