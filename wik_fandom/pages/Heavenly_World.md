# Heavenly World

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Heavenly_World)

---
**Location and Nature**: The Heavenly World is presented as a realm **beyond the Majestic World**. It's a place where Daoist sages reside, and it seems to be the ultimate destination for those who attain the highest levels of cultivation.

**Daoist Influence**: The Heavenly World is heavily associated with **Daoism**. Daoist sages in this world focus on increasing the height of White Jade Capital, adding layer upon layer above its peak, rather than expanding their territories like those in the Majestic World. This suggests a focus on internal development and spiritual refinement over worldly expansion.

**White Jade Capital**: The White Jade Capital is a significant structure within the Heavenly World. It has **four floors** that help cultivators suppress their internal demons using different Daoist techniques. These techniques are described as the most orthodox methods of the Daoist Sect, and they provide a smooth path to suppressing internal demons, which is a dream for many cultivators.

**Immortal Ascension**: The Heavenly World is the place where cultivators aspire to ascend. In ancient times, there were seemingly Immortal Ascending Platforms scattered around the world to aid Qi refiners in their ascension. The sources describe an ascension as a grand event, accompanied by heavenly maidens scattering flowers, iridescent clouds, and brilliant rays of light. Those who ascend with their complete physical body and soul are seen as having received a high-level fated opportunity.

**Connection to the Majestic World**: There are mentions of individuals who have traveled from the Heavenly World to the Majestic World. For example, a Daoist priest is said to have traveled to the south from the Heavenly World, where he engaged in a battle and acquired a treasured pearl. Furthermore, there are mentions of a rift between the two worlds. There is at least one individual who has the power to open a path between these two worlds.

**Rulers and Sages**: The Heavenly World is inhabited by powerful Daoist sages who possess immense power and influence. The Dao Ancestor is said to have a Lotus Flower World which is one of the 36 small worlds, and just a single lotus leaf in that world is larger than the capital city of the Great Li Empire. These sages are focused on developing the White Jade Capital and helping those with a true heart in cultivation, though they also act as gatekeepers.

**Hierarchical Structure:** There is a sense of hierarchy and order within the Heavenly World. There is mention of an Etiquette Sage and rules laid down by them. This suggests a well-established social and spiritual order.

**Relationship with Other Worlds**: The Heavenly World seems to be a realm that stands apart from other worlds, including the small worlds. It seems to be a destination for those who have transcended the limitations of the lower worlds. Some sources suggest that the Heavenly World is a place of immense power and that a chosen few may ascend there, implying a form of reward or recognition for those who achieve a certain level of spiritual progress.

**The End Tier**: The 13th tier of Qi refinement, known as the Ascension Tier, is considered the End Tier, similar to the 10th tier of pure martial arts. This level of cultivation is associated with the ability to ascend into the Heavenly World.
