# Dragon Spring Prefecture

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon_Spring_Prefecture)

---
Dragon Spring Prefecture is a territory of the Great Li Empire on [Eastern Treasured Vial Continent](Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent.md). It was formed when Jewel Small World, the small floating realm that was home to Chen Ping'an's [small town](Small_Town.md), fell to the ground and was annexed by the empire. It was first organized as Dragon Spring County before later being elevated to a full prefecture.

## History

Jewel Small World was the smallest of the 36 small worlds suspended above the Great Li Empire's territory, measuring only around 500 kilometers across.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> Despite its size, it produced an unusually high number of gifted cultivators and martial artists each generation, which made its continued independence from the empire a source of frustration for the Great Li Empire and other powers on the continent.

The small world's fall was tied to the death of its guardian, [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md). Qi Jingchun was killed fighting off a coordinated plot against him and his teachings, and Jewel Small World came crashing down out of the sky as a consequence of his death.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> [Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md), who had engineered the plot, treated the small world's fall as a secondary outcome of his true goal of severing Qi Jingchun's lineage and beliefs from the world.

Once it settled on the ground, the former small world was annexed and reorganized as Dragon Spring County under the Great Li Empire's Eternal Splendor Prefecture. Wu Yuan, previously a subordinate of [Song Changjing](Song_Changjing.md), was installed as its first county magistrate. The empire had a longstanding rule barring the character "dragon" from territory names, but an exception was made for the new county because of its origins as Jewel Small World.

Dragon Spring County was later elevated into Dragon Spring Prefecture. Wu Yuan was promoted from county magistrate to prefectural overseer.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> Since the prefecture kept the "Dragon Spring" name, the subordinate county covering the original small town was renamed Locust Yellow County, and a new county magistrate with the surname Yuan took over its daily administration. The prefectural office was built in the mountains to the north, while the county office stayed in the small town.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

## Geography

Before annexation, the small town was governed by the Four Surnames and Ten Clans, a group of powerful local families with the Lu Clan at their head.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup> After the area became Dragon Spring County, the Great Li Empire's imperial court began appointing formal mountain and river gods throughout the territory, including mountain gods for Cloud Drape Mountain, Lamp Bearer Mountain, and Downtrodden Mountain, along with river gods for the Dragon Whisker River and the Iron Talisman River.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

Among the prefecture's notable mountains is Dragonspine Mountain, which contains a deposit of Dragon Slaying Platform, a rare black stone prized as the finest whetstone material on the continent and capable of sharpening even legendary swords.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> Other notable mountains include Divine Elegance Mountain, Gallant Spear Mountain, Cloud Drape Mountain, and Lamp Bearer Mountain.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> Downtrodden Mountain, along with Treasured Scripture Mountain, Rainbow Cloud Mountain, Immortal Herb Mountain, and True Jewel Mountain, were purchased by Chen Ping'an after the county's mountains were opened up for sale to its former residents.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

## Significance

Dragon Spring Prefecture's reputation for producing talented cultivators, combined with the presence of Dragon Slaying Platform deposits, continued to draw outside cultivators, demons, and spirits to the territory long after it stopped being an independent small world.

## Trivia

- Dragon Spring Prefecture is one of the few territories in the Great Li Empire permitted to keep the character "dragon" in its name, an exception granted because of its origin as Jewel Small World.
