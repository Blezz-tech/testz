## Appearance

Wei Jin is a sword immortal who wears white robes. He carries his renowned sword, High Radiance, in a scabbard at his waist.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> In his earlier years, he was known for traveling with a white donkey and a silver-white Sword Nurturing Gourd, though both items eventually ended up in [A'Liang](A'Liang.md)'s possession. To the children he encounters during his travels, he cuts an impressive figure: his sword arrives before he does, sword qi hovering around his white robes as he descends from the sky.

## Personality

Wei Jin has a cold personality. He likes to keep to himself and wanders from place to place, which [Ruan Qiong](Ruan_Qiong.md) attributes to peculiar idiosyncrasies.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> Even after becoming a sword immortal, he prefers to ride his white donkey rather than fly on his sword, often swaying drunkenly on its back and sharing wine with the animal from his palm. A'Liang once teased him for being pedantic about riding such a slow mount.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

Despite his aloof reputation, he is polite and patient even toward children. [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) once called him "a very good person" for being willing to explain things to them despite his status. [Lin Shouyi](Lin_Shouyi.md), however, noticed that Wei Jin seemed to look down on Chen Ping'an and was the least optimistic about his prospects among the children.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> Years later, Chen Ping'an still recalled Wei Jin's apathetic gaze toward him but continued to consider him a good person.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

He is a passionate swordsman who longs for battle. After hearing A'Liang's stories about the Sword Qi Great Wall, he yearned to visit Stalactite Mountain. Following his clash at Lady Chu's residence, he sighed that he had not fought to his heart's content.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> In matters of the heart, however, he is helpless. He fell in love at first sight with the Daoist nun [He Xiaoliang](He_Xiaoliang.md), widely lauded as one of the brightest talents on the continent, and pursued her for years. His feelings were completely unrequited, and the story became an amusing tale that spread through the cultivation circles of Eastern Treasured Vile Continent.

At the Sword Qi Great Wall, he remained frank and honest in dealing with others. When Chen Qingdu offered him guidance, Wei Jin admitted openly that he had always felt guilty about attributing the lucky circumstances of his advancement to his own abilities.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> He told the old great sword immortal that as the only sword immortal from the Eastern Treasured Vile Continent, he wanted to come to the Sword Qi Great Wall without selfish desires and hoped to leave in a gallant manner.

## Background

Wei Jin is the pillar of the Divine Platform, a small branch of [Wind Snow Temple](Sects.md#wind-snow-temple) with very few disciples. At a young age, he was found by one of Wind Snow Temple's patriarchs during the patriarch's travels and taken on as a disciple. Despite being only around twenty when he arrived at the sect, his seniority through this master meant that cultivators over a hundred years old had to refer to him as grandmaster.

After his master perished during a failed breakthrough attempt, the Divine Platform branch had few bright talents left, and Wei Jin became increasingly detached from the main sect. He spent seven or eight years traveling the world, returning only on his master's death anniversaries. He used his prized Sword Nurturing Gourd not to nurture flying swords but to carry wine, and was often so drunk that he would lie on his donkey's back and let it carry him wherever it pleased. This earned him the reputation of a drunken sword immortal.

At barely forty years old, Wei Jin broke through to the Upper Five Tiers as a terrestrial sword immortal, becoming the youngest Upper Five Tier cultivator on Eastern Treasured Vile Continent. He broke the record previously held by [Wind Lightning Field](Sects.md#wind-lightning-field)'s Li Tuanjing, and together with Song Changjing, the two were regarded as the undisputed "jade walls" of the south and north among the continent's young cultivators. His rapid advancement was partly thanks to guidance from A'Liang, who had sought him out specifically to teach him swordsmanship. A'Liang used a risky method of utilizing Wei Jin's future potential to allow him to break through earlier than expected.

Right after exiting seclusion, Wei Jin rushed to Lady Chu's residence and shattered its night sky barrier with a single strike that severed a mountain range. The Mohist swordsman Xu Ruo blocked his follow-up strike by pushing his own sword just one inch from its scabbard. Wei Jin then accompanied Chen Ping'an and the children on part of their journey north, giving them a Peace and Safety Tablet from Wind Snow Temple and gifting [Li Huai](Li_Huai.md) five small clay statues before departing to sharpen his swords at the Dragon Slaying Platform in Jewel Small World.

He later challenged Heavenly Lord [Xie Shi](Xie_Shi.md) of the [Northern Complete Reed Continent](Northern_Complete_Reed_Continent.md), who was cultivating in seclusion on the Eastern Treasured Vile Continent. Wei Jin lost the fight, but the cultivators of his home continent felt no resentment toward him. Instead, they took pride in his willingness to stand up for their lands.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> Afterward, he left the continent alone, flying on his sword toward Stalactite Mountain and the Sword Qi Great Wall.

At the Sword Qi Great Wall, Wei Jin was given the small thatched cottage behind Chen Qingdu's own dwelling, a privilege previously reserved for Cao Ci and Chen Ping'an. He was fond of Zuo You, one of the few swordsmen on the wall that Zuo You would bother speaking to, and grew to respect Chen Ping'an after witnessing his role as the Hidden Official. When the sword immortals of the Majestic World gathered at the Spring Banner Inn on Stalactite Mountain to negotiate with the intercontinental ship administrators, Wei Jin met with the administrators from his home continent and declared that if he reached the Immortal Tier and survived the war, he would travel to the Northern Complete Reed Continent to challenge Xie Shi again.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

[Chen Qingdu](Chen_Qingdu.md) told Wei Jin that he should not remain at the Sword Qi Great Wall forever and offered him three continents to choose from after the war: the Southern Whirl Continent, the Circling Ascension Continent, and the Golden Armor Continent. When Wei Jin asked if this was his only option, the old great sword immortal replied: "Why are you asking me? Go and ask your sword."<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> Wei Jin chose the Circling Ascension Continent, but resolved to first earn enough battlefield contributions for the Divine Platform to match Xie Songhua's. He felt he owed that much to his late master's memory.

## Trivia

- [Li Baoping](Li_Baoping.md) later said that the small gourd she owned was "swindled" from Wei Jin, and that it was better than her saber, Auspicious Talisman.
- Chen Ping'an long regarded Wei Jin's strike against Lady Chu's barrier as one of the flying sword strikes that left a lasting mark on his understanding of the sword, alongside Zuo You's and Fan Junmao's.
- Li Huai felt Wei Jin was "not as good as A'Liang" and made his clay swordsman statue kneel and beg for forgiveness.
- * Wei Jin was the only sword immortal from the Eastern Treasured Vile Continent at the Sword Qi Great Wall. Chen Qingdu noted that things he did not wish for often came to him in abundance, while things he sorely desired always seemed to pass him by.
- A'Liang's reason for seeking out Wei Jin and teaching him swordsmanship was not out of charity. As Chen Qingdu explained, A'Liang sincerely hoped that Wei Jin could one day stand shoulder to shoulder with him, and he felt the same way toward all like-minded cultivators walking the same path behind him.
