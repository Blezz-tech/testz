# Militarians

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Militarians)

---
The Militarians are a distinct group of cultivators in Eastern Treasured Vial Continent, known for their unique approach to cultivation and their significant role in the empires and the wider cultivation world. They are distinct from Confucianists, Daoists, and Buddhists, though they can also be cultivators, Qi refiners, martial artists, and swordsmen.

**Key Characteristics of Militarians:**

- **Emphasis on Physical and Spiritual Strength:** Militarians cultivate both their physical bodies and their spiritual power, making them formidable in battle. They value strength, discipline, and a pragmatic approach to conflict.
- **Close Ties to Empires:** Militarians often serve in the armies and governments of the empires, acting as generals, bodyguards, and military officers. They are seen as essential to maintaining order and defending the empires from internal and external threats.
- **Pragmatic and Adaptive:** Militarian rules and practices are described as "broad but hollow" [6], allowing for flexibility and adaptability in different situations. This pragmatism sets them apart from other groups like the Confucianists, who are more bound by tradition and strict rules.
- **Tolerance of Karmic Consequences:** Unlike some other cultivation paths, Militarians are less concerned with the karmic repercussions of their actions, particularly when it comes to killing. This willingness to act decisively, even if it means incurring karmic debt, contributes to their reputation as ruthless and effective warriors.

**Cultivation Methods and Techniques:**

- **Seeking Fortune and Avoiding Calamity:** Militarians have an almost instinctive ability to predict the near future and sense danger, allowing them to seek out opportunities and avoid potential threats. This ability is seen as a key advantage in battle and in navigating the dangers of the cultivation world.
- **Nurturing Heroic Spirits:** A powerful, though controversial technique, Militarians can cultivate a heroic spirit within their bodies, merging their own soul with the powerful yin soul of a deceased warrior. This fusion grants them immense strength and combat prowess.

**Notable Militarians and Institutions:**

- **True Martial Mountain:** A sacred land for Militarians in Eastern Treasured Vial Continent. It is a training ground for aspiring Militarians and a source of knowledge and power for those who follow this path. Disciples of True Martial Mountain are known for their independent nature and their adherence to a loose set of rules.
- **Song Changjing:** An influential military leader in the Great Li Empire, responsible for enforcing a rule that restricts Qi refiners from participating in battles. This rule highlights the complex relationship between Militarians and other cultivators within the empire.
- **Ruan Qiong:** A high-ranking Militarian who plays a pivotal role in establishing order and governance in the town. His actions demonstrate the pragmatic and adaptive nature of Militarian leadership, as he sets rules and negotiates with various factions to maintain stability.
- **The Militarian Swordsman:** A character who serves as a mentor to Ma Kuxuan, a young aspiring Militarian. He embodies the values of strength, discipline, and pragmatic action that characterize this path.

**Strengths and Weaknesses of the Militarian Path:**

**Strengths:**

- **Unmatched Combat Prowess:** Their focus on both physical and spiritual cultivation makes them incredibly powerful warriors, capable of facing down even the most formidable opponents.
- **Decisiveness and Ruthlessness:** Their willingness to act without hesitation and their tolerance of karmic consequences make them effective in achieving their goals, even if it means resorting to violence.
- **Strategic Acumen:** Their ability to predict the future and assess situations allows them to make strategic decisions that give them an advantage in conflicts.

**Weaknesses:**

- **Potential for Corruption:** The emphasis on power and the tolerance of killing can lead some Militarians down a dark path, turning them into ruthless tyrants or bloodthirsty warmongers.
- **Lack of Moral Restraint:** Their disregard for karmic repercussions can have negative consequences in the long run, as their actions may generate karmic debt that will eventually need to be repaid.
- **Overreliance on Strength:** Their focus on physical and martial might can sometimes blind them to more subtle approaches to resolving conflicts. This can make them less effective in situations that require diplomacy or compromise.

**The Militarians play a complex and multifaceted role in the world of Eastern Treasured Vial Continent.** They are both protectors and potential threats, wielding their power to maintain order and defend the empires, but also capable of great violence and destruction if they succumb to their darker impulses. Their pragmatism and adaptability make them valuable assets in a world fraught with danger and uncertainty, but their disregard for karmic consequences and their potential for corruption make them a force to be reckoned with.
