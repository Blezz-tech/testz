**Ma Zhan** is the junior brother of [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md) and an unofficial disciple of the [Scholarly Sage](Scholarly_Sage.md). He worked as the janitor of the school in the small town and was killed while escorting the last of its students to Mountain Cliff Academy.

## Appearance

Ma Zhan is an old man with white hair. On the day he led the students out of town, he dressed in a scholar's robe much like Qi Jingchun's, with a jade pendant at his waist, a tall hat, and his hair neatly combed.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>

## Personality

At the school the children knew him as a kindly old man. Once Qi Jingchun was gone, he became stern and commanding, and silenced Li Huai with a single glare when the boy tried to get Li Baoping in trouble.

He held firmly to his teacher's words and could not accept Qi Jingchun's choice to stay in the town and give up his future for its people. He blamed Buddhist ideas for leading his senior brother astray and grew angry when Qi Jingchun said their teacher was not right about everything.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> He enjoyed scholarly talk and drinking, and he quickly warmed to Cui Minghuang over shared discussion of the classics.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

Cui Chan considered him ambitious beyond his ability and the weakest in mind among the Scholarly Sage's students, and said he betrayed his companion for the promise of becoming a mountain master.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> The Scholarly Sage judged that one wrong step led Ma Zhan into many more, and that although he came to his senses, he chose to make amends only through his death.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

## Background

Ma Zhan first met Qi Jingchun about 80 years before the story, when both studied under the same teacher. Before they left for Mountain Cliff Academy, their teacher gave each of them a saying, and Ma Zhan's was about heaven and earth raising a ruler who in turn governs them. In his student days he and Mao Xiaodong were too intimidated by Zuo You's cold manner to approach him.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

He later served as the janitor of Qi Jingchun's school in the small town. While sweeping the yard, he often noticed a boy crouching under the window to listen to lessons, and he later told the coach driver about this boy, Chen Ping'an.

Shortly before leaving, Ma Zhan sat outside Qi Jingchun's study and argued with him over whether to stay in Jewel Small World. Qi Jingchun gave him a reply about knowing when to retreat and when to hold firm, which left Ma Zhan puzzled, and then asked him to take the children to Mountain Cliff Academy. Ma Zhan agreed.

He told the students that Qi Jingchun had stepped down as teacher and mountain master and that he would lead the trip in his place. He set off with Li Baoping, Li Huai, Lin Shouyi, Dong Shuijing and Shi Chunjia, travelling alongside [Cui Minghuang](Cui_Minghuang.md) of Lake View Academy.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> On Cui Chan's orders, Cui Minghuang befriended him and convinced him that he could succeed Qi Jingchun as mountain master, and then that he should break with Mountain Cliff Academy altogether to become the first mountain master of a new academy.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

While checking the children's homework one day, Ma Zhan said he needed to talk over their route with Cui Minghuang. Neither came back, and Li Huai later found Ma Zhan badly wounded and bleeding heavily. Kept alive briefly by the three ancestral locust leaves Li Baoping carried, he held her hand and told her to secretly warn Chen Ping'an not to trust anyone from Lake View Academy or the Great Li capital. According to Cui Chan, Ma Zhan finally saw that he had been fooled and that Qi Jingchun had known of his betrayal all along. He gave up his chance at reincarnation to injure Cui Minghuang so the children could get back to the town. The children buried him on the road.

During his last walk through the town, Qi Jingchun visited the grave of his junior brother.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

## Trivia

- Li Huai joked that Cui Minghuang must be Ma Zhan's illegitimate son or grandson because of how close the two seemed.
- The Scholarly Sage suspected that Ma Zhan's betrayal hurt Qi Jingchun more than all of Cui Chan's plots.
- The Scholarly Sage described Ma Zhan as a piece moved by the real schemer behind the scenes rather than a player in the game himself.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup>
