**Song Jixin** grew up next door to [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) on [Clay Vase Alley](Small_Town.md#clay-vase-alley), where the town believed he was the illegitimate son of the kiln supervision official. He is in fact the eldest son of the Great Li emperor, and he later becomes a prince and the feudal lord of [Old Dragon City](Old_Dragon_City.md).

## Appearance

Song Jixin is a good-looking young man, and Chen Ping'an counts him among the handsome people of his own age he has known. As a boy he liked to sit with his legs crossed and play with a bamboo folding fan, tapping it against his knee or his palm as he talked. *(Chapter 47)* He grows taller than Chen Ping'an after leaving the small town. 

## Personality

As a boy Song Jixin was sharp, proud and quick with a cutting remark. He loved fine and pretty things and looked down on anything crude or plain. When he saw something good that he could not have, he tended to sneer at it. He mocked Chen Ping'an constantly and was sure his neighbour would stay poor all his life.

Much of that scorn grew out of envy. Cui Dongshan explains that Song Jixin had money, books and a maidservant, yet he could never understand why someone as poor as Chen Ping'an had at least had two loving parents when he had none he could claim. *(Chapter 161)* Later he learns that the rumours he grew up with were worse than he knew. The things the women of the small town said about him left him with inner demons that even immortals cannot remove.

He is clever and reads people well. He knows how to bargain, and he flew into a rage when [Fu Nanhua](Fu_Nanhua.md) tried to buy his Mandrill Teapot for far less than it was worth. *(Chapter 17)* He can also be cold. He once thought about having Fu Nanhua kill [Liu Xianyang](Liu_Xianyang.md) simply because it would cost him very little. *(Chapter 39)* He has a playful streak too. In the Fu Clan gardens he spent a happy few minutes teaching a parrot to insult his own royal name.

As he grows older he loosens up. By the time he meets Chen Ping'an again as a prince, he jokes and laughs easily and admits he still does not like the look of his old neighbour. As feudal lord he chooses his own staff and cares only about their ability, not their background.

## Background

### Clay Vase Alley

Song Jixin was born Song Mu, the eldest son of the Great Li emperor Song Zhengchun and an imperial concubine. For the sake of the clan and the empire, his mother sent him to the Jewel Small World as an infant to gather as much fortune and fated opportunity as he could for the Song Clan. The imperial records said he had died soon after birth. His name was crossed out of the genealogy, and his younger brother Song He took both the name and the place of the eldest son. *(Chapter 484 (1))*

[Song Yuzhang](Song_Yuzhang.md), the kiln supervision official, raised him in the small town. Townsfolk whispered that Song Jixin was Song Yuzhang's illegitimate son, and Song Yuzhang never denied it. He even showed the boy something close to a father's affection. Song Jixin resented him and admired him at the same time, and part of him wished the rumour were true. *(Chapter 641 (4))* He studied under [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md) at the town's private school.

When the girl who would become [Zhi Gui](Zhi_Gui.md) escaped from a dragon locking well, she collapsed in the snow outside Chen Ping'an's house. She was drawn by the dragon qi coming from Song Jixin's home next door, so she declared herself Song Jixin's maidservant, and she served him from then on. *(Chapter 86 (1))*

Song Jixin told the young Chen Ping'an that being born on the fifth day of the fifth month had cursed his parents, and that they could not be reborn while he lived in their house. Chen Ping'an broke a promise to his mother because of it. When [Old Man Yao](Old_Man_Yao.md) told him the truth, he ran back to town in the rain and nearly strangled Song Jixin against a wall. The memory haunted Song Jixin for years. *(Chapter 89)* Chen Ping'an later says what truly turned him against Song Jixin was something else. When [Liu Xianyang](Liu_Xianyang.md) was being beaten nearly to death as a child, Song Jixin squatted on a wall and cheered the attackers on.

### Discovering his identity

When the Jewel Small World opened to outsiders, Song Jixin befriended Tao Zi of Sun Scorch Mountain, who called him "Firewood Collecting Brother." He even had his uncle force the Li Clan to let her out so she could visit him. *(Chapter 57)* The new kiln supervision official, [Song Changjing](Song_Changjing.md), turned out to be his uncle, and Song Changjing revealed the truth of his birth.

Qi Jingchun left him six books as a parting gift. Song Jixin looked down on the three elementary ones and left them behind, and they eventually went to Chen Ping'an, just as Qi Jingchun had foreseen. Song Jixin threw the keys to his house into Chen Ping'an's yard and left for the capital with Song Changjing and Zhi Gui.

In the capital his father took him to the White Jade Capital tower, where he struggled up level after level and won the recognition of its twelve flying swords. Moments later the swords were gone, and he broke down in tears. That night his father sat with him and told him plainly that he was his son. As the emperor walked away, Song Jixin called after him, "Father!" *(Chapter 116)*

### Prince of the Great Li Empire

Song Jixin was presented to the world as a new prince and sent on a visit to Old Dragon City. Reunited with his mother, he found he could not return her feelings, and the meeting with her and Song He was awkward for all three of them. Later he visited Chen Ping'an at Mountain Cliff Academy. He spoke frankly about how distant he was from his mother and asked Chen Ping'an for help with the struggling mountain god temple on [Downtrodden Mountain](Downtrodden_Mountain.md). *(Chapter 413 (1))*

After the old emperor died, the question of succession split the court's most powerful men. Song Changjing backed Song Jixin, while Cui Chan backed his own disciple Song He. *(Chapter 479 (2))* Song He took the throne. Song Jixin was made a first-rank king and feudal lord of Old Dragon City, with the task of overseeing the empire's new secondary capital from afar. The Fu Clan gave up half of the inner city for his residence, and he won goodwill by leaving the way the city was run unchanged. *(Chapter 528 (2))* By the time of the war in the Savage World, he is buried in the work of governing. He has to deal with the Fu Clan, Sun Scorch Mountain and Light Breeze City, and he senses that Zhi Gui is becoming harder to read.

## Trivia

- Song Mu is his name in the imperial genealogy. Those who knew him in the small town still call him Song Jixin.
- He kept a large red chest of curios. A green gourd left behind by Song Yuzhang was the piece he felt mattered most.
- When Gao Xuan tried to buy Zhi Gui years later, Song Jixin reminded him that he had once asked 10,000 taels of gold for her, then asked how much the whole Great Sui Nation cost.
- He kept a small four-legged snake as a pet. A horn-like bump later grew on its forehead.
- Qi Jingchun left a wisp of spring wind on three of the town's children: Chen Ping'an, Zhao Yao and Song Jixin.
- There are very few people he resents as much as [Ma Kuxuan](Ma_Kuxuan.md).
- [Pei Qian](Pei_Qian.md), watching scenes from her master's childhood, declared that Song Jixin and Zhi Gui both deserved to die.
