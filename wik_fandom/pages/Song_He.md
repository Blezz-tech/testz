**Song He** is the younger son of the Great Li emperor Song Zhengchun and an imperial concubine. Raised at his mother's side in the capital while his elder brother [Song Jixin](Song_Jixin.md) was hidden in the [Small Town](Small_Town.md), he becomes the Great Li Empire's new emperor and [Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md)'s student.

## Personality

In public Song He is courteous and humble, and he has a fine reputation among the empire's officials. Some of that comes from his mother's careful upbringing, and some from his own ability.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> In private he is sharper and more cynical. Talking with his mother, he wondered aloud whether his uncle meant to seize the throne, and he pointed out that Song Changjing could simply arrange an assassination if he wanted to.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

He has a competitive streak. After meeting [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md), he told his mother that he would not have done much worse in Chen Ping'an's place. She snapped back, asking whether Qi Jingchun would ever have chosen him. He apologised at once and admitted he had only said it out of envy. Even as emperor he stood and took her scolding without a trace of resentment.

He is respectful toward the past. After taking the throne he did not remove a single item his father had left in the imperial study, and he quietly took out a few things he had added himself once the room felt cluttered. He also has firm opinions on art. He thinks the paintings of Bian Ye, a favourite of earlier emperors, are poor in both skill and character.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

## Background

### The younger son

For the sake of the clan and the empire, Song He's mother sent her eldest son away to the Jewel Small World as an infant and had him recorded as dead. Song He took over his brother's name and his place as eldest son. She kept Song He by her side in the capital, where he won over the officials on her behalf, and she poured all her hopes into him.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> When she was later banished to the Palace of Eternal Spring, Song He went with her.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

When Song Jixin came out of the Jewel Small World and met his mother for the first time, Song He wept openly beside them. Song Jixin found the reunion awkward for all three of them.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

### Becoming emperor

Song He was Cui Chan's student. After the old emperor's death, the succession split the two most powerful men in the empire. Song Changjing supported Song Jixin, and Cui Chan supported Song He.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> Song He won the throne and became the emperor ruling more territory than any before him on the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent. Cui Chan worried that someone so newly crowned could easily be goaded into rash decisions by those around him.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

He travelled with his mother on an intercontinental ship to meet Chen Ping'an, carrying himself with great humility. Privately he was not much interested in Chen Ping'an and had only come because his mother insisted.

### Reign

Song He visited the Dragon Spring Prefecture in person with two of his chief ministers to congratulate [Wei Bo](Wei_Bo.md) when the mountain god broke through to the Upper Five Tiers. He presented Wei Bo with a pseudo-celestial treasure from the imperial vault.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

When they are alone, Song He treats Cui Chan as a pupil treats his teacher. After one small court meeting, Cui Chan stayed behind and laid out a plan reaching a hundred years into the future. Song He said he had been waiting for this day all along. He was a little disappointed when Cui Chan advised him that the Song Clan should never try to conquer other continents. He bowed and promised to remember his teacher's words when Cui Chan told him he would only be truly capable once his father's old ministers stayed loyal to him even without Cui Chan in the room.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup>

## Trivia

- An old boxwood chair has stood in the imperial study for about a hundred years. Like his father, Song He touched it as a child but never sat in it.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup>
- His father's paintings in the study are kept in a porcelain brush holder made in a Jewel Small World dragon kiln.
- His mother told him that even as emperor he would have to learn to play dumb, in front of his uncle and in front of the scholarly officials.
