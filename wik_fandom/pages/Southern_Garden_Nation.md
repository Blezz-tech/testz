# Southern Garden Nation

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Southern_Garden_Nation)

---
**Southern Garden Nation** is one of the four lands of the [Lotus Flower Blessed Land](Lotus_Flower_Blessed_Land.md) on the [Parasol Leaf Continent](Parasol_Leaf_Continent.md). Founded by [Wei Xian](Wei_Xian.md) and ruled by the Wei Clan, it was regarded as the most powerful nation in that world. It was the setting of the great battles of the Upper Ten Elites during [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md)'s time in the blessed land. When the blessed land was later divided, Southern Garden Nation became the land owned by Downtrodden Mountain.

## Geography

The Lotus Flower Blessed Land held four regions. These were Southern Garden Nation, Pine Song Nation, [Northern Jin Nation](Northern_Jin_Nation.md) and the grasslands beyond them. *(Chapter 566)* Though it was the strongest of these, Wei Xian later admitted that it was only the size of a few counties of the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md). *(Chapter 404)* It had a population of around twenty million. *(Chapter 530)*

The capital is divided into eighty-one districts. As in many other capitals, the north is wealthy and the south poor, martial artists cluster in the east and scholars in the west. *(Chapter 308)* Notable places in and around the city include Champion Scholar Alley and Bull Mountain, which stands just outside the walls. *(Chapter 307)* The capital is also home to the Vajra Temple, the largest temple in that world and the one with the most monks. *(Chapter 311)* The White River Temple, one of the capital's four major temples, and the small Heart Manifestation Temple are there as well. The nation has its own Five Mountains.

## History

Southern Garden Nation was founded by Wei Xian, who rose from a poor family through the army to become its first emperor. He was remembered as the greatest martial artist of the past thousand years at breaking enemy lines. *(Chapter 314)* Under him, the nation's mounted troops became famous across that world. They drove the grassland horsemen back, and the rulers of the grasslands nearly surrendered. *(Chapter 330)*

After Wei Xian, the nation enjoyed several centuries of peace under rulers who were neither brilliant nor incompetent. *(Chapter 306)* About sixty years before Chen Ping An arrived, the capital was the scene of a chaotic battle among martial artists. During it, [Ding Ying](Ding_Ying.md) won the silver lotus flower hat and fought [Zhu Lian](Zhu_Lian.md). Some time afterwards [Zhong Qiu](Zhong_Qiu.md) settled in the nation and became its imperial preceptor. He spent decades strengthening it, and the court came to openly hope to unite the four lands. *(Chapter 320)*

Chen Ping An came to the capital searching for the Observing Dao Temple. *(Chapter 304)* The Reverence Pagoda chose Bull Mountain as the site of that era's ascension. The great battles that followed, involving Ding Ying, [Yu Zhenyi](Yu_Zhenyi.md), Zhong Qiu, Chen Ping An and others, tore through the capital and its city wall. The emperor placed the city under martial law whenever grandmasters fought. Afterwards Yu Zhenyi built a cottage on the ruins of Bull Mountain, and the court threatened to remove him by force unless he moved his Lake Mountain Faction into the nation. *(Chapter 323)*

The Lotus Flower Blessed Land was later split into four, and the part containing Southern Garden Nation passed to Downtrodden Mountain. There it was called the Lotus Root Blessed Land. *(Chapter 527)* Only the people of Southern Garden Nation kept their souls whole. The inhabitants of the other three regions became like blank paper, alive but without intent. Downtrodden Mountain fed the land immortal coins until it rose to a mid-grade blessed land. It then planned to teach the court how to appoint mountain gods and river gods. Zhong Qiu later said that if the blessed land had not been divided, the nation would have been on course to unite the whole world. *(Chapter 602)*

## Government

The nation is ruled by the Wei Clan. At the time of the great battles the emperor was Wei Liang, and his family included Empress Zhou Shuzhen, Crown Prince Wei Yan, a second prince and a young princess. The empress was secretly a disciple of the Mirror Heart Hall. That sect had agents in the courts of all four lands, and through her it had placed its own people deep inside the palace. She was also the leader of the Reverence Pagoda, which publishes the list of the Upper Ten Elites. *(Chapter 316)*

The imperial preceptor Zhong Qiu lives beside the palace and was the real force behind the court for many years. The nation also kept a network of spies who worked between the court and the martial world. After the blessed land was divided, Wei Liang decided to take up cultivation and stepped down. He was succeeded by Wei Yan, a man of great ambition.

## Culture

Buddhism is favoured over Daoism in Southern Garden Nation. Buddhist temples are everywhere, while Daoist temples are rare and there are none at all in the capital. When the abbot of the White River Temple was caught in a scandal, the emperor had most of its three hundred monks imprisoned and banished the rest. The capital has no Confucian temple honouring the seventy-two sages. Martial arts hold a high place in society, and the grandmasters of the world are closely watched by the court.

Local specialities include bamboo wine and golden ball jujube paste cakes. Statues of the Ba Xia can be found under bridges throughout the nation.

## Trivia

- [Pei Qian](Pei_Qian.md) grew up as a beggar in the capital after her parents died.
- One legend says Wei Xian's mother was found by the roadside with a white dragon coiled around her, shortly before she gave birth to him.
- Chen Ping An learned to speak the capital's dialect fluently while searching for the Observing Dao Temple.
- Chen Ping An took a copy of a building manual from the nation's Ministry of Works back with him and later gave it to Zhu Lian.
- [Cao Qinglang](Cao_Qinglang.md), a boy from the capital, became a student of Zhong Qiu and [Lu Tai](Lu_Tai.md).
