# Buddhism

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Buddhism)

---
Buddhism in Eastern Treasured Vial Continent faces a period of decline, a stark contrast to the flourishing Daoism and Confucianism. The story depict this waning influence, portraying Buddhism as a less prominent force in the world's power dynamics, yet still retaining unique aspects and attracting devoted followers.

**Buddhism's Diminished Status:**

- **Historical Calamities:** Buddhism has suffered several great calamities over the past 1,000 years, weakening its power and influence. [1]
- **Popular Perception:** A common saying in Eastern Treasured Vial Continent reflects Buddhism's diminished standing: "Hear only about the True Lords and Heavenly Masters; know not about the Dharmapala and Bhadanta." This highlights the dominance of Daoist and Confucian figures in the public consciousness. [1]
- **Weakest Force in the Town:** In the specific context of the small town, Buddhism is described as the weakest force, further emphasizing its reduced presence. [2]

**Unique Aspects and Teachings:**

Despite its decline, Buddhism retains unique practices and concepts that shape the lives of its adherents and contribute to the world's diverse philosophical landscape.

- **Karmic Fate:** The concept of karma plays a significant role in Buddhist teachings. Actions have consequences, and individuals experience the repercussions of their past deeds. [2] This emphasis on cause and effect is evident in the interactions between characters, where their choices and actions influence their destinies.
- **Vanquishing Distracted Minds:** Buddhism emphasizes the importance of overcoming desires and attachments to achieve enlightenment. [3] Cultivating a peaceful and focused mind is crucial for spiritual progress.
- **Observing Past and Future Lives:** The belief in reincarnation and the cyclical nature of existence is central to Buddhist teachings. [3, 4] This perspective shapes the understanding of life, death, and the interconnectedness of all beings.
- **Attaining Enlightenment through Renunciation:** While acknowledged as a simplified concept, the idea of attaining enlightenment by renouncing past evil deeds remains a significant aspect of Buddhism. [5] This suggests a path to redemption and spiritual transformation through recognizing and abandoning harmful actions.

**Buddhist Figures and Practices:**

The sources introduce several characters and practices that shed light on the practice and influence of Buddhism in Eastern Treasured Vial Continent.

- **The Old Monk at Heart Manifestation Temple:** Residing in Southern Garden Nation, where Buddhism is more prominent, the old monk embodies the teachings of compassion, wisdom, and acceptance. [6, 7] He engages in thoughtful discussions about karma, enlightenment, and the complexities of good and evil.
- **The Young Monk in White Kasaya:** This monk's dedicated practice and adherence to Buddhist principles amidst the White River Temple controversy demonstrate the resilience and enduring spirit of Buddhism. [8, 9] His quiet presence and commitment to his faith offer a glimpse into the personal devotion that sustains the tradition.
- **"Refrain From Looking Outward":** This Buddhist saying, cited by Chen Ping'an, emphasizes the importance of inner reflection and focusing on one's own spiritual development rather than seeking external validation or comparing oneself to others. [10, 11] This principle encourages self-reliance and a deeper understanding of Buddhist teachings.

**Buddhism, despite its decline, remains a significant presence in Majestic World.** Its teachings on karma, enlightenment, and the cultivation of a peaceful mind continue to resonate with certain individuals. The sources portray a nuanced view of Buddhism, acknowledging its diminished status while highlighting its unique contributions to the world's spiritual and philosophical landscape.
