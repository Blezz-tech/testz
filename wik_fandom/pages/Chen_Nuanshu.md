**Chen Nuanshu**, recorded in Downtrodden Mountain's genealogy as **Chen Ruchu**, is a fire python who serves as [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md)'s little maidservant and the tireless steward of [Downtrodden Mountain](Downtrodden_Mountain.md).

## Appearance

In her human form Nuanshu looks like a small girl in a pink dress, which is why she is so often called simply "the little girl in pink." She is rarely seen without a bucket and towel in her hands and a big bunch of keys clinking softly at her waist. *(Chapter 547 (1))* She always seems to have a handful of roasted seeds ready to share. Her true form is a fire python. 

## Personality

Nuanshu is gentle, shy and endlessly hardworking. Chen Ping'an believes that as long as Downtrodden Mountain exists, she will be busy every single day, and he trusts her more than anyone on the mountain because she is the least likely to make a mistake. She is the one who runs around the mountain the most, cleaning every building, cooking, shopping and keeping accounts. When she goes shopping in the prefectural city, she likes to compare every shop carefully before buying.

She is soft-hearted and cries easily. After each of [Pei Qian](Pei_Qian.md)'s brutal training sessions with [Cui Cheng](Cui_Cheng.md), Nuanshu carried the unconscious girl down from the bamboo building, prepared her medicinal bath and went back up to scrub away the blood, often in tears. *(Chapter 529 (2))* She is also something of a bookworm, having spent years reading in the scripture library she came from.

Among the three girls of the bamboo building she is the peacemaker. When Pei Qian and [Zhou Mili](Zhou_Mili.md) squabble, she smooths things over. Pei Qian bosses her around as the senior sister and once remarked that Nuanshu's book does not contain the word "refusal." Nuanshu is fiercely loyal to her master and will not hear a word against him.

## Background

### The scripture library

Nuanshu was once a fire python living in the scripture library of the Cao Clan's Iris Orchid manor. She was in the second-to-last shedding of her skin, and if she had succeeded at the next one she would have become a flood dragon. A water snake from the river outside the city, the future [Chen Lingjun](Chen_Lingjun.md), was waiting to devour her. The young [Cui Dongshan](Cui_Dongshan.md) came to the library, cowed both creatures with an ancient inkstone that held a sleeping flood dragon, and took them away. When the fire python took human form, she became a little girl in a pink dress, curled up in terror at the top of the stairwell. *(Chapter 163)* She and Chen Lingjun were brought to Downtrodden Mountain to serve Chen Ping'an.

### Downtrodden Mountain

Nuanshu quickly made herself indispensable on the mountain. Chen Ping'an trusted her with the keys to his friends' houses in the [Small Town](Small_Town.md). *(Chapter 182)* When the two children finally chose their own names, the little girl picked Chen Ruchu, meaning "just like before," because she hoped her bond with her master would stay as good as it was at the beginning. Chen Lingjun had come up with the idea of taking the surname Chen. When Chen Ping'an admitted that he had planned to call her Nuanshu, after the first and last characters of a poem about warm spring sunshine, she was so moved that she went home to cry. *(Chapter 467 (2))* Ever after, she recorded herself as Chen Ruchu in the mountain's genealogy, but she told everyone they could call her Nuanshu. Most of the mountain prefers that name.

Chen Ping'an once asked her about partridges in poetry, and she rattled off a long list of poems from memory while Pei Qian nodded off beside her. *(Chapter 478)* When Cui Cheng died, he left a posthumous note on the first floor of the bamboo building with an envelope addressed "To be opened by Nuanshu." *(Chapter 566)*

Her safety is a quiet concern for Downtrodden Mountain. Chen Ping'an, Cui Dongshan and [Wei Bo](Wei_Bo.md) took great care to make sure she could leave the mountain on errands without coming to harm. She carries a sword talisman from the Dragon Spring Sword Sect so she can fly freely, and she wears an illusion-cast Dao robe that once belonged to Cui Dongshan. Chen Ping'an later reflected that whatever danger he and Cui Dongshan faced on their travels, they could always take comfort in the thought of Downtrodden Mountain, and that they had Nuanshu to thank for it. *(Chapter 567 (1))*

### Later

Chen Ping'an gave Nuanshu and Chen Lingjun a pair of Dragon King Baskets he had found in the Northern Complete Reed Continent. He told them they were too precious to entrust to anyone else. *(Chapter 569 (1))* After the ancestral hall was established, he handed over all the spare immortal coins he had earned as a travelling salesman to Nuanshu to manage. She discreetly held up two fingers to remind him to ask for an allowance of fifty grain rain coins. *(Chapter 571 (1))*

While her master is away at the Sword Qi Great Wall, she cleans the Sky Blue Peak ancestral hall every five days. *(Chapter 594 (2))* When Wei Bo brings a letter from Chen Ping'an, she wipes her sleeves before accepting it with both hands. *(Chapter 600)* Chen Lingjun refined his Dragon King Basket long ago, but Nuanshu's progress is very slow, and Chen Lingjun teases her for it. Even so, her talent is not lacking. [Lu Chen](Lu_Chen.md) once nearly took her to the Heavenly World to cultivate. *(Chapter 640 (1))*

## Trivia

- "Ruchu" means "just like before."
- Pei Qian once crowned her the number one Go player on Downtrodden Mountain.
- Zhou Mili moved her whole collection of storybooks into Nuanshu's room one night, and Nuanshu simply looked after them without a word.
- Downtrodden Mountain refers to her, Chen Lingjun and Zhou Mili as its three demons.
- Pei Qian, Zhou Mili and Nuanshu each have their own little bamboo cricket cage and porcelain pot from Chen Ping'an. He suspects Nuanshu will end up tending all three plants.
