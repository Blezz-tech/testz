**Cui Dongshan** is the half of [Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md)'s soul that lived in a young body in the small town of Jewel Small World. After breaking from the old Cui Chan, he took a new name and became the self-appointed student of [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md).

## Appearance

Cui Dongshan looks like a handsome boy with a red birthmark between his brows. *(Chapter 81)* He favours white robes with wide sleeves that billow like clouds when he moves. *(Chapter 381)* His body is an immortal body shed by a flood dragon of the ancient Shu Nation.

## Personality

Cui Dongshan is clever, theatrical and childish on the surface. He flatters his teacher shamelessly, picks fights with Pei Qian, and complains loudly whenever things go against him. He calls himself an insignificant little ninth tier ant when it suits him. *(Chapter 171)* Underneath, he keeps the mind of Cui Chan and plans many moves ahead. Unlike his other half, though, he holds fast to his teacher's way of doing things. When Cui Chan set a trap for Chen Ping'an at Bamboo Scroll Lake, Cui Dongshan suffered alongside his teacher and refused to give up hope. *(Chapter 428)*

He takes great pride in being Chen Ping'an's student. He insists he is closer to his teacher than Pei Qian because he met him first. *(Chapter 480)*

## Background

Cui Chan split his soul and placed half of it in a young body, which entered the small town while the old Cui Chan remained in the capital. *(Chapter 132)* After the events in the town, the young half chose to stop calling himself Cui Chan and took the name Cui Dongshan. *(Chapter 131)* He followed Chen Ping'an south and asked to become his disciple, but Chen Ping'an did not trust him. Chen Ping'an told him that if he harmed the others, only one of them would survive. *(Chapter 133)* At Mountain Cliff Academy's old library, Chen Ping'an finally agreed to accept him as a student if he would truly protect Li Baoping and the other children. Cui Dongshan bowed to him as his teacher. *(Chapter 163)* At the academy he took Xie Xie as his disciple and stayed close to Mao Xiaodong. *(Chapter 170)*

When the two halves met again, the old Cui Chan offered a way to fuse their souls, but Cui Dongshan refused. *(Chapter 239)* He later rejoined Chen Ping'an and helped [Shi Rou](Shi_Rou.md) take control of Du Mao's immortal body. *(Chapter 386)* At Bamboo Scroll Lake he made a bet with Cui Chan over whether Chen Ping'an could escape the trap set for him. The loser would accept the other as the primary self.

He later returned to Downtrodden Mountain to see his teacher and Pei Qian. By then he had reached the Nascent Tier, and he helped Chen Ping'an plan the future of the mountains. *(Chapter 481)* He later returned to the Upper Five Tiers and quarrelled with Cui Chan over their grandfather's choices. *(Chapter 560)* He travelled to the Sword Qi Great Wall with Pei Qian and Cao Qinglang, where he was known as a third-generation disciple of the Scholarly Sage. *(Chapter 608)*

## Trivia

- Pei Qian secretly nicknamed him the big white goose.
- When Cui Chan beat him at Go after their reunion, he lost by a single move.
- At the Great Sui academy, Mao Xiaodong snapped that he was his son, and Cui Dongshan cheerfully answered by calling him father. On the same day he peeked over a wall at a woman bathing and then winced at her figure. *(Chapter 167)*
- He talked Pei Qian into betting copper coins on five-in-a-row and won seven from her. Chen Ping'an later told her she could win them back if she was able. *(Chapter 383)*
- Li Baoping often stamped him on the forehead with her seal. He still remembered the pain years later, and he once walked around with the mark until Chen Ping'an pointed it out. *(Chapter 411)*
- He and Pei Qian once made up a silly folk song about eating stinky tofu with wine. *(Chapter 419)*
- To tease Pei Qian, he hid on the eaves above her window, and when she was about to cry he dropped headfirst onto the ground so she would laugh instead.
