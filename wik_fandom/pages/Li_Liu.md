> **Spoiler Warning:** This article contains spoilers from the *Sword of Coming* (*Jian Lai* / *Unsheathed*) novel and donghua.

**Li Liu** is the older sister of [Li Huai](Li_Huai.md) and the daughter of [Li Er](Li_Er.md). To her family and neighbours she is a quiet, gentle and pretty girl who never speaks much. In truth, she is the reincarnation of one of the most powerful ancient beings of the heavens, born with the full memories of her countless past lives. She has a deep connection to water and an ancient grudge against [Ruan Xiu](Ruan_Xiu.md).

Through all of this, Li Liu cares about very few things in the world, and her family, above all her little brother, is at the top of that list.

## Appearance

Li Liu is slender, delicate and soft-looking, often compared to a willow branch in spring. She is not the kind of beauty who stuns at first glance, but one who grows more beautiful the longer she is looked at, with particularly lovely eyes. She is described as resembling her mother in looks while taking after her father in temperament. When she smiles, her eyebrows curve into crescents. At Lion Peak she carries a golden lion seal and a short sword at her waist, both hidden by concealment restrictions.

## Personality

At home, Li Liu is mild, patient and obedient. She accepts her mother's open favouritism towards Li Huai without complaint, takes every scolding with a smile, and quietly helps in the family shop. She almost never gets angry or laughs loudly. Her mother calls her a lump of soft dough that anyone could bully.

Outside her home, she is a completely different person. Having lived through so many lifetimes, she regards most of the world with indifference and treats lesser beings, even powerful cultivators and water gods, with cold disdain. She can be shockingly ruthless, as when she gouges out a fox spirit's eye on a whim. She has little patience for sentiment, saying she has experienced too many joys, sorrows, separations and reunions to feel much about them anymore. She also openly dislikes sword users of every era.

The one thing that reliably softens her is Li Huai. Whenever her brother is mentioned, her expression turns gentle and she looks like the young girl who used to carry water to the town well. She also reflects that the small town was the only place in all her lives where she met many "genuine people", and she thinks of it with longing.

## Background

Li Liu was born in the small town in a birth deliberately arranged by Old Man Yang of the Yang Family Medicine Shop, who placed her family close to his shop. Unlike other reincarnated beings he guided, she never needed her memories unlocked, because she was born with them. *(Chapter 530 (1))* Old Man Yang told her plainly that if she kept cultivating in her old way, changing bodies and climbing quickly each life, she would remain stuck at the Immortal Tier forever, "a half-assed individual who couldn't even act like a human". Her real assignment in this life, as she sees it, is not to advance in cultivation but to learn how to be a person. *(Chapter 557 (1))*

Old Man Yang is her Dao mentor, unlike Li Er and [Zheng Dafeng](Zheng_Dafeng.md), whom he only calls his disciples. *(Chapter 253 (1))* He once said that Li Liu was talented while Li Huai was blessed with fortune. Her talent is that she was born with intuitive knowledge. *(Chapter 206 (4))*

In the town, Li Liu kept a low profile. She had no close friends, never went to school, and often went to the private school to fetch Li Huai, carrying herself with natural grace. Both [Lin Shouyi](Lin_Shouyi.md) and [Dong Shuijing](Dong_Shuijing.md) fell for her. Qi Jingchun only ever nodded at her through the classroom window. The one time he spoke to her was when he came to drink with Li Er. He told her that what defines a person is not their decaying body but how they live, and he raised his bowl of cheap wine to pay respects to "her kind", as the reason for the existence of his own kind and the world itself.

## Plot

### Early Appearances

Li Liu is first mentioned as Li Huai's older sister, who left the town with their parents. *(Chapter 73)* Li Huai repeatedly "offers" her as a bride to people he admires, including [A'Liang](A'Liang.md) and [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md).

Her family later returns to visit Li Huai at Mountain Cliff Academy. There she quietly helps her brother, refuses the painted puppet he tries to give her, and sews him cloth shoes. Lin Shouyi can barely look at her. *(Chapter 168 (1))* As her family leaves the academy, her eyes meet those of [Cui Dongshan](Cui_Dongshan.md). It is later revealed that she made a deliberately provocative gesture at him, knowing exactly who he was, and Cui Dongshan recalls it with alarm years later.

### Journey North

On the ship to [Northern Complete Reed Continent](Northern_Complete_Reed_Continent.md), her father cripples a group of cultivators who harass the family, then claims the ship's overseer simply scolded them. Li Liu has to suppress a smile at her father finally learning to lie.

Meanwhile, Old Man Yang arranges a deal with the sword immortal [Cao Xi](Cao_Xi.md): the souls of Cao Xi's parents are in Li Liu's possession, and in return for them, Cao Xi must give her his old flying sword, teach her his sword technique and act as her Dao guardian. *(Chapter 235 (2))* A young scholar surnamed Situ travels with the family for part of the journey, and Li Liu's mother hopes in vain that he will marry her.

### Lion Peak

The family settles at Lion Peak, whose master claims a connection to Old Man Yang. Li Er opens a shop in the small town at the foot of the mountain, and a Lion Peak immortal "coincidentally" discovers Li Liu's aptitude.

In reality, Li Liu is the reincarnation of the founder of Lion Peak. After seeing her parents off, the entire leadership of the mountain bows to her as "Esteemed Founder". She walks into a sealed cave whose restrictions even earth immortals cannot break and comes out wearing a golden lion seal. When Cao Xi hands her his short sword, she casually unleashes a stroke of sword qi that splits the sky, having stepped straight into the Middle Five Tiers. *(Chapter 283 (2))* The current mountain master, Huang Cai, was a starving beggar boy she took in during a past life. He became her only direct disciple and has guarded Lion Peak ever since.

Over the next years, Li Liu becomes the second most important figure on Lion Peak, even treated with great respect by the sage of Great Cormorant Academy. Cao Xi follows her to secret realms and old sect ruins, where she simply "picks up" one treasure after another. Her mother is kept completely in the dark, since Lion Peak's cultivators are forbidden on pain of death from approaching the shop, and she believes Li Liu is a lowly maidservant to some mediocre old immortal. Li Liu happily hands over thirty taels of silver of her "allowance", saved for Li Huai's marriage, while her real wealth would astonish the sword immortals of Southern Whirl Continent. *(Chapter 367 (2))* When news comes that Zheng Dafeng has been badly hurt, she smoothly covers for Li Er so that her mother does not realise he is going off to fight.

### Mural City and Treasure Mirror Mountain

At Mural City on Skeleton Shoal, Li Liu appears alongside a young man who kneels before the murals with an ancient jade tablet, presenting the Rain Bringer Divine Maiden with a dilemma. The divine maiden goes pale on seeing her: the immortal realm in the murals was once one of Li Liu's own summer residences.

When the martial artist Yang Chongxuan (in truth Yang Ningzhen of the Great Origin Empire's Lofty Cloud Palace, one of the continent's top ten young cultivators) attacks the divine maiden at Treasure Mirror Mountain, Li Liu intervenes. She claims to have personally given the Yang Clan's founding ancestor the prophecy about the mountain's treasure mirror when he was still a child in patched open-crotch trousers. In the battle that follows, she sacrifices her golden lion seal and a Dao saber but beats him decisively, takes the treasure mirror from the mountain, and hands it to him so that the Yang Clan owes her another favour. *(Chapter 496 (6))*

She then rips out an eye of the fox spirit Wei Taizhen, but changes her mind with a gentle smile when she remembers that Li Huai will soon be travelling and needs a maidservant. She replaces the eye and claims Wei Taizhen for him. She also forces Wei Taizhen's brother to choose between killing his father or dying himself, and, when he chooses to die, approves of it as "the stupidest method, but the most correct choice". Finally, she burns away the thread of love between the Rain Bringer Divine Maiden and her lover, binding the divine maiden to a sixty-year promise. *(Chapter 496 (7))*

### Return to Dragon Spring

Li Liu returns to the Yang Family Medicine Shop to meet Old Man Yang. He urges her to set aside her ancient grudge with Ruan Xiu, a conflict between water and fire from the age of the gods, saying both sides made enormous mistakes. The text reveals that if Li Liu wanted to settle that grudge, she would first need to kill Chen Ping'an, who stood in for her against Ruan Xiu and has a strong affinity with water. She refuses, because it would grieve Li Huai. She also tells Old Man Yang that her mother never needed to go north.

She then visits Ruan Xiu herself with keys to old shattered realms. Ruan Xiu, who has lost her memories, asks who won their final battle, and Li Liu answers that they both lost. The two share Ruan Xiu's desserts while trading threats about eating each other one day. *(Chapter 530 (2))*

At [Downtrodden Mountain](Downtrodden_Mountain.md), she explains the workings of the newly split Lotus Root Blessed Land to [Zhu Lian](Zhu_Lian.md) and recognises his Great Dao as within reach. Zhu Lian, in turn, tells her it is a shame she is not joining Downtrodden Mountain.

### The Water Dragon Sect

Li Liu deliberately seeks out Chen Ping'an at the Water Dragon Sect on the Plenty River. She passes on news of Downtrodden Mountain's debts and shows him a painted scroll revealing a painful truth connected to him. *(Chapter 548 (2))* The sect's hidden water official, Li Yuan, kowtows to her: the Dragon Palace Small World was another of her summer palaces, and the sect's founding was her doing.

She arranges a private island for Chen Ping'an to cultivate on and gives him her "Three Feet Sweet Rain" jade tablet as repayment for his kindness to Li Huai, then slips away so he cannot return it. Every river, lake and sea in the world is described as her territory. *(Chapter 549 (3))* When Chen Ping'an leaves the tablet behind anyway, she gives it to Li Yuan. She also heals the water goddess Shen Lin and promises her a future promotion, and plays Go with Spiritual Master Fire Dragon, who treats her as half friend, half enemy.

### Chen Ping'an at Lion Peak

When Chen Ping'an arrives at Lion Peak, Li Liu's mother tries to match him with her daughter, and the town assumes they are lovers. Li Liu tells him calmly that she likes neither Lin Shouyi nor Dong Shuijing, and that she has never thought about marriage. While Li Er trains Chen Ping'an at the founder's cave, she brings them meals.

During a walk on the mountain, Chen Ping'an makes a light joke: there could be no grass in [Clay Vase Alley](Small_Town.md#clay-vase-alley), because the hens would eat it. Li Liu suddenly bows and thanks him for his guidance, leaving him completely puzzled.

That night she asks her father what the family will do when she must one day take back her true body and her divinity outweighs her humanity. Li Er answers that they will treat her like a child who has become extraordinarily successful, still their daughter, and asks only that she always treat her mother well. She agrees. *(Chapter 558 (2))* Before Chen Ping'an leaves, she mends his Dao robes and hides another precious jade tablet among her mother's gifts for him.

### Visiting Li Huai

At their mother's request, Li Liu visits Li Huai at Mountain Cliff Academy, now that he has grown taller than her. She brings a bag of treasures disguised to look ordinary. Li Huai complains that a maidservant to an old immortal can't save anything good, urges her to reach the Abode Tier quickly, and lectures her not to keep giving gifts to her family once she marries. She smiles throughout, telling him only that he has grown up.

## Abilities

- **Memories of past lives:** Li Liu is born with complete memories of her previous incarnations, so for her death is little more than a change of body. In one past life she was a sect leader at the peak of the Immortal Tier. She has died at the hands of a tenth-tier martial artist and has also killed tenth-tier martial artists.
- **Knowledge:** Her understanding of blessed lands, small worlds and ancient history rivals Jiang Shangzhen's. She has managed several blessed lands before, including the now-shattered Ripple Small World and Emerald Tide Blessed Land.
- **Cultivation:** In this life she entered the Middle Five Tiers in a single step and casually advanced to the Nascent Tier, where she stayed for a time.
- **Water dominion:** Any body of water is effectively her territory. She can travel thousands of kilometres instantly by entering a river, heal a water goddess's shattered godly body by touch, and command ancient water officials.
- **Combat:** She beat Yang Ningzhen, one of the continent's top ten young cultivators, with punches, kicks, immortal treasures and countless mystical abilities. She can draw a golden circle that imprisons others, and hide her aura so that it resembles another person's.

## Possessions

- **Golden lion seal**: retrieved from her old cave at Lion Peak; sacrificed in the fight at Treasure Mirror Mountain.
- **Cao Xi's short sword**: carries 300 years of nurtured sword qi.
- **Dao saber**: also sacrificed at Treasure Mirror Mountain.
- **Hornless dragon jade tablet "Three Feet Sweet Rain"**: the original on which the Water Dragon Sect's tablets are modelled; later given to Li Yuan.
- **Jade tablet "The old flood dragon quells the storm"**: given secretly to Chen Ping'an.
- Several former abodes: Lion Peak, the Dragon Palace Small World, the Southern Fragrance Water Palace, and the immortal realm of Mural City.

## Trivia

- 柳 (liǔ) means willow, which is why Zheng Dafeng calls her "Little Willow".
- Li Huai boasts that he has a 100% win rate against her at jacks.
- The town's women avoid the family shop when she is minding it, because they feel plain beside her. Chen Ping'an suggests hiring less beautiful Lion Peak maidens to shop there in clothes made from the shop's fabric.
- Spiritual Master Fire Dragon names her among the young cultivators of the age whom no one will be able to stop.
