# Eastern Treasured Vial Continent

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent)

---
**Eastern Treasured Vial Continent** is the smallest of the nine continents of the [Majestic World](Majestic_World.md) and the homeland of [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md). Long looked down on by the larger continents, it is home to countless empires and vassal states. Over the course of the story it comes largely under the rule of the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md), and it is later set to become the Majestic World's line of defence against the demon tribe.

## Geography

The continent is the smallest in the Majestic World.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> [Parasol Leaf Continent](Parasol_Leaf_Continent.md), its neighbour to the southeast, is far larger.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> To the north, a stretch of sea separates it from the [Northern Complete Reed Continent](Northern_Complete_Reed_Continent.md), and the [Middle Earth Divine Continent](Middle_Earth_Divine_Continent.md) lies across the sea to the west.

The north of the continent is bleak and poor in resources, while the south is rich.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> The northern region is home to the Great Li Empire, [Great Sui Nation](Great_Sui_Nation.md) and [Yellow Court Nation](Yellow_Court_Nation.md), and it once belonged to the Lu Empire. Before the Great Li Empire built a ferry station on Parasol Tree Mountain, no Kun ship could dock anywhere in the north. The central region holds the Luminous Vermilion Empire, Lake View Academy, [Southern Stream Nation](Southern_Stream_Nation.md), Colorful Garment Nation and [Bamboo Scroll Lake](Bamboo_Scroll_Lake.md). [Azure Phoenix Nation](Azure_Phoenix_Nation.md) and the Cloud Forest Jiang Clan lie in the southeast.

[Old Dragon City](Old_Dragon_City.md) sits at the continent's southern tip. It is the largest trading hub on the continent and the link to Parasol Leaf Continent.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> All of the continent's intercontinental ships belong to its clans.

## History

For a long time the Lu Empire was the strongest nation in the north.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup> The Great Li Empire began as a small state that the older nations to the south regarded as northern savages. With the help of [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md), which hung above its territory, it grew into the largest mortal empire on the continent and destroyed the Lu Empire.

After the death of Qi Jingchun, the Great Li Empire turned south. When [Ceremony Mountain](Sects.md#ceremony-mountain)'s Kun ship was brought down in the central region, Heavenly Lord [Xie Shi](Xie_Shi.md) of the Northern Complete Reed Continent took up position north of Lake View Academy. This cut the continent in two and left the empire free to take the north.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> The Great Li cavalry then marched south. Before long more than half of the continent was under its control.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> The Luminous Vermilion Empire fell, and its Central Mountain became the Great Li Empire's own. [Song Jixin](Song_Jixin.md) was made the empire's feudal lord of Old Dragon City.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

[Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md) planned to turn the resources of the whole continent into the power of one nation. With Old Dragon City as its anchor, the continent would hold back the demon tribe once the [Sword Qi Great Wall](Sword_Qi_Great_Wall.md) fell. Later the Great Li court gathered the continent's leading cultivators and mountain gods to plan defensive lines against the demon army. It was expected to march north after destroying Parasol Leaf Continent.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

## Cultivation world

The continent has always been short of powerful cultivators. When Qi Zhen of the [Divine Edict Sect](Sects.md#divine-edict-sect) reached the Immortal Tier, he became its only Heavenly Lord. Only a handful of Unpolished Jade Tier cultivators could be counted.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> Even years later it still had too few Ascension Tier cultivators.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> Cultivators from the Complete Reed Continent held the continent in low regard.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup>

The Divine Edict Sect is its leading Daoist sect and oversees Daoist orthodoxy across the continent. [Wind Snow Temple](Sects.md#wind-snow-temple) and [True Martial Mountain](Sects.md#true-martial-mountain) are the two ancestral temples of the Militarians. The continent had only two Confucian academies, [Mountain Cliff Academy](Mountain_Cliff_Academy.md) and Lake View Academy, while the Complete Reed Continent had six.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup> Notable figures include Li Tuanjing of the [Wind Lightning Field](Sects.md#wind-lightning-field), long regarded as the strongest cultivator below the Upper Five Tiers.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup> [Wei Jin](Wei_Jin.md) of Wind Snow Temple became the youngest swordsman in its history to reach the Upper Five Tiers. [Liu Laocheng](Liu_Laocheng.md) was its only vagrant cultivator at that level.

As the Great Li Empire expanded, its Five Mountains became the Five Mountains of the continent. [Wei Bo](Wei_Bo.md) of the Northern Mountain is the only mountain god of the Upper Five Tiers on the continent. Four forces, including [Sun Scorch Mountain](Sects.md#sun-scorch-mountain) and the Xu Clan of Light Breeze City, became sects-in-waiting under the empire.

## Trivia

- Almost all Daoist priests on the continent wear fishtail hats.
- The men of the north are known across the continent for being tall and strong.
- The Great Li Empire was the only empire on the continent that made its Qi refiners serve three years in the army.
- Cultivators from Circling Ascension Continent complained that their own continent was only slightly larger than this one.
- One official dialect is spoken across the whole continent.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup>
