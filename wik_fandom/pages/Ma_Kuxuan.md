## Appearance

Ma Kuxuan is a skinny, tanned boy with notably large eyes, short in stature as a child but quick and explosive in his movements, often compared to a nimble monkey. His face is capable of a dazzling, sometimes mocking smile, though when badly hurt he fights on with blood seeping from his mouth, ears and nose without making a sound. He keeps a white cat as a companion, a creature with an unusual nature of its own.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup><sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

## Personality

Ma Kuxuan is quiet and keeps to himself, having grown up pretending to be slow-witted while bullied by the other children of the small town, though Chen Ping'an recognizes early on that he is anything but. He is cold and calculating, willing to feign weakness to lure an opponent into a trap, as he does in his first fight with Chen Ping'an. He has an intense dislike of being underestimated and takes pride in his vow to never lose to anyone of the same cultivation level after entering [True Martial Mountain](Sects.md#true-martial-mountain). As he matures he becomes more ruthless and unpredictable, killing on a whim and treating the deaths of strangers as inconsequential, yet he holds to his own private code, such as never killing someone he considers truly innocent, however thin that distinction can be in practice. Beneath the arrogance and cruelty, he remains capable of real tenderness, which only ever shows through with his grandmother and in his one-sided feelings for Zhi Gui.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup><sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup><sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

## Background

Ma Kuxuan grew up in [Apricot Blossom Alley](Small_Town.md) in the small town, raised by his grandmother, a local midwife known to the other residents simply as [Granny Ma](Ma_Lanhua.md), after his father proved unreliable and uncaring. He first appears as a strange, friendless boy who spends his days alone throwing and skipping stones, a habit that conceals formidable precision and power. He confronts Chen Ping'an after he tracked down Chen Ping'an and Ning Yao for a bounty, leading to a fight in which Ma Kuxuan was defeated and only saved from being killed when a swordsman from True Martial Mountain intervened, taking Ma Kuxuan on as his soon-to-be disciple.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup><sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup><sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

Shortly afterward, his grandmother was killed, slapped to death by a visiting cultivator, the granddaughter of the Sea Tide Cavalry's leader. Grief-stricken, Ma Kuxuan demanded his new master help him find her spirit, but was told her soul had already disintegrated from a lifetime of accumulated sins, including years of quietly attacking passing cultivators and spirits. He swore to avenge her regardless of the rules binding him as a Militarian cultivator.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup><sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup><sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

He left the small town to formally join True Martial Mountain, one of the Militarian ancestral forces of the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent, and rose quickly there, earning a reputation as a one-in-a-century prodigy and remaining undefeated against opponents of equal cultivation. He later crossed paths with Chen Ping'an again on the streets of the Colorful Garment Nation and fought a rematch, deepening his one-sided rivalry.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup><sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

Ma Kuxuan was later given to the sharp-tongued maidservant Zhi Gui as a "fated opportunity" by the fortune-teller Lu Chen, and he willingly agreed to serve and obey her, given his feelings for her even as she needled him for not yet being powerful enough to impress her. His cultivation continued to advance at a startling pace, including killing two Golden Core Tier swordsmen of the Luminous Vermilion Empire in life-and-death duels while still short of twenty years old, a feat that made him the most talked-about young cultivator on the continent, ranked behind only the legendary Li Tuanjing and Wei Jin.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup><sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup>

It later came out that Ma Kuxuan's own parents, who secretly controlled the Ma Clan's dragon kiln in Apricot Blossom Alley, had been directly involved in arranging for Chen Ping'an's bonded porcelain to be shattered when he was still a child, selling out his fortune for their own profit. His grandmother had tried and failed to stop them, the one notable act of conscience the otherwise selfish old woman performed, and Ma Kuxuan had woken during the scheme as a child and absorbed part of the truth, which is part of why he always paid close attention to Chen Ping'an afterward.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup>

In retaliation for his grandmother's death, Ma Kuxuan hunted down and annihilated the entire Sea Tide Cavalry, killing the former leader personally and sparing only his granddaughter, whom he took captive and renamed Shudian, keeping her as a maidservant who travels everywhere with him afterward. He visited his resurrected grandmother, now risen to Dragon Whisker River Guardian and threatened on her behalf until she was granted recognition and a temple as the Iron Talisman River Goddess. He continues to travel with Shudian in a cruel, controlling way, and killing indiscriminately by his own whims while insisting he never kills the truly innocent.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup><sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

## Trivia

- He is a Militarian cultivator, a cultivation path distinct from the orthodox immortal sects and from pure martial artists.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>
- He has a habit of snacking on brined soybeans.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>
- He has promised himself to one day kill Song Jixin, who in turn has vowed to kill him over his feelings for Zhi Gui.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup>
- He secretly watched Chen Ping'an from a distance on at least one occasion in their childhood without revealing himself.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>
