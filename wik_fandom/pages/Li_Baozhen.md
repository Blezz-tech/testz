## Appearance

He is a young man from [Fortune Street](Small_Town.md#fortune-street-and-peach-leaf-alley), described as truly young and filled with unrestrained vigour when he appears in the Azure Phoenix Nation, where he speaks its official dialect fluently. *(Chapter 396 (3))* Chen Ping'an had never actually seen him before their encounter on the road near Lion Garden. Li Baozhen calls himself "nothing more than a scholar who can't withstand a single punch". *(Chapter 398 (2))*

## Personality

Li Baozhen is charming, clever and ambitious. Within the Li Clan he is far more likeable than his quiet elder brother, Li Xisheng. *(Chapter 181)* He is widely regarded as the smartest of the three Li siblings, and he is his mother's favourite. She keeps every letter he sends her in a small red box. *(Chapter 206 (3))*

He is also impatient, reckless and unwilling to admit fault. His father complains that he promised to follow the rules and avoid hidden schemes after leaving the small town, then "decided to act first and ask later". *(Chapter 127)* Li Xisheng says that his brother has never been willing to acknowledge his mistakes since childhood. *(Chapter 191 (1))* He is shrewd and ruthless; Shi Rou, who met him, regards him as such. He sees others as pieces on a Go board, and openly delights in other people's misfortune. Cui Chan, his patron, judges that Li Baozhen's pursuits are nothing special: all he wants is to accumulate merits and climb the official hierarchy. *(Chapter 406 (2))*

Despite his arrogance, he can be candid about his own fears. He admits to a carriage driver that he has never been a courageous person, given how many powerful beings lived in his hometown. *(Chapter 397 (2))*

## Background

Li Baozhen is the second son of Li Hong, leader of the Li Clan of Fortune Street, the younger brother of [Li Xisheng](Li_Xisheng.md) and the older brother of [Li Baoping](Li_Baoping.md). After leaving the small town for the capital of the Great Li Empire, he quickly made a name for himself, finding a powerful backer who arranged for him to study at the imperial academy. That backer later fell from power. *(Chapter 132)*

It was under Li Baozhen's secret direction that the Li Clan maidservant [Zhu Lu](Zhu_Lu.md) tried to assassinate [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) at Pillow Relay Station. A hidden message in his letter to Li Baoping spurred the infatuated Zhu Lu to act "for the sake of love". *(Chapter 392 (1))* Li Xisheng offered Chen Ping'an his peach wood talisman as an apology in his brother's place, and Chen Ping'an promised Li Xisheng that he would spare Li Baozhen once.

Under Imperial Preceptor [Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md), Li Baozhen joins the Great Li Empire's intelligence agency, the Green Ripple Pavilion, as leader of its southeastern division. Cui Chan offered him three postings, and he was sent to the Azure Phoenix Nation to work under the scholar Liu Qingfeng. *(Chapter 398 (1))* When Chen Ping'an unexpectedly appears on the road near Lion Garden, Li Baozhen laments that the intelligence reports have "screwed him over", then secretly reaches for a talisman. Chen Ping'an pins his talisman to the carriage wall with his flying sword, beats him with a single punch, and keeps his promise to Li Xisheng by sparing him. He confiscates Li Baozhen's Dragon Palace jade pendant and Day and Night Wandering Deity True Body Talisman, an heirloom passed down by the Li Clan's ancestors, and later gives both to Li Baoping. By doing so, Chen Ping'an separates Li Baozhen from the rest of the Li Clan in his mind. *(Chapter 408)*

Li Baozhen goes on to complete his task in the Azure Phoenix Nation, undermining the northern scholars' alliance leader Liu Jingting. *(Chapter 414 (3))* He later runs an ambitious grassroots experiment there, sending promising scholars, martial heroes and cultivation prodigies into the region's sects so that even immortal sects could one day be governed by mortal empires. Liu Qingfeng allows it to proceed, observing every step. *(Chapter 535 (3))*

## Trivia

- Li Baozhen's and Li Baoping's names both contain the character "bao" (宝), while Li Xisheng's does not, a detail that later proves significant to the secret of Li Xisheng's birth. *(Chapter 563 (1))*
- According to Li Baozhen himself, Zhu Lu fled to him in the capital in tears after her failure, and he nearly killed her with a single slap.
- Chen Ping'an regards him as a grudge he cannot avoid, alongside Zhu Lu.
- Liu Qingfeng, Li Baozhen and Wei Liang are later named as three people in the Azure Phoenix Nation whose actions could have implications even beyond Eastern Treasured Vial Continent.
