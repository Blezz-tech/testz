**Cai Jinjian** is a cultivator from [Dawn Cloud Mountain](Sects.md#dawn-cloud-mountain) who came to the small town in [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md) hunting for a fated opportunity. Her cruelty toward [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) nearly cost the boy his life, and he answered it by killing her in an alley. [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md) brought her back, and she returned home a changed woman.

## Appearance

Cai Jinjian is a beautiful young woman whose body has been cleansed by cultivation, so that to an ordinary village woman she would look like a goddess. *(Chapter 18)* She carries herself with an easy, natural allure, and a respected senior once said she had "aptitude and beauty in equal measure", which her sect took as a sign she would make a fine Dao partner for someone powerful. *(Chapter 20)* Like most people from Dawn Cloud Mountain, she cannot stand dirt.

## Personality

When she first arrives in the town, Cai Jinjian is polished and sweet on the surface and cold underneath. She sees the townsfolk as ants, smiles while she lies, and works hard to keep up the image of a gentle lady in front of Fu Nanhua while privately loathing Chen Ping'an, especially his eyes. She has real restraint and can recite a calming mantra rather than lash out, but once her pride is stung she is capable of ruining a child's life on a whim. She also carries the weight of her sect's hopes, which makes her hungry for any opportunity that could raise her standing.

Dying changed her. After Qi Jingchun revived her and talked with her, she lost interest in scheming and in cultivating only for power's sake. Back home she seemed listless at first, but she was thinking things through, and she later grew calm, steady and increasingly aloof, with a firm Dao heart. *(Chapter 253 (2))* She is quietly sharp as well. When Fu Nanhua tried to pressure her with threats against her sect's trade, she saw straight through them.

## Background

### Dawn Cloud Mountain

Cai Jinjian is a disciple of Dawn Cloud Mountain, one of the few immortal forces on Eastern Treasured Vial Continent that uses Buddhist cultivation methods. *(Chapter 642 (3))* The sect practices a technique called Observing the Sea of Clouds and lives off the sale of Cloud Root Stones. *(Chapter 37)* Its leaders spent heavily on her because they hoped she would lift the sect's reputation, and her master dreamed making her mountain master one day. Ten years before entering the town, she had traveled with her father to Lake Shujian and glimpsed the River Severing True Lord from afar. *(Chapter 17)*

### The small town

Cai Jinjian entered the town with [Fu Nanhua](Fu_Nanhua.md) of Old Dragon City, posing as a relative of [Gu Can](Gu_Can.md)'s mother, and she chose Gu Can as the opportunity she would pursue. The two struck an alliance and agreed to be open with each other, though Fu Nanhua had already decided to kill her before they set foot inside. *(Chapter 9)*

On [Clay Vase Alley](Small_Town.md#clay-vase-alley) she stepped in a pile of dog dung while walking backward and, furious and humiliated, turned on Chen Ping'an. She tapped his forehead and struck his chest, then smiled and told him he had half a year to live at most before claiming it was a joke. *(Chapter 10)* It was not. The tap forced open his acupoints, leaving his body like a house with no door, and the blow to his chest wrecked the foundations he would need to cultivate. *(Chapter 42)*

She was not acting entirely on her own. [Liu Zhimao](Liu_Zhimao.md), the River Severing True Lord, was hiding in Gu Can's home as a storyteller. He had a hand in her stepping in the dung, delayed Chen Ping'an's warning by a split second so she would take it as spite, and later fooled her into thinking she had reached enlightenment. Years later, Chen Ping'an accused Liu Zhimao of confusing Cai Jinjian with a secret technique and of planting the words "seek death with resolve" in his own heart, so that he would go after Cai Jinjian and Fu Nanhua and die. *(Chapter 438 (1))*

When she and Fu Nanhua were strolling together, Chen Ping'an charged out of a crossing alley. He drove a sharp porcelain shard into her throat, punched her in the chest and shouldered her into the side alley, leaving her in a pool of blood. Everyone took her for dead.

### Revival

Qi Jingchun stepped in, seeing her soul flicker like a candle in the wind. He brought her back to life and, while her body was still badly hurt, drew her soul out so they could talk in the school. He asked about small things in the outside world, like the price of rice and oil and new printing methods, then answered questions about her cultivation that had stumped her master and pointed her to classic Confucian works. When she asked why he saved her, he told her it broke the rules of the world but fit his beliefs.

Before they parted, Mr. Qi asked her for a favor. She was to carry Fleeting Glance Picture Scrolls to the Sword Qi Great Wall, each one showing part of Chen Ping'an's life after leaving the town. The last scroll held Mr. Qi's farewell message to Miss Ning, asking her to treat Chen Ping'an kindly. *(Chapter 386 (4))* She kept the near-death experience secret from her master and her sect.

### After returning

Cai Jinjian came home empty-handed after spending a whole pouch of gold essence copper coins, and her master became a laughingstock on the mountain. *(Chapter 253 (1))* When she visited Old Dragon City with her sect's elders, Fu Nanhua took her aside to find out why she had been saved and to propose an alliance. She turned him down calmly, though the two later became quite close.

Her cultivation then shot up. Within a few years she was a celebrated prodigy at Dawn Cloud Mountain with standing equal to earth immortals of older generations. She spends most of her time in seclusion and keeps a portrait of Mr. Qi that she painted herself, which she has never shown anyone. She now has her own peak and residence, and in quiet moments she still thinks of the scholar she admired most. She knows her feelings were never returned and is content to hold them anyway. *(Chapter 417 (2))*

## Trivia

- Chen Ping'an knew at once that her "joke" about his remaining time was true.
- Chen Ping'an has said that he killed Cai Jinjian for his own sake, while he fought the Mountain-Moving Ape for Liu Xianyang's sake.
- Years later, Chen Ping'an still had the porcelain shards from home and picked out the one he had used on her.
- When Mr. Qi gave her the first scroll, he asked, almost like a shy student, whether sending it would be gilding the lily, then admitted he knew nothing about romance.
- Dawn Cloud Mountain had become one of four sects-in-waiting on the continent.
