## Appearance

Zhu Lian appears as a short, skinny, hunchbacked old man who usually walks with his hands clasped behind his back and his eyes squinted into a smile, looking much like an ordinary old man from the marketplace.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> He remains hunched even when riding a horse, and of Chen Ping'an's four companions from the picture scrolls he is the one who looks most like a humble servant. On Downtrodden Mountain he is often seen in an apron, wiping his hands as he comes out of the kitchen.

This was not always his look. Before going mad in the Lotus Flower Blessed Land, Zhu Lian was known as a refined young master who could put otherworldly immortals to shame.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> He boasts that countless women of the blessed land spent their lives lovesick after a single glance at his portrait.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> When asked why he keeps his current appearance, he explains that he benefited too much from his looks in his hometown and grew tired of it, and that now he is a "senior citizen", he should act the part.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> Cen Yuanji, who reveres him, thinks that although he isn't very handsome now, he must have been very popular with women in his prime.

## Personality

Zhu Lian always wears a faint, kindly smile, yet he is the man who once almost turned the cultivation world of the Lotus Flower Blessed Land upside down.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup> He is easy-going, humorous and shameless, constantly flattering Chen Ping'an, calling himself "this old servant", and making lecherous remarks that earn him the disgust of Shi Rou and many female deities. He openly declares that he wants to capture the heart of a beautiful woman. He reads racy romance novels, writes his own drafts about chivalrous heroines, and covers the walls of his residence with portraits of beautiful women he has seen.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> Yet when Zheng Dafeng asks whether he ever truly loved anyone, he answers seriously that his feelings were always earnest, but that matters of his nation and the martial world kept him from controlling his own fate, and that it is all in the past.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup>

Beneath the clowning lie great insight and loyalty. Cui Dongshan judges him the one with the broadest outlook and highest ambition of the four picture-scroll people: adaptable, fond of good food, and the most likely to become truly loyal to Chen Ping'an.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> He is protective of Pei Qian and Cen Yuanji, a gentle guiding senior to the young people of Downtrodden Mountain, and willing to stand his ground against Chen Ping'an when he thinks his young master is wrong. When Pei Qian accuses him of being a fence-sitter, he describes his own shifts in loyalty as "adjusting to the circumstances like a wise and handsome individual".

He is also more capable than he lets on. He pretended to be a poor Go player while watching Sui Youbian and Lu Baixiang play, but is in fact an extraordinary player.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup> He is an excellent cook, earning the nickname "Old Cook" from Pei Qian, and his cursive calligraphy astonishes onlookers who assumed he was a mere servant.

## Background

Zhu Lian came from a truly wealthy clan in the Lotus Flower Blessed Land.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> He became known as the **Martial Arts Maniac**, the strongest person in the blessed land for several decades before Ding Ying.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> He practised external martial arts, intending to turn inward only after reaching the pinnacle, and tried to kill the nine other superior grandmasters of his generation. In that brutal battle, he grew stronger and deadlier the more wounded he became.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup> He was ultimately killed by Ding Ying, who took his silver-coloured lotus flower hat. Ding Ying never spoke of that battle afterwards. Zhu Lian later remarks that Ding Ying was the most talented martial artist he ever met, and jokes that he should have killed him with a single punch back then.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup>

After the fall of the blessed land, Zhu Lian was one of the four legendary figures preserved in picture scrolls that Chen Ping'an obtained, along with Wei Xian, Lu Baixiang and Sui Youbian. Because of the death he suffered, the old Daoist priest's rules cap his potential at the tenth tier.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup> Adapting to the Majestic World, he reaches the Far Roaming Tier quickly, making him the strongest of the four. Chen Ping'an considers his choice of a "shortcut" correct.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup>

## Trivia

- He introduces himself to the water goddess Xiao Luan as "Little Zhu, Zhu as in young and handsome".
- He once told Pei Qian that when she grew up she would ward off evil spirits better than a door god, meaning she would be so ugly that ghosts would fear her.
- Wei Bo felt that if he had met Zhu Lian years earlier, a long-held knot in his heart would have been untied much sooner.
- He became the senior steward of Downtrodden Mountain, where Chen Ruchu, the "young steward", sometimes manages even him.
- He deliberately provokes Cui Cheng so that he too can receive the old master's beatings.
- After Cui Cheng's death, Zhu Lian could have claimed half of Cui Cheng's scattered martial fortune, but he deliberately declined it.
