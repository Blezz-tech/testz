**Cui Minghuang** is a noble scholar of Lake View Academy and one of the two most famous Confucian scholars on Eastern Treasured Vial Continent. He is a clansman of [Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md) and carried out several of Cui Chan's plans, including the deception and killing of [Ma Zhan](Ma_Zhan.md).

## Appearance

He wears a jade pendant at his waist, which he takes care to protect when sitting on rough ground. *(Chapter 71)*

## Personality

Cui Minghuang is courteous, well read and composed. The schoolchildren who travelled with him found him charming and able to answer any question, and only Lin Shouyi disliked him. *(Chapter 84)* Beneath this polish he is calculating. A swordsman from True Martial Mountain warned that he was shrewd and scheming. *(Chapter 59)* He openly said that Qi Jingchun dying for the town's mortals was a poor trade he would never make himself.

He is cautious around stronger figures. He gave up his seat to Song Changjing, apologised on Chen Songfeng's behalf, and refused to push Old Man Yang for an answer. *(Chapter 50)* When Cui Chan told him a trap had also been laid for him, he showed no resentment and said that working this out would be the pleasure of his life now that he had finished his reading. *(Chapter 132)* Zhou Ju, who could see into people's hearts, saw in him a vast but war-torn land with no small figures in it, and at his core a plain, hardworking old farmer. *(Chapter 247)*

## Background

Cui Minghuang was expected to become the future mountain master of Lake View Academy. He entered Jewel Small World as a representative of Confucianism to collect a treasure. While staying at the kiln supervision office with Liu Baqiao and Chen Songfeng, he hinted to Chen Songfeng that the town would not last long and then went to the school to recover a seal. *(Chapter 45)* He later told Old Man Yang that he was 30 and wanted Cloud Drape Mountain as the site for a new academy, and he promised not to build without Old Man Yang's consent. Afterwards he sat in Qi Jingchun's empty study and remarked that there were few books in it.

Cui Chan, his granduncle, had been cast out of the Cui Clan. Cui Minghuang explained to Wu Yuan how Qi Jingchun's teacher had died, his books had been banned, and Mountain Cliff Academy had lost its standing. *(Chapter 82)* On Cui Chan's orders, he travelled with Ma Zhan and the school's last students and befriended Ma Zhan. He persuaded Ma Zhan that he could succeed Qi Jingchun, and then that he should break with Mountain Cliff Academy to head a new academy instead. On the road the two seemed like old friends, sharing wine and discussing the classics. Then one day Ma Zhan left with him to talk about their route and was later found dying. Ma Zhan had grown suspicious and, at the cost of destroying his own meridians and acupoints, left Cui Minghuang badly hurt. Cui Minghuang reported back to Cui Chan still coughing up blood.

When Cui Chan left the town, Cui Minghuang met him on a hill and agreed to look after the porcelain boy once the new academy was built. Cui Chan told him of the trap set for Wu Yuan and admitted he had prepared one for Cui Minghuang too. On Cui Chan's orders, he later went to Yellow Court Nation to invite an old flood dragon to be the first mountain master of the Cloud Drape Mountain academy, with Cui Minghuang as its deputy. He had the lowest cultivation of those present when the Old Scholar and others gathered there. *(Chapter 147)*

Cui Chan's original plan was for him to leave Lake View Academy with honour and become deputy mountain master of Forest Deer Academy, with the top post expected to pass to him later. After relations between Lake View Academy and the Great Li Empire collapsed, he had to stay at Lake View Academy instead. *(Chapter 464)*

## Trivia

- Liu Baqiao jokingly calls him "Old Man Cui" and "Brother Cui". *(Chapter 45)*
- Li Huai joked that he must be Ma Zhan's son or grandson because the two seemed so close.
- Cui Cheng told Chen Ping'an not to underestimate him.
