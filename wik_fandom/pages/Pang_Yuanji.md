## Appearance

Pang Yuanji is an elegant young man dressed in white, carrying himself with a jaded maturity that seems well beyond his twenty-five years of age. *(Chapter 575)* *(Chapter 576)* Unlike many of his sword immortal peers, he wears no Dao robe and carries no celestial weapon or Militarian armor pellets, relying on a single bonded flying sword instead. *(Chapter 577)*

## Personality

He is composed, self-possessed, and carries a deep-seated disdain for the prosperous, complacent Majestic World, a resentment that is kept from curdling into outright hatred only by his admiration for figures like A’Liang and Zuo You, along with the swordsmen of the Northern Complete Reed Continent. *(Chapter 576)* He has no interest in romance and, despite being considered one of the two young prodigies most suited to become Ning Yao’s Dao partner, could not care less who she ends up liking. *(Chapter 575)* He cares deeply about his standing as a swordsman of the Sword Qi Great Wall and as the Hidden Official’s disciple, and he prefers to keep his attention on cultivation rather than get tangled up in political disputes among the sword immortals. *(Chapter 586)* With his master he drops his usual composure, bickering and grumbling like any ordinary disciple rather than standing on ceremony. *(Chapter 586)*

## Background

Pang Yuanji is one of the three most exceptional young sword talents of the Sword Qi Great Wall, alongside Qi Shou and Gao Yehou, and is widely regarded as the strongest of the three, with the greatest hopes of inheriting the title of Hidden Official from his master in the coming century. Born into a middle-class family, he showed signs of extraordinary potential from the moment he was born and has cultivated under the erratic Lord Hidden Official ever since, making him technically only half her formal disciple. He is also on close terms with the sages of the Three Teachings stationed on the wall, regularly seeking them out to learn and ask questions. *(Chapter 575)*

His bonded flying sword, called Time, has no fixed shape or form, much like the concept it is named for, and its true value lies not in raw destructive power but in how it lets him master an enormous range of immortal techniques and accelerates his cultivation and soul refinement. This versatility earned him the nickname Hundred Schools Pang from a young age. *(Chapter 577)*

When Chen Ping’an arrived at the Sword Qi Great Wall and challenged its young sword cultivators one after another, Pang Yuanji agreed to fight him once Qi Shou had taken his turn, with the winner of their own sparring match first deciding who would face the outsider. After Chen Ping’an defeated Qi Shou, Pang Yuanji accepted the challenge himself, deploying a dozen or more illusory copies of himself to slowly envelop the entire street in a sword formation and transform it into his own small world. Chen Ping’an broke through the scheme at the last moment, and the two traded a final, decisive blow each, with Pang Yuanji ultimately conceding the match rather than letting the fight turn fully lethal. Chen Ping’an later admitted privately that he had won only through careful strategy and the rules of engagement, not because he was genuinely stronger, and that Pang Yuanji had deliberately held back just as he had. *(Chapter 577)* *(Chapter 578)*

Despite losing, Pang Yuanji bore Chen Ping’an no ill will, and the two gradually became friends, with Pang Yuanji becoming a regular, if begrudging, customer at Die Zhang’s wine shop alongside his friend Sword Immortal Gao Kui. *(Chapter 578)* He remained close to his master even after this, often visiting her and even being asked by her to weigh in on political disputes involving the Scholarly Sage’s and Second Sage’s lineages, matters he openly admitted he had little talent for navigating. *(Chapter 586)*

When the Savage World launched a renewed assault on the Sword Qi Great Wall, Pang Yuanji watched from the city wall as the Hidden Official, his own master, suddenly turned on the wall’s defenders, grievously wounding the nearly invincible swordsman Zuo You before defecting to the Savage World alongside the sword immortals Zhu An and Luo Shan. The betrayal left him shattered, collapsing to the ground in tears and sitting in a daze for a long while afterward, unable to understand why it had happened. *(Chapter 609)* *(Chapter 625)* His friend Gao Yehou urged him to one day challenge the Hidden Official and demand an explanation for her betrayal, but Pang Yuanji could only murmur that he was not the kind of person who could do such a thing. *(Chapter 626)*

## Trivia

- The Hidden Official had reportedly considered two potential heirs to her title before her betrayal: Pang Yuanji and, surprisingly, the young swordswoman Guo Zhujiu. *(Chapter 575)*
- He once asked his master why she kept such a close watch on Chen Ping’an, since he clearly wasn’t a spy for the demon tribe, and received only a typically cryptic non-answer for his trouble. *(Chapter 609)*
- He was the only person to win money on the city wall’s informal betting pool regarding the outcome of Chen Ping’an’s challenge matches. *(Chapter 578)*
