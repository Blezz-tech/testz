# Old Dragon City

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Old_Dragon_City)

---
Old Dragon City is a complex and dynamic location characterized by its immense power, rich history, and unique geographical location. It is a place of great wealth and commerce, but also a place where power struggles and conflicts are commonplace. The city is a major hub for trade and travel, and its connections to other continents make it a significant location in the narrative. The presence of powerful clans, mystical elements, and a constant state of tension and change make Old Dragon City a central location for many of the events that unfold in the story.

**General Characteristics:**

- Old Dragon City is located in the **southernmost region of the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent**. This location gives it a unique position, distinct from the northern regions of the continent.
- It is a **massive city**, so large that a person could spend their entire life exploring it without seeing everything.
- The city is divided into the **outer city, the inner city, and the Fu city**, each with its own distinct characteristics.
- It is described as the **most prosperous city** on the entire Eastern Treasured Vial Continent.
- The city is a **central hub connecting three continents**, making it a very diverse and complex place.
- Old Dragon City is known for having a **long and illustrious history**, producing prodigious and powerful cultivators.
- The city has a **unique feature**: a sea of clouds that floats above it. This sea of clouds is a **pseudo-celestial tool** of the city. The city also has an **underground dragon channel**.
- It is said that the city is the burial place of the last true dragon in the world.

**Social and Political Landscape:**

- The city is dominated by **powerful clans**, with the **Fu Clan** being the most powerful. The other major clans include the **Sun Clan**, **Fang Clan**, **Hou Clan**, and **Ding Clan**.
- These clans have deep roots in the city, and they often compete with each other for power and influence.
- The Fu Clan is traditionally the **dominant force in the south**, with a history spanning thousands of years.
- The **Sun Clan** is known for its immense foundation and exceptional business reputation, and its Nascent Tier patriarch. The Sun Clan is also known for its generosity, as they purchase goods from the villages they oversee for higher than market price.
- The city has a **complex social hierarchy**, with powerful cultivators and wealthy merchants at the top and ordinary citizens below.
- The clans in Old Dragon City are **followers of Commercialism of the Hundred Schools of Thought**, and their revered figures are different from Confucian sages.

**Key Figures and Groups:**

- **Fu Nanhua** is the eldest young master of the Fu Clan, described as shrewd and insightful. He is also set to marry a descendant of the Jiang Clan in order to form a marriage alliance.
- **Fu Qi** is the city lord of Old Dragon City and the leader of the Fu Clan. He is an earth immortal and is described as both easygoing and unforgiving.
- **Sun Jiashu** is a member of the Sun Clan who is very wealthy and has a unique ability to see the wealth that has slipped through someone's fingers. He has a complex relationship with Chen Ping'an.
- **Zheng Dafeng** is a powerful ninth-tier martial artist who has a close relationship with Old Man Yang. He is also considered a Dao mentor.
- There are also powerful figures such as **Chu Yang**, the Fu Clan’s premier guest elder who cultivates on the Dragon Scaling Platform.
- **Fan Er** is a member of the Fan Clan.
- There is an individual from the **Jiang Clan** who is set to marry into the Fu Clan.
- There are many **Qi refiners and martial artists** in Old Dragon City, with many at the middle five tiers.

**Economic and Commercial Aspects:**

- Old Dragon City is the **largest business hub** on the entire Eastern Treasured Vial Continent.
- It is a major **trade center**, with ships leading to any place where money can be made.
- The city's economy is driven by the **five major clans** and various smaller businesses.
- The **Sun Clan** is especially renowned for its business reputation.
- There is a **street owned by the Sun Clan** in the outer city.
- The city has many shops and merchants who trade goods from all over the continent.

**Mystical and Spiritual Elements:**

- The **sea of clouds** above the city is said to be a powerful entity that has been there for thousands of years. It acts as a defensive treasure.
- The city is believed to be the burial place of the last true dragon, and this is said to be the reason for the city's unique characteristics.
- There is a **Dragon Scaling Platform** in the city, which is a restricted area. The summit of the platform can only be seen from a ship in the sky.
- Old Dragon City has four **pseudo-celestial tools**, two of which are in the possession of the Fu Clan's leader.
- The city is described as having abundant **yang energy** due to the number of Qi refiners.
- The area around the Sun Clan's ancestral residence is considered a renowned paradise.

**Conflicts and Tensions:**

- There is a constant tension among the five major clans as they struggle for power.
- There have been conflicts and power struggles between the Fu Clan and other major clans.
- There is an awareness of the potential for a large-scale conflict that could damage the city.
- There are also conflicts between the city and outside forces, as well as threats from demonic cultivators.
- There are many **strange occurrences** that take place in Old Dragon City.

**Connections to the Wider World:**

- Old Dragon City is a major **connection point** between Eastern Treasured Vial Continent and Parasol Leaf Continent.
- It has **intercontinental ships** that can travel to other continents, such as Stalactite Mountain.
- The city is a place where powerful figures from other continents often visit.
- There are ships from the city that travel to the Sword Qi Great Wall, although these are few in number.
- The city has connections to the Great Li Empire.
