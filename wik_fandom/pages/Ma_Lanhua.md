**Ma Lanhua**, known in the small town as **Granny Ma**, is the grandmother of [Ma Kuxuan](Ma_Kuxuan.md). She was a notoriously foul-tempered old woman of [Apricot Blossom Alley](Small_Town.md#apricot-blossom-alley) and one of the town's most skilled trash talkers. After being slapped to death by a cultivator, she was made the river guardian of the Dragon Whisker River by Old Man Yang, purely for her grandson's sake. She was later promoted to river goddess.

Timid and obsequious towards the powerful, petty and vindictive towards everyone else, she nevertheless loves her grandson deeply. She also showed a rare flash of conscience over the shattering of [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md)'s bonded porcelain.

## Appearance

As a living woman, Granny Ma was a frail old woman. As a river deity, her appearance grows more youthful by the day. She appears with long hair spreading across the riverbank, or as a clump of waterweeds swaying in the river.

## Personality

In life, Ma Lanhua was infamous in the small town. [Zhi Gui](Zhi_Gui.md) calls her "far too detestable", possessing every bad habit common and uncommon among women of the common class, and has long wanted revenge on her for her spiteful behaviour at the Iron Lock Well. She was one of the few people who could rival Gu Can's mother in an argument, although Gu Can's mother sent her home to "reflect" time and again. *(Chapter 202)*

As a deity, she remains timid and obsequious before anyone stronger, calling Old Man Yang "Great Immortal" and fawning over him. Towards those weaker, she is greedy and petty: she demands bribes from passing mountain spirits, and kills an old tortoise in a fit of anger. Yet she cares deeply for Ma Kuxuan, and she endured everything she suffered as a deity largely so he would not be left alone in the world.

## Background

Ma Lanhua lived on Apricot Blossom Alley with her son and daughter-in-law, who managed to control a dragon kiln. The couple were deeply involved in the shattering of Chen Ping'an's bonded porcelain, along with the Great Li Empire's imperial concubine and the late emperor. In a rare display of conscience, Ma Lanhua tried everything she could to stop them, but her son and daughter-in-law were consumed by greed and she failed. *(Chapter 530 (2))*

## Plot

### Death

Granny Ma is slapped at her doorway by a cultivator who does not control their strength, and dies from the blow. Ma Kuxuan finds her body with her cheek swollen black and blue. A deity tells him that her soul cannot be found because of the sins she committed in life. Ma Kuxuan suspects it is the girl whose cultivation he disturbed on the covered bridge, and enraged, beats up the girl. He later leaves True Martial Mountain to seek out the commander of the Sea Tide Cavalry in connection with her death. *(Chapter 224)*

### River Guardian

Old Man Yang grants her soul the indestructible body of a river guardian. He explains what she must do to win a temple, a golden statue and recognition in the empire's genealogical records. When she asks whether he protects her only because of her grandson, he openly admits that he does. When she presses him on why he let True Martial Mountain take Ma Kuxuan instead of teaching him himself, he punishes her with agonising pain. *(Chapter 63 (1))*

As a river guardian, she becomes younger and stronger. When a group of mountain spirits travelling along the Iron Talisman River approaches her for help, she bluntly demands a gift from each of them before she will escort them west. *(Chapter 96)*

### River Goddess

Ma Lanhua is promoted to formal river goddess of the Dragon Whisker River by the Great Li Empire, and can now briefly come ashore. She begs Old Man Yang for even a small, empty shrine, but he sneers that with her reputation, she would get vats of spit rather than incense. Swimming past the blacksmith shop, she now feels she has turned Ruan Xiu from an enemy into a friend, considers herself half a helper to the Militarian sage, and privately thinks of herself as Old Man Yang's secret disciple. *(Chapter 118 (2))*

When Chen Ping'an returns home with the little boy in azure, who starts a fight in her river, she meets him for the first time as a deity. She hides her identity, telling him that a river deity must sever her mortal past, while privately resenting his good fortune. *(Chapter 180 (1))*

Old Man Yang orders her to go to the source of the Dragon Whisker River and disperse half of her godly body into the water, to increase its yin energy for Ruan Qiong's sword forging. The agony and damage to her Great Dao are immense. *(Chapter 185 (1))* Soon after, the haughty Iron Talisman River Goddess, [Yang Hua](Yang_Hua.md), her superior, visits the waterfall for the first time and leaves without acknowledging Ma Lanhua's timid greeting, to her silent indignation. *(Chapter 201 (2))*

### Reunion with Ma Kuxuan

Years later, Ma Kuxuan returns home and goes first to the river rather than to his parents. Ma Lanhua then emerges from the river, rejuvenated in appearance, and sits with her grandson on the bank, holding his hands. She nags him, badmouths everyone who has wronged her, and pokes his forehead for not listening to his elders.

## Abilities

- **River deity powers:** She can travel through and control the Dragon Whisker River, appear in the water, and, as a river goddess, briefly come ashore.
- **Yin water:** She can turn the river's water "dark", producing water essence beneficial for forging swords.
- **Indestructible body:** Her river guardian body was granted by Old Man Yang.

## Trivia

- 马兰花 (mǎ lán huā) is the name of a flower, the Chinese iris.
- The mountain spirits she extorted were outraged that a "lowly river guardian" demanded bribes after they politely called her a river god.
