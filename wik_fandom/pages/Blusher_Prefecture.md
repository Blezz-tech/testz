# Blusher Prefecture

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Blusher_Prefecture)

---
Blusher Prefecture is a complex and significant location within the narrative, characterized by its unique blend of cultural, political, and supernatural elements.

**Political and Administrative Landscape:**

- **Part of Colorful Garment Nation:** Blusher Prefecture is one of the six prefectures of Colorful Garment Nation. This places it within the administrative structure of a larger political entity, though one that is not considered to be among the most powerful.
- **Prefectural Overseer:** The prefecture is governed by a Prefectural Overseer, a high-ranking official responsible for the region's administration. The Prefectural Overseer of Blusher Prefecture, Lord Liu, is a scholar known for his essays. He is also a fourth-rank official.
- **Official Roads**: The prefecture is connected by official roads.
- **County Magistrate**:  Dragon Spring County was changed to Locust Yellow County after it became part of Dragon Spring Prefecture. There is also a county magistrate, Yuan, in Locust Yellow County, who is different than the previous magistrate, Wu Yuan.
- **Imperial Court Influence**: The imperial court of Colorful Garment Nation has a significant influence on the prefecture.
- **Prefectural Office:** The prefectural office is a significant administrative building, where important decisions and meetings take place.
- **Military Presence:** There is a military presence in the prefecture, with a general (General Ma) and soldiers stationed there. The general is said to have fought in a hundred battles. The general also oversees the prefecture.
- **Relationship with the Great Li Empire**: There is an indication that the Great Li Empire has spies and influence in the prefecture.
- **Taxes**: The prefecture has a history of taxation, and one prefectural overseer had to beg wealthy clans for assistance in raising money for a city god statue.

**Cultural and Social Aspects:**

- **Scholarly Arts:** Blusher Prefecture is known for its passion for scholarly arts, especially Go. There is a strong emphasis on education and intellectual pursuits.
- **Go Players**: There are many skilled Go players in the prefecture, with an elder known to be the number one player.
- **Wealthy Clans:** The prefecture is home to wealthy clans.
- **Traditions:** It has a strong sense of local traditions.
- **Open-Minded**: The prefecture is said to be relatively open-minded.
- **Lanterns**: Colorful lanterns are used to decorate for events and festivals.
- **City God Statue**: The city god statue was gilded using gold leaf, but now it is missing.

**Key Locations and Structures:**

- **City Walls:** The prefecture is surrounded by towering city walls, with guard stations.
- **City God Pavilion:** A structure dedicated to the city god, which plays a significant role in the story. The city god has a physical form.
- **Zhao Clan Residence**: The Zhao Clan residence is located in the center of the city.
- **Embroidery Alley:** A location within the city where there have been strange occurrences.
- **Horse Head Bridge**: A location where there have been strange occurrences.
- **Dangling Copper Tower**: A location where there have been strange occurrences.
- **Lake View Academy**: A notable academy near the border of White Mountain Nation, with ties to the prefecture.
- **Unnamed Temple**: There is a newly constructed temple that needs a couplet.

**Supernatural and Mystical Elements:**

- **Demonic Cultivators**: The prefecture is experiencing a demonic crisis. Demonic cultivators are causing chaos.
- **City God:** The city god of Blusher Prefecture plays a role in the events. The city god is a spirit, and his health is tied to the city.
- **Mountain and River Gods:** There are nearby mountain and river gods who may be called upon in times of crisis.
- **Immortals**: There are immortals and cultivators who get involved in the prefecture's affairs.
- **Heretical Cultivators**: There are also heretical cultivators with unknown backgrounds.
- **Heavenly Master Seal**: A talisman in the form of a Heavenly Master Seal was placed there two hundred years ago by Mount Longhu's Heavenly Master Residence, which may have contributed to the recent demonic problems in the prefecture.
- **Yin Energy**: There is dense yin energy at unmarked graveyards, which seems to have been changed by the geomancy map.
- **Spirit Screens**: There is a reference to spirit screens.

**Key Figures and Groups:**

- **Prefectural Overseer Liu:** The current Prefectural Overseer, known for his literary talent.
- **General Ma**: A military leader in the prefecture.
- **Old Immortal:** An unnamed old immortal is present in the prefecture during the crisis. He is from a foreign nation and is involved in the Go match.
- **Chong Miao:** An old Daoist priest with a Daoist title, whom many suspect is taking advantage of the dire situation.
- **Xu Yuanxia:** A cultivator who is also involved in the crisis.
- **Zhang Shanfeng:** Another cultivator who is involved in the crisis.
- **Liu Gaohua:** The son of Prefectural Overseer Liu, who becomes involved in the events. He is a scholar.
- **The Aide**: The Prefectural Overseer's aide is experienced and knowledgeable, and he assists the Prefectural Overseer by offering advice.
- **Spiritual Horn Force:** A local cultivation force that is expected to arrive in the prefecture to help with the crisis. The Spiritual Horn Force has a colored luan which is used for travel.
- **Old Demon Mi's Disciples**: Three shop attendants from the rice shop are revealed to be disciples of Old Demon Mi.
- **Zhao Liu**: Zhao Liu is from Divine Edict Sect.
- **City God**:  The city god was a scholar who was appointed as a god by the emperor of Colorful Garment Nation. He is also aware of the demonic cultivators.
- **Chen Ping'an:** He is drawn into the events of Blusher Prefecture and plays a key role in addressing the demonic crisis.
- **Gao Shuyi**: A noble of Great Quan Empire who is known to act wantonly, and is related to a duke.
- **Liu Chicheng**: A scholar who is a great demon who can see other's true forms.

**Challenges and Crises:**

- **Demonic Threat:** The prefecture is facing a significant demonic threat. There are demons and demonic cultivators causing problems.
- **Missing Treasures**: A sect treasure of the Spiritual Horn Force goes missing, drawing the attention of the sect away from the prefecture.
- **Internal Corruption:** There are implications of corruption and internal issues within the prefectural administration.
- **City God in Trouble**: The city god is in trouble and needs assistance.
- **Political Instability**: The prefecture is experiencing political instability.

**Interactions with the Wider World:**

- **Other Nations:** The prefecture has dealings with other nations, and there are outsiders coming to help with the crisis.
- **Cultivation World:** The prefecture interacts with the cultivation world, with various sects and cultivators playing roles in the events.
- **Great Li Empire**: The Great Li Empire has influence and spies within the prefecture.
- **Divine Edict Sect:** A sect is mentioned as having disciples in the prefecture.
- **Lake View Academy**: The academy is a location visited by officials of the prefecture, indicating that there is influence between the two locations.

Blusher Prefecture is a prefecture within Colorful Garment Nation, which is a place with a complex interplay of political administration, cultural traditions, and supernatural events. It is a place of scholarly pursuits and wealthy clans, but it is also under threat from demonic forces and political instability. The prefecture's story involves a diverse cast of characters, including officials, scholars, cultivators, and city gods, all navigating the challenges of a world where the mundane and the mystical often intersect. The prefecture is also a location of intense conflict and intrigue, drawing the attention of other nations and cultivation forces, and it serves as an important setting for the unfolding narrative.
