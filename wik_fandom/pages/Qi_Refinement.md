# Qi Refinement

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Qi_Refinement)

---
Qi refinement is a fundamental practice in the sources, involving the manipulation and cultivation of spiritual energy, or Qi, to achieve higher levels of power and longevity.

**Core Principles**:

- **Qi as the Foundation**: Qi is seen as the basic building block of life and cultivation. Cultivators manipulate their own Qi and the Qi of the world to gain power.
- **Body as a Vessel**: The physical body is viewed as a vessel for Qi, which must be strengthened and refined to support higher levels of cultivation.
- **Mind and Will**: Cultivating the mind is also an important component of Qi refinement.
- **Acupoints and Meridians**: Acupoints are key locations in the body that serve as pathways for Qi. Opening and refining acupoints is crucial for absorbing and storing spiritual energy. Meridians are like paths for the flow of Qi and the wider and stronger they are, the better.
- **External vs. Internal**: Qi refiners may draw Qi from within themselves or from the outside world.
- **Balance and Harmony**: The goal of Qi refinement is to balance internal and external energies, to nurture and refine Qi.

**Tiers of Qi Refinement**:

- The sources describe a tiered system of Qi refinement, generally divided into **Lower, Middle, and Upper Five Tiers**.
- **Lower Five Tiers**: Also known as the **Mountain Ascending Five Tiers**, these tiers focus on refining the body. They are the Copper Skin Tier, Grass Root Tier, Willow Tendon Tier, Bone Qi Tier, and Furnace Molding Tier.
  - The first four tiers focus on cultivating the flesh and nurturing a tough physique.
  - At the peak stage of the Mud Molding Tier, one's Qi descends to their Dantian.
  - The final tier of the Physique Refining Tier is the Mercury Tier, where one's blood becomes like mercury.
- **Middle Five Tiers**: These tiers are the Cave Abode Tier, the Sea Observation Tier, the Dragon Gate Tier, the Golden Core Tier, and the Nascent Tier.
  - The **Golden Core Tier** marks a significant step, where a cultivator's sea of Qi condenses into a golden core. This allows them to store more energy, and even borrow spiritual energy from heaven and earth.
  - After reaching the Golden Core Tier, one is regarded as a genuine immortal.
- **Upper Five Tiers**: The sources mention the **Unpolished Jade Tier** as a level of great accomplishment, where cultivators return to a state of natural simplicity.
  - At the Unpolished Jade Tier, one's body is like gold and jade, resistant to natural elements, and one can enjoy a lifespan of 500 to 1000 years.
  - Female Qi refiners at this tier are said to have the ability to beautify themselves without detrimentally affecting their fate.
  - Those at the Upper Five Tiers are much more flexible and free than those at the Nascent Tier.
- There is also mention of an **11th tier**, but this is not discussed in detail in the sources.

**Methods and Practices**:

- **Meditation and Breathing**: Specific breathing techniques are used to gather and refine Qi.
- **Acupoint Development**: Activating and refining acupoints allows for increased absorption of spiritual energy.
- **Visualization**: Visualization is an important component of cultivation, especially for cultivators of Dawn Cloud Mountain.
- **Scriptures and Mantras**:  These can help guide the flow of Qi and enhance cultivation.
- **Talismans**: Drawing talismans is a way to channel spiritual energy for various purposes.
- **Sword Qi**: Qi can be imbued into swords, with sword cultivators focusing on both sword techniques and refining the sword.

**Challenges and Obstacles**:

- **Internal Demons**: Cultivators must overcome their inner demons, which can hinder progress.
- **Tribulations**:  Cultivators face tribulations or challenges at various stages, and these must be overcome to advance.
- **Impurity**: Over time, impurities can accumulate and obstruct the pursuit of the Great Dao.
- **Qi Deviation**: Incorrect methods can lead to Qi deviation, which can severely damage a cultivator.
- **Bottlenecks**: There are difficulties in advancing to higher tiers.
- **External Interference**: Some cultivators can be harmed by others.

**Key Concepts**:

- **Dao Heart**:  Those with a strong Dao Heart, aspiring to the Dao, will be able to progress more quickly in their cultivation.
- **Fated Opportunities**: Cultivators may encounter fated opportunities, which can accelerate their growth.
- **Mental Cultivation**: Mental cultivation is just as important as the development of one's powers.
- **The Great Dao**: This is the ultimate goal of cultivation, but there are many different paths to achieving it.
- **Longevity**: Qi refinement can allow cultivators to extend their lifespans.
- **Inner World**:  Cultivators can create a small world within their bodies, like a blessed land.

**Heritage**: The concept of heritage, which is an intangible concept that's impossible to explain, seems to be connected to Qi refinement.

**Differences from Martial Arts**:

- **Qi Refiners**: Rely on the bestowal of the heavens, and their progress is often linked to luck and fated opportunities. They focus on nurturing and refining Qi.
- **Martial Artists**: Progress step-by-step and rely on breaking through obstacles. They focus on tempering Qi and converting the auras in their acupoints.
- Qi refiners focus on the body only as a vessel, while martial artists focus on the body as a tool.
- Martial artists and Qi refiners take virtually the exact opposite route when it comes to qi refinement.
- Qi refiners at the lower tiers focus on refining their body, which overlaps with martial arts training.

**Tools and Resources**:

- **Flying Swords**: Swords are a way to channel Qi, and swordsmen often refine their swords.
- **Talisman Paper**: Talisman cultivators need to use large amounts of talisman paper.
- **Immortal Treasures**: These can greatly enhance cultivation, with some being linked to the five elements.
- **Jade Slips**: These can contain powerful mantras and provide insights into cultivation.
- **Bonded Items**:  Refining items to become bonded items is an important step in cultivation, and it allows for the storage of spiritual energy.

Qi refinement is a complex and multifaceted practice that involves the manipulation of spiritual energy to achieve greater power, longevity, and understanding of the Great Dao. It is a core element of the world depicted in the sources, with its own set of practices, challenges, and goals. The tiered system, various cultivation methods, and the distinction between Qi refiners and martial artists all contribute to the rich tapestry of this fictional world.
