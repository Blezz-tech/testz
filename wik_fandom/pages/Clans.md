# Clans

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Clans)

---
> **Spoiler Warning:** This article contains spoilers from the *Sword of Coming* (*Jian Lai* / *Unsheathed*) novel and donghua.

**Clans** are the great families. Many hold dragon kilns, intercontinental ships, armies or mountains, and some are tied by marriage or old debts to sects and empires across several continents. 

## Eastern Treasured Vial Continent

### Four Families and Ten Clans

The Four Families and Ten Clans, also called the Four Surnames and Ten Clans, are the leading households of the small town, living on [Fortune Street](Small_Town.md#fortune-street-and-peach-leaf-alley) and Peach Leaf Alley. The four great surnames of Fortune Street are Lu, Li, Zhao and Song, and between them they own ten large dragon kilns. *(Chapter 90)* For centuries the clans schemed and shed blood over the town's thirty or so dragon kilns. After the town became Dragon Spring County, they were forced into an alliance, though each kept its own secret ties to the imperial court. *(Chapter 127)*

The Imperial Song Clan secretly favored them. Under a hidden pact with the sages, each clan could keep the bonded porcelains of one to three cultivation prodigies, who were allowed to cultivate inside the small world free of its usual suppression but could not leave without permission. *(Chapter 471)* When Wu Yuan arrived as county magistrate, the clans gave him little help and mostly watched him struggle.

### Lu Clan

The Lu Clan was the head of the Four Families and Ten Clans inside the town, yet the weakest of them outside it, because the empire it backed, the Lu Empire, was destroyed by the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md). *(Chapter 34)* Its ties to the fallen empire left it worse off than any other clan after Jewel Small World fell to the ground. Lu Zhengchun left with a lady of the Xu Clan of Light Breeze City and became a direct disciple of that clan's ancestral hall. *(Chapter 651)*

### Li Clan

The Li Clan of Fortune Street is led by Li Hong. His children are [Li Xisheng](Li_Xisheng.md), [Li Baozhen](Li_Baozhen.md) and [Li Baoping](Li_Baoping.md). Their grandfather became the first cultivator of the Four Families and Ten Clans to reach the tenth tier. *(Chapter 206)* He is a Nascent Tier earth immortal and a secret top guest elder of the Great Li Empire. [Sun Scorch Mountain](Sects.md#sun-scorch-mountain) left [Tao Zi](Tao_Zi.md) in the clan's care while she was in the town. The Li Clan later moved away, and its two dragon kilns passed to the Wei and Liu clans of Peach Leaf Alley.

### Zhao Clan

The Zhao Clan of Fortune Street keeps quiet about its wealth, unlike the Lu Clan, which likes to show off its power and present itself as a clan of scholars. [Zhao Yao](Zhao_Yao.md) belongs to this clan. *(Chapter 27)* Its head steward, like those of the Song Clan, comes from the town's Chen Clan, and the two sides treat each other almost as family.

### Song Clan

The Song Clan of Fortune Street holds two pouches of gold essence copper coins, the most of any of the Four Families and Ten Clans. It is a separate household from the Imperial Song Clan of the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md).

### Xie Clan

The Xie Clan lives on Peach Leaf Alley. Few in number and in decline, it survived for centuries thanks to a pair of incense entities. It was saved from worse when a long-eyebrowed boy of the clan became Ruan Qiong's unofficial disciple. *(Chapter 187)* Its old ancestor is [Xie Shi](Xie_Shi.md), who returned with the sword immortal [Cao Xi](Cao_Xi.md) and stayed at the clan's residence. The Wind God Xie Clan of the Lu Empire is a separate family.

### Wei Clan and Liu Clan

The Wei and Liu clans of Peach Leaf Alley took over the Li Clan's two dragon kilns after the Li Clan left the town.

### Ma Clan

The Ma Clan is wealthy and owns two dragon kilns, but its ancestors forbade it from ever moving to Fortune Street or Peach Leaf Alley. [Ma Kuxuan](Ma_Kuxuan.md) is a member.

### Chen Clan of the small town

Almost everyone in the small town with the surname Chen works as a servant for the Four Families and Ten Clans, many of them for generations. *(Chapter 57)* According to Chen Songfeng, the town once had two Chen Clans. One stayed and became servants, while the other left long ago for another continent and grew into the [Yingyin Chen Clan](#yingyin-chen-clan). *(Chapter 61)*

### Dragon Tail County Chen Clan

The Chen Clan of Dragon Tail County, also called the Dragon Tail Creek Chen Clan, is a wealthy scholarly clan with more than a thousand years of history. Its ancestral rules forbid it from taking part in other sects' and clans' conflicts, and it knows of the Yingyin Chen Clan only through a distant relationship. Chen Songfeng is its eldest grandson. It later opened a free private school in the small town that even the children of Lu Empire prisoners could attend. *(Chapter 188)*

### Other small town families

- **Yang Clan** runs the Yang Family's medicine shop. It is well off but not part of the Four Families and Ten Clans, and every generation keeps a set of ancestral rules. Old Man Yang has watched over it for generations. *(Chapter 428)*
- **Lin Clan** is a large clan that is not part of the Four Families and Ten Clans and keeps to itself. [Lin Shouyi](Lin_Shouyi.md)'s father was a minor official in the kiln supervision office.
- **Gu Clan** is the family of [Gu Can](Gu_Can.md). His father Gu Tao became a yin god and later the mountain god of the Great Li Empire's old Northern Mountain.

### Imperial Song Clan

The Imperial Song Clan rules the Great Li Empire. It rose from a small vassal state of the Lu Empire to rule the whole north of the continent. *(Chapter 189)* Its Peace and Safety Tablets carry the clan's cloud pattern on their corners. It is a sworn enemy of the [Gao Clan](#gao-clan) of the Great Sui Nation. See [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md).

### Yuan Clan

The Yuan Clan is a pillar clan in the capital of the Great Li Empire. Its ancestor Yuan Xie and the Cao Clan's ancestor Cao Hang came from the same place and fought side by side, and the history books call them the "twin jade pendants of scholarship and martial arts". The two clans once intermarried often, but over the past century or so they became hostile. *(Chapter 191)* Wu Yuan married into the Yuan Clan. The new Wenchang pavilion in the small town honors Yuan Xie.

### Cao Clan

The Cao Clan is the Yuan Clan's rival pillar clan. Cao Hang is shown on the door gods of Dragon Spring Prefecture and honored in its martial sage temple. Its most senior ancestor is the sword immortal Cao Xi of Southern Whirl Continent, who outranks even the ancestor who won the clan its title. Only the eldest son of each generation is told. *(Chapter 201)*

### Guan Clan

The Guan Clan is a pillar clan of the Great Li Empire whose patriarch oversees the Ministry of Personnel. Its direct grandson Guan Yiran served as the Great Li general stationed at [Bamboo Scroll Lake](Bamboo_Scroll_Lake.md). *(Chapter 455)*

### Yu Clan (Great Li)

The Yu Clan is one of two lesser-known pillar clans that rival the Yuan and Cao. It lives in the mountains and seldom takes part in mortal affairs. *(Chapter 450)*

### Gao Clan

The Gao Clan, also called the Geyang Gao Clan, has ruled the [Great Sui Nation](Great_Sui_Nation.md) for all of its 1,200 years. It is a mortal enemy of the Great Li Empire's Song Clan. *(Chapter 13)* Gao Xuan is a member, and the clan's only Unpolished Jade Tier cultivator is an ancestor who works as a storyteller.

### Cui Clan

The Cui Clan has been one of the most respected and highly educated clans on the continent for a thousand years. *(Chapter 243)* It is the clan of [Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md), and its old ancestor is the tenth tier martial artist [Cui Cheng](Cui_Cheng.md).

### Chu Clan

Several unrelated clans share this name. The Chu Clan of Prosperous City is an esteemed guest of the Great Li Empire, and its patriarch is deputy sect leader of Rattle Mountain. One of its young masters was beaten by Ruan Xiu. *(Chapter 94)* The Chu Clan of the Yellow Court Nation is that nation's founding clan. The Chu Clan of Phoebe Creek in the Great Sui Nation was one of the clans Li Er named for punishment.

### Xu Clan

The Xu Clan of Light Breeze City is a powerful force on the continent. It schemed for the armor handed down in [Liu Xianyang](Liu_Xianyang.md)'s family and won it in the small town. *(Chapter 65)* It is known for its Fox Skin Beauty Talismans and later became a firm ally of Sun Scorch Mountain.

### Fu Clan

The Fu Clan has ruled Old Dragon City for thousands of years. Countless emperors rose in the north of the continent, while the Fu Clan stayed dominant in the south. *(Chapter 251)* Its leader, Fu Qi, is city lord, and Fu Nanhua is his favored heir. The clan owns two of the city's six intercontinental ships, a Treasure Swallowing Whale and a floating mountain known as a miniature [Stalactite Mountain](Stalactite_Mountain.md). *(Chapter 252)* Fu Nanhua's marriage to a woman of the Cloud Forest Jiang Clan brought a Nascent Tier maidservant and a young flood dragon as dowry. *(Chapter 361)* After Sun Jiashu and the Ding Clan's Du Yan sided with it, the Fang and Hou clans had to follow its orders. *(Chapter 366)*

### Sun Clan

The Sun Clan ranks second in Old Dragon City, ahead of the Fang, Hou and Ding clans. Every other major clan in the city has changed many times over the past few thousand years, but the Sun Clan has survived. Its own crises were all linked to the Sword Qi Great Wall. It owns the Mountain and Sea Turtle, and its rules forbid spending on things that are not needed. Its leader, Sun Jiashu, later lent Downtrodden Mountain three hundred grain rain coins.

### Fan Clan

The Fan Clan of Old Dragon City owns the intercontinental ship Osmanthus Island and brews osmanthus wine. Its heir is [Fan Er](Fan_Er.md), and his half-sister [Fan Junmao](Fan_Junmao.md), a Nascent Tier cultivator, holds more sway than the whole ancestral hall. *(Chapter 362)* A separate Fan Clan rules Pond Water City beside Bamboo Scroll Lake, where Fan Yan acted the fool while serving the Great Li Empire.

### Fang Clan

The Fang Clan has no Nascent Tier cultivator, but two seventh tier martial artists and a Golden Core Tier swordsman give it great sway over the southern mortal empires. It owns countless banks, escort agencies, pawnshops and inns there. *(Chapter 260)* One of its descendants defiled and killed a woman of the Dust Medicinal Shop, which led Zheng Dafeng to storm its manor.

### Hou Clan

The Hou Clan relies on guest elders of the Middle Five Tiers. Its foothold in Old Dragon City comes from a branch descendant who became a virtuous scholar of Lake View Academy. It also controls the most routes from the city to Northern Complete Reed Continent.

### Ding Clan

The Ding Clan nearly lost its place among the five major clans. A disciple of the Parasol Leaf Sect, Du Yan, married into it and kept it in power. It competes with the Fan Clan.

### Cloud Forest Jiang Clan

The Cloud Forest Jiang Clan was the first prominent clan of the Middle Earth Divine Continent to move to Eastern Treasured Vial Continent. Its manor stands on the southeastern coast and stretches close to twenty kilometers out into the sea. Its marriage alliance with the Fu Clan reshaped Old Dragon City, and the [Azure Phoenix Nation](Azure_Phoenix_Nation.md) lies beside its lands.

### Imperial Tang Clan

The Imperial Tang Clan rules the Azure Phoenix Nation. It grants royal titles without lands and deals firmly with the immortals of the mountains. *(Chapter 376)* See [Azure Phoenix Nation](Azure_Phoenix_Nation.md).

## Southern Whirl Continent

### Yingyin Chen Clan

The Yingyin Chen Clan, also called the Pure Confucian Chen Clan, was once the smallest of eight Yimen Chen Clan branches on the Middle Earth Divine Continent before it moved to Southern Whirl Continent to escape conflict. After its ancestor appeared, carrying a sun and a moon on his shoulders, the clan was transformed. A Confucian school and an academy were built on its land, and the road to its gates is lined with archways honoring its descendants. It is the only clan granted the title "Pure Confucian". Its clan leader is Chen Chun'an, and Chen Dui is a direct lineal descendant. Liu Xianyang's ancestors were graveyard attendants for the clan, and he went to Southern Whirl Continent to study there. *(Chapter 571)*

## Parasol Leaf Continent

### Yao Clan (Great Quan Empire)

The Yao Clan commands a border army of the Great Quan Empire. Its plainly armored cavalry is famous along the border. *(Chapter 330)* Chen Ping'an met its troops on his travels through the empire.

### Huan Clan

The Huan Clan holds the Flying Eagle Fortress in Agarwood Nation. It moved there from Lasting Yi Nation more than six hundred years ago to escape war, and its ancestral hall is called Zhongying Hall. *(Chapter 291)* The fortress had once been famous for almost a century, but had long since declined.

## Northern Complete Reed Continent

### Sui Clan

The Sui Clan is an old official clan of Five Mountain Nation, led in name by Old Assistant Minister Sui Xinyu. Chen Ping'an escorted four of its members to the Great Seal Script Empire, and its daughter Sui Jingcheng was known as the clan's Jade Person. *(Chapter 519)*

### Yang Clan of Lofty Cloud Palace

> *Main article: [Sects](Sects.md#lofty-cloud-palace)*

The Yang Clan of the Great Origin Empire's Esteemed Mystery Department is known for repaying kindness many times over, and hostility just as fully.

## Pure White Continent

### Liu Clan

The Liu Clan is the foremost clan of Pure White Continent and is called the God of Wealth of the Majestic World. It owns the Ape Havoc Estate, one of the four great private residences on Stalactite Mountain. *(Chapter 269)* Its heir is Liu Youzhou, the "Treasure Child". The clan is said to have built its fortune from a single copper coin.

## Middle Earth Divine Continent

### Yu Clan (Shaoyuan Empire)

The Yu Clan is an ancient top-tier clan of the Middle Earth Divine Continent. *(Chapter 597)* Its daughter Yu Juanfu, a pure martial artist, came to the Sword Qi Great Wall under Zhou Shenzhi's protection. Once she regains her standing, she is expected to control half the national power of the Shaoyuan Empire.

## Sword Qi Great Wall

The great clans of the city north of the Sword Qi Great Wall each hold their own top sword technique. The Chen Clan's strengthens bones, the Dong Clan's cleanses marrow, the Qi Clan's tempers souls, the Ning Clan's sharpens bonded swords, the Yao Clan's nourishes sword qi, and the Nalan Clan's joins sword qi and sword intent. *(Chapter 277)*

### Chen Clan (Sword Qi Great Wall)

The Chen Clan is [Chen Qingdu](Chen_Qingdu.md)'s clan. Like the Dong Clan, its strength rests on generations of sword immortals. *(Chapter 574)* Its current leader, Chen Xi, has carved a character on the wall, but he still feels like a child before the old great sword immortal. For that reason, its members are the most reluctant of all the clans to visit the city wall. *(Chapter 596)* Chen Sanqiu is a young master of the clan.

### Dong Clan

The Dong Clan rose after its ancestor charged into the Savage World alone and slew three Upper Five Tier great demons. Almost every leader since has slain great demons of the Unpolished Jade Tier or higher. *(Chapter 272)* Dong Sangeng is its old ancestor. When Chen Qingdu killed Dong Guanpu as a demon spy, the clan leader nearly came to blows with him.

### Qi Clan

The Qi Clan's sword technique tempers souls, and its men are famous for their looks. Its sword immortal Qi Tingji has carved a character on the wall. Qi Shou, a young sword cultivator of the clan, fought Chen Ping'an.

### Ning Clan

The Ning Clan is the clan of [Ning Yao](Ning_Yao.md). It had fallen from grace by the time Ning Yao's mother married into it, and it offered its Dragon Slaying Cliff as a marriage gift. Both of Ning Yao's parents later died in battle south of the wall.

### Yao Clan (Sword Qi Great Wall)

The Yao Clan is the clan of Ning Yao's mother. It refused to approve her marriage into the declining Ning Clan, and she married without her elders' blessing. Its clan leader is Yao Chongdao, and the old sword immortal Yao Lianyun also belongs to it.

### Nalan Clan

The Nalan Clan is one of the most powerful clans on the wall, though often forgotten because its leader Nalan Shaowei spends so long in seclusion. With the Yan Clan, it leads the wall's trade with the Majestic World.

### Yan Clan

The Yan Clan owes its power to wealth rather than sword immortals. It serves the Sword Qi Great Wall much like a Ministry of Revenue and controls nearly half of the resources moving through it. *(Chapter 572)* Its leader is Yan Ming, and its heir is Yan Zhuo.

### Guo Clan

The Guo Clan of Ritual Baton Street is led by the sword immortal Guo Jia and has about a hundred members. His daughter Guo Zhujiu is an innate sword talent and a future pillar of the clan. *(Chapter 584)* The clan stood by the Ning Residence after its fall.
