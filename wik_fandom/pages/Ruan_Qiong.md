## Appearance

Ruan Qiong is a tall, broad-shouldered man.

## Personality

Ruan Qiong is direct and unyielding. He does not grant special treatment to those he considers unworthy of it and holds firm under pressure, as shown during the Great Mo Villa incident when he refused to yield even while severely injured.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> He enforces his own rules without exception, killing cultivators who violated the no-fly rule in Dragon Spring Prefecture on his first day as overseer and continuing to do so afterward.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup><sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

He is selective about disciples and places character above talent. He turned down cultivators sent by the imperial court who he felt lacked the right qualities, and half of his unofficial disciples eventually left for Sun Scorch Mountain.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> He declined to take Chen Ping'an as a disciple not because of any personal failing on Chen Ping'an's part, but because they are not walking the same path.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

Despite his stern exterior, Ruan Qiong is capable of warmth in private. He visited Old Man Yang with a bottle of wine and found the company better than he had expected.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> He secretly travelled to Downtrodden Mountain to watch Ruan Xiu meet with Chen Ping'an, then claimed to Wei Bo that he had only been taking a stroll.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup>

His deepest ambition is to forge a truly living sword, one capable of cultivation, death, and reincarnation, something no swordsmith before him has achieved.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

## Background

Ruan Qiong was formerly affiliated with Wind Snow Temple and forged swords for its members before leaving to establish his own sect.

When Qi Jingchun died, Ruan Qiong became the overseer of Jewel Small World, a role backed by the Yingyin Chen Clan.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup> On the first day he took up the post, he killed cultivators who violated the rules he had set, making clear from the outset that no exceptions would be made.

The Great Mo Villa incident took place while he was still a 9th-tier cultivator. A terrestrial sword immortal expected Ruan Qiong to step aside and queue-jumped in front of him. Ruan Qiong refused. The sword immortal attacked, injuring Ruan Qiong severely, but Ruan Qiong did not back down. The sword immortal was ultimately detained.

He later left Jewel Small World and founded Dragon Spring Sword Sect at Divine Elegance Mountain in Dragon Spring Prefecture on the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent. His three opening disciples included Dong Gu, a former mountain creature who became the sect's first Golden Core cultivator, and Xu Xiaoqiao, known as Third Senior Sister.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> The imperial court of Great Li sent twelve unofficial disciples to train under him, though Ruan Qiong's standards meant not all remained.

He enforced a strict no-fly rule across Dragon Spring Prefecture and wounded or killed those who broke it, including Cao Jun. Dragon Spring Prefecture came to be regarded as a forbidden area, and Ruan Qiong was considered the undisputed top-tier guest elder of the Great Li Empire across two successive reigns.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup>

Among the swords he forged for Chen Ping'an was one called Subduing Demons, which Chen Ping'an used against a flood dragon.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup> Ruan Qiong has vowed to one day forge a sword unlike anything that has come before.

## Trivia

- At the time of his confrontation at Great Mo Villa, Ruan Qiong was a 9th-tier cultivator. By the time he founded Dragon Spring Sword Sect, he had reached the 11th tier.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup>
- He is widely regarded as the finest swordsmith on the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup>
- When Ruan Xiu and Chen Ping'an met at Downtrodden Mountain, Ruan Qiong watched from a distance and asked Wei Bo afterward whether Chen Ping'an was blind.
