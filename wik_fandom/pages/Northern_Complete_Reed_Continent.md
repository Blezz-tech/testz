# Northern Complete Reed Continent

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Northern_Complete_Reed_Continent)

---
**Northern Complete Reed Continent**, often called simply the **Complete Reed Continent**, is one of the nine continents of the [Majestic World](Majestic_World.md), lying north across the sea from the [Eastern Treasured Vial Continent](Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent.md). It is famous for its sword cultivators and its harsh, proud culture, and it was the main setting of [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md)'s later travels.

## Geography

The continent actually lies in the northeast of the Majestic World. It still calls itself the northern continent, which leaves Pure White Continent, the true northern continent, without the title. *(Chapter 210)* A stretch of sea separates it from the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent. Its southern tip and the north of the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md) are the closest points between the two. *(Chapter 513)*

Skeleton Shoal in the far south is one of the continent's ten major ancient battlefields. Its ferry station is the main hub of the southern region, and the [Flax Robe Sect](Sects.md#flax-robe-sect) stands above it. *(Chapter 485)* Nearby lies Malicious Ghost Valley, a small world held in a vortex of time and ruled by ghosts. *(Chapter 489)* The Great Origin Empire is the largest empire in the central region. *(Chapter 494)* A great river runs through the centre of the continent from east to west, and the [Water Dragon Sect](Sects.md#water-dragon-sect) is built upon it. *(Chapter 542)* Other powers include the Great Seal Script Empire and the Tail Feather Empire, one of the largest on the continent. *(Chapter 283)* [Ceremony Mountain](Sects.md#ceremony-mountain) lies in the southwest. *(Chapter 207)*

## History

Sword immortals of the continent once led the charge to [Stalactite Mountain](Stalactite_Mountain.md), among them the patriarch of the Hulü Clan. *(Chapter 213)* For a long time the continent had no full Daoist Heavenly Lord. [Xie Shi](Xie_Shi.md), its Dao Master, was on the verge of becoming the first. *(Chapter 191)* [He Xiaoliang](He_Xiaoliang.md) of the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent later founded the [Refreshing Sect](Sects.md#refreshing-sect) there, becoming the youngest sect leader in the continent's history. *(Chapter 487)*

As the war at the [Sword Qi Great Wall](Sword_Qi_Great_Wall.md) worsened, an army of the continent's swordsmen flew south to reinforce it. By [Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md)'s count, half of its top sword cultivators had been drawn away. *(Chapter 479)* Another two hundred sword cultivators went to Pure White Continent, and the leaders of sects such as the [Great Emblem Sword Sect](Sects.md#great-emblem-sword-sect) left for the Great Wall. *(Chapter 526)*

## Cultivation world

Sword cultivators make up the largest share of the continent's cultivators. Strength is valued above all. A single look can start a fight, and many people travel under false names. *(Chapter 367)* The imperial courts favour martial arts over scholarship, and the sages who guard its Confucian academies are unusually strong fighters. Once a sword cultivator reaches the Unpolished Jade Tier, other sword immortals almost always challenge them. *(Chapter 508)* The strongest sword immortal of the north is Bai Shang of the [Jade Forest Sect](Sects.md#jade-forest-sect).

Buddhism has more followers than Daoism on the continent. The Lofty Cloud Palace of the Great Origin Empire acts much like the head of the Daoist orthodoxy there. *(Chapter 496)* Its young talents are ranked as the ten dragons and phoenixes, and its strongest as the top ten cultivators of the mountains. Its cultivators, and especially its sword cultivators, are known for looking down on everyone else. *(Chapter 506)* They held the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent in particular contempt.

## Trivia

- The novel gives two counts of its Confucian academies. One passage says six, another says three.
- The Flax Robe Sect of Skeleton Shoal alone has three Unpolished Jade Tier ancestors, a sign of how deep the continent's foundations run.
- Chen Ping An chose to travel there because it was the place most like the Sword Qi Great Wall. *(Chapter 280)*
