# Middle Earth Divine Continent

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Middle_Earth_Divine_Continent)

---
**Middle Earth Divine Continent** is the largest of the nine continents of the [Majestic World](Majestic_World.md). It sits at the centre of the world and is home to the Confucian temple, [Mount Longhu](Sects.md#mount-longhu) and [White Emperor City](Sects.md#white-emperor-city). Cultivators from the other eight continents often regard it as the place where the strongest figures and the oldest powers are found.

## Geography

Middle Earth is so vast that only the other eight continents put together can match its size. *(Chapter 565)* It has the richest resources of any continent, and is described as being worth eight continents on its own. *(Chapter 558)* Several major empires stand on its land, and the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md) would need to swallow half of the [Eastern Treasured Vial Continent](Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent.md) before it could compare with any of them. The ruins of a great nation that was once one of the nine major empires of the world also lie there. *(Chapter 258)*

A sea separates Middle Earth from Eastern Treasured Vial. Tassel Mountain stands near the eastern coast, and a deity in golden armour sits upon it. *(Chapter 208)* This deity is one of the gods of the continent's Five Mountains and is said to have the worst temper on the whole continent. *(Chapter 351)*

The continent's most famous waterway is a great river, and White Emperor City is built on its banks. *(Chapter 455)* Mirage Lake is another well known body of water. It is governed by a lake lord of the Upper Five Tiers whose water residence is called Mianchi Palace. *(Chapter 552)*

## History

After the ancient war that ended the age of deities, the Majestic World built the Confucian temple in Middle Earth and raised nine Mighty Suppressing Pagodas that tower between heaven and earth. *(Chapter 588)* Since then the temple has been the seat of Confucian authority across all nine continents. Its seventy two sages are honoured there, and the [Scholarly Sage](Scholarly_Sage.md) was once given the fourth seat among them. *(Chapter 346)* The Confucian school on Middle Earth also holds the power to approve the ranks of mountain and water deities. *(Chapter 374)*

Many figures who shaped events elsewhere began their lives here. [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md) left Middle Earth to found [Mountain Cliff Academy](Mountain_Cliff_Academy.md) in Great Li. *(Chapter 402)* [Zuo You](Zuo_You.md) is also from Middle Earth, and he is remembered there for crippling many young sword prodigies of the continent. *(Chapter 591)*

## Cultivation world

Middle Earth has more academies than any other continent and is home to many large Daoist and Confucian powers. *(Chapter 209)* [Mount Longhu](Sects.md#mount-longhu) is home to the Heavenly Master Residence of the Zhang Clan, whose Daoist line stands apart from the branches of the Heavenly World. White Emperor City is ruled by a city lord who is both a feared demonic cultivator and a Go master so strong that no tenth dan is ever awarded in his lifetime. *(Chapter 229)*

The list of the top ten cultivators of each continent is compiled by Middle Earth, and its own list is treated as the most prestigious. *(Chapter 270)* The [Northern Complete Reed Continent](Northern_Complete_Reed_Continent.md) calls itself the strongest continent outside Middle Earth, which shows how far above the others Middle Earth is held to be. *(Chapter 525)* In martial arts, [Cao Ci](Cao_Ci.md) is regarded as peerless among the young martial artists of the continent. The Shaoyuan Empire is known for its many sword cultivators, much like the Luminous Vermilion Empire of Eastern Treasured Vial.

## Trivia

- Cultivators from Middle Earth speak an official dialect of their own, and many look down on cultivators from smaller continents such as Eastern Treasured Vial. *(Chapter 571)*
- [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md) asked Spiritual Master Fire Dragon to sell most of his colored glaze roof tiles to the Colored Glaze Pagoda of White Emperor City.
- [Cui Dongshan](Cui_Dongshan.md) says that it is remarkable if even one or two people from the other eight continents can squeeze into Middle Earth's top ranks.
