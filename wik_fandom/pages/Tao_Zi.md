**Tao Zi** is the granddaughter of Tao Kui, the Pure Yang Sword Patriarch of Sun Scorch Mountain, and is regarded as the sect's most gifted young cultivator in five centuries. She travels to [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md) under the protection of [Bai Yuan](Mountain-Moving_Ape.md), who serves as her guardian throughout the visit.

Her name is a homonym for the word meaning "peach", which is how she came to be affectionately nicknamed Peach.

## Appearance

Tao Zi is a young girl whose skin is so fair and delicate that she is repeatedly compared to fine porcelain, with faint veins visible beneath the surface in strong light. *(Chapter 7)*

## Personality

Tao Zi is lively and warm, quick to make friends and genuinely curious about the world. She struck up a fast friendship with Song Jixin and kept up an easy, natural conversation with him that surprised both of them with how long it lasted. *(Chapter 57)*

She is not soft, however. When Chen Ping'an's slingshot shattered the food containers in the birdcage and her taller maidservant panicked and ran toward the wall to look, Tao Zi grabbed the maidservant's wrist, stopped her, and then slapped her across the face to snap her out of the panic and order her to raise the alarm. *(Chapter 48)* She read the situation quickly, noted that the shots had not been aimed at her, and warned Bai Yuan not to drop his guard regardless.

She was raised strictly and was not a child who made trouble for her seniors, even when left alone and bored. *(Chapter 45)*

## Background

Tao Zi's physical constitution, cultivation aptitude, personality, and natural gifts were said to rank near the top of every Sun Scorch Mountain sword immortal born in the past five hundred years. The sect viewed her as a prodigy with enormous upside and no apparent weaknesses, and invested heavily in her development. *(Chapter 45)*

She grew up hearing about her family's ancestral humiliation at the hands of Wind Lightning Field and knew the history of the ancestor whose body still sits on Wind Lightning Field's sparring arena with a sword through her skull. The adults in her family always told the story in as neutral a tone as they could manage, but Bai Yuan never did. *(Chapter 45)*

Her family owns three Sword Nurturing Gourds. Her grandfather's is gray. Another elder's is small and releases dozens of miniature flying swords. A third, belonging to a senior called Big Sister Su, is golden-purple in color and rarely taken out. *(Chapter 57)*

## Plot

Tao Zi arrived in [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md) with Bai Yuan, who had come to retrieve a sword scripture. She was placed in the care of the Li Clan during his activities in the town and stayed within their manor as instructed, though she found the people there thoroughly incompetent. *(Chapter 48)*

When Chen Ping'an used a slingshot to smash the food containers in a birdcage inside the courtyard, her maidservants were thrown into confusion. Tao Zi kept her head, assessed that the boy had not been shooting at her, and promptly slapped the taller maidservant back to her senses before ordering the alarm raised. She then warned Bai Yuan, when he returned, not to assume everything was fine. *(Chapter 48)*

Song Jixin arranged to have her brought out of the Li Clan's residence so they could spend time at the kiln supervision office. He gave her an emerald-green Sword Nurturing Gourd as a gift. She called him "firewood collecting brother" because of the characters in his name, and he did not mind. *(Chapter 57)*

She was disappointed that Bai Yuan was unable to carry a mountain out of the small world for her. He had done this once before, bringing a mountain from a distant place all the way to Sun Scorch Mountain as a private estate for another person she admired, and Tao Zi had always wanted one of her own. *(Chapter 48)*

## Trivia

- She had to beg her senior Big Sister Su many times before being allowed to touch her golden-purple Sword Nurturing Gourd, and it was put away again quickly afterward. *(Chapter 57)*
- Even though she was very young, she had already heard enough about the dangers of cultivation to understand the stakes involved in what Bai Yuan was doing in the town. *(Chapter 45)*
