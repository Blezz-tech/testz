# Demon Tribe

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Demon_Tribe)

---
The demon tribe is a significant force in the world described in the sources, and they are often in conflict with humans and other groups.

**General Characteristics**

- The demon tribe is not monolithic; it encompasses a wide variety of beings with different abilities, goals and origins.
- Demons are often referred to as "mountain-dwelling cultivators" and are considered to be beings who have attained some level of the Great Dao.
- Many demons adopt human forms, with some taking pride in doing so. This is often a step in their cultivation journey as the human body is considered a treasure.
- Demons who have adopted human forms are considered half-cultivators and are generally not oppressed by the Great Li Empire, instead being allowed to establish their own sects.
- Some demons are said to be innately powerful, with superior physiques compared to humans.
- Demons can be spirits, ghosts, and other kinds of fiends.

**Motivations and Goals**

- A primary motivation for the demon tribe is the **acquisition of resources** and **better living conditions**. Their own world, the Savage World, is described as harsh with thin spiritual energy, making the Majestic World, where humans reside, a desirable target.
- The demon tribe is driven by a desire for **land, cultivation resources, and descendants**, leading to conflicts with humans.
- Some demons seek to **cultivate the Great Dao**, and see adopting human form as a step toward achieving that goal.
- Some demons **collect human souls** for their own cultivation.
- Demons can **refine multiple golden cores** using their original demon core as a gateway.
- Some demons aim to **achieve immortality** and godhood.

**Society and Culture**

- The demon tribe **respects strength and power**, regardless of personal feelings.
- They can **be incredibly cunning** and adept at using illusions.
- There are **benevolent demons**, although they seem to be exceptions.
- The demon tribe can display **unexpected levels of respect** for those they consider worthy.
- Some demons **value reputation** and will avoid actions that could lead to public scorn.
- Demons may have complex social structures, including hierarchical systems and leadership.

**Conflict with Humans**

- The demon tribe is often in conflict with humans, particularly in places like the Sword Qi Great Wall, which they attack relentlessly.
- They are known for launching frequent assaults, sometimes involving large numbers of casualties.
- The demons will go to great lengths to **eliminate human prodigies**, as they perceive them as a threat.
- There is a constant struggle for territory and resources, with the demon tribe viewing the human world as a source of wealth and cultivation materials.
- Humans have developed various methods to defend themselves against demons, such as the use of talismans, formations, and powerful weapons.
- Some demons and humans form alliances, sometimes for mutual benefit or due to specific circumstances.
- **Swordsmen are often at the forefront of the fight** against the demon tribe, developing their skills in the face of great danger.

**Types of Demons**

- **Great Demons:** Powerful demons that have reached high levels of cultivation. Some are noted as being equal in power to immortals and some even appear more divine.
- **Demonic Cultivators:** Demons who cultivate the Great Dao through demonic methods, often associated with the collection of souls.
- **Yin Entities**: These are ghosts and spirits that exist in the world, and they can sometimes be controlled by demonic cultivators.
- **Mountain and Water Spirits**: Demons that inhabit specific areas, and can include those related to flood dragons.
- **Fox Demons:** Known to be mischievous, with some also having literary talent, and some seeking to marry humans for cultivation benefits.
- **Slug Demons**: These demons can consume other spirits, and absorb the cultivation of those they eat. They are also adept at hiding and fleeing, and known to be incredibly difficult to kill.

**Key Locations**

- **Savage World**: The demon tribe's home world, described as a harsh environment with little spiritual energy.
- **Sword Qi Great Wall**: A major site of conflict between humans and demons, where many swordsmen are summoned to defend against demon invasions.
- **Stalactite Mountain**: A location connected to the Sword Qi Great Wall, where swordsmen pass through to reach the front lines.
- **Burial River**: A location with a high concentration of water demons, as well as wronged souls and malicious ghosts.

**Additional points of interest**

- There are some demons, like the little boy in azure, that begin their journey towards the Great Dao by obtaining a human form.
- The demon tribe keeps records of human prodigies using secret techniques.
- Some demons may choose to live among humans, disguising their true nature.

The demon tribe is a diverse and complex group with a range of motivations, from the basic desire for survival to the higher pursuit of the Great Dao. Their interactions with humans are often fraught with conflict, but there are also instances of unexpected alliances and respect.
