**He Xiaoliang** is a Daoist cultivator from the [Eastern Treasured Vial Continent](Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent.md) whose fortune is spoken of across two continents. She was once the Jade Girl of Divine Edict Sect, the most envied young talent among that continent's Daoist orthodoxy, but she cut ties with the sect and the master who raised her to become a direct disciple of the Daoist Branch Master [Lu Chen](Lu_Chen.md) instead, later founding the Refreshing Sect on [Northern Complete Reed Continent](Northern_Complete_Reed_Continent.md) and advancing to the Unpolished Jade Tier.

Lu Chen bound her fortune to [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md) as part of one of his schemes, tying the two of them together through an uneven, uncertain connection. Chen Ping'an would rather never see her again, while she has come to regard him as both a danger to her Great Dao and the person she is most likely to end up spending her life with.

## Appearance

He Xiaoliang is widely considered one of the two most beautiful women on Eastern Treasured Vial Continent, rivaled only by Su Jia of [Sun Scorch Mountain](Sects.md#sun-scorch-mountain), and cultivators across the continent argue endlessly over which of them truly deserves the title. Some say she lacks Su Jia's warmth and comes across as cold and distant instead. During her time in the small town she kept an unusual white deer at her side, one able to transform into a horse. Its white coat was later revealed to be an illusion cast by Qi Zhen, hiding what was actually a Five Colored Deer. *(Chapter 405)*

## Personality

He Xiaoliang is composed, distant, and given over almost entirely to the pursuit of the Great Dao, treating most else as unimportant by comparison. Her Dao Heart rarely wavers, and she tends to judge things in blunt, uncompromising terms. She speaks her mind freely, even to her own master, and she can turn ruthless when provoked, as when she kills two of Xu Xuan's maidservants and strips him of his weapons rather than tolerate his pursuit of her. She has said more than once that she understood her own nature from a young age and never had to search for it the way others do.

The one thing that reliably unsettles her is Chen Ping'an. Her calm slips when she learns their fortunes have been tied together, and she eventually admits that she cannot stop thinking about him.

## Background

He Xiaoliang entered [Divine Edict Sect](Sects.md#divine-edict-sect) as an unremarkable initiate with no notable background. Enlightened Saint Profound Talisman, senior brother to sect master Qi Zhen, was the only one who saw promise in her, and he took her on as his sole disciple. Under him she rose at a pace that soon outstripped every expectation, until she was named Jade Girl alongside Qi Zhen's own prized disciple as the new Golden Boy and Jade Girl of [Eastern Treasured Vial Continent](Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent.md), a pairing the sect hoped would end in marriage between the two.

[Wei Jin](Wei_Jin.md) of Wind Snow Temple loved her for years and was turned down every time. When he finally asked whether she would still go through with becoming Dao partners with someone chosen for her even if she never loved him, she told him she would, and that she would not come to regret it even if it left her unhappy. He left in tears. *(Chapter 176)*

As she grew older, her master's regard for her became far less innocent. He began pressing her to become his Dao partner in truth, offering to abandon the sect for her, or failing that, to carry the arrangement on in secret behind the appearance of master and disciple. When she refused, he turned to intimidation and outright coercion to force her hand. She held him off without ever openly retaliating, privately pitying him for choosing a path she viewed as too narrow to ever lead anywhere worth reaching. A fellow senior within the sect, unwilling to see her treated this way, confronted her master over the matter directly, and when Wei Jin passed through the region not long after, her master mistook him for an ally she had summoned, though she turned Wei Jin down just as she always had. In the end she chose to cut ties with Divine Edict Sect entirely rather than depend on anyone else's protection. Qi Zhen declined to brand her a traitor for leaving despite the sect elders' fury, and personally barred her master by force when he tried to chase after her. *(Chapter 211)*

## Plot

### Jewel Small World

He Xiaoliang visits the small town together with her junior brother, the Golden Boy, on a mission for their sect. One night by the stone cliff above the Dragon Whisker River, her white deer approaches Chen Ping'an, and she has a little girl translate for her, telling him that he values his own fortune, but that the two of them share too shallow a fate to ever become fellow Daoists. *(Chapter 40)* Chen Ping'an gives her a snake gall pebble before she leaves.

### Becoming Lu Chen's Disciple

Having already left her old sect, He Xiaoliang travels onward aboard a Kun ship, where she returns Chen Ping'an's old snake gall pebble for a finer one, only for Lu Chen to pluck her from the ship without warning. He had secretly tested her by nudging her into believing that she and Chen Ping'an were fated to be Dao partners, and only once she works out for herself that the two of them are in truth only loosely connected by fate does she pass his test. Lu Chen accepts her as his sixth direct disciple and permits her to found a sect of her own on Northern Complete Reed Continent. 

### Founding the Refreshing Sect

He Xiaoliang's swift rise on Northern Complete Reed Continent and the sect she founds there, the Refreshing Sect, draw attention throughout the continent. Watching from the White Jade Capital, Lu Chen notes the threads of fate still linking her to Chen Ping'an. *(Chapter 494)* She later takes in the Deer Rider Divine Maiden, one of the female heavenly officials who stepped out of her own mural at Mural City, as a disciple. *(Chapter 488)*

At Skeleton Wall City in Malicious Ghost Valley, she and the city lord Gao Cheng quietly plot against Chen Ping'an. Jiang Shangzhen tries to provoke a reaction out of her, but she stays silent throughout, offering Gao Cheng only a way to build his own cycle of reincarnation before leaving without warning. *(Chapter 497)* She later tells her disciple that a Dao partner shares in one's fortune as much as one's misfortune, and that her presence in a city so opposed to her own nature was meant to grind down the fortune she had built up through her bond with Chen Ping'an. Chen Ping'an later blames this uncertain tie to her for much of the danger he faces on the continent.

### Conflict with Xu Xuan

Xu Xuan of the Jade Forest Sect pursues He Xiaoliang, hoping to become her Dao partner, and is willing to abandon his own sect for her. She rejects him, and when he pushes the matter further the two of them fight in open country, reshaping the land for kilometers around. She kills two of his maidservants and takes his saber and sword, leaving them outside the Refreshing Sect's gate for him to reclaim if he dares. The fight sets his cultivation back by years and leaves the Refreshing Sect isolated from its former allies, though He Xiaoliang remains unmoved by the fallout. *(Chapter 555)*

### Meeting on the Coast

After reaching the Unpolished Jade Tier, He Xiaoliang nearly slips back down a tier for no clear reason. She seeks out Chen Ping'an on the west coast of Northern Complete Reed Continent, claiming she was simply passing by. *(Chapter 553)* Walking with him through an alley, she asks how he saw through her scheme with Gao Cheng, remarks on how much he has changed since the small town, and suggests that perhaps he has done nothing wrong at all, and is himself the mistake. When he asks whether they might each walk their own path from here on, she tells him to take the question up with her master instead.

Chen Ping'an tells her that the Dao she chases holds the same place in her heart that [Ning Yao](Ning_Yao.md) holds in his. She understands him completely, but says she still cannot bring herself to accept it. After a long silence, she tells him that only now has she come to feel that becoming Dao partners with him would not be an obstacle at all, but the best match she could hope for. She says she will wait for him at the summit of the Majestic World, then leaves, remarking as she goes that romance between men and women is, in the end, a small thing. *(Chapter 554)*

- **Fortune:** spoken of across two continents as one of the most fortunate cultivators alive, ranked just behind Huang Ting. *(Chapter 631)*
- **Cultivation:** Unpolished Jade Tier. She expects reaching the Immortal Tier will take her centuries.
- **Treasures:** a large collection of pocket and minimization treasures, among them a fallen nation's jade seal, an antique inkstone, a jade book, and a lotus leaf.
- **Combat:** strong enough to defeat Xu Xuan, whose advancement to the Upper Five Tiers was close at hand.

## Trivia

- Wealthy cultivators commission face masks made to resemble her, though shops are careful never to make them too accurate. *(Chapter 230)*
- It is later remarked that Qi Zhen of Divine Edict Sect should have gone to any length, even crippling and imprisoning her, to keep a disciple as fortunate as her within the sect. *(Chapter 559)*
- When Chen Ping'an eventually tells Ning Yao about his history with He Xiaoliang, Ning Yao takes it in stride, remarking only that He Xiaoliang had gone a little too far, and saying she would like to meet her someday. *(Chapter 581)*
