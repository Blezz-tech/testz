# Lotus Flower Blessed Land

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Lotus_Flower_Blessed_Land)

---
The Lotus Flower Blessed Land is a dynamic and multifaceted setting with its own intricate system of governance, culture, and power dynamics. Its connections to other worlds and the presence of otherworldly immortals make it a significant location in the narrative. The challenges, opportunities, and unique characteristics of the Lotus Flower Blessed Land make it a compelling setting for conflict, growth, and exploration.

**General Characteristics:**

- The Lotus Flower Blessed Land is described as a **medium-grade blessed land**. This is according to otherworldly immortals, who consider factors like the abundance of spiritual energy and the population of the world.
- It is considered small by otherworldly immortals.
- It is one of the **72 blessed lands**.
- The blessed land is linked to the **Lotus Flower World**, a small world where the Dao Ancestor is said to relax. This connection is unique, with only one other such place existing in the four worlds.
- The Lotus Flower Blessed Land is not particularly famous compared to the Cloud Cave Blessed Land.
- The land is described as a **labyrinth** in a small area, making it difficult for those with cultivation to move around and act as they normally would.
- The rules of the Heavenly Dao are difficult to manifest within the Lotus Flower Blessed Land.
- The flow of time within the Lotus Flower Blessed Land can vary compared to the Majestic World depending on the mood of a certain person.
- The land is known for producing martial arts prodigies and is used by otherworldly immortals to temper their minds.
- Entering the Lotus Flower Blessed Land is said to be more difficult than entering other blessed lands. It depends on the mood of a certain individual or their first impression.
- It is possible to ascend from this blessed land.

**Political and Social Landscape:**

- The Lotus Flower Blessed Land contains several nations, including the **Northern Jin Nation** and the **Southern Garden Nation**.
- These nations have their own political structures, military forces, and cultural practices [See the previous overviews of these nations].
- There is a complex interplay of power between the different nations.
- The land is influenced by the presence of otherworldly immortals, who often arrive to temper their minds.
- There are various sects and factions with their own goals and ambitions.
- The land's elites are known throughout the Lotus Flower Blessed Land [See the previous overviews of the Northern Jin Nation and Southern Garden Nation].

**Key Figures and Groups:**

- A mysterious person, represented by a **young child holding a plantain fan**, is responsible for overseeing the Lotus Flower Blessed Land and interacts with those who have the right to know about the blessed land.
- **Ding Ying** is a prominent figure in the Lotus Flower Blessed Land, knowledgeable about otherworldly immortals and has fought them. He is considered a peerless martial artist. He was once considered to be the strongest martial artist in the world.
- **Zhou Fei** is an otherworldly immortal who has retained his true body and soul within the Lotus Flower Blessed Land. He is the leader of the Jiang Clan from the Jade Tablet Sect and controls the Cloud Cave Blessed Land.
- **Yu Zhenyi** is a leader of the Lake Mountain Faction and is a significant figure within the Lotus Flower Blessed Land.
- **Zhong Qiu** is a powerful figure, serving as the imperial preceptor of the Southern Garden Nation.
- **Lu Tai** is an otherworldly immortal who became one of the Upper Ten Elites within the Lotus Flower Blessed Land.
- **Wei Xian** is the founding emperor of the Southern Garden Nation.
- **Sui Youbian** is a female sword immortal who is considered to be very fortunate.
- There are various other powerful martial artists and cultivators, as well as many ordinary people and scholars.

**Interactions with Other Worlds:**

- The Lotus Flower Blessed Land is linked to the **Lotus Flower World**, a small world associated with the Dao Ancestor.
- Otherworldly immortals come to the Lotus Flower Blessed Land to temper their minds and navigate challenges.
- People from the Majestic World view the Lotus Flower Blessed Land as small and limiting.
- There is an awareness of other blessed lands, such as the Cloud Cave Blessed Land and the Limpid Pond Blessed Land.
- The Great Li Empire and other forces from the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent are known.
- The Lotus Flower Blessed Land is known in the Majestic World.
- There is interaction between the Lotus Flower Blessed Land and the Middle Earth Divine Continent.

**Unique Aspects and Challenges:**

- The Lotus Flower Blessed Land is a complex area with many powerful figures.
- The rules of cultivation, and the understanding of the world, are different than what is found in other places, like the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent.
- The land has a unique link to the Lotus Flower World.
- There are conflicts and power struggles between various factions and individuals.
- The Lotus Flower Blessed Land is influenced by the mood of a certain person, which can impact the flow of time and the events that occur within it.
- The presence of otherworldly immortals creates unique challenges and interactions within the blessed land.
- There are a number of fated opportunities in the Lotus Flower Blessed Land, but they are difficult to obtain.
