**Cao Qinglang** is a young scholar from the Lotus Flower Blessed Land, a medium grade blessed land on Parasol Leaf Continent. His entire family was murdered when he was still a small child, and Chen Ping'an, newly arrived in that world at the time, ended up living beside him and raising him, and later took him on as a disciple.

## Appearance

He is once described as a young boy dressed in the plain azure robes typical of a private school pupil.

## Personality

As a child Cao Qinglang is consumed by grief and a hunger for revenge he has no way to act on, and for a long time afterward he withdraws into himself, going quiet and numb around people who try to reach him. He teaches himself to cook and keep house rather than lean on anyone, and only slowly warms up again, largely thanks to Chen Ping'an and to Pei Qian, who ends up living in his courtyard for a stretch as a child herself. The two of them bicker constantly and he loses every argument, but he does not hold grudges, and he is generous even when he is the one who comes up short. Grown, he reassures Pei Qian rather than resenting her for once wanting him gone, and shows no interest in competing with her or anyone else for Chen Ping'an's attention. He reads far more than he trains, and he is content to fall behind in cultivation while he does it.

## Background

Cao Qinglang's parents and grandparents are killed in his own home by Zhou Shi and Ya'er, acting on the orders of the demonic Paramount Leader Ding Ying, who mistakenly believes that the boy's body is hiding the soul of the otherworldly immortal Tong Qingqing.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> Officials take the grieving boy to identify the bodies before he is left to fend for himself in the family home.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

Chen Ping'an, who has recently arrived in the Lotus Flower Blessed Land, moves into the alley outside his courtyard and gradually starts looking out for him, and the two of them end up sharing meals in near silence most nights. Cao Qinglang is soon joined in the courtyard by a ragged, sharp-tongued little girl, later revealed to be Pei Qian, and the three of them live together for a time.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> Chen Ping'an goes on to defeat Imperial Preceptor Zhong Qiu in a sparring match and kills Ding Ying himself in a brutal duel at Bull Mountain, ending the threat that orphaned the boy.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> He later tracks down and deals with Zhou Shi and Ya'er specifically because of what they did to Cao Qinglang's family, something he considers unforgivable regardless of the wider politics involved.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup><sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

Cao Qinglang is largely raised and schooled by [Zhong Qiu](Zhong_Qiu.md), who comes to think of him as something between a student and a disciple, and he is later also taught by Lu Tai, the descendant of the Daoist Lu Chen, who at one point entrusts him with the valuable Five Mountains True Appearance Picture Scroll.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> When Chen Ping'an eventually leaves the Lotus Flower Blessed Land, he takes Pei Qian with him as his traveling disciple but leaves Cao Qinglang behind, worried for the boy's safety on the road. Chen Ping'an regrets this for years afterward and never stops thinking of him, and when Pei Qian finally visits again she confirms that Cao Qinglang is recognized as their master's disciple too, not merely a boy he once took pity on.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> He is formally listed as a student of Chen Ping'an's household at the founding of Downtrodden Mountain's ancestral hall.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

By adulthood Cao Qinglang has reached the Abode Tier, a step behind Pei Qian's Sea Observation Tier, something she needles him about without much effect. He spends most of his time reading rather than training, and does not seem troubled by lagging behind his fellow disciples.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup>

## Trivia

- The Lotus Flower Blessed Land is later split into four pieces, one of which is absorbed into Downtrodden Mountain and renamed the Lotus Root Blessed Land.
- Zhong Qiu tells him he hopes he will one day speak up for the world rather than let it be reduced to a Go board where people are treated as disposable pieces.
