## Appearance

Du Mao has a short, diminutive frame, to the point that he had to look up slightly even at a younger, towering pure martial artist standing only a couple of steps away from him.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> He has a habitual gesture of raising a thumb and wiping it across his own lips whenever he is about to go all out in a fight, a tell recognized by anyone who knew him well.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

## Personality

He is arrogant and openly contemptuous of anyone weaker than himself, dismissing a cultivator still below the Upper Five Tiers as worthless trash regardless of potential.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> He subscribes wholeheartedly to the idea that power is above all else, and he is willing to try to reason his way out of a losing fight the moment the odds turn against him, showing none of the pride he projects when he has the upper hand.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> He is also deeply selfish, ultimately willing to sacrifice an entire small world purely to save himself when cornered, a final act that left even his own sect disillusioned with him.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

## Background

Du Mao was the strongest cultivator of the Parasol Leaf Sect and, by reputation, the most powerful cultivator on Parasol Leaf Continent aside from the old Daoist priest of the East Sea. After the sect’s only other Ascension Tier cultivator died, leaving it in a difficult transitional period, Du Mao exhausted the sect’s wealth refining a bonded celestial weapon called the Sword Devouring Ark, forged over six centuries from the complete carcass of a giant ancient Treasure Swallowing Whale, before retreating into seclusion for centuries more. The first thing he did upon emerging was travel to the Jade Tablet Sect atop his ark to challenge an Unpolished Jade Tier sword immortal to a battle to the death, killing him and devouring his bonded flying sword with the ark in the process.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>

Years later, Du Mao traveled to Old Dragon City on the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent with three goals in mind: to see the swordsman Zuo You’s skill for himself, to test how much Divine Lord Yang of Jewel Small World would tolerate, and to quietly further the Parasol Leaf Sect’s reach into the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent. He also hoped to use the opportunity to sever the Scholarly Sage’s scholarly lineage if the chance arose. To lure Zuo You out, he brutally beat down the pure martial artist Zheng Dafeng, crippling him, and then cornered Chen Ping’an, mocking his weak cultivation and threatening to kill him and take his treasures once Zuo You failed to appear.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>

Zuo You never came, but a white-robed sword spirit intervened in his place. Du Mao failed to glean anything about her strength through divination, since the area had already been sealed off, and he underestimated her badly, ordering his Sword Devouring Ark to strike her. The weapon instead trembled in open fear and refused to obey him, and when he eventually tried to force it to attack anyway, the sword spirit swatted it down and later destroyed it entirely after amusing herself with its vaunted reputation. Realizing he could not win, Du Mao abandoned his pride and tried to negotiate, but the sword spirit toyed with him regardless, sealing him inside a scenic picture scroll to fight his yang soul avatar to destruction while his true body’s yin soul fled back to Parasol Leaf Continent. He escaped with his life, but at the cost of the Sword Devouring Ark and any hope of intimidating Chen Ping’an or the Scholarly Sage again.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

Some time later, Du Mao attempted to force his ascension from the world. Zuo You intercepted him and killed him in a decisive sword strike, ending his ascension attempt for good. In his final moments, Du Mao chose to destroy an entire small world in a desperate bid to survive, a selfish act that disgusted the Parasol Leaf Sect’s own disciples and elders and left the sect leader openly disappointed in him, even though the sect leader admitted he likely would have done the same in Du Mao’s place. Du Mao’s remains scattered as colored glaze golden body fragments, some of which fell to the Parasol Leaf Sect while others were lost elsewhere; Chen Ping’an came into possession of at least one such fragment and later gave it away as a gift.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

Without Du Mao’s overwhelming strength to lean on, the once-feared Parasol Leaf Sect fell into sharp decline, having grown too dependent on his domineering reputation and too accustomed to using his name as an intimidation tactic against outsiders.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

## Trivia

- His immortal body was later inhabited by a female bone ghost named [Shi Rou](Shi_Rou.md), who went on to serve as one of Chen Ping’an’s stewards alongside the Far Roaming Tier martial artist Zhu Lian.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>
- He once offered to make Jiang Shangzhen, then still only at the Golden Core Tier, the next sect leader of the Parasol Leaf Sect if he advanced to the Upper Five Tiers. Jiang Shangzhen turned him down and left for the Northern Complete Reed Continent instead.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>
- The injury he inflicted on Chen Ping’an with the Sword Devouring Ark left behind energy that continued to eat away at Chen Ping’an’s soul long after the wound itself had healed, proving far more troublesome than an ordinary physical injury.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>
