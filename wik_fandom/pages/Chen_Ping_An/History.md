## Volume 1: Caged Sparrow (Chapters 1 to 84)

After the kilns closed, Chen Ping'an earned coins delivering letters at the town's eastern gate. He took in an injured young swordswoman, [Ning Yao](../Ning_Yao.md), and the two became close. When Chen Ping'an's life was in danger, [Qi Jingchun](../Qi_Jingchun.md) asked the town's old locust tree for a blessed leaf on his behalf. A single leaf fell bearing the character "Yao," and Chen Ping'an vowed to save any member of the Yao Clan connected to his old master, even at the cost of his life.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>

When outsiders came to the town for its treasures, he killed [Cai Jinjian](../Cai_Jinjian.md) in an alley with a porcelain shard after she shattered his bridge of immortality. After the [Mountain-Moving Ape](../Mountain-Moving_Ape.md) of Sun Scorch Mountain left [Liu Xianyang](../Liu_Xianyang.md) near death, Chen Ping'an used his locust leaf to keep his friend alive and then took on the ape himself. Qi Jingchun arranged for him to receive the Sword Nurturing Gourd, holding the flying swords First and Fifteenth. With the golden copper coins he received, he bought five mountains around the town, [Downtrodden Mountain](../Downtrodden_Mountain.md) among them, and lent three of them out.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> 

## Volume 2: Traveling Boy (Chapters 85 to 178)

At Qi Jingchun's request, Chen Ping'an escorted [Li Baoping](../Li_Baoping.md), [Li Huai](../Li_Huai.md), [Lin Shouyi](../Lin_Shouyi.md) and other children from the town to [Mountain Cliff Academy](../Mountain_Cliff_Academy.md) in the Great Sui Nation. He wove a bamboo bookcase for the road and travelled with [A'Liang](../A'Liang.md), who taught him the Eighteen Stops, and with [Zhu He](../Zhu_He.md) and his daughter [Zhu Lu](../Zhu_Lu.md).

On the way he met the old scholar, the [Scholarly Sage](../Scholarly_Sage.md). Over wine one night, the old scholar asked whether he was willing to follow Qi Jingchun's arrangement and become his disciple.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> The young Cui Chan, later known as [Cui Dongshan](../Cui_Dongshan.md), joined the group. Chen Ping'an agreed to accept him as a student on one condition, that he sincerely protect the children.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> After delivering the children safely, Chen Ping'an set off home alone.

## Volume 3: Golden Saber Coin (Chapters 179 to 238)

Back home, Chen Ping'an trained under a barefoot old man in the bamboo building on Downtrodden Mountain. The old man was [Cui Cheng](../Cui_Cheng.md), Cui Dongshan's grandfather, and he beat Chen Ping'an through countless sessions until he became a third tier martial artist.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup> Chen Ping'an traded his jade hairpin for the flying sword Fifteenth, then set out south with his swords to find Ning Yao. On the road he travelled with the young Daoist [Zhang Shanfeng](../Zhang_Shanfeng.md) and the martial artist [Xu Yuanxia](../Xu_Yuanxia.md), and he helped the ghost scholar Yang Huang and settled the trouble in the Colorful Garment Nation's prefectural city. He then prepares to cross the sea to [Stalactite Mountain](../Stalactite_Mountain.md) to deliver Ning Yao's sword.

## Volume 4: Approaching Sword Qi (Chapters 239 to 295)

Chen Ping'an passed through the Water Combing Nation and met the old sword saint Song Yushao. At [Old Dragon City](../Old_Dragon_City.md) he befriended the Fan Clan and saw [Zheng Dafeng](../Zheng_Dafeng.md) again, and by then he had reached the fourth tier. He sailed from Osmanthus Island to Stalactite Mountain and finally reached the [Sword Qi Great Wall](../Sword_Qi_Great_Wall.md), where he was reunited with and confessed his feelings to Ning Yao.

There he met the old great sword immortal [Chen Qingdu](../Chen_Qingdu.md) and his senior brother [Zuo You](../Zuo_You.md), and he fought a series of duels on the city wall against fourth tier opponents, among them [Cao Ci](../Cao_Ci.md) of the Great Duan Empire. He then headed north again. He travelled for a time with [Lu Tai](../Lu_Tai.md) of the Lu Clan and passed through Flying Eagle Fortress.

## Volume 5: Dao Observing Dao (Chapters 296 to 359)

After parting with Lu Tai, Chen Ping'an stumbled into the [Lotus Flower Blessed Land](../Lotus_Flower_Blessed_Land.md). The martial artists there fought for a chance to ascend, and he clashed with [Ding Ying](../Ding_Ying.md), [Zhong Qiu](../Zhong_Qiu.md), [Yu Zhenyi](../Yu_Zhenyi.md) and others. He took the street orphan [Pei Qian](../Pei_Qian.md) under his wing, and on leaving the Blessed Land he broke through straight to the fifth tier.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

Travelling on with Pei Qian, he crossed paths with the Yao Clan of the Great Quan Empire, whose locust leaf linked back to his childhood vow. He passed through [Fox Town](../Fox_Town.md) and helped the water god of Burial River with the aid of the scholar [Zhong Kui](../Zhong_Kui.md).

## Volume 6: Little Teacher (Chapters 360 to 455)

Chen Ping'an returned to Old Dragon City, then travelled the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent. He met old friends far from home and took Pei Qian and his companions north to Mountain Cliff Academy. He then went to Bamboo Scroll Lake. There [Gu Can](../Gu_Can.md) had become the disciple of [Liu Zhimao](../Liu_Zhimao.md) and killed many innocent people. When Gu Can refused to see his wrongs, Chen Ping'an settled for the next best thing. He stayed on the lake for a long time, tracking down the victims and their families and making amends on his friend's behalf, a period that shook him deeply.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup>

## Volume 7: Dragon Raising Head (Chapters 456 to 515)

After handing Gu Can his account books, Chen Ping'an left Bamboo Scroll Lake and finally returned to Downtrodden Mountain, where Cui Cheng beat him in another round of training. He then travelled to the Northern Complete Reed Continent. He went through Skeleton Wall City and its ghost cities, and on the road he called himself Chen Haoren, a name meant to remind himself not to swing from one extreme to the other.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

## Volume 8: No Evil Thoughts (Chapters 516 to 570)

Chen Ping'an escorted [Sui Jingcheng](../Sui_Jingcheng.md)'s group for part of their journey, crossed swords with the sword prodigy [Qi Jinglong](../Qi_Jinglong.md) and his disciple [Bai Shou](../Bai_Shou.md), and sparred with [Li Er](../Li_Er.md). On Lion Peak he broke through to the seventh tier, the Vajra Body Tier.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup> He returned home to set up Downtrodden Mountain's ancestral hall, then left again for the Sword Qi Great Wall.

## Volume 9: Moon in the Sky (Chapters 571 to 660 - Ongoing)

Chen Ping'an returned to the Sword Qi Great Wall and Ning Yao. He fought a run of challenges from the city's young sword cultivators and became the second shopkeeper of a wine shop. Chen Qingdu agreed to act as his matchmaker in proposing to the Ning Clan.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> When the Savage World attacked, he fought on the wall and was badly hurt, falling several tiers as a Qi refiner. Liu Xianyang arrived and taught him his family's sword scripture.

After the previous Hidden Official left, Chen Ping'an was named the new Hidden Official. He was the youngest in the Sword Qi Great Wall's history and had the lowest cultivation base of any who had held the post.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> He reorganised the Hidden Official's lineage, survived attempts on his life and led sword cultivators onto the battlefield.
