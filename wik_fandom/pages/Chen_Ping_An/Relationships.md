## Friends

Chen Ping'an has formed several key friendships throughout the story, each with its own unique dynamics and importance. These relationships are often complex, going beyond simple camaraderie and touching on themes of loyalty, obligation, and mutual growth. Here is a breakdown of some of his most significant friendships:

- **[Gu Can](../Gu_Can.md):** Chen Ping'an is a significant figure in Gu Can's life. It seems that Gu Can has accepted Chen Ping'an as his only close companion outside his mother. Chen Ping'an also takes on a mentor-like role, advising Gu Can to look after his mother and be careful of his master. This relationship highlights Chen Ping'an's protective nature and his willingness to look out for those who are vulnerable.
- **[Ning Yao](../Ning_Yao.md):** Ning Yao's relationship with Chen Ping'an is complex and evolves throughout the sources. She initially appears as someone who is unwilling to accept help, but later accepts Chen Ping'an's help, stating that she will repay him for saving her life. She becomes more familiar with him, and their interactions are marked by a certain level of banter. She also recognizes his kind nature and makes an agreement to repay him for his help. **Their relationship is significant because it shows a deep connection and understanding, and it is also suggested that they have some fated ties**. She openly admits she does not dislike him, but also does not give a clear answer when asked if she likes him. **She is also the person he intends to account to for any money he spends**. They are so close that she can understand his character by looking at the gifts he has given her.
- **[Liu Xianyang](../Liu_Xianyang.md):** While not as extensively detailed as other relationships, Liu Xianyang is mentioned as one of the people whose safety and happiness Chen Ping'an cares about. Chen Ping'an also appears to value Liu Xianyang's friendship, as he was willing to give away three pouches of coins to him. Chen Ping'an acknowledges that they became friends in the first place because of a specific reason, perhaps a shared experience or value. Chen Ping'an is also willing to help Liu Xianyang's business, and to introduce Fan Er to Liu Xianyang if the opportunity arises.
- **Song Jixin:** Despite being neighbors, Chen Ping'an does not consider Song Jixin a true friend. Chen Ping'an dislikes Song Jixin because of the way he treats others. **Chen Ping'an cannot befriend people who are willing to add to the burdens of others**.  Song Jixin, however, sees Chen Ping'an as someone he should befriend. This relationship highlights Chen Ping'an's strong moral compass and his inability to form genuine connections with those who do not share his values. Chen Ping'an makes a point to highlight how different his relationship with Song Jixin is from his friendship with others.
- **[A'Liang](../A'Liang.md):** A'Liang is a complex character who becomes a significant figure in Chen Ping'an's life. **Chen Ping'an is suspicious of A'Liang, yet he also considers him to be a very interesting person**. He enjoys A'Liang's company and is willing to have conversations with him.  A'Liang also acts as a mentor figure, giving him advice. A'Liang even seems to be interested in Chen Ping'an's romantic relationships. They have many conversations and A'Liang explains many things, which Chen Ping'an often finds confusing.
- **[Li Huai](../Li_Huai.md):** Chen Ping'an has a close and caring relationship with Li Huai. Li Huai sees Chen Ping'an as a good person. Chen Ping'an has taught Li Huai important principles and supported him in various ways, and Li Huai recognizes all that Chen Ping'an has done for him. This relationship reflects Chen Ping'an's protective nature and his willingness to guide and support those younger than him.
- **Lin Shouyi:** Chen Ping'an's relationship with Lin Shouyi is somewhat different from his relationships with others. Lin Shouyi notes that Chen Ping'an seems to care for Li Baoping more than for him.  Chen Ping'an is aware of this, and he feels he has to explain situations to Li Baoping because he has a better understanding of her. Despite this, Chen Ping'an is willing to give Lin Shouyi valuable items and share his knowledge with him. **Lin Shouyi recognizes that Chen Ping'an is more seriously injured than others, but that he is hiding his injuries well**. He is also the person Chen Ping'an chooses to gift the 'Mountain Scouring Album' to.
- **[Li Baoping](../Li_Baoping.md):**  Chen Ping'an has a very close relationship with Li Baoping. Chen Ping'an explains things to her because he feels it's important she understands them. Chen Ping'an prioritizes her understanding and safety, indicating that he feels a sense of responsibility towards her. She is also one of the three people, along with Liu Xianyang and Gu Can, whose happiness and safety he prioritizes above all others. It is also suggested that Li Baoping will get angry for Chen Ping'an's sake if she feels like others are not treating him well.
- **[Zhang Shanfeng](../Zhang_Shanfeng.md) and [Xu Yuanxia](../Xu_Yuanxia.md)**: Chen Ping'an displays a high level of care and loyalty towards Zhang Shanfeng and Xu Yuanxia. He considers them his true friends and would offer help or advice without hesitation. **When they meet again after some time apart, Chen Ping'an is overjoyed, stating that they are the only ones who would be willing to help him with a thankless task**. Chen Ping'an also values these relationships so much that he is very willing to go to great lengths to help Xu Yuanxia with a situation, even if it means risking his own safety.
- **[Liu Baqiao](../Liu_Baqiao.md):** Liu Baqiao and Chen Ping'an have a complicated relationship.  Liu Baqiao is very friendly and considers Chen Ping'an to be a close friend.  Chen Ping'an, however, does not reciprocate the same level of friendship. He does not take Liu Baqiao seriously no matter how much the latter proclaims they are brothers. Despite this, Chen Ping'an still has a positive impression of Liu Baqiao.  **It is noted that even after only two meetings, Chen Ping'an and Liu Baqiao already seem to be very close friends, while Sun Jiashu and Fu Nanhua could be friends for decades and never be very close**.
- **[Sun Jiashu](../Sun_Jiashu.md):** Chen Ping'an and Sun Jiashu's relationship is primarily described in terms of business and strategic value. Sun Jiashu does not see Chen Ping'an as a close friend. Chen Ping'an also states that he is no longer friends with Sun Jiashu because he is not sufficiently earnest when it comes to certain things.  Chen Ping'an notes that Sun Jiashu sets a price tag on everyone he encounters, regarding them as a business asset.
- **[Fan Er](../Fan_Er.md):** Chen Ping'an has a close friendship with Fan Er, and he is willing to reciprocate those feelings. Fan Er sees Chen Ping'an as a very close friend. Chen Ping'an genuinely envies Fan Er, who never seems to have a care in the world.
- **[Cui Chan](../Cui_Chan.md):** Chen Ping'an is aware that Cui Chan's intentions are not pure. Chen Ping'an does not trust Cui Chan and hopes that he will stay away from him. Despite this, Chen Ping'an and Cui Chan have a complex relationship that has a sense of interconnected fate to it.

These friendships reveal different facets of Chen Ping'an's character. He is loyal, protective, and deeply values genuine connections. He prioritizes the well-being of his friends, and does not form friendships easily with those who do not share his values.  He is also willing to help those in need, and he is often shown giving valuable gifts to those he considers friends. Chen Ping'an also recognizes that he cannot befriend everyone, and that some relationships are merely transactional. His interactions with others highlight his strong sense of morality and his preference for genuine, meaningful relationships over superficial ones.

## Foes

Chen Ping'an has a number of key enemies, and these relationships are often complex and deeply intertwined with his personal journey and the larger conflicts in the world around him. These relationships are not just about simple antagonism, but also about exploring themes of fate, justice, and the nature of good and evil. Here is a breakdown of some of his key enemies:

- **[Fu Nanhua](../Fu_Nanhua.md)**: Fu Nanhua is a significant enemy of Chen Ping'an, with their animosity stemming from a personal conflict in Chen Ping'an's hometown. Chen Ping'an almost killed Fu Nanhua in their first encounter. Fu Nanhua holds Chen Ping'an in contempt and is determined to kill him. **Their conflict is personal, but also reflects the clash between different social classes and values, with Fu Nanhua viewing Chen Ping'an as a "piece of scum".** Chen Ping'an knows he must be careful due to his history with Fu Nanhua.
- **[Sun Scorch Mountain](../Sects.md#sun-scorch-mountain):** The people from Sun Scorch Mountain are a major threat to Chen Ping'an. **They are described as a well-established and reputable sect, with members who are far more powerful than people like Fu Nanhua**. Lu Zhengchun hates Liu Xianyang and wants to see him dead. Bai Yuan from Sun Scorch Mountain is convinced that Chen Ping'an is a pawn of a powerful mastermind, and is constantly looking for him. The Mountain-moving Ape from Sun Scorch Mountain is also noted as one of Chen Ping'an's mortal enemies that he must exact revenge on. These interactions are indicative of the conflicts between different factions and highlight the dangers Chen Ping'an faces.
- **[Ma Kuxuan](../Ma_Kuxuan.md)**: Ma Kuxuan is a recurring enemy who detests Chen Ping'an. Their conflict seems to be a matter of fate, with their paths destined to clash. Their relationship is further complicated by the conflict between their fathers, of which Chen Ping'an is unaware. Chen Ping'an dislikes Ma Kuxuan just as much as Ma Kuxuan dislikes Chen Ping'an. They had previously fought to a draw, but Ma Kuxuan has advanced three tiers in a single year, making him an even more dangerous opponent. **Their conflict represents a clash of destinies, and is possibly indicative of a larger, long-term rivalry that may play out through generations**.
- **[Cui Chan](../Cui_Chan.md):** Cui Chan is a complex figure who acts as both a potential ally and an enemy. Chen Ping'an does not trust Cui Chan, and believes his intentions are not pure. Despite this, their fates seem interconnected. Cui Chan orchestrated a plot against Chen Ping'an and Qi Jingchun. **Cui Chan also views Chen Ping'an as an obstacle in his pursuit of the Great Dao**. He is an extremely cunning individual who is adept at hatching elaborate schemes. Cui Chan had been eavesdropping on Lin Shouyi, and he becomes enraged when he is insulted. Despite not trusting Cui Chan, Chen Ping'an acknowledges he is very intelligent. Cui Chan's motivations and schemes often make him a hidden enemy, whose actions directly affect Chen Ping'an's circumstances.
- **[Song Jixin](../Song_Jixin.md):** Although Song Jixin is Chen Ping'an's neighbor, they are not friends. Chen Ping'an dislikes him because of the way he treats others. Song Jixin was seen encouraging those who were beating Liu Xianyang. Chen Ping'an feels that Song Jixin would add to the burdens of others simply because he thinks it is fun. Song Jixin, however, views Chen Ping'an as someone he should befriend. **This relationship highlights Chen Ping'an'an's strong moral compass and his inability to form genuine connections with those who do not share his values. While Song Jixin may not be an outright enemy, he represents a type of person that Chen Ping'an cannot accept.**
- **The [Chen Clan](../Chen_Clan.md)**: Chen Ping'an's own clan also appears to be his enemies. His clan does not see Chen Ping'an as one of their own, and they are hoping that he will be killed so that they can benefit from his death. Chen Ping'an's branch of the Chen Clan has fallen to a miserable state. The Chen Clan in the small town only has one member who is not a servant or a maid. The enemies of the Chen Clan also see Chen Ping'an as an enemy because of his relation to the clan.
- **Ding Ying:** Ding Ying was once considered the most powerful person in the world, and was an enemy of Chen Ping'an. Chen Ping'an ultimately defeats Ding Ying, killing him in a head-on battle. **Ding Ying is an example of an enemy who represents a large and powerful organization, whose intentions are difficult to discern.** Ding Ying also mistakenly believed that Cao Qinglang was related to Mirror Heart Hall's Tong Qingqing.
- **Yu Zhenyi**: Yu Zhenyi is another powerful enemy who allies himself with Ding Ying. However, Yu Zhenyi is also an enemy of otherworldly immortals, meaning that Chen Ping'an is also one of his targets. Yu Zhenyi is only willing to target other otherworldly immortals. Chen Ping'an is one of the people Yu Zhenyi fears most due to his ability to kill Ding Ying. **Yu Zhenyi's motivations are complex and are tied to his own ambitions and his role in a larger power struggle. Chen Ping'an does not believe that Yu Zhenyi's actions were justified.**
- **[Pei Qian](../Pei_Qian.md):** Although Pei Qian becomes a companion of Chen Ping'an, she is also described as someone Chen Ping'an genuinely detests. **Despite traveling together, Pei Qian's actions and behaviors are described as a mirror by which Chen Ping'an uses to look back at himself, which is indicative of their conflicting personalities.** They have a difficult and complicated relationship.
- **[Old Man Yang](../Old_Man_Yang.md):** Old Man Yang is not necessarily an enemy, but he is a complex character who is implied to have some connection to Chen Ping'an's past. Old Man Yang was aware of what was going to happen to Chen Ping'an's father, but he did not intervene. Chen Ping'an may resent him for his inaction. The fact that Old Man Yang may have known about the events surrounding Chen Ping'an's father and did not intervene creates a complicated relationship dynamic where he is not necessarily an enemy, but is not a friend either.
- **Unnamed individuals:** There are several unnamed enemies mentioned as well. There are assassins from the Case Purchasing Pagoda and Grandmaster Lin Gushan from Colorful Garment Nation who team up to attack Chen Ping'an. There is also a powerful woman in the Great Li Empire who resents Song Changjing and who orchestrated the shattering of Chen Ping'an's bonded porcelain. There are also those who are betting on when Chen Ping'an will be killed.

These enemies and rivalries highlight the complex and dangerous world Chen Ping'an navigates. They reveal a world where personal grievances, political schemes, and the pursuit of power all intertwine. Chen Ping'an is not simply a passive observer in these conflicts, but a significant player whose actions and choices shape his relationships and his own destiny. These relationships also serve to highlight his strong moral compass, as well as the kind of person he is.

## Mentors

Chen Ping'an has several key mentors who have significantly influenced his life, each relationship marked by unique intricacies and importance. These mentors have shaped not only his skills, but also his understanding of the world and his place in it.

- **[Old Man Yao](../Old_Man_Yao.md):** Old Man Yao, a potter, is one of Chen Ping'an's early mentors. Although he was often stingy with his best skills, Chen Ping'an diligently practiced whatever Old Man Yao was willing to teach him. Chen Ping'an would also commit all of Old Man Yao's stories to memory. This relationship highlights Chen Ping'an's dedication and willingness to learn from even the most reluctant of teachers. Despite Old Man Yao's reluctance to fully teach Chen Ping'an, he still had a significant impact on Chen Ping'an's life.
- **Mr. Qi([Qi Jingchun](../Qi_Jingchun.md)):** Mr. Qi is a crucial mentor figure for Chen Ping'an. **Mr. Qi provides Chen Ping'an with background information about his parents and the world, and gives him the jade hairpin, a representation of the deep relationship between Mr. Qi and his teacher**. Chen Ping'an trusts Mr. Qi, and believes in the rules Mr. Qi once followed. Mr. Qi also tries to persuade Chen Ping'an not to kill Fu Nanhua. Chen Ping'an deeply respects Mr. Qi.
- **[Ruan Qiong](../Ruan_Qiong.md):** Ruan Qiong, a blacksmith, takes Chen Ping'an in as a laborer. This relationship is significant because Master Ruan allows Chen Ping'an to purchase five mountains. **Chen Ping'an relies on the promises and rules set by Master Ruan, but does not necessarily trust him implicitly**, which highlights Chen Ping'an's cautious nature. Master Ruan also gives Chen Ping'an maps.
- **Cui Chan's grandfather:** [Cui Cheng](../Cui_Cheng.md) teaches Chen Ping'an his fist techniques, although he does not teach Chen Ping'an everything. The less he teaches Chen Ping'an, the more it reflects his lofty expectations of him. **This mentorship is important because it provides Chen Ping'an with a foundation for his martial arts training**, and suggests a path for growth based on individual experience rather than strict instruction.
- **[A'Liang](../A'Liang.md):** A'Liang is an enigmatic figure who also acts as a mentor to Chen Ping'an. He teaches Chen Ping'an persuasive speaking techniques. **A'Liang's approach is often unconventional, using complicated words and profound reasoning to explain simple things, which helps Chen Ping'an understand complex concepts**. A'Liang also believes that there are too many distractions on the path to cultivation, and he attempts to help Chen Ping'an in his cultivation. Chen Ping'an respects A'Liang and his knowledge.
- **[Zhu He](../Zhu_He.md):** Zhu He is a martial arts master and a patriarch of the Li Clan. He provides Chen Ping'an with valuable knowledge about martial arts, and explains the Wooden Fetus Tier. Zhu He also advises Chen Ping'an to cherish his bonds with Master Ruan and Ning Yao. **His guidance on martial arts and his emphasis on enduring hardship are significant for Chen Ping'an's growth**.
- **[Scholarly Sage](../Scholarly_Sage.md)**: The Scholarly Sage is Mr. Qi's teacher, and is also an indirect mentor to Chen Ping'an. Chen Ping'an is interested in comparing the books of the Scholarly Sage with those of the Second Sage. **Chen Ping'an respects and admires the Scholarly Sage**, which is evident in his desire to learn from the Scholarly Sage's teachings. The Scholarly Sage offers to take Chen Ping'an as his final disciple.
- **[Zheng Dafeng](../Zheng_Dafeng.md):** Zheng Dafeng becomes Chen Ping'an's Dao mentor, though their relationship is initially marked by friction. Zheng Dafeng does not warm up to Chen Ping'an and views him as too boring. **Despite this, their interactions lead Zheng Dafeng to recognize Chen Ping'an's unique path, and ultimately to acknowledge him as his Dao mentor**. Zheng Dafeng acknowledges that Chen Ping'an's path has to be acknowledged for getting him to that point.
- **External Heavenly Master from Mount Longhu**: This old Daoist priest is an external Heavenly Master from Mount Longhu, who is Zhang Shanfeng's master. This mentor is important because he acknowledges that Chen Ping'an's bonded item is filled with an immortal aura.

These mentors each contribute to Chen Ping'an's development in different ways. Some provide practical skills, others offer moral and philosophical guidance, while others challenge him to grow and question his own beliefs. These relationships highlight Chen Ping'an's openness to learning from diverse sources, and his ability to integrate these lessons into his personal growth.
