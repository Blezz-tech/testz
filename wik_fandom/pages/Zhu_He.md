## Appearance

Zhu He is a sturdy martial artist with rough features and a usually solemn face. On the road south, he walks beside his horse and carries a string of red Demon Chiming Bells given to him by the Li Clan's patriarch. *(Chapter 96)* After his battle with the black python on Go Table Mountain, he sits cross-legged and drenched in blood, yet visibly glowing with energy. *(Chapter 100 (1))*

## Personality

Zhu He is kind, generous and easygoing, described as "a kind and pure man". Although a fifth-tier martial artist, he apologises to the young Li Huai after [Zhu Lu](Zhu_Lu.md) splatters him with mud, and promises to buy him new clothes. *(Chapter 97)* He readily shares his knowledge, and patiently explains the early martial tiers to Chen Ping'an during their night watch. *(Chapter 100 (2))* He is disciplined in his craft: when A'Liang offers him wine, he refuses, saying he cannot drink before reaching full mastery of martial arts. *(Chapter 88)* He takes his duty to protect Li Baoping seriously and genuinely adores her.

In battle, he shows an extremely resolute will. Facing the black python, he declares that since he will die either way, he would rather die fighting, and his aura climbs higher the more he is injured. *(Chapter 99)*

His great weakness is his daughter. Her eyes resemble her mother's, so he can rarely bring himself to scold her, and Chen Ping'an later observes that Zhu He is a good person who does not know how to discipline his daughter. When Zhu Lu's assassination attempt fails, Zhu He begs A'Liang to let her go and offers to take his own life in her place. *(Chapter 110)*

He also has an innate dislike for scholars, picked up from Li Baozhen's habit of ridiculing them. *(Chapter 106)*

## Background

Zhu He is a hereditary servant of the Li Clan of [Fortune Street](Small_Town.md#fortune-street-and-peach-leaf-alley). When his aptitude for martial arts was discovered, the Li Clan immediately invested heavily in his development, and later did the same for his daughter. *(Chapter 92 (1))* His own aptitude is limited, but the Li patriarch privately praised him as a brilliant martial arts teacher, one who would be treated as an esteemed guest even by the affluent clans of the Great Li capital. *(Chapter 87 (1))* He taught Zhu Lu that training fist techniques the wrong way ruins the body, while someone who grasps their true essence can slay gods with a single punch. *(Chapter 87 (2))*

Having spent his whole life in the small town, Zhu He had never fought a true life-and-death battle before the journey. He firmly believed, like most martial artists, that the ninth tier was the very pinnacle of martial arts, unaware of the tenth. *(Chapter 134 (1))* He was stuck at the peak of the fifth tier.

He and Zhu Lu were assigned to accompany Li Baoping to Mountain Cliff Academy. On Go Table Mountain, he performs a Soil Molding Technique to summon the mountain god, accidentally calling up the mountain lord Wei Bo, who thrashes his calf with a bamboo cane. *(Chapter 98)* After surviving the black python, he feels he has found a catalyst for a breakthrough. When the treasures are shared out, he chooses the Purple Qi Guide for his daughter and a Hero's Gall Pill from True Martial Mountain, which leaves him dreaming of the seventh tier. *(Chapter 104)* Both are later confiscated by A'Liang after Zhu Lu's assassination attempt, and the pair leave the group.

## Trivia

- Zhu He jokingly admits he would happily accept A'Liang as a son-in-law, or even call him father-in-law, if A'Liang's boasts were true. *(Chapter 90)*
- When sparring with Chen Ping'an on Go Table Mountain, Zhu He suppressed his strength to the third tier. The fight helped Chen Ping'an discover three acupoints that the "fire dragon" in his body dared not enter.
- Chen Ping'an often compared A'Liang to Zhu He, to A'Liang's great annoyance.
- One later passage describes Zhu He as having left Jewel Small World as a fourth-tier martial artist, contradicting the many passages that call him a fifth-tier martial artist.
