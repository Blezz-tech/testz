**Xie Shi** is a Daoist cultivator who grew up on Peach Leaf Alley in the small town of Jewel Small World. He became the most powerful Daoist on the Northern Complete Reed Continent and is known there as its Dao Master. He is the ancestor of Xie Ling and one of the town's most famous descendants, along with [Cao Xi](Cao_Xi.md).

## Appearance

Xie Shi looks like a middle-aged man.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> Despite his standing, he looks much like one of the rough farmers of the small town. Few would guess that he has read widely and knows the teachings of all three schools.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

## Personality

Across several continents Xie Shi is known as an honest and upright man who never acts against his conscience. People compare him to the sun in the sky.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> Even his enemies respect him. He speaks little and dislikes chatter. He has no patience for Cao Xi's teasing and tells him plainly that he does not trust him.

He takes others seriously regardless of their age. Even with a child many generations younger, he gives his full attention and never lets his mind wander. He holds his descendants to a high standard. He would have given up on Xie Ling if the boy had shown any wish to leave his teacher [Ruan Qiong](Ruan_Qiong.md) for a more powerful one.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

Being forced into a task he finds dishonourable weighs on him. He admitted to Xie Ling that he felt guilty, and told him that the Daoist path is one where examining one's own conscience matters most. Before his branch master [Lu Chen](Lu_Chen.md) he is nervous and obedient, quick to stand and quick to obey.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

## Background

Xie Shi was born into the Xie Clan of Peach Leaf Alley. As a child he wrote his name on the wall of the small temple along with other children such as Cao Xi.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> He came close to dying twice in the town, and both times he was saved by [Zhi Gui](Zhi_Gui.md). She once said he would have been swept away by a flood if not for her.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> Like everyone born in the town, he has a bonded porcelain held by a buyer outside, which gives that buyer power over his life.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

He left the small world and rose to become the Dao Master of the Northern Complete Reed Continent. Several of the Five Mountains of various empires there hold sects of his lineage, and his faction is the strongest Daoist one on the continent. That continent has no full Heavenly Lord, and he is described as being half of one.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

After [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md)'s bonded porcelain was broken, the buyer's backers used Xie Shi's own porcelain to make him travel south and press the Great Li Empire. He returned to the town on New Year's Day. When a girl at his clan's door did not believe him, he told her to check the genealogy, where his name was written with one stroke missing. He then offered incense in the ancestral hall and chose the long-browed boy, Xie Ling, as the only descendant worth his attention.

At the relay station, he demanded that the Great Li Empire hand over He Xiaoliang, Ma Kuxuan or [Li Xisheng](Li_Xisheng.md). He offered to guard Summer Resort Mountain against Lake View Academy if they agreed, and threatened trouble on the empire's northern border if they refused. Cao Xi, Xu Ruo, Ruan Qiong, Cui Chan and an old martial artist all gathered around him. When Xu Ruo challenged him to a fight to the death, an oriole sent by Xie Shi's elder landed on his shoulder and relayed an offer that eased the deal.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup>

During this time, Lu Chen visited and gave Xie Ling a gift. He also ordered Xie Shi to keep He Xiaoliang alive for the next sixty years. Xie Shi then took Xie Ling to Old Man Yang's medicine shop and paid with his own promise for a treasure and a promise for the boy.

The sinking of Ceremony Mountain's Kun ship gave Xie Shi the reason he needed to move into the central region of the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent. There he stood guard north of Lake View Academy and cut the continent in two, so the Great Li Empire could conquer the north without interference. He held back the academy and took on three challengers in a row.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> [Wei Jin](Wei_Jin.md) of Wind Snow Temple later fought him. The result was never made public, but Xie Shi stayed on the continent, so it was taken that he had not lost.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup>

He eventually returned to the Northern Complete Reed Continent. He supported He Xiaoliang in founding the Refreshing Sect.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup> When she offended the Jade Forest Sect and Bai Shang, one of his direct disciples went to her sect in anger.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup> Xie Shi himself scolded her and sent her to apologize.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup>

## Trivia

- The "Shi" in his name is written with one dot missing in the Xie Clan genealogy.
- Cao Xi taunts him by calling him Old Man Xie, and the two have never got along.
- He gave Xie Ling two treasures. One was the flying sword of a dead sword immortal, called Peach Leaf, for Xie Ling to refine into a bonded item. The other was a high-grade Sword Nurturing Gourd named Full Moon.<sup>[[16]](#ref-16)</sup>
- He once taught Dao techniques to a young man from a powerful Daoist clan without taking him as a disciple, because he was said to be unable to take disciples. Elsewhere the novel mentions his direct disciples.
- He is ranked among the top ten cultivators of the Northern Complete Reed Continent.
- After his rise became known, the Xie Clan of Peach Leaf Alley went from a declining family to one of note in the town.
