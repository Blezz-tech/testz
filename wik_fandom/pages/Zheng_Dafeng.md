## Appearance

Zheng Dafeng is a hunchbacked man. By the time he takes up residence at Downtrodden Mountain, his posture has worsened noticeably compared to his years in Jewel Small World. *(Chapter 461)*

## Personality

Zheng Dafeng is foul-mouthed, lecherous, and cheerfully shameless, yet underneath all of that he operates by a strict personal code that he rarely advertises. He is a habitual thief of small things, a compulsive gossip, and an unembarrassed admirer of women, but he pays his debts in full when they matter. When a young woman who worked at his Dust Medicinal Shop was killed because of circumstances tied to him, he picked a fight with the entire city of Old Dragon City rather than walk away owing her a book. *(Chapter 361)*

He is slow to trust and quicker to needle than to praise. He spent years dismissing Chen Ping'an as a boring country boy with whom he had nothing in common, only to eventually acknowledge him as his Dao mentor. *(Chapter 254)* He is candid about his own weaknesses, admitting that his broad knowledge, while an advantage in many areas, narrowed the purity of his fist intent and made his ninth tier breakthrough far harder than it needed to be.

He enjoys wine, Go, and the company of people who can match him in banter. At Downtrodden Mountain he falls into an easy companionship with Zhu Lian and Wei Bo, bickering over Go games and sharing wine late into the night.

## Background

Zheng Dafeng is a disciple of [Old Man Yang](Old_Man_Yang.md) and the junior martial brother of [Li Er](Li_Er.md). Old Man Yang never formally identified himself as their Dao mentor, only their master, and kept both disciples at arm's length regarding the details of his plans. *(Chapter 252)*

In Jewel Small World, Zheng Dafeng worked as the gatekeeper of the eastern gate, collecting pouches of copper coins from outsiders seeking entry. He also taught Chen Ping'an a basic breathing technique during this period, though he considered it rudimentary and did not hold Chen Ping'an in particularly high regard at the time.

After the small town was absorbed into Dragon Spring Prefecture, Zheng Dafeng relocated to Old Dragon City, where he ran the Dust Medicinal Shop and served as Dao guardian to a young man from the Fan Clan, guiding him toward a breakthrough past the third tier bottleneck. He was also secretly responsible for ensuring things went smoothly for Chen Ping'an in the city, on Old Man Yang's instructions.

When a female employee at his shop was killed after being caught up in a conflict tied to him, Zheng Dafeng challenged the Fu Clan and fought his way through a succession of their fighters. He defeated Chu Yang, a Golden Core Tier cultivator who broke through to the Nascent Tier mid-battle, and then severely wounded a Nascent Tier Fu Clan ancestor wielding a pseudo-celestial tool, taking heavy injuries himself in the process. The confrontation forced every major clan in Old Dragon City to choose sides and sent the city into upheaval. At the year's end he met Fu Qi, the city lord, in a battle to the death on the Dragon Scaling Platform. Chen Ping'an arrived beforehand and gave him a vial of Seated Meditation Pills to help suppress his injuries. Zheng Dafeng prevailed, trapping Fu Qi's pseudo-celestial sword with an azure sword-suppressing talisman inscribed by Zhong Kui until Fu Qi surrendered. *(Chapter 373)*

It was only after the battle, upon reading the book he had taken from her, that Zheng Dafeng fully understood what that text had meant to him in terms of his own cultivation. He recognized then that Chen Ping'an had played a role in his ninth tier breakthrough, and that he was in fact his Dao mentor.

After the events in Old Dragon City, Zheng Dafeng returned to the small town for a time before eventually settling at the foot of Downtrodden Mountain, where he took on the role of gatekeeper once again. He became a regular part of life there alongside Zhu Lian and Wei Bo, helping to plan the development of the Lotus Root Blessed Land and acting as one of the mountain's official guest elders. *(Chapter 527)*

## Trivia

- Zhu Lian once speculated that, in a past life, Zheng Dafeng may have been one of the four divine generals of the Heavenly Court.
- Despite his appearance and manner, Zheng Dafeng was considered by those who knew him to be, at heart, the most scrupulous of men. *(Chapter 820)*
- He gave Chen Ping'an a jade tablet minimization treasure and a copy of the Proper Sword Scripture while in Old Dragon City. *(Chapter 283)*
