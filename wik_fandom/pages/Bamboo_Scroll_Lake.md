# Bamboo Scroll Lake

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Bamboo_Scroll_Lake)

---
> **Spoiler Warning:** This article contains spoilers from the *Sword of Coming* (*Jian Lai* / *Unsheathed*) novel and donghua.

The **Bamboo Scroll Lake** is a vast lake in the central region of [Eastern Treasured Vial Continent](Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent.md), dotted with roughly a thousand islands and rich in spiritual energy. For centuries it was a lawless haven for vagrant cultivators, ruled by force rather than by any nation or sect. It is where [Liu Zhimao](Liu_Zhimao.md) took [Gu Can](Gu_Can.md) and his mother, and where [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md) spent about three years trying to deal with what Gu Can had become. After the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md) seized the region, the lake became the seat of the True Realm Sect, the Jade Tablet Sect's lower sect on the continent.

## Geography

The lake holds about a thousand islands of every size, scattered across the water like stars. Around half of them are owned or rented by Qi refiners, and Cyan Gorge Island, the base of Liu Zhimao, is the largest.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> Its abundant spiritual energy makes the bigger islands ideal private abodes for one or two Golden Core Tier cultivators, especially those with an affinity for water.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

The lake lies south of the Stone Brush Nation. Travelers from the north have to cross that nation and pass the Thousand Bird Border Pass to reach it. It also borders the Luminous Vermilion Empire, which made it a prize that both the Great Li Empire and the Luminous Vermilion Empire wanted.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> The wider central region is overseen by Lake View Academy.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

Four major cities stand on its shores. Pond Water City sits on the western bank and is the third largest of them. Green Parasol City lies near Hibiscus Mountain, an outlying island known across the region for Hibiscus Stone, a prized material for carving seals.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup> Cloud Pagoda City is also on the lakeshore. Beyond the lake to the south lies a stretch of mountains full of miasma, spirits and evil cultivators.

Three foundational water veins carry most of the lake's water fortune. Palace Willow Island owns one outright, Cyan Gorge Island holds most of a second, and Azure Mound Island, Heavenly Governess Island and Millet Grain Island share the third. Countless smaller branch veins are claimed by the remaining islands.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

Autumn and winter bring thick mists and winter mirages.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> Locals count rain, snow and dew as "rootless water", so a heavy snowfall is welcomed because it raises the lake's spiritual energy.

## History

### Origins

The lake was once an ordinary place with little spiritual energy. That changed when a traveling Confucian sage from the [Middle Earth Divine Continent](Middle_Earth_Divine_Continent.md) attained the Great Dao there. The resonance between heaven and earth turned the lake into a blessed land.

### Era of the island masters

The lake never had a lasting ruler. The most powerful native cultivator in its history was Liu Laocheng of Palace Willow Island. After he reached the Upper Five Tiers he was entitled to found a sect, but instead he installed a female cultivator close to him as "ruler of the cultivation world" and left the lake. After she died, Palace Willow Island was left to fall into ruin, and the lake split back into feuding islands.

The islanders guarded that independence fiercely. Once an official Nascent Tier cultivator and a Golden Core Tier sword cultivator held a banquet on one of the islands, declaring themselves the lake's new leaders. The island masters all praised them at the table, then joined forces and killed both of them with close to nine hundred immortal treasures and two hundred cultivators sent to their deaths. Locals still told the story with pride centuries later.

### Rise of Cyan Gorge Island

Liu Zhimao, the River Severing True Lord, built Cyan Gorge Island into the strongest force on the lake. He took Gu Can and his mother there from the small town, and in return [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md) barred him from leaving the lake for thirty years.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> Gu Can's flood dragon, Tan Xue, killed and devoured cultivators across the islands. After it massacred an entire sect, Cyan Gorge Island became the unofficial leader of every island on the lake.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

### The election and Chen Ping'an's stay

When Liu Zhimao seemed close to uniting the lake, around a hundred island masters gathered on Palace Willow Island to elect a new ruler. Behind the scenes, the Great Li Empire had long since infiltrated the region, and [Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md) treated the lake as a board on which to test Chen Ping'an. Chen Ping'an arrived around the autumnal equinox and took a post as an honorary elder of Cyan Gorge Island. He read the chronicles and genealogies of the islands and visited them one by one.

Liu Laocheng secretly came back to the lake. He then attacked Cyan Gorge Island and crushed Tan Xue in the waters off its shore, but he spared Gu Can and Chen Ping'an. Liu Zhimao won the title of ruler, yet Liu Laocheng refused to let even that title stand. He declared that the lake would no longer have a ruler at all.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> To bargain with Liu Laocheng, Chen Ping'an used a sage's jade tablet to start draining the lake's spiritual energy and water fortune, forcing him to the table.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup>

### Conquest by the Great Li Empire

Great General Su Gaoshan of the Great Li Empire then came to the lake and threw his whip into its water. He gave every vagrant cultivator a choice. They could give up everything they owned and leave, or hand over half and serve with his army. Anyone who refused within ten days would die. Liu Laocheng and a group of outside cultivators staying on Palace Willow Island stormed Cyan Gorge Island and locked Liu Zhimao in a water prison beneath Palace Willow Island.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup> Island after island surrendered half its wealth and its genealogical records. After that, anyone entering the lake needed a passport, and the Great Li general Guan Yiran held more sway there than Liu Zhimao ever had.

### The True Realm Sect

It later became known that the Jade Tablet Sect of Parasol Leaf Continent had chosen the lake as the site of its lower sect. The new True Realm Sect named Liu Laocheng its chief guest elder and took on Liu Zhimao as a guest elder too. Under Liu Zhimao's restructuring, many islands grew stronger and wealthier. Many former vagrant cultivators also found themselves official immortals of a sect-level force, and the continent's most lawless region turned orderly almost overnight.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup>

The sect built its ancestral hall on Palace Willow Island. When Jiang Shangzhen returned to lead the Jade Tablet Sect, Sword Immortal Wei Ying became the new sect leader. The lake then settled into three camps, with Liu Laocheng leading its old powers.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup>

## Culture

Under the old order there was no line between good and evil cultivators, only strength and cunning. People drove out of other places often came to the lake to start over, and the only rule that held was paying the agreed price for breaking a rule. Grudges were expected to end with the enemy wiped out completely, sometimes along with the whole island.

The local dialect differs from the continent's official language, though the official dialect of the Luminous Vermilion Empire is widely understood. The lake is famous for crow caw wine, golden garb crabs (the rarest breed is called the bamboo branch), and shredded whitebait. Liu Laocheng named three of its fish as banquet delicacies in the Luminous Vermilion Empire. One of them, the winter carp, tastes best in the coldest, snowiest weather.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup> News travels through reports sold by Willow Catkin Island, whose cultivators make their living gathering information.

## Trivia

- Early chapters call it Lake Shujian, which literally means a letter.
- The lake's nicknames for Gu Can included "Little Devil Gu" and "Prince of the Lake". He and his friends called themselves the Ten Heroes of Bamboo Scroll Lake even though there were only nine of them.
- According to Gu Can, the longest election for ruler dragged on for almost half a year. The shortest ended within a month, when one island master killed more than twenty of the others in anger and his wife was elected that same day. She was the only woman ever to hold the title.
- In late spring, an old man cast twenty-four bamboo slips into the lake, one for each solar term. Only Jiang Shangzhen, Liu Laocheng and Zhou Fenglu sensed it, and none of them could find him.
- Jiang Shangzhen offered Downtrodden Mountain six islands on the edge of the lake as a gift.
