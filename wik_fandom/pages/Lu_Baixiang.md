## Appearance

Lu Baixiang is described as tall, with a sturdy and handsome build. He is usually seen in a Confucian robe, with the saber Halting Snow hanging at his waist, its tip often resting against the ground as he leans on the hilt.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup><sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

## Personality

Lu Baixiang is calm, composed and easygoing, able to strike up a conversation with almost anyone. He is also a jack of all talents outside of martial arts, skilled at the guqin, Go, calligraphy and painting, and takes a quiet pride in all of them. Despite his relaxed manner, he carries a sharp, dangerous edge underneath, and in the history of the Lotus Flower Blessed Land he was known for his ruthlessness and decisiveness as the founder of its demonic orthodoxy. He is patient and good-humored with the people he is close to, including as a teacher, but he can turn cold and openly threatening when someone close to him might be betrayed.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup><sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup><sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

## Background

Lu Baixiang grew up an orphan in the Lotus Flower Blessed Land and had a hard early life, at one point killing a man in self-defense and being thrown in jail before leaving his hometown to survive on his own. Finding that the martial arts world of his time had no true righteous or demonic factions, just sects competing for impressive-sounding names, he founded his own demonic orthodoxy as a kind of game, deliberately holding it to higher standards than any righteous sect for the novelty of it. He eventually became the most powerful martial artist in the Lotus Flower Blessed Land and achieved invincibility for decades, but his unmarried, wandering lifestyle eventually left him vulnerable, and he was ambushed and killed by dozens of elite cultivators working together.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup><sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup><sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

His cultivation base survived afterward as one of four picture scrolls kept in the Lotus Flower Blessed Land, alongside Wei Xian, Sui Youbian and Zhu Lian. Chen Ping'an brought him out of his picture scroll and back to life in the Majestic World during the battle at Old Dragon City, where Lu Baixiang fought General Xu Qingzhou and was badly wounded before the battle ended.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup><sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup>

Afterward, he stayed close to Chen Ping'an and the other three revived martial artists, acting as a strategist, companion and occasional teacher to Pei Qian during their travels, and eventually settled on Downtrodden Mountain. He later took in a pair of twins, Yuan Bao and Yuan Lai, as his in-house disciples after leading a group of former soldiers against a reclusive demonic sect, and brought them to Downtrodden Mountain to broaden their horizons. He also helped recover a dragon boat and a water palace for Downtrodden Mountain, and set up his own sect as a subsidiary to it, splitting his time between Downtrodden Mountain and travels through the cultivation world.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup><sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup><sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

## Trivia

- Pei Qian nicknamed him "Little Bai," using the character for elephant in his name as a pun.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup>
- He is a frequent Go opponent of Sui Youbian and considered himself close to unrivaled at the game during his years in the Lotus Flower Blessed Land.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>
