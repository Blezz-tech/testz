# Savage World

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Savage_World)

---
The Savage World is a distinct and dangerous realm, characterized by its harsh environment and the powerful demons that inhabit it. It is in constant conflict with the Majestic World, particularly with the sword cultivators of the Sword Qi Great Wall.

**Geographic and Environmental Characteristics**:

- The Savage World is located **south of the Sword Qi Great Wall**. This wall serves as a border between the Savage and Majestic Worlds.
- It has a **harsh environment with thin spiritual energy**, making it difficult for beings to cultivate. This is in stark contrast to the Majestic World, which has more abundant spiritual energy.
- The Savage World is described as a **breeding ground for powerful demons** due to its unforgiving nature.

**Inhabitants and Their Nature**:

- The primary inhabitants are **demons**, who are physically powerful and skilled in battle.
- These demons are characterized by their **recklessness, violence, and lack of benevolence, righteousness, or morality**. Reason is not effective in this world, and might makes right.
- They are driven by their need for survival and expansion and are constantly seeking ways to invade the Majestic World.
- It's mentioned that **demon prodigies seem to be in hiding**, which is unusual and suggests the Savage World is planning something significant.

**Conflict with the Majestic World**:

- The Savage World is in **constant conflict** with the Majestic World.
- The demons frequently launch attacks on the **Sword Qi Great Wall**, which acts as a crucial defense for the Majestic World.
- The demons view the Majestic World as a **source of resources**, comparing it to "a big piece of fatty meat". They seek to expand their territory and take the resources of the Majestic World for their own survival.
- The fighting is extremely brutal, and it is said that **"everything is settled by the sharpness of one's sword or the hardness of one's fists"**.

**Unique Celestial Features**:

- The Savage World is distinguished by its night sky having **three moons**: one full, one half-full, and one crescent. This is a unique feature that separates it from the Majestic World, which has one moon.

**Lack of Reason**:

- In the Savage World, **reason does not work**, and normal moral standards are nonexistent. This contributes to its violent and chaotic nature.

**Training Ground for Swordsmen:** Despite the dangers, the Savage World serves as a training ground for sword cultivators. By fighting in this harsh environment, they temper their sword dao and grow stronger.

**No United Front**: The sources indicate that the rulers of the Savage World haven't considered combining their power to overcome the Sword Qi Great Wall.

The Savage World is a realm of **intense conflict and brutal power dynamics**. It is a hostile and desolate place, populated by powerful demons who seek to conquer the Majestic World. The lack of morality and reason, combined with the constant violence, makes it a highly dangerous place. Despite this, the Savage World plays a crucial role in the story as a constant threat and a training ground for sword cultivators from the Majestic World.
