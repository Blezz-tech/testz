# Southern Stream Nation

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Southern_Stream_Nation)

---
Southern Stream Nation is a powerful and influential nation within the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent, known for its strong Daoist traditions and the presence of the Divine Edict Sect, which is one of the top immortal forces on the continent. The nation's religious and political landscapes are closely intertwined, with the sect leader holding significant spiritual and political authority. The nation's wealth and resources contribute to its standing, and it has connections with other nations, both on the continent and beyond. The nation is the location of the Limpid Pond Blessed Land, an important location for cultivation. The nation is also a destination for people from all around the world to come and attend ceremonies.

**General Characteristics:**

- **Daoism:** Southern Stream Nation is primarily known for **promoting Daoism above all else**. This is a central aspect of the nation's identity and culture.
- **Central Location**: It is located in the central region of the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent.
- **Resources**: The southern region of Eastern Treasured Vial Continent is described as being **abundant with resources**, which suggests that Southern Stream Nation likely benefits from this.
- **Proximity to Lake View Academy:** Southern Stream Nation is not far from Lake View Academy, one of the 72 Confucian academies.
- **Unique Identity**: It is suggested that the nobles of Southern Stream Nation are happy to establish ties with Great Sui Nation, perhaps because of their mutual northern location.
- **Geographic Traits**: Southern Stream Nation has some distinct geographic features, such as a mountain that extends all the way up into the clouds, and behind that mountain is a forest.
- **Ceremony**: There is a Daoist ceremony that is held in the nation which draws people from outside of the nation to attend. It is mentioned that this ceremony recently concluded.
- **Wealthy**: It is implied that it is a wealthy nation

**Daoist Influence:**

- **Divine Edict Sect:** The **Divine Edict Sect** is the leading Daoist sect of Eastern Treasured Vial Continent, and it's located near the border of Southern Stream Nation. This sect is extremely influential.
- **Limpid Pond Blessed Land:** The Divine Edict Sect possesses the **Limpid Pond Blessed Land**, one of the 72 blessed lands. This indicates the sect's importance and spiritual power. The sect has sole ownership of the land.
- **Incense and Offerings:** The Divine Edict Sect receives the vast majority of incense and offerings among the three Daoist sects of Eastern Treasured Vial Continent.
- **Heavenly Lord:** The sect leader of the Divine Edict Sect, Qi Zhen, was promoted to the position of **Heavenly Lord**. He is a spiritual master and is also recognized as a True Lord in four different nations.
- **Daoist Temples**: There are many Daoist temples located in the nation, and they are described as being top-notch.
- **Other Daoist Sects**: The presence of multiple Daoist temples and priests suggests a strong Daoist culture and practice within the nation.

**Political and Social Aspects:**

- **Imperial Preceptor:** The sect leader of the Divine Edict Sect is also the imperial preceptor of Southern Stream Nation. This highlights the close relationship between the religious and political power within the nation.
- **True Lord:** The sect leader is honored as the highest-ranking True Lord in four different nations, demonstrating the widespread recognition of their spiritual authority.
- **Rulers of Surrounding Nations:** The rulers of several surrounding nations have visited the sect to confer the title of True Lord on the sect leader.
- **Powerful Immortal Force:** The Divine Edict Sect is considered one of the top immortal forces.
- **Respect for Immortals:** The mortal emperors, True Lords, and terrestrial gods encountered by travelers from the nation are treated with the utmost respect without exception.
- **Wealthy Rulers**: The emperor of the nation is described as wealthy.

**Key Figures:**

- **Qi Zhen:** The sect leader of the Divine Edict Sect, who is also the imperial preceptor and a highly respected True Lord. He is a Dao Master of the continent. He has profound Dao powers.
- **Old Immortal Qi:** This is another name for Qi Zhen.
- **Daoist Priests**: Daoist priests are mentioned in multiple contexts, showing that they are important members of the culture. A young Daoist priest in the Divine Edict Sect, who has a surprisingly senior status, was invited to a sect that controlled the Divine Edict Sect.
- **Daoist Nun:** There is a young Daoist nun mentioned in the text.

**Interactions with the Wider World:**

- **Eastern Treasured Vial Continent:** Southern Stream Nation is a key player in Eastern Treasured Vial Continent, with its dominant Daoist sect and its influence on surrounding nations.
- **Other Continents**: The Divine Edict Sect receives funding from the Middle Earth Divine Continent, which shows a connection between the two places.
- **Travel**: People travel to the nation to attend ceremonies.
