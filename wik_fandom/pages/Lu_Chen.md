**Lu Chen** is the third disciple of the Dao Ancestor and one of the three Branch Masters of the Daoist Sect, which dominates the [Heavenly World](Heavenly_World.md). He is the current master of the White Jade Capital. Born in the Majestic World but raised in the Heavenly World, he is a carefree, mischievous figure who appears as a young Daoist priest in a lotus flower hat. He likes running fortune-telling stalls, reading young women's palms, and setting up grand games of Go across the world.

To Chen Ping'an, he first appears as "Daoist Lu", the kindly fortune teller who saved Ning Yao and wrote him a medicinal recipe. Later, Chen Ping'an realises that Lu Chen schemed against him several times, and Lu Chen becomes someone Chen Ping'an deeply distrusts.

## Appearance

Lu Chen looks like a young, carefree Daoist priest, wearing Daoist robes and a lotus flower hat. He can split himself into multiple clones at once.

## Personality

Lu Chen is playful, lazy and shameless. He haggles with a rival fortune teller, eats as a guest while being made to cook, and flirts openly with young women while reading their palms. He cares little for Confucian rites or even for the strict rules of Daoism. His first senior brother is meticulous and his second careful, but Lu Chen has always been scolded for ignoring the rules.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>

Beneath this, he is a supreme schemer whose plans span centuries, and who treats the world like a Go board. Whether people receive fortune or misfortune from him depends largely on his mood. He describes himself as someone who "holds grudges the best". He can be genuinely kind, as with the little boy in azure, but also coldly ruthless, as when he contemplates crushing his own junior brother. Many of the Dao principles he promotes seem foolish at first, but prove profound after hundreds or thousands of years.

## Background

Before ascending, Lu Chen loved to sail the four seas in a small boat. His boatman, who was in his thirties when they set out, returned to land alone after Lu Chen ascended in the North Sea, only to find that his home nation had long since vanished.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> That boatman later became the first boatman of Osmanthus Island, and he warned Chen Ping'an not to ruin his Great Dao. Lu Chen's descendant, Lu Tai, deliberately chose a name meaning "land rising", in opposition to his ancestor's "land sinking".

Under the rules set by the Etiquette Sage, when Lu Chen enters the Majestic World, he can use at most the power of a thirteenth-tier Qi refiner.

## Plot

### Daoist Lu

Lu Chen introduces himself as "Daoist Lu" with no Dao title, running a fortune-telling stall in the small town. He saves [Ning Yao](Ning_Yao.md) when she is close to death.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> He tells Chen Ping'an that he can survive if he goes to the south of town and becomes the disciple of the blacksmith Ruan.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> He reveals to Chen Ping'an the sinister things [Cai Jinjian](Cai_Jinjian.md) had done to him, and that Chen Ping'an was already brimming with an aura of death at the time.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

He leaves Chen Ping'an a three-page medicinal recipe in plain, regulation-style handwriting that Ning Yao finds soulless, stamped with an azure jade seal reading "Signed, Lu Chen".<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> Chen Ping'an is deeply grateful and practises his handwriting from it. Years later, when Chen Ping'an writes Lu Chen's name in the snow while imitating it, Master Bai turns around from far away, and heaven and earth seem to turn upside down.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> Lu Chen also tells Chen Ping'an that the snake gall pebbles in the Dragon Whisker River can be sold, starting him gathering them.

### Return to the Small Town

Lu Chen returns to [Clay Vase Alley](Small_Town.md#clay-vase-alley) with his disciple-to-be [He Xiaoliang](He_Xiaoliang.md), whom he tricked into leaving Divine Edict Sect. He discusses the Dao with [Wei Bo](Wei_Bo.md), and tells He Xiaoliang of a "senior brother" of his, who is also her true junior uncle. He predicts that this senior brother will one day surpass him at Go and fortune telling, and defeat the demon of White Emperor City.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> He sets up his stall again, where the dying Emperor Song of the Great Li Empire comes to him. Lu Chen mocks him over the replica of the White Jade Capital he built without permission, and remarks that he could extend the emperor's life by a thousand years. He also confronts [Song Jixin](Song_Jixin.md), calling him by his real name, Song Mu.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup> He later visits Zhi Gui and tells her he extracted a promise from Song Zhengchun that he wanted.

Through He Xiaoliang, he returns a snake gall pebble to Chen Ping'an, declaring that they no longer owe each other anything. His real plan is revealed later. The snake gall pebbles and the recipe pages form the "banks of a river", creating a bridge of fortune between Chen Ping'an and He Xiaoliang, so that the fortune released by the fall of Jewel Small World would be split evenly between them.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> He then formally accepts He Xiaoliang as his disciple, and explains that his second senior brother chose [Ma Kuxuan](Ma_Kuxuan.md) because their personalities were alike. He also solemnly orders Xie Shi to protect [Li Xisheng](Li_Xisheng.md), threatening to return from the White Jade Capital and break the rules of both worlds if anything happens to him.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup>

### Chen Ping'an's Grudge

During the battle at the Flood Dragon Trench, a "decree of Lu Chen" restrains the participants. Chen Ping'an later says that Lu Chen plotted against him two or three times in that battle, and that he retaliated by making Lu Chen deliver his final words to a friend. Chen Ping'an later calls him "that bastard" who screwed him over. In Chen Ping'an's eyes, Lu Chen's bridge of fortune with He Xiaoliang and his attempts to lure him to the White Jade Capital left lasting negative marks.

### Master of the White Jade Capital

Back in the Heavenly World, Lu Chen oversees the White Jade Capital. He watches events in the Majestic World from its railings, casually severs a thread of fate, and drives away a crowd of immortals with palm strikes. He guides the Dao Ancestor's final disciple, a young Daoist priest who has suffered at the bottom of the world, enjoyed the White Jade Capital's fortune, and died once. Lu Chen tells him it is now time to learn how to live a good life, and that a few decades or a thousand years would both be normal.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup> Lu Chen reveals that Qi Jingchun's final game of Go was described to the Dao Ancestor by Qi Jingchun himself. He also urges his junior brother not to lose to someone surnamed Qi again.

## Abilities

- **Cultivation:** A Branch Master of the Daoist Sect, among the most powerful beings in the Heavenly World. In the Majestic World he is limited to the power of a thirteenth-tier Qi refiner.
- **Divination and calculation:** A master fortune teller who weaves plans across centuries and continents.
- **Mystical abilities:** He can create clones, drag others into small worlds, travel instantly across vast distances, and "scoop" people to his side from afar.
- **Name:** Even writing or speaking his name draws the attention of the world's most powerful beings.

## Trivia

- According to rumour, Lu Chen captured a male oriole, while the founder of Syncretism raised the female.
- His name, 陆沉, is a phrase meaning "a continent sinking".
- He haggles with a fellow fortune teller over the price of advice and pays with a real piece of silver.
- Zhi Gui complains that he was "quite unkind" to give Long Eyebrows a pagoda without explaining its downsides.
