# Emerald Lake

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Emerald_Lake)

---
The Emerald Lake is a significant mystical realm with unique origins, notable features, and connections to a larger narrative. It serves as a location for travel, exploration, and interactions among diverse characters, while also hinting at deeper spiritual and cultivation aspects tied to its history and unique characteristics. The lake's beauty and the lore surrounding its origins make it a memorable location within the overall story.

**General Characteristics:**

- The Emerald Lake is a **mystical realm** that is a remnant of the **Hanging Flower Blessed Land**. The Hanging Flower Blessed Land was destroyed due to a backlash from the Heavenly Dao, scattering its remains across the world.
- It is described as a place with a **vast and rippling surface** where mists rise.
- The lake is approximately **three hundred kilometers across**.
- It is known for containing **renowned spring water** that was collected by a female immortal when she was still alive. This water is said to be ideal for brewing tea, particularly if one can identify and draw from the subtle veins that contain its essence.

**Features and Structures:**

- There are around **one hundred pagodas floating above the lake**, each connected to others via small paths. These pagodas serve as residences and locations for various activities.
- Each pagoda is provided with **two or three small boats**, allowing passengers to explore the lake and enjoy the scenery.
- There are areas with **large patches of lotus flowers**, where young women row boats and harvest lotus seeds, humming tunes as they work.
- The **Lingering Shade Mountain Pagoda** is a notable structure with covered walkways enclosed by white jade railings and a small dock with two small boats.
- There are also shops run by the intercontinental ship within some of the pagodas where passengers can purchase goods.

**Significance and Lore:**

- The lake's origin as a remnant of the Hanging Flower Blessed Land gives it a unique mystical quality.
- The **female immortal** associated with the Hanging Flower Blessed Land and the spring water adds a layer of lore and importance to the lake.
- It is considered a place where one can find fated opportunities and treasures, and those with a discerning eye may find pleasant surprises.
- The quality of the water from a particular well in the area is considered unusually good, despite well water generally being considered unsuitable for tea.

**Travel and Commerce:**

- The Emerald Lake is accessible via a **ferry station** located within the **Treasure Swallowing Whale**.
- Passengers traveling on the Treasure Swallowing Whale can use high-grade land-shortening talismans in the form of embroidered brocade rugs to quickly reach their destinations, such as the Emerald Lake.
- It is a popular destination for those seeking fated opportunities or a place to reside while traveling.
- There is an intercontinental ship that makes stops in the Emerald Lake and has various shops to sell goods to travelers.
- There are small boats that people can use to explore the lake and enjoy the scenery.

**Interactions and Encounters:**

- Characters such as Chen Ping'an, and Lu Tai, visit the Emerald Lake, each with their own goals.
- Lu Tai, an otherworldly immortal, demonstrates his ability to navigate the area and is seen acquiring boxes of makeup while there.
- The lake serves as a location for interactions between travelers and those who live in the mystical realm, where they engage in conversations about life and their perception of the world.

**Spiritual and Cultivation Aspects:**

- The spring water is of high quality and has a special essence to it, making it valuable for those who are particular about tea.
- The lake's water may have unique properties due to its link with the Hanging Flower Blessed Land.
