# Stalactite Mountain

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Stalactite_Mountain)

---
**Stalactite Mountain** is a hovering mountain in the sea off Southern Whirl Continent, made from the largest mountain seal in the world. It belongs to the lineage of the Dao Ancestor's second disciple and stands on the border between the Majestic World and the Savage World, serving as the gateway to the [Sword Qi Great Wall](Sword_Qi_Great_Wall.md). [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md) first came there to deliver a sword to [Ning Yao](Ning_Yao.md).

## Geography

The mountain began as a small, finely made seal. Once it came down from the sky, it grew into a towering mountain.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> It hangs upside down above the sea, its peak pointing toward the earth and its base toward the sky.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> It measures about fifty kilometers in radius, but as a lone mountain in the sea it controls a wide region around it. Ships cannot fly straight to it. Even those that can fly have to come down to the sea and follow one of the paths of cloud and water that hang in the air between the mountain and the sea.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> Southern Whirl Continent is the closest continent, and several islands nearby are home to righteous cultivators who keep watch on the criminals hiding on the mountain.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

The Daoist lineage owns nine structures on the mountain, and the rest of the land, buildings and shops have been sold to people from all over the world. Eight of the nine sit on eight different sides of the mountain. They are the Catch and Release Pavilion, the Sword Reverence Pagoda, the Incense Offering Tower, the Lightning Lake Platform, Ganoderma Inn, Milu Cliff, the Daoist Seal Hall and the Daoist Saber Room. Lone Peak, a restricted area at the center, holds the tallest building on the mountain, and the gate to the Sword Qi Great Wall stands at its foot.

The four great private residences on the mountain are the Ape Havoc Estate of the Liu Clan of Pure White Continent, the Spring Banner Inn, the Plum Blossom Garden and the Water Essence Palace, which belongs to the Rain Dragon Sect.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

## History

The second disciple of the Dao Ancestor, True Invincible, made the mountain and drove it into the Majestic World like a nail. [Wei Bo](Wei_Bo.md) describes it as indestructible and as the first defensive pass between the two worlds, and perhaps the last.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> Confucian sages take it as an insult, and the Daoist branch master even made the Qi refiners passing through to the [Sword Qi Great Wall](Sword_Qi_Great_Wall.md) sign a "mountain alliance". It is also treated as forbidden ground for Confucian disciples, so much of the news from beyond it never reaches the rest of the world.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup>

The Catch and Release Pavilion is named for an event from the mountain's early days. A twelfth tier great demon slipped past the Sword Qi Great Wall to the newly placed mountain seal, expecting it to be empty, but the first person it met was the branch master himself. The mountain once came close to destruction when a sword spirit split it with a single slash. Only the Second Sage stepping in stopped it from collapsing.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

When the war at the Sword Qi Great Wall grew worse, the Great Heavenly Lord of Lone Peak worked with two sword immortals to open a second gate to the wall for traders. After a serious incident that the mountain kept secret, it brought in a curfew and checked the badge of every cultivator on the mountain in a single day.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

## Administration

The Great Heavenly Lord who lives on Lone Peak is a direct disciple of the second disciple and is called the embodiment of the mountain's rules. His junior, a young-looking Daoist priest from White Jade Capital, is second in command. Lu Chen tricked him into coming, and after a quarrel with his senior brother he left all affairs to the Great Heavenly Lord and simply guarded the gate.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> The third in command is the Flood Dragon True Lord, the Great Heavenly Lord's first disciple, famed for slaying flood dragons in the South Sea. A sword-hugging man from the Sword Qi Great Wall also guards the gate as punishment for losing a battle to a young great demon.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup>

The first rule of the mountain is that anyone who injures another person dies. The Daoist saber priests will pursue a case even decades later, which makes the mountain unusually peaceful. Because the rule also protects wanted men, many evil cultivators flee there to escape pursuit and sell their spoils. Stalactite Mountain does not regard itself as part of the Majestic World and observes the fasting days of the Heavenly World.

Passage to the Sword Qi Great Wall is closely controlled. Visitors are given time slots at the gate and are turned away if they miss them. The Sword Qi Great Wall forbids its own cultivators below the Golden Core Tier from going to the mountain to cultivate, so Ning Yao was criticized for sneaking there as a girl.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup>

## Trade

Swordsmen from across the world are summoned to the mountain whenever the demon tribe goes to war, and merchants gather there to sell swords and supplies to the wall at steep prices. Intercontinental ships from eight continents trade there. The first ever to arrive was the Water Pillow of Southern Whirl Continent, and the early ships carried goods to the wall with no thought of profit. 

## Trivia

- [Qi Jinglong](Qi_Jinglong.md) saw that the mountain's eight attractions are the eyes of a formation, and that the mountain is a celestial weapon with both offensive and defensive power, not just a seal.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup> Later the narration states that the eight attractions and Lone Peak form an ancient formation from the Master of Three Mountains and Nine Lords, something close to an ancient ascension platform.
- The wine shop that sells Forgetting Sorrow Wine is the last surviving mystical realm of the Golden Millet Blessed Land. Three Pure Incense is a local specialty.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup>
- People born and raised there tend to be well informed and speak with confidence about sages and heavenly lords alike.
- Fewer than one in ten people there understand the official dialect of Eastern Treasured Vial Continent.
- The Daoist Seal Hall displays every kind of seal except mountain seals, since the mountain is one itself. This is why it is also called the Missing One Hall.
- The Daoist Saber Room once put up a bounty for Song Changjing, on the grounds that Eastern Treasured Vial Continent did not deserve an eleventh tier martial artist.
- It was said that if the Sword Qi Great Wall fell, the second disciple would take the mountain back to the Heavenly World.
