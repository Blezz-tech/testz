**Qi Jingchun**, known to the people of the small town simply as **Mr. Qi**, was the schoolteacher of the town in [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md) and a disciple of the [Scholarly Sage](Scholarly_Sage.md). Outside the town he was the mountain master of Mountain Cliff Academy and a Confucian sage of the Upper Five Tiers. He spent his last years keeping the town safe and died holding off the powers that came to break it.

## Appearance

Qi Jingchun is a middle-aged scholar with graying sideburns.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> He dresses plainly in a scholar's robe, often an azure one, and keeps his hair in a bun held by an ordinary green jade hairpin that once belonged to his teacher. People in town found him quiet and a little dull, and most of the time he carries a calm, faint smile.

In the days before his death his hair went gray very quickly, and by the end it had turned completely white.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> When his soul leaves his body, it takes the form of an immortal in flowing white robes.

## Personality

Qi Jingchun is gentle, patient and very hard to provoke. Bai Yuan, the Mountain-Moving Ape, insulted and humiliated him again and again, and he simply let it pass. He would rather explain than argue, and he treats the poorest boy in Clay Vase Alley with the same courtesy he shows visiting nobles. He once told [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) that he, too, had started out as a poor village youth who made up his mind to read.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

Under that softness sits a will that does not bend. When [Zhi Gui](Zhi_Gui.md) tested him, he made her kowtow three times. When the four great powers came for their treasures and his enemies closed in, he could have saved himself by giving up the town, but he chose to stay. He is also far sharper than he lets on. His teacher said he beat the Scholarly Sage himself at Go not long after learning the game, and he saw through the trap laid for him well before it was sprung.

He does not judge people by where they come from. Having traveled widely with his senior brother [Zuo You](Zuo_You.md), he knew there were hypocrites among scholars and admirable people among demons, and he treated the great demon Liu Chicheng with a fairness that surprised even Liu himself.

## Background

### Mountain Cliff Academy

Qi Jingchun was one of the Scholarly Sage's disciples, alongside [Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md) and [Zuo You](Zuo_You.md). Before sending him to Mountain Cliff Academy, his teacher told him that one must never stop learning and that the student should surpass the teacher, which showed how much hope the old man placed in him.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> He rose to become the mountain master of the academy, one of the three schools and seventy-two academies of Confucianism.

Confucian sages normally have one kindred character, a word that adds a sliver to the sage's cultivation every time someone writes or speaks it. Qi Jingchun had two: "Tranquil" and "Spring". Their weight was so great that he was banished to the sealed small world of the town because of them.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

### The small town

Qi Jingchun guarded the town for 59 years.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> He worked as its schoolteacher the whole time, and the townsfolk never realized that every teacher who had come and gone over the years was the same man.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> A regal jade tablet, left by one of the Second Sages, sat on his desk and anchored the formation over the town. While it remained, he could see everything that happened inside the small world.
When a secret letter told him his teacher had been humiliated and had "passed away", he wept alone at his desk and blamed himself for doing nothing while he clung to his post.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> The Scholarly Sage was in fact still alive.

During the final months he quietly arranged a future for the town's children. He took Chen Ping'an to the old locust tree and asked it for a leaf carrying the character "Yao". Later he gave the boy his jade hairpin as a farewell gift, along with four seals. He carved a spring seal for [Zhao Yao](Zhao_Yao.md), his book boy, and left six books for [Song Jixin](Song_Jixin.md). When Chen Ping'an struck down [Cai Jinjian](Cai_Jinjian.md) in an alley and nearly killed [Fu Nanhua](Fu_Nanhua.md), Qi Jingchun stepped in. He sent Fu Nanhua away from the small world with a gift of opportunity, and he brought Cai Jinjian back to life, then sat with her in the school and talked about everyday things like the price of rice and oil before answering questions about her cultivation that even her master could not.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

He also arranged an excursion to Mountain Cliff Academy for his last five students, the trip the old janitor [Ma Zhan](Ma_Zhan.md) later led in his place. Ma Zhan, who counted as half a disciple of the same teacher, betrayed him, and the Scholarly Sage later said this hurt Little Qi more than all of Cui Chan's schemes.
