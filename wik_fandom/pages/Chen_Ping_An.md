**Chen Ping'an** is the protagonist of *Sword of Coming*. An orphan from Clay Vase Alley in the small town of Jewel Small World, he becomes the closing disciple of the [Scholarly Sage](Scholarly_Sage.md), the mountain master of [Downtrodden Mountain](Downtrodden_Mountain.md), and later the Hidden Official of the [Sword Qi Great Wall](Sword_Qi_Great_Wall.md).

## Appearance

As a boy, Chen Ping'an is thin and looks frail, though years of hard labour have made him far stronger than he appears.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> In the town he wears straw sandals that he weaves himself.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> After losing the hairpin Qi Jingchun gave him, he bought a plain jade hairpin and carved the same eight-character phrase onto it himself, and he wears that one in its place.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

## Personality

Chen Ping'an is careful, patient and steady beyond his years. He grew up poor and learned early that nobody owes anyone kindness, so he remembers every favour done for him and tries to repay it.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> He is frugal to the point that friends call him a miser, yet he gives freely to people he cares about.

He thinks before acting and tries to see situations from every side. During his journey with [A'Liang](A'Liang.md), he constantly second-guessed himself and tried to read what the swordsman wanted of him. He later recognised this as one of the hardest tests he ever faced.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup> He keeps the principles he learns from Qi Jingchun, A'Liang, Old Man Yao and others, and holds himself to them.

## Background

Chen Ping'an's parents died when he was very young, leaving him alone in the family home on [Clay Vase Alley](Small_Town.md). He worked as an apprentice at the dragon kilns under [Old Man Yao](Old_Man_Yao.md). His father had shattered his [bonded porcelain](Natal_Porcelain.md) before he turned six. This cut him off from the fated opportunities other town children had, but it also freed him from the ties that came with them.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> He met [Ning Yao](Ning_Yao.md) after being brought to his house due to her injuries, and the two grew close.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> Before leaving the town, [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md) gave him a green jade hairpin as a parting gift. The hairpin came from Qi Jingchun's own teacher.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

With the wealth he gained in the town, he bought five mountains from Ruan Qiong's map, including [Downtrodden Mountain](Downtrodden_Mountain.md), which later became the seat of his own mountain.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup> At fourteen, he set out to escort Li Baoping, Li Huai and Lin Shouyi to Mountain Cliff Academy, travelling part of the way with A'Liang.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> Back in his home region, an old man who called himself Cui Chan's grandfather put him through brutal training in fist techniques.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> The old man was later revealed to be Cui Cheng, a former head of the Cui Clan. During his travels Chen Ping'an took Pei Qian as his opening disciple.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup>

## Trivia

- He was born on the fifth day of the fifth month. The townsfolk believed it was the unluckiest day of the year, when the five poisonous creatures come out, and some neighbours were wary of him because of it.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup>
- "Ping'an" means safe and sound, and can also mean peace.
- As a small child he went to the Yang family medicine shop again and again to buy medicine for his sick mother, often without enough money. Old Man Yang took pity on him and gave him a cheap recipe, but his mother died that winter.
- The first time he went into the mountains to collect herbs, the basket was nearly as big as he was. His shoulders hurt so much that he dropped it and cried at the foot of the mountain.
- Old Man Yao never formally accepted him as a disciple because of his mediocre talent for porcelain. Liu Xianyang was the master's favourite instead.
- He spent 100 taels of silver on four jade hairpins on his first journey. He kept one for himself and carved the other three for Li Baoping, Lin Shouyi and Li Huai, writing "Huaiyin" (locust shade) on Li Huai's so that it would have two characters like the others.
- He only began practising fist techniques at around fourteen.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup>
- When he returned to Mountain Cliff Academy, he had become the Scholarly Sage's closing disciple and the junior brother of Qi Jingchun and Zuo You.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup> Mao Xiaodong greeted him as the junior brother he had long been waiting for.
- He once hid his identity under the name "Chen Haoren", which means "good person".
- At the Sword Qi Great Wall, people called him the Ning Residence's son-in-law.
