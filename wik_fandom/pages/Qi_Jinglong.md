## Appearance

Qi Jinglong is an elegant and refined young man who dresses in Confucian robes rather than the garb of an ordinary sword cultivator, and he carries no visible sword when traveling outside the mountains, relying instead on a single bonded flying sword and illusion techniques to disguise his true cultivation. *(Chapter 523)* *(Chapter 524)*

## Personality

He speaks with calm conviction and genuinely enjoys debating principles and philosophy rather than showing off his strength, to the point that Chen Ping’an felt he could easily have passed for a Confucian scholar even though he isn’t one. *(Chapter 523)* He dislikes drinking wine and considers persuading others to drink a mark against his own character, though he can be talked into a sip or two. *(Chapter 523)* As a teacher he is patient and even-tempered, never holding his disciple’s shortcomings against him and preferring to let him compare himself against his own past self rather than against others. *(Chapter 596)* He keeps his word without fail, a trait his disciple grudgingly admits is his one unambiguously good quality, and he genuinely dislikes being too narrow-minded or small about money and favors. *(Chapter 596)*

## Background

Qi Jinglong’s name in the genealogical records of the Great Emblem Sword Sect’s ancestral hall is actually Liu Jinglong, a matter he considers a private clan affair he prefers not to explain to outsiders. Known as the Terrestrial Flood Dragon of the Northern Complete Reed Continent, he was not born with innate sword talent and his own Dao mentor was only a middling Dragon Gate Tier sword cultivator whose Great Dao eventually decayed with age. His true Dao guardian was instead an older senior sister who had been stuck at the peak of the Golden Core Tier since before Qi Jinglong even entered the mountains, and whom he personally helped save from a crisis that nearly drove her to demonic cultivation, suffering severe wounds of his own in the process. Despite the odds against him, the sect took the unusual step of naming him a direct disciple of its ancestral hall once he reached the Abode Tier, a decision that was eventually proven correct many times over. *(Chapter 526)*

Qi Jinglong first crossed paths with Chen Ping’an and his disciple Sui Jingcheng by a riverside pavilion in the Northern Complete Reed Continent, where the two debated at length over the nature of strength, rules, and whether might could ever truly be called right. Chen Ping’an came away deeply impressed, bowing to him with full Confucian courtesy even though Qi Jinglong held no formal standing in any Confucian school, and privately hoping that Cao Qinglang might grow up to think in a similarly balanced way. *(Chapter 523)* *(Chapter 524)* At the time he held Nascent Tier cultivation and was ranked third among the Northern Complete Reed Continent’s top ten young cultivators, a tier below the continent’s top two prodigies and well ahead of peers such as Gu Mo and Rong Chang. *(Chapter 524)* *(Chapter 526)*

When Deer Cutter Mountain made repeated attempts on Chen Ping’an’s life, Qi Jinglong intervened and negotiated a deal with the mountain’s master, agreeing that the master’s young assassin apprentice would become his own disciple if a final assassination attempt failed. The boy, later known as Bai Shou, did fail and was bound by the agreement, cursing his new master even as he reluctantly accepted him, in no small part because his old master had clearly wanted this outcome for him all along. *(Chapter 536)*

Qi Jinglong later solidified his standing as a sword immortal of the Northern Complete Reed Continent through three consecutive sword duels forced upon him by other cultivators, including one against Sword Immortal Li Cai of Duckweed Sword Lake, emerging from the battles with his Sword Dao measurably strengthened. The Great Emblem Sword Sect’s sect leader, Han Huaizi, openly spoke of wanting to help him ascend even further, hoping Qi Jinglong might one day become the sect’s first Ascension Tier sword immortal. *(Chapter 582)*

He later traveled with [Bai Shou](Bai_Shou.md) to Stalactite Mountain aboard Osmanthus Island, posing as an ordinary traveling scholar and his attendant, quietly fending off at least two groups secretly tailing them without ever revealing his full strength or raising concern. During the trip he secured a Sword Nurturing Gourd for Bai Shou as a gift befitting a new direct disciple of the sect’s ancestral hall. *(Chapter 595)* *(Chapter 596)*

## Trivia

- Despite being a renowned sword immortal, he is afraid of ghostly apparitions in water, having once gone pale with fright after a corpse-like face surfaced from a clump of river grass. *(Chapter 593)*
- He purchased a replica sword called Lightning Devourer from Hatred Sword Mountain for Bai Shou. *(Chapter 571)*
- Though he appears young, cultivators who reach his level live far longer than ordinary mortals, and he jokingly told Sui Jingcheng his true age would put him in his eighties by mortal reckoning. *(Chapter 523)*
