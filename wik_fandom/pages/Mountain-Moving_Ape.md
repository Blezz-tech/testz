**Bai Yuan**, known throughout Eastern Treasured Vial Continent as the **Mountain-Moving Ape**, is a great demon of [Sun Scorch Mountain](Sects.md#sun-scorch-mountain) and the devoted guardian of [Tao Zi](Tao_Zi.md), the granddaughter of the sect's Pure Yang Sword Patriarch. He spent over three thousand years as a guest elder of Peace and Tranquility Mountain on Parasol Leaf Continent, guarding its well prison, before betraying the sect during a great calamity. He is one of the most powerful beings Chen Ping'an encounters in the small town of [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md).

## Appearance

Bai Yuan is a white ape as tall as a grown man. He chooses to maintain his original ape form rather than taking on human appearance, and is almost always seen in black robes with a sword carried across his back. Despite his size he moves with startling speed, capable of changing direction mid-leap and clearing rooftops in a few strides.

His true form is an ape 3,000 meters tall, said to be capable of uprooting a mountain and carrying it on its back, though whether this has ever actually been witnessed is disputed. *(Chapter 45)*

## Personality

Bai Yuan is blunt, proud and quick to anger. He makes no effort to conceal his contempt for those he considers beneath him and will insult powerful figures to their faces without hesitation. He provoked Qi Jingchun at length, calling out his master's disgraced legacy and daring him to retaliate, and was visibly unsatisfied when Qi Jingchun declined to rise to the bait. *(Chapter 63 (1))*

He is also capable of loyalty and real devotion. His bond with Tao Zi is genuine as he kneels before her in shame when he fails her, speaks to her gently, and explains things he technically should not be telling her because he feels she deserves to know. *(Chapter 45)* Tao Zi calls him "Grandpa Yuan" and trusts him completely.

Despite his ferocity, he has enough self-control to walk away from a fight when the stakes demand it. He knew Light Breeze City was using him to kill Liu Xianyang and understood the trap, yet went in anyway because Sun Scorch Mountain's interests required it — and then accepted the consequences. *(Chapter 45)*

## Background

Bai Yuan is the guardian of Sun Scorch Mountain and deeply invested in its centuries-old war with Wind Lightning Field. The root of that war is a female ancestor of Sun Scorch Mountain who challenged the master of Wind Lightning Field, died fairly in battle, and was never returned to the sect. Her body still lies on Wind Lightning Field's sword sparring arena with a sword driven through her skull, a humiliation that has shaped Sun Scorch Mountain for 300 years. Every sword immortal the sect has produced since has felt that weight. *(Chapter 45)*

He served Peace and Tranquility Mountain on Parasol Leaf Continent for more than 3,000 years as a guest elder and warden of its well prison. In recognition of his unparalleled service, the sect leader of that era awarded him one of the sect's four ancient swords — a rare honor over which many objected. *(Chapter 350 (2))* He was in seclusion attempting to break from the Unpolished Jade Tier to the Immortal Tier when a calamity struck Peace and Tranquility Mountain. He exited seclusion early, having already broken through, and turned against the sect. *(Chapter 350 (1))*

## Plot

### The Small Town

Bai Yuan arrived in [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md) accompanying Tao Zi, sent to retrieve a sword scripture that the traitor who defected to Wind Lightning Field had originally stolen from Sun Scorch Mountain. Light Breeze City had manipulated events to engineer a confrontation between Bai Yuan and Liu Xianyang, knowing Bai Yuan would be the one to bear the consequences. Bai Yuan understood the trap and jumped in regardless, striking Liu Xianyang down and nearly killing him. *(Chapter 45)*

Chen Ping'an, who had watched Liu Xianyang grow up with him, resolved to respond. He climbed to a rooftop above Clay Vase Alley and shot an arrow at Bai Yuan's eye from point-blank range. The arrow pierced Bai Yuan's palm when he raised it to block, drawing blood and leaving the old ape shaken despite the trivial nature of the wound. A long rooftop chase followed, with Chen Ping'an luring Bai Yuan onto a derelict house whose roof collapsed under the ape's weight. *(Chapter 48)*

Bai Yuan eventually unleashed his true form (a 3,000-meter ape) in an attempt to carry a mountain out of the small world. Qi Jingchun intervened. With a single stomp, he drove Bai Yuan into the earth. A second stomp sent him deeper. Bai Yuan surrendered and left, though not before raising a thumb toward Qi Jingchun's back as he walked away.

### Peace and Tranquility Mountain

During the calamity that struck Peace and Tranquility Mountain, Bai Yuan emerged from seclusion having already attained the Immortal Tier. He used the ancient sword the sect had awarded him to bring the mountain-protection formation to a temporary halt, then used it to kill Zhong Kui, a noble scholar of Great Subduing Academy at the Nascent Tier, who had come to defend the mountain. Bai Yuan cut him in two at the waist and noted, without apparent emotion, that it was "a heroic and righteous death." *(Chapter 350 (1))*

## Trivia

- Tao Zi had hoped Bai Yuan would be able to carry a mountain out of [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md) as a gift to her as he had apparently done this before, bringing a mountain all the way to Sun Scorch Mountain as a private estate for her. *(Chapter 48)*
- Liu Baqiao of Wind Lightning Field said he was not afraid of "an old monkey" from Sun Scorch Mountain, but those who witnessed Bai Yuan knew better. *(Chapter 45)*
- Ning Yao, who clashed with Bai Yuan outside the town, described him as the most powerful of the outsiders who had entered the small world at that time, this was more dangerous to her than even the eunuch from Great Sui who had injured her on arrival. *(Chapter 46)*
- He was not originally from Peace and Tranquility Mountain, and many within the sect resented the ancient sword being given to an outsider demon. *(Chapter 350 (2))*
