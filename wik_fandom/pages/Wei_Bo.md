**Wei Bo** is a mountain deity. Once the formal god of the Divine Water Nation's Northern Mountain, he was demoted to the lowly mountain lord of Go Table Mountain after the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md) conquered his nation. After a fateful encounter with [A'Liang](A'Liang.md) and [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md), he rose to become the formal god of the Great Li Empire's Northern Mountain, seated on Cloud Drape Mountain beside the small town. He later became the first mountain god in the history of [Eastern Treasured Vial Continent](Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent.md) to reach the Upper Five Tiers.

Handsome, ethereal and seemingly carefree, Wei Bo is one of Chen Ping'an's most dependable friends and allies. He is a frequent visitor to [Downtrodden Mountain](Downtrodden_Mountain.md), and Chen Ping'an's partner in Ox Horn Mountain.

## Appearance

Wei Bo's true form is a strikingly handsome young man, sometimes described as more beautiful than a woman, in white robes with large flowing sleeves that billow like clouds. He wears a pale gold earring with illusion powers and, as mountain lord, carried a green bamboo cane signifying his status. When first encountered, he hides behind the form of a tiny, white-haired, dishevelled old man who barely reaches Zhu He's waist. Even [Zhu Lu](Zhu_Lu.md) admitted he was the living embodiment of "spirited and handsome", and others say that only [Cui Dongshan](Cui_Dongshan.md), Lu Tai and [Cao Ci](Cao_Ci.md) can compare with him in looks.

## Personality

At Go Table Mountain, Wei Bo appears cowardly and obsequious, grovelling before A'Liang and scheming to survive the two pythons that bullied him for centuries. Once restored to power, his true personality emerges: relaxed, witty, generous and extremely sociable. He remembers everyone's name and background, and is renowned for being easy to approach despite his status. He describes himself as easily persuaded by reason but impossible to cow by force, and as someone who will refuse to do anything if he can be idle.

Beneath his charm, he is shrewd and politically astute. The little boy in azure accuses him of "hiding blades behind his smiles". He keeps a careful balance between his loyalty to the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md) and his friendship with Chen Ping'an, and will firmly discipline subordinates and rivals when needed. He reveres A'Liang deeply, and is said to carry a long-held, quiet devotion to a woman from his past.

## Background

Wei Bo was made the formal god of the Northern Mountain by the final emperor of the Divine Water Nation. When the Great Li Empire conquered the nation, he secretly protected refugees fleeing the war, including the ancestors of the people of Red Candle Town, who still tell stories praising him.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> This enraged the founding emperor of the Great Li Empire. Wei Bo's golden statue was shattered and thrown into the water, and someone painstakingly pieced his body back together. He was demoted to a mere mountain lord of Go Table Mountain, with his territory cut by 150 kilometres in radius.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

Records in the Great Li Empire's Ministry of Rites say that he had delayed forming his godly body for many years for the sake of a certain woman, and that he refused to let her go.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> He feels guilt towards the [Liu Clan](Clans.md) of the Divine Water Nation. He is also the last surviving deity of that nation, and secretly kept 15,000 litres of its spring water before it fell.

## Plot

### Go Table Mountain

When [Zhu He](Zhu_He.md) accidentally summons him with a Soil Molding Technique, the "old" mountain lord appears and furiously beats Zhu He's calf with his cane. He reveals that he has spent centuries hiding from a black python and a white python that rule his mountain, and as the black python closes in, he even urges it to eat the children instead.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> A'Liang then appears beside him unnoticed and runs him through with his bamboo saber. Being a deity, Wei Bo is unharmed, but he is struck by lightning when he tries to flee.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

Wei Bo then reveals his true, handsome form and fawns on [A'Liang](A'Liang.md), whom he calls "great immortal". A'Liang kills the white python and makes Wei Bo and the black python pay a "farewell gift" from their hoard: a yellow ebony box full of treasures, from which Li Huai, Li Baoping, Lin Shouyi, Zhu He and Zhu Lu each pick an item. Chen Ping'an takes a withered golden lotus seed. A'Liang also cuts down a great swathe of Wei Bo's precious courage bamboo, descended from the famed bamboo of the Bamboo Sea Small World's Azure Divine Mountain. Wei Bo agrees to build a bamboo building for Chen Ping'an on Downtrodden Mountain, make A'Liang a new bamboo saber, and move the black python there.

After A'Liang's departure, Wei Bo saves the life of the Great Li cultivator Liu Yu, whom A'Liang had struck down. He is also approached by Xu Ruo, a Mohist swordsman serving the Great Li Empire. Wei Bo trades valuable information about the newly promoted river goddess of the Iron Talisman River, [Yang Hua](Yang_Hua.md), asking in return only for a smoother path to immortality for a certain new disciple of the Palace of Eternal Spring.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> The Great Li Empire then offers him the position of god of its new Northern Mountain, which he accepts, joking that surviving A'Liang's saber surely qualifies him.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup>

### Northern Mountain God

When Chen Ping'an returns from Great Sui, Wei Bo greets him as his new neighbour on Cloud Drape Mountain and shows him the finished bamboo building.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> He deliberately takes Chen Ping'an on a tour of the surrounding mountains, including the ferry station on Parasol Tree Mountain and Ox Horn Mountain's Cloth Wrapper Shop. This is a clear signal to every immortal sect in the area that Chen Ping'an is under the protection of the new Northern Mountain God.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

He brings Chen Ping'an medicine worth tens of thousands of taels of silver during Cui Cheng's brutal training, finds him the Sword Nurturing Gourd "Jiang Hu", carves locust wood scabbards for his swords, and tells him stories of the Sword Qi Great Wall and A'Liang's legendary battles. Wei Bo comes to understand that the strike from A'Liang's bamboo saber at Go Table Mountain greatly strengthened his godly body, allowing him to govern the thirty-odd mountains of the region with ease. Looking south, he quietly wishes a certain someone a good life, adding that she should never fall for a scholar again.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup>

He plants Chen Ping'an's golden lotus seed in a pond behind the bamboo building, filled with water from the fallen Divine Water Nation. The seed is revealed to be a relic of the Daoist [Lu Chen](Lu_Chen.md) that keeps the nation's remnant fortune alive. Lu Chen himself visits and pins Wei Bo helplessly to the ground. Wei Bo's relationship with Downtrodden Mountain's own mountain god, the loyal Great Li official Song Yuzhang, gradually sours.

While Chen Ping'an is away, Wei Bo writes him detailed secret letters, sending him two secret techniques from the Divine Water Nation's imperial clan and armoury. He also brokers his purchases of mountains in Dragon Spring Prefecture and oversees the pond's growing lotus spirit.

### Partnership with Downtrodden Mountain

When Chen Ping'an returns from Bamboo Scroll Lake, Wei Bo becomes the go-between for his mountain purchases. Ox Horn Mountain and its immortal ferry station are placed under Wei Bo's name, and the two privately agree to split the profits evenly. Chen Ping'an lends him a valuable jade tablet for a hundred years in exchange for courage bamboo, and gives him the larger of two colored glaze golden body fragments of the Ascension Tier cultivator Du Mao. Such fragments are priceless, and even more valuable to deities than to cultivators.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> Wei Bo later quietly confirms that he can refine it within ten years.

Wei Bo witnesses Chen Ping'an's land contract with the Great Li Empire at Forest Deer Academy and hosts his first Night Travel Banquet for the deities of his territory. When the river goddess Yang Hua tries to provoke Chen Ping'an, Wei Bo stops her. On a walk along the river, he calmly lectures her on respect and then strips her temple's incense essence and river fortune to make his point.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup> That same night, he helps Chen Ping'an with a crisis of conscience, telling him that "one's heart must contain both a sun and a moon".

Wei Bo becomes close friends with [Zhu Lian](Zhu_Lian.md), whom he regrets not having met sooner, and with [Zheng Dafeng](Zheng_Dafeng.md). The three play Go together often and jointly run Downtrodden Mountain's affairs during Chen Ping'an's absence. After Downtrodden Mountain acquires part of the shattered Lotus Flower Blessed Land, they negotiate how to fund raising it to a medium-grade blessed land. Wei Bo hosts three Night Travel Banquets in quick succession and opens a Cloth Wrapper Shop branch to raise immortal coins, taking only a 10% share.

### Upper Five Tiers

Wei Bo enters seclusion, guarded by Xu Ruo, and breaks through. Ruan Qiong observes that the barrier he should have faced had been shattered long ago in a very subtle way, so the challenge for Wei Bo was not breaking through, but reaching a more flawless Unpolished Jade Tier.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup> His breakthrough fills his territory with auspicious phenomena and makes him the first Upper Five Tier mountain god in the history of the continent. The new emperor, Song He, personally climbs Cloud Drape Mountain with two head ministers to congratulate him and gives him a pseudo-celestial tool that lets him command the water fortune of his territory.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup>

Wei Bo then travels to the new Central Mountain to confront its god, Jin Qing, who had neglected to show proper respect to the Northern Mountain. When Jin Qing defies him, their Dao Manifestations clash on the Central Mountain's summit. Wei Bo's manifestation, over five hundred feet taller even on enemy ground, forces Jin Qing's to its knees. Wei Bo tells him to visit Cloud Drape Mountain, with generous gifts.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup> He later explains that he intervened because Jin Qing is the only Central Mountain candidate he can stand. He then visits Xu Ruo, telling him he brought no gift because the favour he owes him is too great to repay.

Wei Bo welcomes Chen Ping'an home, takes him into the Lotus Root Blessed Land, keeps Pei Qian hidden from prying eyes after her rapid martial breakthroughs, and helps choose the site for Downtrodden Mountain's ancestral hall. He privately warns Chen Ping'an to stay true to himself now that he rules a blessed land of forty million people. At the hall's founding, Wei Bo leaves first, implicitly granting Chen Ping'an authority over decisions concerning Cloud Drape Mountain.

## Abilities

- **Mountain god powers:** As Northern Mountain God, he can sense everything within his territory, transport people instantly across it, summon the black python, manipulate mountain mist, and channel the fortune of all mountains and rivers under his jurisdiction.
- **Cultivation:** He reached the Unpolished Jade Tier, the first mountain god of Eastern Treasured Vial Continent to enter the Upper Five Tiers. His godly body was greatly strengthened by A'Liang's saber strike.
- **Dao Manifestation:** He overpowered the Central Mountain God's manifestation on the latter's own ground.
- **Illusions:** His pale gold earring hides his true form, and he can conceal treasures from cultivators below the tenth tier.
- **Knowledge:** He has encyclopaedic knowledge of treasures, the cultivation world, mountains and rivers, and politics. He is also a skilled Go player.

## Possessions

- **Pale gold earring**: an illusion treasure.
- **Green bamboo cane**: the symbol of his status as mountain lord.
- **Courage bamboo forest**: descended from the bamboo of Azure Divine Mountain.
- **Colored glaze golden body fragment**: from Du Mao, gifted by Chen Ping'an.
- **Jade tablet**: on loan from Chen Ping'an for a hundred years.
- **Pseudo-celestial tool**: gifted by Emperor Song He, granting control of water fortune.
- **Parasol leaf umbrella**: the entrance to the Lotus Root Blessed Land, held for Downtrodden Mountain.
- **Share of Ox Horn Mountain**: co-owned with Chen Ping'an.

## Trivia

- Despite his lofty status, he picked up a children's folk song about stinky tofu from Pei Qian.
- Others on Downtrodden Mountain joke that he is vain and easily offended, and they take care not to hurt his pride over money.
- In Go games among Wei Bo, Zhu Lian and Zheng Dafeng, whoever Zheng Dafeng helps always wins.
