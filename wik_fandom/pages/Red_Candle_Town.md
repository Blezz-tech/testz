# Red Candle Town

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Candle_Town)

---
Red Candle Town is a bustling and complex location within the narrative, serving as a significant point of transit and a microcosm of the larger world.

**Location and Geography:**

- **Three Rivers:** Red Candle Town is situated at the confluence of three rivers: **Rushing Tranquil River**, **Embroidered Flower River**, and **Fine Wine River**. This location makes it a busy hub for river traffic.
- **Near Go Table Mountain:** It's located near Go Table Mountain, and is close to the border of the mountain.
- **Border Town**: It is near the border of the Great Li Empire.
- **Former Jewel Small World:** Red Candle Town is located near where Jewel Small World landed after it fell.
- **South of Great Li Empire:** Red Candle Town is situated to the south of the Great Li Empire.

**Transportation and Trade:**

- **Busy Dock:** Red Candle Town has a busy dock with numerous ships coming and going. It serves as a major point of transit for goods from all corners of the Great Li Empire.
- **Relay Station:** The town features a relay station called the Pillow Relay Station, which serves as a place for travelers and officials to stay.
- **Official Post Road:** The town is located on one of the three main post roads leading from the southern regions of the Great Li Empire to the capital. This makes it a vital stop for merchants, officials, and scholars.
- **Escort Boats:** There are escort boats that are available for travel, and the town has many merchant ships.
- **Fu Inlet**: There is an inlet called the Fu Inlet where many decorative boats dock.

**Commerce and Entertainment:**

- **Street Market:** The town has a street market on the west side, offering a variety of goods and trinkets.
- **Night Market:** Red Candle Town is known for its lively night market.
- **Taverns and Wine Shops**: There are many wine shops and taverns in town.
- **Decorated Boats:** The town is known for its decorated boats, which are unique to the area. These boats offer entertainment and companionship.
- **Brothels:** The town has brothels with hostesses and prostitutes, some of whom are managed by the capital's Imperial Music Office.
- **Bookstores**: There are bookstores in Red Candle Town, including one known for its high prices and eccentric owner.
- **Variety of Goods**: Red Candle Town has a wide variety of goods available for sale, including medicinal ingredients, ointments, and more.

**Culture and Society:**

- **No Curfew:** Red Candle Town does not have a curfew.
- **Diverse Population:** The town's population includes merchants, officials, scholars, and people from various backgrounds.
- **Stories and Legends:** The town has its own local stories and legends.
- **Temples:** There are two temples, a Wenchang pavilion and a martial sage temple.

**Notable Features:**

- **Beacon Towers:** The town has beacon towers, that are used to signal messages.
- **River Gods:** Two river gods are worshipped in shrines along the riverbanks.
- **Fu Inlet:** A crescent-shaped river bend is where the decorated boats are found.
- **A Money Sink**: Fu Inlet is known to be a money sink due to the cost of the entertainment provided there.
- **Unique Local Products:** The town is known for its apricot blossom wine.

**Political and Historical Context:**

- **Former Jewel Small World:** It is near the location where the Jewel Small World fell, which gives it a unique historical context.
- **Great Li Empire:** Red Candle Town is part of the Great Li Empire, and the empire's influence is felt in the town’s administration and culture.
- **Imperial Music Office**: The Imperial Music Office manages some of the boat hostesses and prostitutes, indicating the town's connection to the empire's central government.
- **Mountain Lord Wei Bo**: A mountain lord of Go Table Mountain protected the ancestors of Red Candle Town's residents, but was later demoted for it.
- **Site of conflict:** Red Candle Town is the location of a conflict between a palace minister and a swordsman.

**Specific Individuals and Groups:**

- **Cheng Sheng:** The relay station deputy at the Pillow Relay Station, who assists travelers and is familiar with the town.
- **A’Liang:** He is familiar with Red Candle Town, particularly the area around the two river banks.
- **Li Jin**: A man who has lived in the town for two hundred years, and is familiar with the waterways and customs.
- **Old Man Yang**: A man who came to the town with a travel diary and is familiar with the history of the town.
- **Officials and Scholars:** Many officials and scholars pass through Red Candle Town on their way to the capital.

**Challenges and Problems:**

- **Corruption:** The possibility of corruption within the town's administration is implied.
- **Uneven Application of Law:**  The application of laws may vary depending on the type of officials, with scholars being more strict and martial artists being more lenient.
- **Potential for Trouble:** The presence of various types of people, including merchants, officials, and possibly unsavory characters, creates the potential for conflicts and problems.
- **Ghosts**: There are rumors of ghosts in Red Candle Town who have driven some people insane.

Red Candle Town is a vibrant and crucial location, serving as a nexus for trade, travel, and cultural exchange within the Great Li Empire. Its unique geographical position, combined with its bustling economy and diverse population, make it a significant setting in the narrative.
