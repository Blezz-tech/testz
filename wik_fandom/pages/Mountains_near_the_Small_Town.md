# Mountains near the Small Town

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Mountains_near_the_Small_Town)

---
> **Spoiler Warning:** This article contains spoilers from the *Sword of Coming* (*Jian Lai* / *Unsheathed*) novel and donghua.

The **mountains near the Small Town** are the peaks of the former [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md), which became Dragon Spring County and later Dragon Spring Prefecture after the small world fell to the ground. After the fall, the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md) put 61 of them, apart from Cloud Drape Mountain, up for sale.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> The smallest cost a single gold essence copper coin, and the largest, such as Divine Elegance Mountain and Incense Mountain, cost 25 to 30 coins each.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md) bought five of them, the beginning of [Downtrodden Mountain](Downtrodden_Mountain.md). 

### Dragon Head Mountain

Dragon Head Mountain is hidden by a formation that keeps mortal eyes from seeing it. From the small town, it can only be seen from the summit of Cloud Drape Mountain, and reaching it is counted as a fated opportunity.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

### Cloud Drape Mountain

Cloud Drape Mountain is the tallest mountain in the region. A Heavenly Master Seal is said to have been buried under it long ago to suppress the dragon qi of the small world.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> Cui Minghuang chose it as the site of a new academy, later named Forest Deer Academy, which was meant to become one of the 72 Confucian academies within fifty years.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

When the small world fell, the emperor wanted to break the rules and raise Cloud Drape Mountain to the Northern Mountain of the empire.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> The court first appointed formal mountain gods to Cloud Drape Mountain, Incense Mountain and Downtrodden Mountain.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> In the end, Wei Bo, once the mountain lord of Go Table Mountain, became the formal god of the Great Li Empire's Northern Mountain.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

### True Jewel Mountain

True Jewel Mountain is the smallest mountain the Great Li Empire offered, little more than a hill, and its price was a single Greeting Spring Coin. It was the first mountain Old Man Yao took Chen Ping'an to when they went into the mountains to search for soil. Chen Ping'an bought it and planned to keep it for himself for 300 years.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

### Divine Elegance Mountain, Lamp Bearer Mountain and Gallant Spear Mountain

[Ruan Qiong](Ruan_Qiong.md) asked the Great Li Empire for these three mountains as the site of his future sect.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> Lamp Bearer Mountain and Gallant Spear Mountain lie about ten kilometers west and north of Divine Elegance Mountain, and the three stand in a triangle like three giants.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> Bonus artifacts were attached to Divine Elegance Mountain, and Lamp Bearer Mountain was given a mountain god. Divine Elegance Mountain became the home of the Dragon Spring Sword Sect's ancestral hall.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup>

### Treasured Scripture Mountain, Rainbow Cloud Mountain and Immortal Herb Mountain

These three mountains lie within fifty kilometers of Ruan Qiong's mountains. Chen Ping'an bought them along with True Jewel Mountain and Downtrodden Mountain. Immortal Herb Mountain is barely larger than True Jewel Mountain, but it is lush and full of tall trees. Ruan Qiong said he would have chosen Treasured Scripture Mountain over Gallant Spear Mountain if not for the needs of his sect's protective formation. Before leaving the town, Chen Ping'an planned to leave Downtrodden Mountain, Treasured Scripture Mountain, Rainbow Cloud Mountain and Immortal Herb Mountain to Liu Xianyang, Gu Can, Ning Yao and Ruan Xiu if he never returned. He later lent the three mountains to Ruan Qiong's sect for 300 years.

### Lamp Flame Mountain and Yellow Lake Mountain

Lamp Flame Mountain and Yellow Lake Mountain are two of the five mountains near Ruan Qiong's peaks. Yellow Lake Mountain has a small lake partway up its slope. It later became the newest subsidiary mountain of Downtrodden Mountain, where the old Daoist priest Jia spends most of the year.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup>

### Downtrodden Mountain

> *Main article: [Downtrodden Mountain](Downtrodden_Mountain.md)*

Downtrodden Mountain is the mountain Chen Ping'an bought almost by accident. It grew into the seat of his own mountain force.

### Incense Mountain

Incense Mountain is one of the largest mountains offered by the Great Li Empire. Old Man Yang said that apart from Cloud Drape Mountain and Incense Mountain, Downtrodden Mountain was the most interesting of them all. Incense Mountain was given one of the empire's three new formal mountain gods.

### Dragonspine Mountain

A large piece of natural Dragon Slaying Platform appeared halfway up Dragonspine Mountain, which is the real reason the surrounding mountains were sealed off. The Great Li Empire granted the Dragon Slaying Platform to Wind Snow Temple and True Martial Mountain to share. Ruan Qiong warned that not even an Upper Five Tier cultivator would dare to claim the mountain without powerful backers.

### Go Table Mountain

Go Table Mountain lies on the smallest of the three official roads running north from the Great Li Empire's southern border. The road was once used for porcelain, tea and salt, and its stretch across the mountain runs more than 150 kilometers through four prefectures, including Eternal Splendor Prefecture and White Cloud Prefecture. Its summit is a wide, flat plateau that looks like a grain-sunning ground, as if an immortal had sheared off the peak with a sword. Red Candle Town lies to its south.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup>

Its ruler was Wei Bo. The last emperor of the Divine Water Nation had named him a mountain god in charge of thousands of kilometers of land around the mountain.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup> The Great Li Empire demoted him to a mountain lord for secretly protecting refugees fleeing the Divine Water Nation's wars, but the people of Red Candle Town still praise him for it.<sup>[[16]](#ref-16)</sup> Two pythons, one black and one white, grew strong on the mountain under his watch. With no official title, he needed them to keep the mountain's spiritual energy from being taken by others.<sup>[[17]](#ref-17)</sup>

Chen Ping'an crossed the mountain with A'Liang and the children on his way to the [Great Sui Nation](Great_Sui_Nation.md). A'Liang's sword strike there broke the restrictions on Wei Bo. The white python was killed, and the black python later moved to Downtrodden Mountain.<sup>[[18]](#ref-18)</sup> A hidden patch of bamboo on the mountain grew very slowly for centuries under generations of mountain lords. It was later revealed to be descended from courage bamboo, one of the ten celestial bamboos of the Bamboo Sea Small World.<sup>[[19]](#ref-19)</sup> Chen Ping'an made the frame of [Lin Shouyi](Lin_Shouyi.md)'s bookcase from this bamboo. Wei Bo also found a golden lotus seed on the mountain while fleeing the fall of the Divine Water Nation.

### Parasol Tree Mountain

The peaks of the whole Parasol Tree Mountain range have been sliced flat. A ferry station for ships that sail across the sea of clouds stands there, west of the town, and it is where Chen Ping'an boarded Ceremony Mountain's Kun ship.<sup>[[20]](#ref-20)</sup>

### Ox Horn Mountain

Ox Horn Mountain is a low, gentle mountain owned by a cultivator from [Southern Stream Nation](Southern_Stream_Nation.md), with buildings winding from its foot to its peak. It is home to the Cloth Wrapper Shop, and with Divine Elegance Mountain it was one of the two most important mountains in the region.<sup>[[21]](#ref-21)</sup> Chen Ping'an later bought it after the Cloth Wrapper Shop left.

### Other mountains

- **Cool Wind Mountain** has the busiest mountain god temple in the western mountains because it lies close to the prefectural city. Dong Shuijing's wonton shop is near it.
- **Gray Veil Mountain** is very large and the closest mountain to Downtrodden Mountain. Chen Ping'an bought it later, along with Cinnabar Mountain (formerly held by the Xu Clan of Light Breeze City), Ao Back Ridge, Exuberant Dawn Peak and Sword Worship Plateau.<sup>[[22]](#ref-22)</sup> Gray Veil Mountain and Ao Back Ridge were later leased to Bejeweled Hairpin Island.
- **Golden Stalk Mountain** lies just north of Downtrodden Mountain and Gray Veil Mountain. Ruan Qiong bought it to build a new forge.
