# Hundred Schools of Thought

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Hundred_Schools_of_Thought)

---
The Hundred Schools of Thought represent a diverse and influential collection of philosophical and intellectual traditions within Majestic World. They encompass a wide range of disciplines, from ethics and politics to warfare and economics, shaping the world's intellectual landscape and impacting the lives of cultivators and ordinary people alike.

**Key Insights into the Hundred Schools of Thought:**

- **Competition and Collaboration:** While the Hundred Schools are often portrayed as competing factions, vying for influence and power, the sources also highlight instances of collaboration and exchange between different schools. This suggests a dynamic relationship where ideas and practices intermingle, enriching and evolving each tradition. [1-8]
- **Influence Beyond the Three Teachings:** Confucianism, Buddhism, and Daoism are acknowledged as the three most prominent teachings in Majestic World. However, the Hundred Schools, despite not reaching the same level of widespread recognition, exert significant influence. They offer alternative paths to cultivation and advancement, attracting followers seeking specialized knowledge or defying the conventions of the three dominant teachings. [2, 9-15]
- **Practical Applications:** The Hundred Schools are not merely theoretical frameworks. Their principles and teachings are applied to real-world situations, impacting governance, warfare, commerce, and even interpersonal relationships. This practical focus is evident in the actions of characters like Cui Chan, who employs techniques from various schools to achieve his goals. [3, 7, 8, 16, 17]
- **Historical Significance:** The Hundred Schools have shaped the history of Majestic World, influencing the rise and fall of empires, the development of cultivation techniques, and the course of major conflicts. Their legacy is evident in the numerous academies, temples, and historical sites scattered across the continents, serving as reminders of their enduring impact. [18-25]
- **Diversity of Disciplines:** The sources mention various schools within the Hundred Schools, each specializing in a particular area of knowledge:
- **School of Naturalists:** Skilled in manipulating fortune and karma, often through cultivation techniques. [3, 5]
- **Fictionists:** Capable of influencing reality through storytelling, with their power tied to their cultivation level and the scope of their narratives. [11, 20, 26, 27]
- **Mohists:** Known for their focus on universal love, logic, and practicality, often engaging in acts of chivalry and contributing to the development of technology. [4, 7, 11, 22, 28]
- **Commercialists:** Devoted to the principles of trade and commerce, often viewed as pragmatic and opportunistic, with a significant following despite lacking official endorsement from empires. [8, 29, 30]
- **Diplomatists:** Skilled in negotiation and diplomacy, playing crucial roles in inter-empire relations and alliances. [8]

**Chen Ping'an's Encounters and the Hundred Schools:**

Chen Ping'an's journey brings him into contact with individuals and teachings from various schools, broadening his understanding of the world and shaping his own philosophical outlook.

- He interacts with scholars and cultivators from the School of Naturalists, Fictionists, Mohists, and Commercialists, witnessing their unique abilities and learning about their principles.
- He engages in discussions about the strengths and weaknesses of different schools, comparing their approaches to cultivation, ethics, and governance.
- He observes how the teachings of various schools are applied in practical situations, from warfare and diplomacy to personal growth and self-cultivation.

Through these encounters, Chen Ping’an gains a nuanced perspective on the Hundred Schools, recognizing their diversity, complexity, and lasting impact on Majestic World. His journey serves as a microcosm of the broader intellectual landscape, showcasing the interconnectedness of different traditions and the enduring relevance of their teachings.

**The Hundred Schools of Thought, far from being mere historical relics, remain vibrant and influential forces within Majestic World.** They offer alternative paths to power, enlightenment, and understanding, shaping the destinies of individuals and empires alike. Their teachings continue to be debated, interpreted, and applied, ensuring their lasting legacy in this vast and wondrous realm.
