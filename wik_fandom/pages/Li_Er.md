Li Er is a side character of the series [Sword of Coming](Donghua.md).

## Appearance

Li Er is a rugged, middle-aged man with a sturdy and burly build that reflects a life of manual labor. He has a weather-beaten face, simple black hair usually kept in a practical style, and dark, unassuming eyes. He is almost always seen wearing coarse, drab commoner’s clothing made of hemp or linen, typically in shades of brown or grey. His most recognizable feature is the large bamboo basket he carries on his back, often appearing barefoot or in simple straw sandals, which makes him look like a typical laborer rather than a legendary figure.

## Personality

Li Er is exceptionally stoic and low-key. He has no interest in the fame or politics of the immortal world and prefers to live a quiet, unremarkable life. He is a man of very few words, but he is defined by a fierce, silent loyalty to his family. While he is a protective father to his son, Li Huai, and his daughter, Li Liu, he is also known for being humorously submissive to his wife. He approaches conflict with a blunt, "straight-line" logic, relying on his overwhelming physical presence and fearless attitude to settle matters directly, often willing to challenge entire sects or empires if they threaten his children.

## Background

Li Er’s background begins in the mysterious Lizhu Cave Heaven, the same small town where the story starts. While many youths in the town were recruited by great sects for their spiritual potential or scholarly talent, Li Er took the difficult path of a Pure Martial Artist, focusing entirely on physical tempering and endurance. He eventually left the small town to settle at Lion Peak in the Southern Baoyue State. Throughout the story, he serves as a symbol of peak physical strength, proving that a person who looks like a common worker can command enough power to rival the world's strongest beings. He appears early in both the novel and the donghua, initially blending into the background as just another ordinary resident of the town.
