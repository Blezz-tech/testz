# Mountain Cliff Academy

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Mountain_Cliff_Academy)

---
Mountain Cliff Academy is a prominent educational institution in the sources, with a rich history, complex internal dynamics, and a significant role in the political landscape.

**Historical Background and Founding:**

- **Founder:** Mountain Cliff Academy was founded by **Qi Jingchun**. He was a dedicated scholar who, despite coming from a poor background, overcame many hardships to gain admittance into the academy.
- **Original Location:** The academy was originally established in the northern wilderness of the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent, within the Great Li Empire. It was founded in an effort to educate the general public and elevate the Great Li Empire from its former status as a settlement of northern savages.
- **Move to Great Sui Nation:** Due to political turmoil and the death of Qi Jingchun's teacher, Mountain Cliff Academy was moved to Great Sui Nation. It was constructed on the most scenic mountain in Great Sui Nation’s capital city, Eastern Splendor Mountain.
- **Significance:** The academy is considered one of the 72 academies of Confucianism, and was once the equivalent of the imperial academy of the Great Li Empire.

**Curriculum and Teaching:**

- **Confucian Teachings:** The academy focuses on Confucian principles and the way of managing a nation. It emphasizes the importance of knowledge as a vessel for carrying the Dao.
- **Emphasis on Books:** The academy has a vast book collection that is considered the best on the continent. Students are encouraged to read extensively.
- **Open to All:** Qi Jingchun offered lessons to everyone in the continent while residing in the Great Li Empire.
- **Rule of No Food**: Accommodation is provided, but not food, so students from impoverished backgrounds would earn money by copying scriptures. This rule is more lenient at the new academy in Great Sui Nation.
- **High Standards**: Mountain Cliff Academy has produced many high-ranking officials. It is a very selective school, and many powerful clans try to send their most promising young members to study there.

**Key Figures and Internal Dynamics:**

- **[Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md):** The founder and first mountain master of the academy. He was a revered figure, and his teachings and ideals are still influential even after his death.
- **[Mao Xiaodong](Mao_Xiaodong.md):** A former junior brother of Qi Jingchun and the deputy mountain master of the new academy in Great Sui Nation. He effectively oversees the educational affairs of the academy.
- **Mountain Master of Great Sui Nation**: The head minister of Great Sui Nation’s ministry of rites is the mountain master in name only.
- **Teachers**: The academy employs many reputable and knowledgeable teachers.
- **Students**:
  - **[Li Baoping](Li_Baoping.md):** A spirited young girl who deeply admires Qi Jingchun and is eager to study at the academy. She is confident and outspoken
  - **Li Huai:** A student who traveled from the Great Li Empire and has a strong sense of loyalty and determination.
  - **[Lin Shouyi](Lin_Shouyi.md):** A diligent student who is also a cultivating prodigy. He is often seen reading in the scripture library.
  - **[Yu Lu](Yu_Lu.md)**: A student who came from the fallen Lu Empire, and traveled with Cui Chan to the academy.
  - **[Xie Xie](Xie_Xie.md)**: A student who came from the fallen Lu Empire, and traveled with Cui Chan to the academy. She is also a prodigious cultivator.
  - **[Cui Dongshan](Cui_Dongshan.md):** Though not officially a student, he is considered Chen Ping'an's "pupil" and has a strong presence in the academy. He is also known as the Myriad Treasure Immortal and Cai Clan's Ancestor. He is handsome and cheerful, making him a popular figure at the academy.
  - **[Gao Xuan](Gao_Zhen.md):** A member of the imperial family of Great Sui Nation, who has been officially enrolled in the academy.
- **Factions:** Like the imperial court, there are factions within the academy. Students from the Great Li Empire are often given preferential treatment, creating some discontent among local students of Great Sui Nation. There is also conflict between those students who traveled from Great Li Empire and those who had already been studying at the academy.
- **Disciples of Qi Jingchun**: The academy places a great deal of importance on Qi Jingchun's direct disciples.

**Political and Strategic Significance:**

- **Relationship with Great Li Empire:** The academy’s move to Great Sui Nation reflects the ongoing conflict between the two empires.
- **Great Sui Nation's Hopes:** The Great Sui Nation has high expectations for the new academy and hopes that it will bring about a new generation of scholars.
- **Puppet Academy:** The Great Li Empire is attempting to create a new academy on Cloud Drape Mountain to take the place of the Mountain Cliff Academy. The new academy will be overseen by a puppet of the Great Li Empire.
- **Potential for Conflict:** The academy is a potential source of conflict between the Great Sui Nation and the Great Li Empire, with both sides vying for influence. The academy is being carefully watched by the imperial court of Great Sui Nation.
- **Neutral Stance**: The academy attempts to remain neutral in the conflict between the Great Sui Nation and the Great Li Empire.
- **Preservation of Heritage:** Even though the Mountain Cliff Academy has been stripped of its status as one of the 72 academies, it is seen as important to preserve its heritage..
- **Impact on Great Li Empire:** The loss of the Mountain Cliff Academy is seen as a severe blow to the official hierarchy of the Great Li Empire.

**Challenges and Changes:**

- **Loss of Status:** Mountain Cliff Academy was stripped of its status as one of the 72 Confucian academies.
- **Internal Conflict:** There are some tensions and conflicts within the academy, particularly between students from different backgrounds.
- **Instability:** The academy has experienced some turmoil since its move to Great Sui Nation, including an assassination attempt.
- **Adaptation:** The academy is working to adapt to its new environment in Great Sui Nation and trying to maintain its identity while also dealing with the political changes.

Mountain Cliff Academy is more than just a school; it is a symbol of knowledge, tradition, and resilience. It is deeply embedded in the political and social fabric of the world, and its story reflects the complex interplay of power, ambition, and the pursuit of wisdom. It serves as a microcosm of the larger conflicts and changes occurring in the world of the sources.
