# Yellow Court Nation

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Yellow_Court_Nation)

---
Yellow Court Nation is a significant, though not the most powerful, political entity in the sources, and it presents a complex picture of a nation navigating its place in a world dominated by larger empires.

**Political Status and Relationships:**

- **Subsidiary Nation:** Yellow Court Nation is a **subsidiary nation of the Great Sui Empire**. This status significantly limits its autonomy and influences its internal and external policies.
- **Tribute:** As a subsidiary nation, it is implied that Yellow Court Nation pays tribute to Great Sui.
- **Influence of Great Sui:** The influence of Great Sui is considerable, impacting the appointment of mountain and river gods within Yellow Court Nation, which are few.
- **Relationship with Great Li:** Yellow Court Nation is located south of the Great Li Empire, making it a neighbor and a point of interest for the expanding Great Li Empire.
- **Observing the Great Li Empire:** Yellow Court Nation, along with other nations to the north and south, is carefully observing the Great Li Empire’s expansion.
- **Potential Annexation**: There is a possibility that Yellow Court Nation will become a subsidiary nation of the Great Li Empire in the future, which is why they are giving preferential treatment to traveling scholars from the Great Li Empire.

**Geographical and Resource Limitations:**

- **Limited Territory:** The northern region of Yellow Court Nation has limited territory, and mountain and river gods are quite rare.
- **Lack of Resources:** Compared to the southern region of the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent, Yellow Court Nation is implied to be lacking in resources.

**Cultural and Social Aspects:**

- **Confucianism:** Like the Great Sui Empire, Yellow Court Nation appears to have a culture influenced by Confucianism, but there are also other influences.
- **Academies:** The nation has some academies of Confucianism, but they do not seem to have the same status as those in Great Sui or Great Li.
- **Treatment of Scholars:** Traveling scholars from the Great Li Empire are given preferential treatment.
- **Corruption:** It is suggested that the Hong Clan, which has a strong influence in Yellow Court Nation, is "rotten to the core," implying systemic corruption.
- **Limited Cultivation**: The cultivation paths available are limited, and even if the Hong Clan could appoint as many gods as they pleased, they would still be limited by the nation.

**Military and Defense:**

- **Weak Armies**: The armies of Yellow Court Nation are not very strong. They are not a match for the Great Li Empire.
- **Reliance on Great Sui**: As a subsidiary nation, they rely on Great Sui for protection.
- **Lack of Assigned Gods:** Yellow Court Nation lacks mountain and river gods assigned by the emperor of the Great Sui Empire.

**Key Figures and Groups:**

- **Hong Clan:** The founding clan of Yellow Court Nation. They appear to hold significant influence and are possibly corrupt.
- **Chu Clan:** The Chu Clan is mentioned as the family that owns the Great Water Palace, but this is proclaimed to be a front to the public.
- **Cold Food Great River God:** A high-ranking god of Yellow Court Nation who has a lavish palace outside the cities and towns. He is powerful within the nation.
- **Gao Xuan:** A prince of Yellow Court Nation.
- **Spies:** The Great Sui Nation has spies within the Great Li Empire.

**Specific Locations and Structures:**

- **City God Temple:** The nation has city god temples, and the wall murals within them are said to be very realistic.
- **Great Water Palace:** A lavish palace owned by the Cold Food Great River God, but proclaimed to be owned by the Chu Clan.
- **Pillow Relay Station**: A relay station at Red Candle Town where guests of the empire can stay.
- **Mountain Forests in the North**: A villa in the northern forests near a river that features cliff carvings.

**Challenges and Internal Issues:**

- **Internal Strife:** There is a possibility of internal conflict and instability.
- **Limited Potential**: Due to their limited territory and resources, there is a limit to the potential achievements of those within the nation.
- **Unease about the Great Li Empire:** The nation is aware of the growing power of the Great Li Empire and its potential threat.
- **Fear of Collapse:** There is an implied fear that the nation could collapse, much like the Lu Empire before it.

**Interactions with Others:**

- **Great Li Empire:** They are trying to maintain good relations with scholars from Great Li Empire.
- **Great Sui Nation:** They are a subsidiary nation and subject to the will of the Great Sui Empire.
- **Cultivators**: Cultivators from other nations and sects have entered Yellow Court Nation.
- **Chen Ping'an's impact**: Chen Ping'an, though not from Yellow Court Nation, interacts with people in Yellow Court Nation, influencing local events and relationships.

Yellow Court Nation is a nation that is trying to maintain stability and navigate its place in a world dominated by larger empires. It is subject to the will of the Great Sui Empire, and it is also under the watchful eye of the Great Li Empire. The nation struggles with internal corruption, limited resources, and a weak military. Despite these challenges, the people of Yellow Court Nation, like those in other nations, have their own culture and pride.
