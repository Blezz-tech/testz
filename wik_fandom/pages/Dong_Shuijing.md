## Appearance

As a boy, he is noted for his calm, natural expression, which stays composed even in danger.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> When [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) returns from his travels years later, Dong Shuijing has grown far taller, and now greets customers outside his wonton shop on Cool Wind Mountain.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

## Personality

Dong Shuijing is simple, honest and reserved, a quiet boy whom [Li Huai](Li_Huai.md)'s mother likes for being "a good and honest guy" like [Li Er](Li_Er.md).<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> Li Er himself prefers him to [Lin Shouyi](Lin_Shouyi.md), since their personalities are alike.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> Li Baoping describes him as the most selfless of the five remaining students when they plan to trick the suspicious coach driver.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup> Li Huai, less kindly, calls him a "blockhead". Li Huai's mother feels he is honest and responsible, but always lacking "a little something".

Beneath his modesty, he is a shrewd, diligent and principled businessman. He never compromises on the quality of his wontons or raises their prices, stays open until the last customer has eaten, and cooks for late guests himself instead of keeping his workers late. Chen Ping'an describes him as kind but shrewd, and counts him and Sun Jiashu as the two people he knows who excel most at making money. Dong Shuijing's attitude stays unchanged even as he becomes very rich.

He also has a quiet, enduring pride. When classmates teased him about the story behind his name, he never tried to explain, and let them say what they liked.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

## Background

Dong Shuijing is from [Apricot Blossom Alley](Small_Town.md#apricot-blossom-alley) in the small town. According to town gossip, his mother went into labour while drawing water at the Iron Lock Well, which is how he got his name. He grew up poor, often eating free meals at Li Huai's house, where he blushed whenever he saw Li Liu.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> Apart from Mr. Qi, Li Er and Chen Ping'an were among the few people who truly respected him in his formative years.

He was one of the last five students of [Mr. Qi's](Qi_Jingchun.md) private school, along with [Li Baoping](Li_Baoping.md), Li Huai, Lin Shouyi and Shi Chunjia.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> After Ma Zhan's murder, he helped Li Huai lead the suspicious coach driver on a detour so that Li Baoping could warn Chen Ping'an. When the others left for Mountain Cliff Academy, Dong Shuijing chose to stay behind, taking Chen Ping'an's old job as a labourer at the blacksmith shop and waiting for the town's school to reopen.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup> When Chen Ping'an returned, Dong Shuijing visited him somewhat sheepishly, as if he had done something wrong. Chen Ping'an did not think his decision was wrong at all.

He soon left school, sold one of his clan's two ancestral residences, bought half a street of mansions in the new prefectural city, and opened a wonton stall.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> The stall grew into a famous wonton shop halfway up Cool Wind Mountain, beside its mountain god temple. He bought treasures cheaply from stubborn town elders who refused to sell to outsiders, sold them at the Cloth Wrapper Shop on Ox Horn Mountain, and used the profits to buy land through officials who ate at his shop. Before long, he was rumoured to own half of Dragon Spring Prefecture's city.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup>

The Mohist cultivator Xu Ruo took an interest in him, quizzing him about Mohism over a bowl of wontons.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup> Xu Ruo later planned to nurture him into a genuine "saber-lending cultivator".<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup> After testing his judgement of the local officials, Xu Ruo gave him a Peace and Safety Tablet of the Great Li Empire, a token worth as much as several cities, in the hope of future dividends.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup>

## Trivia

- 董水井 (Dǒng Shuǐjǐng) literally means "water well".
- He is nicknamed "Half City Dong" for the many residences he owns in Dragon Spring Prefecture.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup>
- Both he and Lin Shouyi have liked Li Liu since childhood. Li Liu later tells Chen Ping'an that she likes neither of them.
- He makes his own rice wine, which Chen Ping'an and Gu Can both drink.
- Chen Ping'an felt a particular affinity with him, most likely because of their similar backgrounds.
- He befriends the Great Sui prince Gao Xuan, who visits his shop, and hands Gao Xuan his keys so he can climb the mountain to watch the sunrise.
