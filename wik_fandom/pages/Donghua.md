The Swords donghua is based on the [Light Novel](Light_Novel.md). It started broadcasting August 15, 2024. on Tencent Pictures.

## Synopsis

Chen Ping’an grew up in a small, quiet town, living a humble and peaceful life. But when mysterious outsiders arrived, his ordinary world unraveled, exposing him to a reality where immortal cultivators, mythical dragons, and extraordinary powers were no longer just stories. Strange occurrences began to ripple through his town, pulling him into a grand, unpredictable destiny he never imagined.

Born in Lizhu Cave Heaven, Chen Ping’an was destined for greatness, but his path was fraught with misfortune. The breaking of his natal porcelain robbed him of his natural fortune, leaving him overlooked as his friends rose to prominence. Yet, even as powerful figures sought to manipulate his fate, Chen Ping’an refused to be a pawn, setting out on his own path.

Armed with nothing but a wooden sword and an unwavering spirit, Chen Ping’an embarks on an epic journey. From moving mountains and turning seas upside down to confronting gods and demons, his sword becomes a tool to reshape the world and defy destiny itself. Along the way, he encounters wonders of the Midland world: a scholar who cleaves a waterfall of the Milky Way, a nameless Daoist seeking only the cool breeze, an old monk spinning tales with chicken soup, and a blind painter commanding golden-armored puppets to shift entire mountains.

Chen Ping’an, the boy of peace, takes his first step southward, determined to transcend his humble beginnings and forge an extraordinary legend in a world filled with boundless wonders.
