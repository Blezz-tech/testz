**A'Liang** is a swordsman who spent a century at the Sword Qi Great Wall. He is an old friend of the [Scholarly Sage](Scholarly_Sage.md) and his disciples, and he escorted [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) and the children of the small town on part of their journey to Mountain Cliff Academy.

## Appearance

A'Liang looks unremarkable. He wears a conical bamboo hat even when there is no sun or rain, carries a slender saber in a green bamboo scabbard, and keeps an old silver wine gourd at his waist. He travels with a white donkey that he never rides. *(Chapter 88)* The bamboo hat hides his aura, since without it he would draw far too much attention. *(Chapter 112)*

## Personality

A'Liang is boastful, playful and lazy about small matters. He jokes constantly, teases the children, and gets annoyed when Chen Ping'an compares him to Zhu He. *(Chapter 93)* Cui Chan noted that unlike scholars, A'Liang never boasted, meaning his wild claims after each battle were simply true. *(Chapter 161)*

Behind the joking he is watchful and principled. He tested Chen Ping'an from their first meeting at Dragon Whisker Creek to see what kind of person he was. *(Chapter 111)* He told the children that a strong person's freedom ends where a weak person's freedom begins. He is fiercely loyal to Qi Jingchun and grew furious with anyone who used his friend's name as a shield. *(Chapter 114)*

## Background

A'Liang met the Scholarly Sage before Cui Chan did. He first meant to beat the old scholar up but was talked round, and the two became close friends. Among the old scholar's disciples he was closest to Cui Chan, Qi Jingchun and Zuo You, and he fought many great battles for Qi Jingchun and Zuo You. Angered by how the Confucian schools treated the Scholarly Sage, he forced his way into two of them, then crossed two continents and passed Stalactite Mountain to reach the Sword Qi Great Wall. *(Chapter 115)* He honed his sword there for a hundred years, never losing to the great demons of the Upper Five Tiers. *(Chapter 350)* He often ventured deep into Savage World alone to fight its great demons. He came to be known as the one who had killed the most demons at the Great Wall. *(Chapter 189)* In Savage World he also befriended Liu Cha, who tried to give him his sword. *(Chapter 626)*

A'Liang sought out Wei Jin of Wind Snow Temple and taught him the sword. Wei Jin gave him the white donkey and the sword nurturing gourd. *(Chapter 649)* He arrived near the small town after Qi Jingchun's death, spoke the town's dialect, and agreed to lead Chen Ping'an, Li Baoping, Li Huai, Lin Shouyi, Zhu He and Zhu Lu south. He told Chen Ping'an that the hairpin Qi Jingchun gave him came from Qi Jingchun's teacher and was a token of their lineage. *(Chapter 91)* At Go Table Mountain he shared out a box of treasures among the group, first come first served. *(Chapter 104)* At Pillow Relay Station he dealt with Zhu He and Zhu Lu after they turned on the group, leaving the donkey, the gourd and other gifts to the children before parting.

He then went to the Great Li capital, struck the replica White Jade Capital with his saber, and confronted Song Zhengchun, Luan Changye and Cui Chan. He left his saber, Auspicious Talisman, with the emperor to be returned to Li Baoping. When called from the heavens, his aura rose from the peak of the 12th Tier to the peak of the 13th Tier, and he broke through the sky to fight there. A'Liang had come for a sword that chose Chen Ping'an instead, and before parting he told the boy he expected him to become strong enough to carve characters at the Great Wall someday. *(Chapter 190)*

## Trivia

- He introduces himself with the line "Liang as in kindness". *(Chapter 189)*
- He taught Chen Ping'an the Eighteen Stops, a breathing technique he had refined himself.
- He spent over a hundred years pestering Yin Chen at the Great Wall and boasted of winning more than eight hundred arguments with him. *(Chapter 650)*
- Chen Qingdu said A'Liang hoped Wei Jin and others on the same path would one day stand beside him.
