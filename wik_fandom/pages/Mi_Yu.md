## Appearance

Mi Yu is a sword immortal popular with women both on and off the Sword Qi Great Wall, having gotten into numerous romantic entanglements over the years despite, or perhaps because of, his pretentious reputation. *(Chapter 607)*

## Personality

He comes across as pretentious and mediocre to most of the sword immortals around him, and he is widely looked down upon despite his genuine cultivation base, becoming something of a running joke on the Sword Qi Great Wall for being sincere in a way that nobody credits him for. *(Chapter 623)* Underneath the mockery, he is more thoughtful and conscientious than he is given credit for, willing to risk his own safety to save someone else even when badly outmatched, and quick to call out hypocrisy or cruelty even when it makes him uncomfortable to do so. *(Chapter 630)* He has a habit of taking things for granted when life goes smoothly for him, something that has cost him at least one genuine relationship in the past. *(Chapter 627)*

## Background

Mi Yu only managed to reach the Unpolished Jade Tier after burning through an enormous amount of immortal coins, resources made available largely thanks to the sacrifices of his far more talented older brother, Mi Hu, who could otherwise have already reached the Immortal Tier himself. Because of this, Mi Yu is widely regarded on the Sword Qi Great Wall as pretentious and undeserving of his own status, a reputation that has nothing to do with his actual swordsmanship or cultivation base. He was counted among the Sword Qi Great Wall’s handful of unique personalities for this very reason, specifically for failing to be recognized for his sincerity no matter what he did. *(Chapter 607)* *(Chapter 623)*

When Zuo You caught him mocking the Scholarly Sage’s lineage in front of Cui Dongshan and his companions, Mi Yu was left too frightened to even move, his sword formation torn apart with a single strike and his composure shattered in front of onlookers. *(Chapter 607)*
When the Hidden Official’s branch was reorganized to include outsider swordsmen during the war against the Savage World, Mi Yu found himself the only Upper Five Tiers cultivator among a group of far weaker but sharper-minded strategists, led by Chen Ping’an after he was unexpectedly named the new Hidden Official. Mi Yu was initially given the task of liaising with the Sword Qi Great Wall’s own native swordsmen rather than the more prestigious Upper Five Tiers sword immortals, a decision that stung his pride until Chen Ping’an gently explained that the established hierarchy among sword immortals would only work against him in that role. *(Chapter 626)* *(Chapter 627)*

Mi Yu often accompanied Chen Ping’an personally as a bodyguard of sorts during this period, and when the sword immortal Lie Ji suddenly launched a surprise attack meant to assassinate Chen Ping’an, Mi Yu immediately intervened, knocking Lie Ji’s flying sword just enough off course to spare Chen Ping’an a direct hit to the heart before drawing his own blade against Lie Ji. Despite his efforts, Lie Ji’s attack still appeared to kill Chen Ping’an outright, with Lie Ji sacrificing his own yin soul to guarantee the kill, leaving Mi Yu to be detained under suspicion of complicity in the plot. *(Chapter 630)* He was ultimately spared full punishment and kept close by the sword immortal Chou Miao, only for Chen Ping’an to reappear very much alive shortly after, revealing that the entire incident had been engineered to draw out a traitor. Mi Yu was furious and deeply unsettled to learn how coldly calculated the scheme had been, openly confronting Chen Ping’an about whether he ever felt guilty manipulating even those closest to him, including his own disciple, Guo Zhujiu. *(Chapter 630)*

## Trivia

- His bonded flying sword is named Radiant Sunset, and his high-caliber longsword was a gift from his brother, Mi Hu. *(Chapter 630)*
- He drinks regularly with fellow underappreciated sword immortal Lie Ji to commiserate about their shared lack of respect on the Sword Qi Great Wall, which made Lie Ji’s betrayal all the more shocking to him. *(Chapter 630)*
- A female Nascent Tier cultivator from the Middle Earth Divine Continent became so infatuated with him that she took on the thankless job of administering an intercontinental ship just to stay near him, only to be left heartbroken and vengeful when nothing came of it. *(Chapter 630)*
