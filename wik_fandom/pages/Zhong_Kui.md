## Appearance

Zhong Kui is a **scholar** who is often described as wearing **azure robes**. He is also referred to as a "noble scholar". At one point, he appears in red official garb. He is also described as being impoverished and is referred to as an impoverished scholar.

Despite his scholarly appearance, he is also presented as a complex figure who is not always serious and dignified. He is depicted as having a **mischievous** chuckle and a **dazzling smile**. He is also portrayed as being capable of acting theatrically, and at times, he is described as having a **fearless** and **gallant** expression.

While he often presents himself as an ordinary scholar, he is also capable of displaying a powerful aura and can appear solemn. He is described as having a "righteous" aura that he nurtures from reading sagely classics. He is also shown to be **observant** and **thoughtful**. He has a habit of leaning on the counter at the inn.

## Personality

Zhong Kui is a complex character with a blend of scholarly and unconventional traits. He is a **noble scholar** from the Great Subduing Academy. Although he is a scholar, he is not always serious or dignified. He is portrayed as having a **mischievous** chuckle and a **dazzling smile**. He can be theatrical, and at times, he has a **fearless** and **gallant** expression. He is sometimes described as **shameless**.

Despite his sometimes frivolous behavior, Zhong Kui possesses a strong sense of **righteousness** that he nurtures by reading sagely classics. He is very knowledgeable about Confucian principles, and he understands the importance of respecting the rules established by the Confucian sect. He can be **observant** and **thoughtful**, often contemplating the deeper meaning behind things. He is **courageous**, and he does not hesitate to stand up for what he believes is right. He can be **impulsive**, but he also possesses a strong sense of responsibility.

Zhong Kui's personality includes some contradictions:

- He is described as an impoverished scholar, yet he is also depicted as a noble scholar.
- He is a scholar who is familiar with the rules and etiquette of the Confucian sect, yet he does not always adhere to them.
- He is capable of displaying a powerful aura, yet he often presents himself as ordinary.
- He often expresses concern over his image, but then behaves in a way that might harm that image.

Zhong Kui is portrayed as being **kind and generous**, and he is also someone who enjoys drinking and socializing. He is depicted as being particularly fond of Jiu Niang, and he goes out of his way to impress her. He can also be humorous, often joking with Chen Ping'an. He is aware of his own shortcomings, and is willing to admit them. Despite his flaws, Zhong Kui is presented as a likeable character who is capable of growth and change. He is not afraid to show his emotions, and he is not afraid to be himself. He is capable of acting in ways that are both noble and unconventional. He is sometimes described as having a "youthful shamelessness".

## Background

Zhong Kui is a **noble scholar** from the **Great Subduing Academy**. He is from the most orthodox lineage of the Second Sage, and his master, the mountain master of Great Subduing Academy, also graduated from the Second Sage Residence in Middle Earth Divine Continent. He became a virtuous scholar at Great Subduing Academy before the age of twenty. Despite this, he had a "tattered" reputation due to his unrestrained behavior and was disliked by many pedantic old scholars. He is the youngest noble scholar in the history of the Great Subduing Academy. He is a Confucian scholar who is very knowledgeable about Confucian principles, and he understands the importance of respecting the rules established by the Confucian sect. He is also very familiar with the Scholarly Sage's works and philosophies. His knowledge of Confucian classics is profound.

Zhong Kui's family background is not mentioned in the sources. He is described as being an "impoverished scholar" at one point, but he is also referred to as a noble scholar, indicating that he is from a well-respected scholarly background. He is said to have a teacher who treats his azure talisman paper like his own child. His master was also said to have used one of his azure talisman papers when crossing the seas, and another one upon arriving in Parasol Leaf Continent.
