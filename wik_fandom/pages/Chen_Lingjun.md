**Chen Lingjun** is a water snake of the flood dragon lineage who once ruled the Imperial River of the Yellow Court Nation. Subdued by [Cui Dongshan](Cui_Dongshan.md) and given to [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md), he lives on [Downtrodden Mountain](Downtrodden_Mountain.md), where he is better known as the little boy in azure.

## Appearance

In human form Chen Lingjun looks like a little boy, and he likes to dress in azure and green. His hands end in claw-tipped fingers. In his true form he is a huge water snake, big enough to carve a path through mountains and forests as he swims.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> 

## Personality

Chen Lingjun is a show-off with a big mouth and a habit of bragging about his grand connections. He calls himself the "Chivalrous Young Man from the Imperial River," a nickname borrowed from a martial arts hero he idolises, and he worries about bringing shame to it. He loves to boast about his close friendship with the river god of the Imperial River, whom he calls his good brother. Wei Bo has bluntly told him that the river god simply took him for a fool.

Beneath the bluster he is a coward in front of real power. When Cui Dongshan revealed what he was carrying, Chen Lingjun dropped to his knees and kowtowed at once, offering to find him beautiful maidservants. Back in the Yellow Court Nation he was considered a sixth tier great demon, yet in the Jewel Small World he was too frightened to go outside in case some passerby smashed him with a single punch.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> He has an endless craving for snake gall pebbles and will plead, roll on the floor or threaten to steal to get one.

He is smarter and more observant than he lets on. He cannot bear to watch [Pei Qian](Pei_Qian.md) suffer in her fist training, so he keeps away from the bamboo building when she is in it. When [Cui Cheng](Cui_Cheng.md) once asked why he stayed on Downtrodden Mountain, he answered quietly that it was because of his kind-hearted master.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> He often teases [Chen Nuanshu](Chen_Nuanshu.md), but he is also the one who tells her not to rush her cultivation.

## Background

### The Imperial River

For centuries Chen Lingjun lorded over the Imperial River outside a city in the Yellow Court Nation. He lurked on the riverbed waiting for the fire python in the Cao Clan's scripture library to shed its skin so he could devour her before she became a flood dragon. When the young Cui Dongshan arrived at the library, Chen Lingjun tried to recruit him to help eat the python. Cui Dongshan injured him, then pulled out an ancient inkstone holding a sleeping flood dragon. The snoring of the tiny dragon terrified both creatures into submission.

[Lu Chen](Lu_Chen.md) later wanted to take him to the Heavenly World because of some connection between them. When Chen Lingjun refused, Lu Chen left behind a golden lotus seed and asked Chen Ping'an to help the water snake swim along a great river in the Northern Complete Reed Continent and transform into a dragon.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

### Downtrodden Mountain

Chen Lingjun and the fire python were brought to Downtrodden Mountain to serve Chen Ping'an, who kept them supplied with snake gall pebbles. When the two finally chose their names, the boy named himself Chen Lingjun, and it was his idea that both take the surname Chen.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup> Chen Ping'an admitted he had planned to call him Jing Qing, after a line of poetry about clear air after summer rain. Chen Lingjun wanted to inscribe his own "impressive and radiant" name all over the pastry shop to bring it good luck, but Pei Qian stopped him.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

He wandered the mountains of the region freely and made friends with cultivators all over the western mountains. One of them, a Dragon Gate Tier cultivator on Yellow Lake Mountain, asked him to find a buyer for the mountain. Chen Lingjun haggled the price down to just ten grain rain coins, and Downtrodden Mountain bought it. Chen Ping'an recorded it as Chen Lingjun's first real contribution to the mountain.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup>

### The river journey

During his travels in the Northern Complete Reed Continent, Chen Ping'an walked along the great rivers to scout a route for Chen Lingjun's future journey.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> Back home, he spent a whole night explaining the route along Plenty River while Chen Lingjun took notes in messy handwriting. Chen Lingjun then confessed that he did not want to go at all and just wanted to live out his days on Downtrodden Mountain. Chen Ping'an told him he was not angry, and that the journey could wait until Chen Lingjun truly wanted to make it himself.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

Chen Ping'an later gave him one of a pair of Dragon King Baskets found in the Northern Complete Reed Continent. Chen Lingjun first tried to refuse, suggesting it go to the black python of Go Table Mountain instead. Chen Ping'an told him the treasure was too precious to entrust to anyone else.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> Chen Lingjun refined his basket long before Nuanshu managed hers.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> When Chen Ping'an left for the Sword Qi Great Wall, Chen Lingjun went down to Dragon Riding Alley to help Shi Rou run the pastry shop.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup>

## Trivia

- As a flood dragon, if he reaches the Golden Core Tier he will easily outclass an ordinary Golden Core Tier cultivator.
- He keeps a bowl of water that lets him spy on distant places. He once used it to peek at a celestial maiden from a cultivating sect.
- He once gave his river god brother a precious Peace and Safety Tablet.
- He feels a strong, instinctive dislike for Wei Bo, who regularly punishes him.
- He likes to watch the Go games on Downtrodden Mountain and shout advice to the players, even though watching usually puts him to sleep.
