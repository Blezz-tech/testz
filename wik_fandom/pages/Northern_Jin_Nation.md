# Northern Jin Nation

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Northern_Jin_Nation)

---
The Northern Jin Nation is a complex entity with a rich history, powerful military, and unique culture. Its position in the Lotus Flower Blessed Land makes it a key player in regional conflicts and a point of interaction for people from various other regions and continents. The nation also faces internal challenges and is influenced by both earthly powers and otherworldly forces.

**Geographic and Political Location**:

- The Northern Jin Nation is located on the **Parasol Leaf Continent**.
- It has a long border with difficult mountain paths.
- It is situated near a large lake known as the Four Hundred Kilometer Lake, which contains an island with an illegal temple.
- It is one of the four nations in the Lotus Flower Blessed Land, alongside the Southern Garden Nation, Pine Song Nation, and others.
- The nation has a **vastly renowned Tathagata Temple**.
- The Mirror Heart Hall has influence in the imperial court of the nation.
- It is a powerful nation with a history of conflict with the Southern Garden Nation.

**Culture and Society**:

- The nation's culture is distinct from that of the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent.
- It is known for its martial arts.
- The younger generation lacks elites to inherit the skills and power of the previous generations.
- The nation has a complex political structure with various clans and factions vying for power.
- There are many powerful families in the nation.
- There is a **Tathagata Temple** which is renowned, and has a large rock called the Stone Lotus Platform where a Bodhisattva is said to have attained enlightenment.
- The capital of the nation is a grand city.

**Military and Power**:

- The Northern Jin Nation has a strong military, including a powerful cavalry.
- **Dragon General Tang Tieyi** is a pillar member of the nation and a powerful general.
- The nation has a border army that is considered very powerful.
- The nation has elite military scouts and reconnaissance teams.
- The **Yao Clan**, originally from the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent, serves in the border army of the Northern Jin Nation.

**Key Figures and Groups**:

- **Tang Tieyi** is the most powerful general in the nation, known for his skill with sabers.
- **Yu Zhenyi** is the leader of the Lake Mountain Faction, one of the Upper Ten Elites.
- The Mirror Heart Hall focuses on infiltrating the imperial court rather than interfering in the cultivation world.
- **Lu Fang** remained on Bird's Eye View Peak and did not involve himself in worldly matters.
- **Ding Ying** is a significant figure who is knowledgeable about otherworldly immortals and has fought against them.

**Conflicts and Challenges**:

- The nation is engaged in conflicts with other nations, such as the Southern Garden Nation.
- There are internal power struggles among the various factions.
- There is a presence of otherworldly immortals who descend into their world.
- There are issues with corruption and abuse of power by officials.
- A great demon in the lake has appointed itself as a water god, and the imperial court is unable to punish it.
- The nation is in competition with the Southern Garden Nation which was once renowned for its powerful mounted troops.
- The imperial court of the nation has faced challenges with rebellions.

**Interactions with Other Regions**:

- The nation is aware of and interacts with the Great Li Empire.
- It is also aware of the Southern Garden Nation and other regions within the Lotus Flower Blessed Land.
- The nation's elites are known throughout the Lotus Flower Blessed Land.
- The nation's elites often look down upon those from the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent, with the exception of people like Cui Chan and Qi Jingchun.
- There are connections with people from the Middle Earth Divine Continent.
- There are also connections to the Great Quan Empire, especially through the Yao Clan, which originated from the Great Li Empire.

**Unique Features**:

- There is a large rock called the Stone Lotus Platform that wobbles with the presence of just one person, which no one can explain.
- The nation's border is extremely long and the mountain paths are difficult to traverse.
