Ning Yao is the main female protagonist of the series [Sword of Coming](Donghua.md).

## Appearance

Ning Yao first appears in the small town as a black-clothed girl in a veiled hat that covers her face, with a white-scabbarded sword and a green-scabbarded saber at her waist. In her later years at the Sword Qi Great Wall she is often seen in a dark green robe, usually resting a hand on her scabbards. *(Chapter 15)*

## Personality

Ning Yao is blunt, proud and supremely confident, firmly believing she will become the most powerful sword immortal under the heavens, not merely one of the most powerful. She speaks her mind without much concern for anyone's feelings, including Chen Ping'an's, and rarely explains herself twice. She has a strong, almost old-fashioned sense of fairness, telling Chen Ping'an early on that in her hometown no one cares whose parents you have, only whether you can win the fight fair and square. She rarely gets truly angry, but when she does, the people around her know to tread carefully, as seen in how harshly she punished friends over an offhand joke at Chen Ping'an's expense. She deliberately avoids discussing her parents' deaths and dislikes being pitied for it, preferring to treat the subject matter-of-factly rather than let outsiders make her a figure of tragedy. Underneath the bluntness she is deeply loyal, patient with the friends she trains, and quietly besotted with Chen Ping'an even while needling him constantly. *(Chapter 52)* *(Chapter 275)* *(Chapter 577)*

## Background

Ning Yao's name combines her father's and mother's surnames, Ning and Yao. Her parents, Master Ning and Mistress Yao, were both twelfth-tier Sword Immortals of the Sword Qi Great Wall, where the Ning and Yao Clans lived only two streets apart but were famously estranged. Mistress Yao married into the then-declining Ning Clan against her own clan's wishes, after a Daoist sage's reading of their compatibility came back unfavorable, and the Yao Clan never fully reconciled with her for it. Ning Yao's birth began to thaw the rift, and in a family lottery over her surname, held between a dowry dagger and a piece of Dragon Slaying Platform, the infant Ning Yao drew the Dragon Slaying Platform, securing her the Ning surname and visibly delighting her mother. *(Chapter 574)*

She was recognized as a top-tier sword prodigy from birth and left home to travel the world, eventually arriving badly wounded when she was in the small town, where the Daoist priest Lu Chen saved her life and left Chen Ping'an to nurse her back to health. During her recovery she stayed in Chen Ping'an's home, bonding with him despite her guarded manner, and helped him deal with threats from outsiders such as the Mountain-moving Ape of Sun Scorch Mountain, at one point offering to use her bonded treasure, a mark on her forehead gifted by her mother, as a trump card against him. She left the small town suddenly after receiving a letter from Stalactite Mountain, borrowing Chen Ping'an's sword scabbard. *(Chapter 15)* *(Chapter 53)* *(Chapter 71)*

It turns out both of her parents had been killed, in connection with a battle in which the Sword Qi Great Wall itself nearly fell to its enemies. Her childhood friend Dong Huafu, nicknamed Charcoal, felt honor-bound to personally defend her after her parents and her teacher A'Liang were both gone, and her remaining elders on the Wall, Bai Lianshuang and Nalan Yexing, took on the role of her guardians. *(Chapter 253)* *(Chapter 275)*

Chen Ping'an traveled to the Sword Qi Great Wall years later to personally deliver a sword forged for her by Ruan Qiong, and the two reunited there, where he confessed his feelings for her for the first time, and she returned it. After giving him ten years to get stronger, he returned on a later visit, having spent the intervening years growing stronger and more sure of himself, and their relationship deepened. On his second return, Ning Yao had become the undisputed top prodigy of her generation at the Sword Qi Great Wall, regarded by the old great sword immortal and the sword cultivator A'Liang as being in a class of her own. She was still working through her Golden Core Tier bottleneck on the way to the Nascent Tier, slowed less by her qi refinement, which came easily to her, than by the item refinement side of her cultivation. *(Chapter 270)* *(Chapter 271)* *(Chapter 577)*

Chen Ping'an ultimately asked her guardians, Bai Lianshuang and Nalan Yexing, for permission to formally propose marriage to her according to the customs of the Sword Qi Great Wall, offering his sword Sword Immortal and his celestial robe Golden Sweet Wine as betrothal gifts despite their value, and naming the reclusive old great sword immortal, [Chen Qingdu](Chen_Qingdu.md), as his intended matchmaker. Around the same time, he fought and defeated her rival suitor Qi Shou in single combat after Qi Shou was accused of coveting the Ning Clan's Dragon Slaying Cliff through her, cementing his place among her circle of friends and rivals at the Wall. *(Chapter 575)*

## Trivia

- Her bonded treasure is hidden in a mark on her glabella, a gift from her mother. *(Chapter 53)*
- She once got drunk and declared she could beat up a hundred Chen Ping'ans with one hand, a boast her friends never let either of them forget. *(Chapter 572)*
- Her father once nearly named her "Yao Ning" instead of "Ning Yao," leading to the only real argument he and her mother ever had. *(Chapter 574)*
- Her friends at the Sword Qi Great Wall have taken to physically punishing anyone who brings up her parents' deaths around her. *(Chapter 574)*
