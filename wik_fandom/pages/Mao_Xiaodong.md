**Mao Xiaodong** is the true backbone of [Mountain Cliff Academy](Mountain_Cliff_Academy.md) and a secret, unofficial disciple of the [Scholarly Sage](Scholarly_Sage.md). He stood beside [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md) when the academy was founded in the Great Li Empire, led its teachers and students into exile in [Great Sui Nation](Great_Sui_Nation.md) after Qi Jingchun's death, and became the sage presiding over the new academy on Eastern Splendor Mountain.

Stern, upright and fiercely protective of his students, he looks after [Li Baoping](Li_Baoping.md), [Li Huai](Li_Huai.md), [Lin Shouyi](Lin_Shouyi.md) and the other children of the small town. He greets [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) as his long-awaited junior brother, and personally guides him through the refinement of his golden scholarly core.

## Appearance

Mao Xiaodong is a tall, sturdy old man with a bright red nose, which makes him very recognisable. Although over ninety years old when first introduced, he looks only fifty or sixty. He always carries a redwood teacher's ruler at his waist, which he massages when thinking, and which also stores treasures. He has a habit of dozing in the academy's halls and stroking his beard.

## Personality

Mao Xiaodong is principled, protective and plain-spoken. He treats everyone equally, regardless of whether he is teaching them or simply talking. He can be sharply sarcastic, especially with Cui Chan. He follows the rules in all he does, which the Scholarly Sage says is the one thing still holding him back from his teacher's level. Of all the Scholarly Sage's disciples, he is the most like his teacher when it comes to teaching and answering questions. *(Chapter 450 (2))*

He cares deeply for the academy's people, saying that its heart lies in its students and teachers, not its name. He is wary of Chen Ping'an straying too far towards Legalism and repeatedly reminds him that laws are merely tools of governance. He has one vice he hates himself for: he cannot give up Go.

## Background

Mao Xiaodong was by Qi Jingchun's side when Mountain Cliff Academy was founded in the Great Li Empire, and is regarded as the academy's undisputed top figure in cultivation, seniority and morality alike. After Qi Jingchun's death, he played a pivotal role in leading the academy's original teachers and students out of the Great Li Empire to the new academy in Great Sui. He is known as one of the Scholarly Sage's "secret disciples". *(Chapter 160)*

## Plot

### The Academy in Great Sui

When Great Sui's officials complain that the children from the small town are poor students, Mao Xiaodong asks them whether Qi Jingchun was a better scholar and judge of students than all of them. He bluntly tells them that it is Great Sui that expects the children to be geniuses who will pledge allegiance to it. *(Chapter 162 (1))* Through a little girl's translation, Cui Chan delivers to him two messages: a parting message from the old scholar, and Qi Jingchun's final words, which Chen Ping'an was originally meant to pass on. *(Chapter 164)*

He bickers constantly with Cui Chan, now calling himself Cui Dongshan. He tells him not to wreak havoc on Eastern Splendor Mountain and threatens to call himself Cui Chan's grandson if he so much as glances at him. *(Chapter 167 (1))* When Cui Chan clashes with the cultivator Cai Jingshen, Mao Xiaodong withdraws the academy's protective formations and lets them fight.

When [Li Er](Li_Er.md) arrives over his son Li Huai's mistreatment, Cui Dongshan introduces Mao Xiaodong as Qi Jingchun's junior brother and the one who truly calls the shots. Li Er, who had ignored everyone else, greets him warmly as "Li Huai's father", an honour owed to Qi Jingchun's opinion of Mao Xiaodong. *(Chapter 169)* As Li Er fights his way through the imperial palace, Mao Xiaodong walks deliberately slowly through the secret passageway, then tells the emperor that it is time to end the farce. He brings Li Er before the emperor and the court's storyteller, trying hard not to laugh. *(Chapter 170 (2))* Afterwards, he reminds Li Er not to burn bridges while his son studies there, and remarks that Li Er has truly understood life. He tells Cui Dongshan that, whatever Great Sui does, the children who left the Great Li Empire with him are still the academy's students. *(Chapter 171 (1))*

He warns that he will personally kill the young imperial preceptor if Great Sui wrongly blames the academy and talks fail. When an unknown Nascent Tier cultivator attempts an assassination inside the academy, he chases the attacker off, his voice thundering across the sky. *(Chapter 304 (1))* As a Nascent Tier mountain master, he wields the power of an Unpolished Jade Tier cultivator within the academy's grounds. *(Chapter 345 (2))*

### Senior Brother to Chen Ping'an

When Chen Ping'an arrives at the academy, Mao Xiaodong leads him sternly to his study, then smiles and bows to him, thanking him for protecting the children of the scholarly lineage. He calls him "Junior Brother" and says he has been waiting for him for a very long time. *(Chapter 402 (1))* He has already gathered most of the natural treasures needed to refine Shen Wen's golden scholarly core into a bonded item, and agrees that Chen Ping'an can repay their market value within twenty years. *(Chapter 402 (3))*

He takes Chen Ping'an through the capital, to a small restaurant famous for its rice wine, and to the Confucian temple to secure scholarly fortune. He urges Xu Yuanxia to visit one day as an unofficial teacher. When assassins strike during their outing, he tells Chen Ping'an to focus on protecting himself, teases him that he will beat him in their teacher's place if he is still a "worthless Nascent Tier" at Mao Xiaodong's age, and complains that Chen Ping'an still calls him "Mountain Master Mao" rather than "Senior Brother Mao". *(Chapter 409)* Afterwards, he privately warns Chen Ping'an about Cui Dongshan, and when the assistant mountain master who had invited Zhao Shi to lecture resigns in protest and insults him, Mao Xiaodong sets Cui Dongshan on him.

On the mountain's summit, presiding over the academy as its sage, he guides Chen Ping'an through the refinement of the golden scholarly core, pouring the scholarly fortune of ceremonial artifacts into the cauldron. When the core transforms into a tiny Confucian scholar that speaks, he asks what it means, and is satisfied by Chen Ping'an's answer. *(Chapter 414 (2))* He then recites poems to guide him and chats with him daily. In a rare moment, he tells Cui Dongshan that while he admits he is inferior to Qi Jingchun, he is not inferior to Cui Chan.

- He laments, "Why have scholars become businessmen?"
- Conspirators plotting against the academy once rallied each other with cries of "Death to Mao Xiaodong!"
- Mao Xiaodong leads an academy excursion to the Great Li Empire to watch Wei Bo's Night Travel Banquet, visiting Forest Deer Academy and meeting Prince Gao Xuan.
- Although he is at the Nascent Tier, with the power of the Unpolished Jade Tier within Mountain Cliff Academy.
- He can turn the academy into its own small world, observe everything within it, and control part of the river of time there.
- He has deep knowledge of refining scholarly cores, learned in part from obscure books.
