# Heavens Beyond Heavens

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Heavens_Beyond_Heavens)

---
The "heavens beyond heavens" are presented in the story as a realm or set of realms that exist above the Heavenly World, representing a further level of existence with its own characteristics and powerful beings. 

**Hierarchical Structure and Vastness**

- The term itself, "heavens beyond heavens," implies a **hierarchical structure** where the Heavenly World is just one layer in a multi-layered universe.
- These realms are described as being **incredibly vast and expansive**, beyond the comprehension of those in lower realms.
- They are considered the origin point of "true" power, suggesting that even the most powerful figures in the Heavenly World are subject to the rules and forces of the heavens beyond heavens.
- **Access to these realms is not easy**, and requires immense power or unique circumstances to reach.

**Inhabitants and Their Powers**

- The most prominent inhabitants of the heavens beyond heavens are the **sages and patriarchs of the various teachings and sects**. These include figures like:
  - The **Dao Ancestor**, whose power is so immense that his disciples' actions can cause shockwaves through lower worlds. He also possesses a "Lotus Flower World" that is said to be bigger than the capital of the Great Li Empire, and may reside in the heavens beyond heavens.
  - The **Buddha**, who is speculated to reside in the western pure land of Sukhavati, which is described as a realm beyond the Heavenly World where scriptures and Buddhism are discussed.
  - Figures from the **Confucian academies** also reside in the heavens beyond heavens, observing the mortal world.
- These beings possess **powers that are beyond the scope of those in the Heavenly World**. They can manipulate reality, influence the fate of worlds, and even alter the fundamental laws of existence.
- It is suggested that beings in the heavens beyond heavens are **less bound by the limitations of the lower realms**, having transcended ordinary concepts of space and time.
- Some beings in the heavens beyond heavens can also **see through disguises and illusions** that would fool those of lower realms, and they are able to peer into different worlds.

**Interactions with Lower Worlds**

- The beings in the heavens beyond heavens **do not frequently interfere directly in the affairs of the lower worlds**, but their influence is still felt.
- They sometimes **observe the lower realms**, using them as places to test disciples and explore philosophical concepts.
- They may **bestow opportunities** upon those in the lower worlds, often in the form of fated encounters and spiritual insights, though these fated opportunities can be both good and bad.
- Powerful beings from lower worlds may **seek to ascend** to these higher realms, either to join them or to challenge their authority.
- The **actions of those in the heavens beyond heavens can affect events** in lower worlds, with the shattering of the Jewel Small World having ramifications across the world.
- **Conflicts can sometimes spill over** from the heavens beyond heavens into the lower realms, though such occurrences are rare.

**Key Concepts**

- **The Great Dao**: The heavens beyond heavens are seen as the origin point of the Great Dao, the ultimate principle governing the universe, so it is often the aim of powerful cultivators to pursue the Great Dao.
- **Ascension**: The concept of ascension often involves leaving the lower realms and entering the heavens beyond heavens, which is often seen as the ultimate goal for cultivators, though the process for ascension is extremely difficult.
- **Heavenly Mandate**: The idea that beings in the heavens beyond heavens can grant or revoke the mandate to rule, or can decide the fate of different beings.
- **Testing of the Heart**: The heavens beyond heavens are presented as a realm where flaws in one's mind's realm are magnified, meaning that inner demons can grow to monstrous sizes when one reaches those high levels.

**Additional Points of Interest**

- The heavens beyond heavens are not static, with the Daoist sages in the heavens beyond heavens seeking to **expand the height of the White Jade Capital**.
- There are also suggestions that there are **different types of heavens beyond heavens**, with the Buddhist pure land of Sukhavati being a separate plane of existence.
- The concept of the heavens beyond heavens can also be a metaphor for a **higher state of consciousness or spiritual enlightenment**.
- The beings of the heavens beyond heavens **may or may not conform to traditional concepts of "good" and "evil,"** as they exist on a plane beyond human morality.
- Some beings in the lower worlds who seek to become as powerful as the beings in the heavens beyond heavens might attempt to create a new heaven, which could be interpreted as a new set of rules for how existence itself works.

The heavens beyond heavens are depicted as a **complex and multi-faceted concept**, representing a realm of immense power, ultimate authority, and the origin of fundamental principles. They serve as a destination for those who have reached the pinnacle of cultivation and a place of mystery and wonder that motivates those in lower worlds to strive for greater heights.
