**Chen Qingdu** is the strongest sword cultivator on the [Sword Qi Great Wall](Sword_Qi_Great_Wall.md) and one of the most powerful beings in existence, said to be only slightly beneath the Paramount Sage, the Dao Ancestor, and Buddha. *(Chapter 584)* He guarded the wall for over ten thousand years without once leaving, not even to attend weddings or funerals within his own [Chen Clan](Chen_Clan.md). *(Chapter 571)* On the Sword Qi Great Wall, Chen Qingdu was the highest principle there was. *(Chapter 578)*

## Appearance

Chen Qingdu is a hunchbacked old man who has been unable to truly straighten his back throughout the past ten thousand years. He habitually clasps his hands behind his back and lives in a thatched cottage at the very top of the Sword Qi Great Wall.

## Personality

Chen Qingdu is a man of few words who nonetheless becomes surprisingly chatty around people he is fond of. He has a dry, sometimes vulgar sense of humor, and enjoys needling [Zuo You](Zuo_You.md) about his sword skills despite genuinely respecting them. Beneath his detached manner he keeps careful watch over every swordsman on the wall and cares deeply about matters most would consider beneath someone of his stature, such as whether children in the city are learning to read and write, which he sees as addressing the fundamental question of why sword cultivators fight and die at all.

He holds firmly to the belief that sword cultivators exist to stand with the weak and challenge the strong. Those who view others as ants, he says, are nothing more than ants themselves. *(Chapter 649)* He is not sentimental about death. In his eyes, no one is above it, including those he cares for. *(Chapter 615)* Yet he is far from cynical. When asked whether humanity had disappointed him over the millennia, he replied that he had seen much more hope than despair. *(Chapter 633)*

He once told [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) that a person should be willing to act unreasonably once or twice every hundred years, a lesson Chen Ping'an took to heart. *(Chapter 279)*

## Background

Chen Qingdu is one of the earliest humans to learn the sword. His sword lineage, one of four original lines, carries the greatest destructive power of any. In the ancient era, he was among the humans who rose up against the deities who had kept humanity as livestock. After that great war ended, the sword cultivators who fought in it were treated as prisoners and exiled to the Sword Qi Great Wall, tasked with holding the line between the Majestic World and the Savage World indefinitely.

In the distant past, Chen Qingdu led Long Jun and Guan Zhao to challenge the demon ancestor at Holding Moon Mountain. Guan Zhao was killed. Long Jun's soul was shattered. Chen Qingdu returned alone, and Holding Moon Mountain was left in ruins behind him. *(Chapter 613)* He himself has died once, in a sense, during those ancient battles, though he survived and continued his vigil.

For over ten thousand years he never left the wall. The Sword Qi Great Wall was Chen Qingdu, and Chen Qingdu was the Sword Qi Great Wall. *(Chapter 626)* Even visiting sages of the Three Teachings trod carefully around him. A Confucian sage once came to complain about the wall's conduct. Chen Qingdu told him they could talk after a fight. The sage fought three bouts, won two, lost one to the Hidden Official, and departed in low spirits. The Daoist second disciple, for his part, chose not to fight Chen Qingdu at all.

During the events of the novel, Chen Qingdu killed Dong Guanpu, a demon tribe spy from a prominent sword cultivator clan, with a single offhand sword strike after Dong Guanpu attempted to act against Chen Ping'an. He later lent his own sword, Lasting Qi, to Chen Ping'an for a critical journey. *(Chapter 322)*

When the demon tribe proposed a trade of worlds to avoid further bloodshed, Chen Qingdu flatly refused. *(Chapter 589)* After the Mirage Market assassination attempt on [Ning Yao](Ning_Yao.md), he wounded the [Chen Clan](Chen_Clan.md) leader for failing to prevent it.

Chen Qingdu took a particular interest in Chen Ping'an's growth. He served as matchmaker for Chen Ping'an and Ning Yao's marriage proposal, helped Chen Ping'an refine his fifth bonded item and nurture his bonded flying sword,Chapter 619 and ultimately appointed him as the new Hidden Official after the previous one betrayed the wall. *(Chapter 627)* He also deliberately suppressed Ning Yao's cultivation advancement using the Sword Qi Great Wall's sword dao, believing it was not yet time for her to break through.

He had a complicated relationship with a tall woman who was the spirit of one of the oldest swords in existence. She was his senior by a wide margin, and her former owner had been one of the first people to teach Chen Qingdu the sword. When she visited the wall, she told Chen Qingdu to get lost and said she had come to visit "her master," meaning Chen Ping'an. *(Chapter 587)*

[A'Liang](A'Liang.md) spent a hundred years on the Sword Qi Great Wall. Chen Qingdu was fond of him despite comparing his constant chatter to a fly droning beside his ear. When A'Liang finally departed, Chen Qingdu told him not to come back.

Chen Qingdu's ultimate opponent was the old man in gray, a manifestation of the Savage World's Great Dao itself. During the battle at the wall, the old man in gray faced one of Chen Qingdu's sword strikes directly.

Before the final chapter of the wall's struggle, Chen Qingdu revealed two long-held secrets to Chen Ping'an concerning the true origins of the exiled sword cultivators' hometowns and the nature of the martial arts path as a road to godhood. *(Chapter 650)*

He had a habit of passing down the small thatched cottage behind his own to whichever young swordsman he was most taken with at the time. First it went to Cao Ci, then to Chen Ping'an, then to [Wei Jin](Wei_Jin.md).

## Trivia

- Chen Qingdu's sword is named Lasting Qi, which he once turned into a joke at the old man in gray's expense.
- Despite never leaving the Sword Qi Great Wall in ten thousand years, he was aware of events across the Majestic World in remarkable detail.
- He offered to teach [Wei Jin](Wei_Jin.md) sword techniques drawn from the foundation of one of his old friends, but only in exchange for battlefield merit.
