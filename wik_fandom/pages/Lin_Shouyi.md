**Lin Shouyi** is one of the last five students of [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md)'s private school in the small town, and the eldest of the three children whom [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) escorts to [Mountain Cliff Academy](Mountain_Cliff_Academy.md) in [Great Sui Nation](Great_Sui_Nation.md), alongside [Li Baoping](Li_Baoping.md) and [Li Huai](Li_Huai.md). An illegitimate son of the Lin Clan, he is cold, proud and reserved, but sharp-minded and deeply loyal to those who earn his respect.

He becomes a Qi refiner on the journey thanks to [A'Liang](A'Liang.md)'s wine and a Daoist lightning scripture, and later grows into one of the most celebrated young cultivation prodigies in Great Sui's capital. He has been in love with [Li Liu](Li_Liu.md), Li Huai's older sister, since childhood.

## Appearance

As a child, Lin Shouyi is fair-skinned from his privileged upbringing and first appears wearing a black fox fur coat with a cold, aloof expression. He grows into a handsome, elegant young man who dresses plainly, wearing the jade hairpin Chen Ping'an carved for him. By his later years at the academy, he is described as a refined young master with an ethereal air, and young women from the capital's leading clans come to the academy just to catch a glimpse of him.

## Personality

Lin Shouyi is cold, taciturn and proud, and he struggles to open up to people. He dislikes being in anyone's debt, especially to people he distrusts. He prefers owing favours to good people, whom he can repay on his own terms, rather than to schemers like Cui Dongshan. He is calm in danger, quick to see through people, and comfortable viewing the world in the most negative light possible.

His sense of self is shaped by his background. As an illegitimate child despised by his own family, he once said he didn't care if he died. He is sensitive about money and status, and was the "biggest misfit" in the travelling group, quietly feeling left out while appearing indifferent. Yet he has a strong sense of right and wrong, is kind-hearted beneath his aloofness, and, in his own words, never resents someone for treating others better than him as long as they treat him kindly.

His name, 守一, means "remaining loyal to one thing", which fits his single-minded devotion to cultivation and to Li Liu. *(Chapter 119 (1))*

## Background

Lin Shouyi is from the Lin Clan, a large but low-profile clan of the small town that is not among the Four Families and Ten Clans. His father was a low-ranking official in the kiln supervision office who quietly held more power than he showed, and served three successive kiln supervision officials. Lin Shouyi is his illegitimate son, the child of a maidservant according to [Zhu Lu](Zhu_Lu.md), and both his father and his mother treat him coldly.

At school, he had beautiful handwriting that Mr. Qi praised, did not get along well with the other children, and was openly scornful of rich children from [Fortune Street](Small_Town.md#fortune-street-and-peach-leaf-alley) like Li Baoping. When Mr. Qi gave his last students each a sheet with the character "Qi" as homework, Lin Shouyi kept his safely at home.

## Plot

### Leaving the Small Town

On the excursion with [Ma Zhan](Ma_Zhan.md), Lin Shouyi is the only child who distrusts [Cui Minghuang](Cui_Minghuang.md) from the start. After Ma Zhan's murder, he suspects the coach driver was involved, and it is his idea, refined into a plan by Li Baoping, to trick the driver. When the five children swear a pact before carrying it out, he declares that as a despised illegitimate child, he doesn't care if he dies. *(Chapter 84 (1))* Afterwards, he and Li Huai catch up with Chen Ping'an's group and insist on going to the academy, admitting frankly that they would never make it there alone.

### Journey to Great Sui

Lin Shouyi is the first of the group to sense that A'Liang is far more than he appears. After his first sip from A'Liang's wine gourd following a sighting of demons, he grows close to A'Liang, drinking a little every day and discussing everything from swordsmanship to Chen Ping'an's hidden injuries. A'Liang tells him he is the first person to realise there are benefits to befriending him, and that he has good intuition on the path of cultivation. *(Chapter 100 (2))*

When Chen Ping'an offers to make Li Huai a bamboo bookcase after his tantrum on Go Table Mountain, he turns and offers to make one for Lin Shouyi too. This small gesture means far more to Lin Shouyi than anyone realises at the time.

When the Go Table Mountain treasures are shared out, Lin Shouyi tears his eyes away from a beautiful saber and chooses an ancient Daoist book, the *Recite Atop Clouds Scripture*, saying he is not suited to martial arts. *(Chapter 104)* The scripture turns out to be a secret manual of the Five Lightning Righteous Technique.

In [Red Candle Town](Red_Candle_Town.md), he receives a curt letter from his father containing a 300-tael banknote, telling him to wait to be collected and adding that his corpse would not be worth the transport fee. That night, a woman on a pleasure boat singles him out. She is the grand elder of the Palace of Eternal Spring, a major immortal sect of the Great Li Empire, and later offers to take him as a disciple. Lin Shouyi politely refuses, saying that while he cannot choose his clan or surname, he can choose his own path. *(Chapter 110)* Before leaving, A'Liang gives him a stack of yellow paper talismans and assigns a [Yin god](Yin_god.md) to escort the group, and Lin Shouyi becomes the one who communicates with the Yin god.

On the ship along the Embroidered Flower River, Lin Shouyi reveals that A'Liang's wine is what made him a Qi refiner, and is quietly hurt that Chen Ping'an clearly favours Li Baoping and Li Huai. He watches nervously as Chen Ping'an hacks quickly at precious bamboo for his bookcase, pleading with him to add curves and take care. Chen Ping'an teases him by threatening to make his last, and in the end it is the best of the three.

During the encounter with the ghost [Lady Chu](Lady_Chu.md), the Yin god tells Lin Shouyi to use a Barrier Shattering Talisman, which leads the group out of the "path to the underworld" on the mountain road. *(Chapter 124)* Afterwards, Chen Ping'an gives him the *Mountain Scouring Album* that a blind old Daoist priest gave in exchange for a snake gall pebble. Lin Shouyi buys a Go set and teaches Chen Ping'an to play, and later plays against [Xie Xie](Xie_Xie.md), [Yu Lu](Yu_Lu.md), and the spirit of the Azure Maiden Temple, who gives him a precious Go manual. He is furious when he learns that drunken scholars had defiled her temple, and salutes her statue before leaving.

At the Autumn Reed Inn, Lin Shouyi angrily accuses Chen Ping'an of being insane for spending 2,000 taels of silver on one night's lodging, not realising it was for the sake of his own cultivation. *(Chapter 138)* Cultivating greedily in the inn's rich spiritual energy, he nearly overloads himself until [Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md) stops him. By then he has reached the peak of the third tier within a few months, thanks to his aptitude and A'Liang's wine. *(Chapter 140 (2))* He later apologises to Chen Ping'an, having expected a beating that never came. On the final stretch, he drives off bandits with his lightning technique and fights a horde of ghosts in a burial ground, looking like a divine lightning general wreathed in purple qi.

### Mountain Cliff Academy

At the academy, Lin Shouyi skips lessons to read in the scripture library, where he is drawn to everything except the Confucian classics. His plain dress and cold manner make him wildly popular with female students. The Confucian scholar Dong Jing, a renowned expert in both Confucianism and Daoism, takes him under his wing, taking him to Iron Tree Mountain during thunderstorms. Dong Jing believes Lin Shouyi has a realistic chance of reaching the tenth tier.

When Li Huai's belongings are stolen, Lin Shouyi gives him money from his father's banknote to offer as a reward. *(Chapter 162 (2))* He then steps in when Li Baoping is insulted. After beating two local students, he fights the eldest grandson of the Chu Clan, who wields an heirloom Cloud Lightning Zither, and holds his own before losing. Soon afterwards, the son of the late general Pan Maozhen takes a lightning strike head-on and beats Lin Shouyi badly, spitting on him as he leaves. *(Chapter 165 (1))* Lying injured, Lin Shouyi finally understands how heavy a burden Chen Ping'an carried in making every decision on their journey. He later reveals he knowingly risked his entire cultivation future by helping Li Huai.

During Li Huai's family visit, he can hardly bring himself to look at Li Liu, drinks with Li Er, and is left holding an undelivered letter as she leaves, murmuring "next time".

### Rising Prodigy

Over the following years, Lin Shouyi becomes the academy's most celebrated prodigy, balancing study and cultivation and attracting the attention of officials across Great Sui's capital. A respected teacher secures a private courtyard for him and teaches him lightning techniques. On a three-month trip to Great Sui's Northern Mountain, the teacher catches lightning by hand and gives Lin Shouyi a Thunder Belly Bottle full of it. *(Chapter 400 (2))* Still, he often thinks of Li Liu and of the long-gone days on the road, sensing that the group has drifted apart since Chen Ping'an left.

When Chen Ping'an returns to the academy, he gives Lin Shouyi a rare, incomplete lightning scripture. Lin Shouyi accepts it, explaining that he knows he will one day repay it with something more valuable. *(Chapter 403 (2))* He agrees to cultivate each morning in Cui Dongshan's courtyard, insisting that the invitation come from Chen Ping'an so that he owes the favour to him rather than to Cui Dongshan.

During an assassination attempt on the academy, Lin Shouyi can do little as a sword cultivator attacks, and nearly loses consciousness from being dragged in and out of the river of time. Chen Ping'an deliberately sits beside him afterwards so that he won't feel useless. Cui Dongshan notes that Lin Shouyi's cultivation is deliberately being held back by Dong Jing, and that he will eventually overtake Xie Xie if she doesn't work hard.

Finally, Lin Shouyi reveals why he is so grateful to Chen Ping'an. It was the offhand offer of a bookcase in the mud on Go Table Mountain, which made him feel included when he was the group's misfit. He adds that he doesn't resent Chen Ping'an for loving the others more. Chen Ping'an, in turn, tells him what he is most grateful for: Lin Shouyi once told him that not all parents are good parents, which made Chen Ping'an realise how rich he was in the love of his own parents. *(Chapter 417 (1))*

### Later Years

On an academy excursion to Dragon Spring Prefecture, Lin Shouyi visits the kiln supervision office where his father worked. His clan has moved to the Great Li capital, and he and his family remain mutually estranged. They show no interest in his growing fame. *(Chapter 471 (2))* Meanwhile, [Dong Shuijing](Dong_Shuijing.md), his rival for Li Liu, admits he could never match him academically, but claims he would take it better if Li Liu chose someone else.

Officials of the Great Li Empire responsible for recruiting talent have contacted his father about him, only for his father to deny having such a son. Mao Xiaodong judges him the most well-rounded of the five students, but worries his cultivation is going so smoothly that he may abandon his studies. *(Chapter 570 (1))* When Chen Ping'an announces Downtrodden Mountain's new ancestral hall, Lin Shouyi offers his congratulations and is invited to cultivate on its mountains. He narrowly misses Li Liu's visit to her brother, to his great disappointment. Li Huai, now his good friend, can't understand what he sees in her.

## Abilities

- **Qi refining:** Lin Shouyi became a Qi refiner through A'Liang's wine and reached the peak of the third tier within months. As of the assassination attempt (ch. 410), he had not yet reached the Middle Five Tiers, partly because Dong Jing deliberately restrains his progress.
- **Five Lightning Righteous Technique:** He learned it from the *Recite Atop Clouds Scripture*, guided by Dong Jing and another academy teacher. He is especially gifted at the calculation and deduction needed to use the scripture's sparse instructions.
- **Talismans:** A'Liang's stack of yellow paper talismans, including the Barrier Shattering, Pearl Within a Plate, Fiery Rain and gold dust talismans.
- **Intellect:** He is a skilled strategist and a strong Go player, though Xie Xie, Yu Lu and Cui Chan all outclass him.

## Possessions

- ***Recite Atop Clouds Scripture***: a Daoist manual of the Five Lightning Righteous Technique.
- **A'Liang's yellow paper talismans.**
- ***Mountain Scouring Album***: an illustrated bestiary of mountain spirits, given by Chen Ping'an.
- **Azure Maiden's Go manual.**
- **Incomplete lightning scripture**: a solitary copy from Stalactite Mountain's Ganoderma Inn, given by Chen Ping'an.
- **Thunder Belly Bottle**: filled with lightning by his academy teacher.
- **Bamboo bookcase**: made by Chen Ping'an from Go Table Mountain courage bamboo, the best of the three.
- **Jade hairpin**: engraved "Shouyi" by Chen Ping'an.
- **Mr. Qi's "Qi" character**: kept at home.

## Trivia

- 守一 (shǒu yī) means "remaining loyal to one thing". When Chen Ping'an threatens to make his bookcase last, Lin Shouyi protests that someone named Shouyi can't go back on his word.
- Li Huai gives conflicting reports on his family's preferences: he once says his mother prefers Dong Shuijing and his sister probably prefers Lin Shouyi, and later says his mother prefers Lin Shouyi and his father prefers Dong Shuijing.
- Before Chen Ping'an left Great Sui, he asked Li Baoping in his farewell letter to keep in touch with the aloof Lin Shouyi so their bond would not weaken.
