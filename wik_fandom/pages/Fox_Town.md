# Fox Town

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Fox_Town)

---
Fox Town is a complex location that is much more than what it appears to be on the surface. It is a border town with a blend of ordinary people, petty officials, and hidden elites, a place where the mundane and the mystical intersect. It is a place where both the local residents and outside travelers are aware of hidden agendas, and power struggles are commonplace. The town's unique location, its unusual residents, and the constant stream of travelers make it a place of intrigue and potential conflict within the narrative.

**General Characteristics:**

- Fox Town is a **border town** of the Great Quan Empire. This location gives it a unique character as a crossroads of different cultures and influences.
- It is considered a **small town** with a mix of ordinary residents, petty officials, and hidden elites.
- The town is known to be **haunted by ghosts and spirits**, which can lead people astray. This has reportedly caused some travelers to go insane.
- The town is not particularly large or significant and is viewed as being unimportant by some of the more powerful figures.
- The town's residents are described as **spirited people**. They are not necessarily powerful but are not stupid either.
- The town has a **local dialect** that can be difficult for outsiders to understand.

**Social and Political Landscape:**

- The town has a **constable** named Ma Ping, whose family has held the job for generations. Ma Ping is described as arrogant and egotistical.
- The town's officials are considered **informal** and **petty**, not regarded as true officials. They are described as arrogant and egotistical.
- The town has a mix of **commoners**, as well as individuals with hidden identities or agendas, including some powerful elites and cultivators.
- There is a sense that the town is not peaceful, with some people believing that the unrest is due to the presence of demons.
- The town residents have a tendency to **gossip**, creating rumors about various events.

**Key Figures and Locations:**

- **Jiu Niang** is the innkeeper of an inn in Fox Town and is described as "foxy" and likes to seduce men. She is a respected figure in the town and has the ability to resolve local disputes.
- There is a **lame young boy** who works at Jiu Niang's Inn. He is perceptive, and can sense the approach of large cavalry troops.
- **Third Grandpa** is a figure of some significance in the town.
- There is a **pharmacy** in the town.
- There is a sense that some people in the town **know more than they reveal** and that they have witnessed many interesting things.
- The inn where Chen Ping'an stays is described as both **mystical and sinister** by the town's residents.

**Conflicts and Interactions:**

- **Outsiders** are sometimes picked on and bullied by the local children.
- There is a tension between the town's residents and outsiders, with a potential for conflict.
- There have been **battles** both inside and outside the inn involving cultivators, which have caused quite a stir in the town.
- The town has been visited by a **True Dragon**, leading to rumors about the nature of the town and its residents.
- A **Yao Clan convoy** passes through the town, with connections to the imperial court.
- Some residents of Fox Town like to visit **Hanging Armor Military Town** in order to see the Yao Clan's cavalry.
- There is an awareness of the **Yao Clan's** activities and their influence, even at a distance.
- There is a sense that powerful figures are hiding within the town.

**Mystical Aspects:**

- There are **mighty figures** hiding in the town that are stronger than those seen in Colorful Garment Nation.
- The presence of **demons** is believed by some to be the reason for the lack of peace in the town.
- The town's location on the border may make it a place where **spiritual energies** gather or mix in interesting ways.
- **Zhong Kui**, a noble scholar, is keeping an eye on the town, implying that there are forces at play that warrant such observation.

**Connections to the Wider World:**

- The town is located on a **border of the Great Quan Empire**, placing it in contact with other regions and powers.
- The town is a stop on the route to other places, like the capital, and serves as a **relay station** of sorts.
- It is located near a well-traveled **official road**, which implies that the town sees its fair share of travelers and merchants.
- There is knowledge of places like **Mirage City** and **Heavenly Palace Peak**, showing that the town is not entirely isolated.
