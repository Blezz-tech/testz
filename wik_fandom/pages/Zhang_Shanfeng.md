**Zhang Shanfeng** is a young Daoist priest from Ground Lying Peak on the Northern Complete Reed Continent and the youngest disciple of Spiritual Master Fire Dragon. He met [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md) on the Ceremony Mountain Kun ship and became one of his closest friends.

## Appearance

Zhang Shanfeng is a young man who wears a Daoist hat and robe and carries a peach wood sword on his back. When he first travels with Chen Ping'an, his clothes are so worn that he feels embarrassed next to the well-dressed Daoist priests of the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> He has unusually long arms, which make his sword stances look smooth and natural.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> He is taller than Chen Ping'an and has to bend down to put an arm around his shoulders.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

Because he follows the talismanic branch of Daoism, he relies on talismans and his sword more than on his body. As a result he is physically frail. A heavy storm once drained him so badly that Chen Ping'an had to carry him on his back.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> In later years he also carries the bronze sword True Martial alongside his peach wood sword.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

## Personality

Zhang Shanfeng is kind, generous and honest. Shortly after meeting Chen Ping'an he insisted on giving him his own Bai Ze Album, and he would only accept one minor snow coin for it. He is shy and gentle by nature, and he tends to dwell on his mistakes until he drives himself into a corner.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> He took it hard when he failed to protect a family that had paid him to drive away a demon, and he blamed himself even though the surviving children did not.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup>

He hates being in debt. After learning that the armor pellet Chen Ping'an gave him was worth far more than Chen Ping'an claimed, he could not rest until he had paid something back.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> Despite his mild manner, he does not shy away from danger. When a group of Divine Edict Sect priests came to punish Yang Huang and Zhuo Jing, he stood up to them, even after one of them tied him up with a rope.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

He knows his cultivation is slow but never gives up. What disappoints him is only that he is not yet strong enough to protect people from demons.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> He cares little for status. When he learned that he had missed the chance to become an external Heavenly Master of Mount Longhu, he barely minded. He was far sadder about having parted with Chen Ping'an twice.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> He has always felt out of place in the wider world. He is at ease only when cultivating at Ground Lying Peak, travelling with his friends, or walking alone among quiet mountains and rivers.

## Background

Zhang Shanfeng once held a scholarly title from the imperial examinations but gave it up to pursue the Dao. He had nowhere to go, and Spiritual Master Fire Dragon took him in as his final disciple. Zhang Shanfeng believed his aptitude was mediocre. He grew up as the youngest disciple at Ground Lying Peak, knowing little about how strong his master and senior brothers really were. When he left to slay demons, his master told him there were none nearby, so he travelled across continents to find some. Several of his senior brothers secretly watched over parts of his journey without his knowledge.

He reached the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent on the Ceremony Mountain Kun ship with almost no money left. Chen Ping'an lent him his jade badge so he could watch a battle from the premium seats, and the two became friends. At first he introduced himself as Zhang Shan, a member of a distant branch of Mount Longhu's Zhang Clan that was not recorded in its genealogy. They left the ship together at Southern Stream Nation and travelled south. At that time he was a third tier Qi refiner practising the Five Lightning Righteous Technique.

At an old estate during a storm, he and [Xu Yuanxia](Xu_Yuanxia.md) tried to protect the ghost couple Yang Huang and Zhuo Jing. When Zhao Liu's group from Divine Edict Sect called him a fraud, he named himself a disciple of Spiritual Master Fire Dragon of Heavensoar Sect. Later he admitted to Chen Ping'an that his real name was Zhang Shanfeng. Chen Ping'an then gave him the damaged armor pellet, saying he could pay later. In the fighting at Blusher Prefecture, the armor saved Daoist Chong Miao from a fatal blow.

At a ferry station on the border of Water Combing Nation, he sold a pair of bamboo chopsticks and gave the money to Chen Ping'an to repay part of his debt. Chen Ping'an then bought him the sword True Martial. Zhang Shanfeng wanted to attend a Liberation Rite of Water and Land in the southeast but was ashamed to break his promise to accompany Chen Ping'an to Old Dragon City. Chen Ping'an sent him off with Xu Yuanxia anyway.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup>

He spent about two years travelling the Azure Phoenix Nation with Xu Yuanxia. When vagrant cultivators hunted a gentle earth bull, he and Xu Yuanxia defended it. They feared its death would set off a deadly earthquake, and Zhang Shanfeng's shoulder was pierced by a bonded flying sword.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup> Chen Ping'an arrived and settled the matter. Soon after, Spiritual Master Fire Dragon appeared, and Chen Ping'an gave Zhang Shanfeng a city god seal made by a former Great Heavenly Master. His master then took him away to visit Mount Longhu. On the way they visited a man in azure living on an island, who praised the fist technique Zhang Shanfeng had created himself.

At Mount Longhu his master hoped he would be recognized as a future external Heavenly Master, but the Heavenly Master Residence declined. Master and disciple then travelled the Middle Earth Divine Continent on foot and obtained water elixir at Mirage Lake.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup> At the Pure Confucian Chen Clan, Zhang Shanfeng met Chen Ping'an's friend [Liu Xianyang](Liu_Xianyang.md) and told him about their journey together.

He later found Chen Ping'an at Paddling Island in the Water Dragon Sect, gave him a bottle of water elixir and stayed nearby while Chen Ping'an refined his bonded item. Back at Ground Lying Peak he told the young Daoist priests stories about slaying demons. Afterwards he set out again with his senior brother Yuan Lingdian.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup>

## Trivia

- He has a low tolerance for alcohol and does not really like drinking, yet he is easily talked into it.
- He made up short verses for Xu Yuanxia and himself, and promised to come up with something grand for Chen Ping'an later.
- He once called Chen Ping'an's name a little tacky, thinking it was a false name like his own.
- His fist technique is deliberately slow. Chen Ping'an could not copy it, though he later practised its stances to calm his mind.
- He sometimes calls himself a Great Heavenly Master in his sleep.
- He believes his master is a mediocre cultivator, unaware of his master's true standing on the Northern Complete Reed Continent.
