# Lady Chu

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Lady_Chu)

---
## Appearance

Lady Chu is a female ghost who appears in a bright red wedding dress made of real silk, carrying an oil-paper umbrella. *(Chapter 122)* She first approaches along a muddy mountain path, letting the mud stain her dress rather than using her powers to repel it. She can peel the skin from her face with a swipe of her hand and plaster it back on. With it in place, she looks like a beautiful young bride-to-be, apart from her deathly pale, sickly complexion. *(Chapter 123)* She can speak without moving her lips, her voice sounding directly beside her listeners' ears.

After [Wei Jin](Wei_Jin.md)'s sword strike shatters her barrier, beads of blood seep from her wedding dress. *(Chapter 126)* Later, when she agrees to leave her residence to the Yin god, she is no longer wearing the wedding dress at all. Chen Ping'an later admits he found her too creepy on their first meeting to notice what she actually looked like.

## Personality

Lady Chu is proud, volatile and terrifying when angered. When a Daoist priest addresses her as "Young Miss", she roars at him to call her "Lady", since she is wearing a wedding dress, and a storm breaks out over the mountains. Yet she can also appear soft and graceful. She welcomes the trapped children into her residence with a curtsy, calling them esteemed guests and apologising that her husband has not yet returned from a distant journey. *(Chapter 125)* She is described as the type of resolute person who would choose to fight to the death without hesitation if pushed too far.

At heart, her cruelty comes from grief. Once a kind young woman deeply in love with a scholar, she came to believe he had betrayed her, and turned her heartbreak on every scholar who passed through her territory. She killed them and planted their corpses in her backyard as "flowers and plants". *(Chapter 476 (2))* [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) later reflects that she loved the scholar very, very much, yet killed many innocent scholars because of it, and that this was a wrong that could not be easily righted. *(Chapter 200 (2))*

## Background

Lady Chu rules a stretch of mountains and rivers along the Embroidered Flower River, where the Great Li Empire has long recognised her residence as her territory. Above its gate hangs a plaque reading "Beautiful Water Noble Wind". *(Chapter 420 (1))* A previous emperor of the Great Li Empire became indebted to her, and her residence still displays a Golden Characters Iron Certificate inscribed "Eternal Harmony of Mountains and Rivers". After an "unfortunate event", successive emperors tacitly allowed her to vent her anger on passing scholars. *(Chapter 124)* The Mohist swordsman Xu Ruo explains that her actions once helped the Song Clan extend the life of the empire, but that the Song Clan later did something that left them feeling guilty towards her. *(Chapter 129)*

Long ago, an impoverished, skinny scholar passed her residence on a rainy night, reciting the Confucian classics to keep up his courage, on his way to take the imperial examinations. *(Chapter 145 (2))* The two fell in love. The scholar hoped to earn the title of virtuous scholar through his merits, so that the imperial court would acknowledge his marriage to a ghost. However, before the Great Li Empire's rise, ambitious scholars were persecuted, and he was preyed upon by a descendant of a powerful clan. He died at Lake View Academy, his corpse sinking to the bottom of its lake. Believing he had abandoned her, Lady Chu began killing scholars.

When [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md)'s group passes along her mountain path, she traps them on a "path to the underworld" and later inside her residence. The [Yin god](Yin_god.md) fights her to a standstill, Wei Jin of Wind Snow Temple shatters her barrier with a single strike, and she is cut apart, retreating into her plaque before reforming. *(Chapter 127)* Xu Ruo warns her that if another scholar vanishes, he will personally take her to the water dungeons.

The [Yin god](Yin_god.md), [Gu Tao,](Yin_god.md) later offers her a way out: she may leave to find her scholar's corpse at Lake View Academy and seek revenge, if she hands her residence to him for a hundred years. She agrees. Years later, when Chen Ping'an returns intending to settle their old grudge, he learns that she has gone to Lake View Academy to collect the corpse of a scholar who died there. *(Chapter 426)* Barred from the academy as a ghost, she forced her way in and nearly died. She used the last of her goodwill with the imperial court to take his remains, and learned the truth: he had never betrayed her, and his love for her was what led to his death. The truth drove her insane. After Gu Tao leaves the residence, she returns in mourning clothes with a coffin, telling anyone who will listen that she is waiting for someone. *(Chapter 567 (3))*

## Trivia

- Her full name is never revealed. "Lady Chu" is how she asks to be addressed.
- Chen Ping'an counts her, along with [Zhu Lu](Zhu_Lu.md) and [Ma Kuxuan](Ma_Kuxuan.md), among the very few people he has ever truly detested. His encounter with her left him with a lasting dislike of troublesome ghosts and yin entities.
- For a long time, Chen Ping'an regarded Wei Jin's strike against her barrier as the pinnacle of flying sword strikes, until he saw Zuo You at the Flood Dragon Trench.
- Her residence later falls within the territory of [Wei Bo](Wei_Bo.md) as Northern Mountain God, although its affairs are managed by the Ritual Office of the Great Li Empire's Ministry of Rites.
- Zhu Lian was disappointed not to learn whether she was beautiful.
