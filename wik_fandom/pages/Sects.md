# Sects

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Sects)

---
**Sects** are the immortal forces of the cultivation world in Sword of Coming. They range from small temples and villas to sect-level forces that hold mountains, blessed lands and the loyalty of whole nations. Most are tied to one of the major teachings, such as Daoism, Buddhism or the Militarians, and many are lower sects of a larger force on another continent. This page lists them by continent, in the order [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md) encounters them.

## Eastern Treasured Vial Continent

### Sun Scorch Mountain

Sun Scorch Mountain is one of the oldest sword sects of the [Eastern Treasured Vial Continent](Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent.md). Besides the main path to its summit, the mountain has a series of "sword paths" opened by its sword immortals. Six had been completed by the start of the story, and two more were expected within a century.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> Its peak is filled with sword intent, and the sword spirits of past ancestors are said to appear there.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

The sect began small and weak. After its founder died in battle, its guardian, the great demon known as the [Mountain-Moving Ape](Mountain-Moving_Ape.md), became its strongest fighter.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> It shares the same origins as the Wind Lightning Field, and the two have been sworn enemies for centuries. The remains of a Sun Scorch Mountain matriarch lie on the sword arena of the Wind Lightning Field, left in the open with a sword driven through her head.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> For hundreds of years the Wind Lightning Field's master Li Tuanjing held the sect down on his own.

The sect sent the Mountain-Moving Ape into the small town of Jewel Small World to guard [Tao Zi](Tao_Zi.md), granddaughter of the Pure Yang Sword Patriarch Tao Kui. While there, the ape struck down [Liu Xianyang](Liu_Xianyang.md) to keep his family's sword scripture away from the Wind Lightning Field. In a later public duel on the Divine Platform of Wind Snow Temple, its celestial maiden Su Jia was defeated by Huang He of the Wind Lightning Field in three exchanges.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup> After Li Tuanjing's death the balance turned, and Sun Scorch Mountain began to rise while its rival was forced to lie low.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> It became a firm ally of the Xu Clan of Light Breeze City and one of the leading powers serving the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md).<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup>

### Dawn Cloud Mountain

Dawn Cloud Mountain is one of the few sects on the continent that follows Buddhism. It was founded by Patriarch Dawn Cloud and traces its roots to one of the five branches of Buddhism.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> Its wealth comes from the Cloud Root Stone mined on the mountain, a pure material prized by Daoist alchemists across several continents. It is not counted among the top sects, but it never lacks resources.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup> It sent [Cai Jinjian](Cai_Jinjian.md) into the small town in search of a fated opportunity, and she met her end there. The sect later became one of four sects-in-waiting under the Great Li Empire.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup>

### Wind Lightning Field

The Wind Lightning Field is a sword sect and the sworn enemy of Sun Scorch Mountain. A sword prodigy who betrayed Sun Scorch Mountain was given shelter there for most of his life, and the sword scripture handed down in Liu Xianyang's family came from him. Its master Li Tuanjing reached the tenth tier at forty and was long regarded as the strongest cultivator on the continent below the Upper Five Tiers. He died at the end of a later battle between the two sects, and his disciple Huang He took over, alongside the swordsman [Liu Baqiao](Liu_Baqiao.md).<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup>

### Wind Snow Temple

Wind Snow Temple and True Martial Mountain are the two great Militarian sects of the continent, one in the north and one in the south.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup> Wind Snow Temple has six branches and places great weight on refining the body. It is fiercely protective of its own. When a villa in the Water Talisman Empire wronged [Ruan Qiong](Ruan_Qiong.md), all six branches struck back until the villa fell to a second-rate sect.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup> Its smallest branch, the Divine Platform, produced [Wei Jin](Wei_Jin.md), the youngest swordsman on the continent to reach the Upper Five Tiers. The temple's Divine Platform also served as neutral ground for the duel between Sun Scorch Mountain and the Wind Lightning Field.

### True Martial Mountain

True Martial Mountain is the other Militarian ancestral temple of the continent. It is more involved in mortal empires than Wind Snow Temple, and many of its cultivators serve as generals in the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md), [Great Sui Nation](Great_Sui_Nation.md) and the fallen Lu Empire. It is said that people there can speak directly with ancient deities in one of its great halls.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup> [Ma Kuxuan](Ma_Kuxuan.md) of Apricot Blossom Alley was taken there by one of its swordsmen and became its most famous young cultivator.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup>

### Divine Edict Sect

The Divine Edict Sect of [Southern Stream Nation](Southern_Stream_Nation.md) is the foremost of the continent's three Daoist sects and receives most of its incense and offerings. It owns one of the 72 blessed lands, the Limpid Pond Blessed Land. It is itself a lower sect of a larger Daoist branch.<sup>[[16]](#ref-16)</sup> Its sect master Qi Zhen became the continent's only Heavenly Lord and holds the authority to recognise Daoist True Lords across it.<sup>[[17]](#ref-17)</sup> The sect is also home to [He Xiaoliang](He_Xiaoliang.md), its Jade Girl. Its elder Zhao Liu once led a group to punish the former disciple Yang Huang and his wife.<sup>[[18]](#ref-18)</sup>

### Sword Water Villa

Sword Water Villa is a martial arts villa in Water Combing Nation, home of the sword saint Song Yushao and a large waterfall deep in its mountains.<sup>[[19]](#ref-19)</sup> It hosts the election of the alliance leader of the nation's martial world.<sup>[[20]](#ref-20)</sup> Chen Ping'an stayed there with [Xu Yuanxia](Xu_Yuanxia.md) and [Zhang Shanfeng](Zhang_Shanfeng.md) on his first trip south.

### Golden Osmanthus Temple

The Golden Osmanthus Temple is a Daoist temple at the top of Azure Waist Mountain near [Azure Phoenix Nation](Azure_Phoenix_Nation.md). It is closed to outsiders for most of the year. Its master is regarded as a true immortal close to the Golden Core Tier, and martial artists from several nations climbed the mountain when it opened its doors to take nine disciples.<sup>[[21]](#ref-21)</sup>

### Dragon Spring Sword Sect

The Dragon Spring Sword Sect was founded by Ruan Qiong in Dragon Spring Prefecture after he left Wind Snow Temple. It holds Divine Elegance Mountain and many surrounding peaks.<sup>[[22]](#ref-22)</sup> It began with very few disciples. Later the Great Li court chose a dozen talented children to train there.<sup>[[23]](#ref-23)</sup>

### True Realm Sect

The True Realm Sect was founded at [Bamboo Scroll Lake](Bamboo_Scroll_Lake.md) as a lower sect of the Jade Tablet Sect of Parasol Leaf Continent. Under its sect leader Jiang Shangzhen, it brought the lake's islands and their vagrant cultivators under its rule. Many of them became guest elders and official immortals.<sup>[[24]](#ref-24)</sup>

### Downtrodden Mountain

> *Main article: [Downtrodden Mountain](Downtrodden_Mountain.md)*

Downtrodden Mountain in Dragon Spring Prefecture is Chen Ping An's own mountain. It grew from a single purchase into a sect with its own ancestral hall, and it later came to own the Lotus Root Blessed Land.

## Northern Complete Reed Continent

### Ceremony Mountain

Ceremony Mountain is a large force in the southwest of the [Northern Complete Reed Continent](Northern_Complete_Reed_Continent.md). At its peak it had two immortals of the Upper Five Tiers, but none had appeared there for over 120 years. Its Kun ship makes a yearly round trip across three continents, carrying passengers and cargo, and [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md) travelled on it to the [Eastern Treasured Vial Continent](Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent.md)'s south.<sup>[[25]](#ref-25)</sup> The ship was later struck down over the Luminous Vermilion Empire by Mr. Sword Urn, a sacrifice planned so that [Xie Shi](Xie_Shi.md) could move against Lake View Academy.<sup>[[26]](#ref-26)</sup>

### Flax Robe Sect

The Flax Robe Sect stands above Skeleton Shoal in the far south of the continent, with Mural City at its foot. It runs an intercontinental ship and is home to many righteous ghostly cultivators.<sup>[[27]](#ref-27)</sup> Its long-standing enemy is Gao Cheng, the ghostly lord of Skeleton Wall City in Malicious Ghost Valley. Even the sect's past leaders admitted he was a great figure among ghosts.<sup>[[28]](#ref-28)</sup>

### Lofty Cloud Palace

The Lofty Cloud Palace belongs to the Yang Clan of the Great Origin Empire's Esteemed Mystery Department. Its owner is both the department's leader and the empire's imperial preceptor, and three of his ancestors reached the Upper Five Tiers. The clan is known across the continent for repaying kindness many times over. Its young heir Yang Ningxing is one of the continent's ten dragons and phoenixes.<sup>[[29]](#ref-29)</sup>

### Spring Dew Garden

The Spring Dew Garden sits between Skeleton Shoal and the Fine Wood Mountain Range. It earns its living from an immortal ship that sells rare plants it raises. It keeps three heirloom treasures, one of them a ten-thousand-year-old locust tree. Every New Year's Eve it hosts the Farewelling the Year Banquet, where thousands of wandering merchants gather.<sup>[[30]](#ref-30)</sup> Its patriarch is a Nascent Tier cultivator.

### Jade Forest Sect

The Jade Forest Sect grew rich through trade. It used that wealth to push its founder to the Unpolished Jade Tier, which made it a sect-level force, though other cultivators mocked how he got there.<sup>[[31]](#ref-31)</sup> It is the home of Bai Shang, the strongest sword immortal of the north, and his only disciple Xu Xuan.<sup>[[32]](#ref-32)</sup>

### Refreshing Sect

The Refreshing Sect was founded by [He Xiaoliang](He_Xiaoliang.md) of the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent. She found a small world on the continent and tamed many great demons and spirits there, and with Xie Shi's support she quickly established it as a powerful sect.<sup>[[33]](#ref-33)</sup> After she clashed with Xu Xuan and offended Bai Shang, the sect was left isolated, and Xie Shi sent her to apologise to the Jade Forest Sect.<sup>[[34]](#ref-34)</sup>

### Golden Scale Palace

The Golden Scale Palace lies deep in the mountains on the border of the Great Seal Script Empire, and it is on very bad terms with that empire.<sup>[[35]](#ref-35)</sup> Its talismans grant the power of flight. Its chief guest elder is a Golden Core Tier swordsman, and Chen Ping An once passed himself off as one of its swordsmen.<sup>[[36]](#ref-36)</sup>

### Deer Cutter Mountain

Deer Cutter Mountain is an assassin sect in the south of the continent. Its founder's rule is that it sends assassins only once per job. If the attempt fails, it returns half the payment and never tries again.<sup>[[37]](#ref-37)</sup> Its assassins, led by a Nascent Tier cultivator, made an attempt on Chen Ping An and failed.<sup>[[38]](#ref-38)</sup>

### Duckweed Sword Lake

Duckweed Sword Lake is a sword sect led by the sword immortal Li Cai. Her disciple Rong Chang is a Nascent Tier sword cultivator, though she has said he will not be the next sect leader.<sup>[[39]](#ref-39)</sup>

### Great Emblem Sword Sect

The Great Emblem Sword Sect is a sect-level sword sect in the north of the continent. Its sect leader and another senior sword immortal travelled to the Sword Qi Great Wall to fight, leaving the sect in a dangerous position.<sup>[[40]](#ref-40)</sup> It is home to the sword immortal Han Huaizi and to [Qi Jinglong](Qi_Jinglong.md), its newest sword immortal, who travelled with Chen Ping An under the name Qi Jinglong.<sup>[[41]](#ref-41)</sup>

### Ground Lying Peak

Ground Lying Peak is the home of Spiritual Master Fire Dragon, who oversees four other major branches through disciples who founded their own peaks. Its disciples include [Zhang Shanfeng](Zhang_Shanfeng.md). Few outside know how strong the peak truly is.<sup>[[42]](#ref-42)</sup> Zhang Shanfeng once called it Heavensoar Sect.

### Lightning God Manor

The Lightning God Manor is known for its five lightning talismans. Their full power depends on the five ancestral seals of its ancestral hall, and its earth immortal spiritual masters inscribe them by hand.<sup>[[43]](#ref-43)</sup>

### Water Dragon Sect

The Water Dragon Sect is an ancient sect built on the great river that runs east to west through the centre of the continent. Its ancestral hall was once one of the river's three ancient water temples.<sup>[[44]](#ref-44)</sup> It owns the Dragon Palace Small World, one of the thirty-six small worlds, and Paddling Island, where Chen Ping An refined a bonded item.<sup>[[45]](#ref-45)</sup> Its sect leader is Sun Jie.

## Parasol Leaf Continent

### Parasol Leaf Sect

The Parasol Leaf Sect in the north and the Jade Tablet Sect in the south are the two most powerful sects of the [Parasol Leaf Continent](Parasol_Leaf_Continent.md).<sup>[[46]](#ref-46)</sup> Almost all of the continent's ferry stations lie in the north, which is part of why the Parasol Leaf Sect can keep the Jade Tablet Sect in check.<sup>[[47]](#ref-47)</sup> It is on good terms with the Ding Clan of [Old Dragon City](Old_Dragon_City.md), and one of its cultivators once helped that clan recover from near collapse.<sup>[[48]](#ref-48)</sup>

### Jade Tablet Sect

The Jade Tablet Sect is the leading power of the south. Its Jiang Clan has controlled the Cloud Cave Blessed Land for generations. [Jiang Shangzhen](Jiang_Shangzhen.md) of the clan entered the Lotus Flower Blessed Land under the name Zhou Fei and later became the first leader of its lower sect, the True Realm Sect of Bamboo Scroll Lake.

### Planchette Writing Sect

The Planchette Writing Sect is a sect-level force in the central region of the continent. Its two Unpolished Jade Tier cultivators are Dao partners. The sect leader spent his whole fortune building the bustling Sky Calling Street for his partner, who loved raising spirits.<sup>[[49]](#ref-49)</sup> Later a great demon of the Upper Five Tiers destroyed almost half the sect, killing one of the two and wounding the other.<sup>[[50]](#ref-50)</sup>

### Peace and Tranquility Mountain

Peace and Tranquility Mountain is the most powerful sect in the central region, at least the equal of the Planchette Writing Sect, but so secluded that it almost never appears in the world. It gathers the knowledge of every branch of alchemy.<sup>[[51]](#ref-51)</sup> For thousands of years it held demons captive in a well, guarded for over three thousand years by the [Mountain-Moving Ape](Mountain-Moving_Ape.md). When its sect master left to stop a twelfth tier great demon from reaching the sea, the prison opened. More than half the captive demons escaped, and the central region fell into chaos.<sup>[[52]](#ref-52)</sup> Its Daoist priests led the fight that followed. Many of them died, earning the respect of the Confucian academies that came to help.<sup>[[53]](#ref-53)</sup> The Daoist nun Huang Ting, who lived in the Lotus Flower Blessed Land as Tong Qingqing and Fan Wan'er, belongs to this sect.<sup>[[54]](#ref-54)</sup>

### Golden Summit Daoist Temple

The Golden Summit Daoist Temple is a small Daoist force regarded as heretical but well respected, with fewer than a hundred priests. Its priests like to cultivate their minds among mortals, often hiding who they are. Its abbot is a five-hundred-year-old Nascent Tier earth immortal renowned in the north of the continent.<sup>[[55]](#ref-55)</sup>

### Lotus Flower Blessed Land

The [Lotus Flower Blessed Land](Lotus_Flower_Blessed_Land.md) has its own forces, which [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md) met during his time there.

#### Observing Dao Temple

The Observing Dao Temple is the home of the old Daoist priest who owns the blessed land. Chen Ping An searched the capital of [Southern Garden Nation](Southern_Garden_Nation.md) for it without success. In truth the temple does not exist as a building, and the whole blessed land is the old Daoist's place for observing the Dao.<sup>[[56]](#ref-56)</sup>

#### Mirror Heart Hall

The Mirror Heart Hall is a holy land of the martial world led by Tong Qingqing. Rather than chasing power among cultivators, it works to place its women in the courts of all four lands.<sup>[[57]](#ref-57)</sup> Its disciple Zhou Shuzhen rose to become empress of Southern Garden Nation.

#### Spring Tide Palace

The Spring Tide Palace was led by Zhou Fei, one of the Upper Ten Elites, who was in truth Jiang Shangzhen of the Jade Tablet Sect. The palace was later moved to Parasol Leaf Continent.<sup>[[58]](#ref-58)</sup>

#### Lake Mountain Faction

The Lake Mountain Faction of Pine Song Nation was led by [Yu Zhenyi](Yu_Zhenyi.md), the first person in the blessed land's history to switch from martial arts to cultivation.

#### Bird's Eye View Peak

Bird's Eye View Peak in [Northern Jin Nation](Northern_Jin_Nation.md) is the home of the sword immortal Lu Fang.<sup>[[59]](#ref-59)</sup>

#### Reverence Pagoda

The Reverence Pagoda compiled the list of the Upper Ten Elites, the greatest fighters of the blessed land. Its leader was Zhou Shuzhen. After the great battles in Southern Garden Nation, it announced it would no longer rank the Upper Ten Elites.<sup>[[60]](#ref-60)</sup>

## Middle Earth Divine Continent

### Mount Longhu

Mount Longhu on the [Middle Earth Divine Continent](Middle_Earth_Divine_Continent.md) is a holy land of Daoism and the seat of the Heavenly Master Residence, which belongs to its Zhang Clan.<sup>[[61]](#ref-61)</sup> Its priests follow the talismanic branch of Daoism, relying on talismans and peach wood swords rather than the body.<sup>[[62]](#ref-62)</sup> Heavenly Masters are ranked in tiers. The first rank are immortals of the Upper Five Tiers, honoured in the ancestral hall, while external Heavenly Masters who do not carry the Zhang surname rank lower. Spiritual Master Fire Dragon of Ground Lying Peak is one of its external Heavenly Masters. When he asked the residence to recognise his disciple [Zhang Shanfeng](Zhang_Shanfeng.md) as a future external Heavenly Master, it declined.<sup>[[63]](#ref-63)</sup>

### White Emperor City

White Emperor City is a city among the iridescent clouds of the Middle Earth Divine Continent and one of the ten wonders of the world.<sup>[[64]](#ref-64)</sup> Its city lord is the greatest Go player in the world. [Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md) lost to him but put up a strong fight. Because of the city lord, no empire dares to award the tenth dan in Go to any player.<sup>[[65]](#ref-65)</sup>

## Heavenly World

### White Jade Capital

The White Jade Capital of the [Heavenly World](Heavenly_World.md) is the seat of Daoism's three branches, known for its five cities and twelve towers.<sup>[[66]](#ref-66)</sup> [Lu Chen](Lu_Chen.md) is one of its three branch masters, and he once tried to draw Chen Ping An there. Not every Daoist sect falls under it. The Planchette Writing Sect, for example, does not. The [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md) built a small replica of the White Jade Capital in its own capital as a weapon, able to strike down powerful cultivators north of Lake View Academy.<sup>[[67]](#ref-67)</sup>

## Other sects

### Rain Dragon Sect

The Rain Dragon Sect stands on an island far out at sea, so distant from the three nearest continents that its word is law in its waters. Its ancestor rebuilt the statue of an ancient rain god said to have commanded the True Dragons that brought rain. By old tradition, its female cultivators choose their Dao partners by tossing down embroidered balls made by the sect.<sup>[[68]](#ref-68)</sup> Fu Ke is the youngest Golden Core Tier cultivator in its history.<sup>[[69]](#ref-69)</sup>

### Green Goat Palace

Green Goat Palace stands on Heavenly Palace Peak, which hosts a large immortal ferry station. Its six branch palaces each worship a different Daoist immortal. Its master Lu Yong is a Nascent Tier cultivator known for refining pills and for being one of the most peaceful cultivators alive. After Jiang Shangzhen settled an old grievance there, the Jade Tablet Sect agreed to buy and sell every pill the palace makes.<sup>[[70]](#ref-70)</sup>

### Golden Crow Palace

The Golden Crow Palace is led by a Golden Core Tier swordsman known for fearing his wife, the daughter of a great mountain lord.<sup>[[71]](#ref-71)</sup> He badly wounded the patriarch of the nearby Yellow Wind Valley.<sup>[[33]](#ref-33)</sup>
