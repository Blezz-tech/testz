**Song Changjing** is the younger brother of the Great Li emperor and the most feared general in the empire. He arrived in the [Small Town](Small_Town.md) as the new kiln supervision official, revealed himself as [Song Jixin](Song_Jixin.md)'s uncle, and left the Jewel Small World as one of only a handful of tenth tier martial artists on the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent.

## Appearance

Song Changjing usually wears a white robe and a jade belt, and he has a habit of resting his hand on the belt as he talks. The robe is the Flowing Water Robe, one of the finest immortal artifacts in the Great Li Empire. *(Chapter 114)* His most recognisable item is an expensive but very old fox fur coat that he keeps wearing long after anyone else would have replaced it. Even his own coach driver teases him about it. *(Chapter 66)* Years later, standing at the head of a Mohist war fleet, he is still wearing the same shabby coat. 

## Personality

Song Changjing is bold, blunt and openly arrogant. It is said that his three great pleasures in life are winning glory on the battlefield, killing prodigies and fighting gods. *(Chapter 49)* When [Bai Yuan](Mountain-Moving_Ape.md), the Mountain-Moving Ape, picked him as a target to vent his anger on, Song Changjing charged straight at him down Fortune Street and forced the ape back a step. He looks down on scholars as all talk and no action, though he grudgingly admits that some of their sayings hold truths a soldier could never put into words.

He is hard and ruthless. He has massacred cities, and he nearly killed Chen Dui on the spot for talking back to him, stopping only when Song Jixin pointed out it would hurt his reputation. *(Chapter 50 (2))* He also has a sense of honour. After years of fighting the Lu Empire's general Ye Qing, whom he cursed as a stubborn old fart, the first thing he did on breaking into Lu territory was offer wine and incense at Ye Qing's grave. *(Chapter 93 (1))*

He is sharp at reading people. He saw at once that Chen Ping'an was the root of Song Jixin's inner demon, and he pushed his nephew hard to face it. *(Chapter 47)* Under the teasing he cares about Song Jixin, and once, on the road out of the small town, he patted the boy's head with unexpected gentleness. Above all he is devoted to martial arts. He tells Song Jixin his only ambition is to see the scenery that lies beyond the End Tier.

## Background

### The small town

Song Changjing joined the army as a young prince and spent more than twenty years fighting on the empire's borders. Within a single year he conquered the Lu Empire, the Great Li's former overlord. He came to the small town as the new kiln supervision official and introduced himself to Song Jixin with the words "I'm an extremely unfortunate person. I'm actually your uncle!" *(Chapter 33)*

In the town he sized up [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) and gave him two tasks, the first being to find a chance to kidnap the little girl travelling with the old ape of Sun Scorch Mountain. He also explained to Song Jixin, with cold frankness, why [Liu Xianyang](Liu_Xianyang.md) had been struck down: the boy had attracted envy with his great fortune and had no backers to protect him. That night he exchanged three punches with Bai Yuan on Fortune Street and taunted Qi Jingchun to step in.

### Li Er and the tenth tier

On the road out of the Jewel Small World, a plain middle-aged man blocked the path. It was [Li Er](Li_Er.md). Song Changjing sent the carriages on ahead and fought him in the pouring rain until his white robe was soaked with mud, feeling that the fight would decide whether he lived or died, and whether he would wake up at the ninth tier or the tenth. *(Chapter 62)* The battle was savage. At one point Song Changjing crashed down from the sky into a crater near the carriages, and at another he punched Li Er into a hillside. Li Er had the upper hand the whole time and could have beaten him to death. On Old Man Yang's secret instructions he held back, and the fight became the catalyst that let Song Changjing break through. He became only the second End Tier grandmaster on the continent, while still under forty. *(Chapter 168 (2))*

He reached the capital pale and wrapped in his fox fur coat. His brother greeted him warmly, pleased that he had reached the tenth tier. *(Chapter 112)* When a deadly attack struck during Song Jixin's trial at the White Jade Capital, Song Changjing refused to retreat even after a golden thread sliced open his robe. [A'Liang](A'Liang.md) tossed him his own saber, Auspicious Talisman.

### Interim ruler

Song Changjing won great loyalty in the army and was allowed to act on the emperor's behalf. During a night of upheaval in the capital, he sat guard on the outer city road from dusk until dawn beside Luan Changye. Many officials would not have been surprised if he had declared himself emperor the next morning. *(Chapter 206 (3))*

After the emperor's death he became interim ruler. The late emperor had actually wanted Song Changjing to take the throne, but Song Changjing refused and burned the will in front of Cui Chan. In the succession dispute he backed Song Jixin, while Cui Chan backed Song He. *(Chapter 479 (2))* Song He became emperor, and Song Changjing remained the true master of the Great Li military. He led the southward campaign himself. When a mountain god's Dao Manifestation swung a giant sword at his flagship, he shattered its godly body with a single punch. *(Chapter 456 (2))*

Years later, when Song Jixin is struggling as feudal lord of Old Dragon City, Song Changjing visits him and promises to do one thing for him. *(Chapter 631 (6))*

## Trivia

- Song Changjing is still at the Thriving Qi Tier, the first of the tenth tier's three minor tiers. Li Er, who reached the tenth tier after him, has already moved on to the Simplification Tier. *(Chapter 385 (1))*
- The Daoist Saber Room on Stalactite Mountain once posted a bounty on his head. The reason given was that a continent as small as the Eastern Treasured Vial did not deserve a tenth tier martial artist.
- Li Er later said Song Changjing's aptitude was very good, but his punches were too soft.
- A general of the Water Combing Nation, Chu Hao, regards Song Changjing as his teacher in military strategy, though the two have never met.
- He killed a cultivator from the School of Naturalists in the capital after the man tried to turn the emperor into a puppet.
