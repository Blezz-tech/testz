# Azure Phoenix Nation

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Azure_Phoenix_Nation)

---
The **Azure Phoenix Nation** is a nation in the southeastern region of [Eastern Treasured Vial Continent](Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent.md), ruled by the Imperial Tang Clan. Small by the continent's standards but wealthy and proud, it is known for its many Daoist and Buddhist temples and for the grand debate between Buddhism and Daoism that its emperor staged in the capital. [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md) passed through it with [Pei Qian](Pei_Qian.md) after leaving Old Dragon City, and the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md) quietly drew it into its plans for the continent.

## Geography

The nation lies in the southeast of the continent. Old Dragon City runs a louchuan route to its border. *(Chapter 373)* On that border stands Wasp Tail Ferry Station, a former small town where the Azure Phoenix Nation, Fortune Mountain Nation and Lofty Sky Nation meet. The Imperial Tang Clan has fought its two neighbors over the town for centuries, with both brush and blade. The capital lies around eight hundred kilometers from the ferry station. *(Chapter 374)* A second, slightly larger immortal ferry station sits in the east, with routes to nearby immortal abodes and to the Fishing Platform in the north, where travelers cast lines into a sea of clouds. *(Chapter 398)*

The nation borders the lands of the Cloud Forest Jiang Clan. Of the three nations around Wasp Tail Ferry Station it is the strongest, but it is still a minor power when set against the whole continent. *(Chapter 390)*

## History

The Imperial Tang Clan founded the nation with the help of an earth immortal who called himself Wei Qian. The founding emperor raised pagodas and commissioned portraits for twenty four founding ministers. Wei Qian was ranked low among them, yet he outlived the other twenty three and all their great-great-grandchildren, simply changing his name to Wei Liang. *(Chapter 399)* The founding emperor also named an ancient apricot tree near Wasp Tail Ferry Station the Emperor Tree.

For several hundred years the nation stayed calm, with none of its distant governors or nobles ever rising against the throne. *(Chapter 376)* While war raged across the central region of the continent, tens of thousands of nobles and scholars fled south, and the Azure Phoenix Nation took in more of them than any of its neighbors.

After the Azure Phoenix Nation held a Liberation Rite of Water and Land, its two neighbors answered by holding Heavenly Worship Ceremonies at the same time. Emperor Tang Li responded by announcing a debate between Buddhism and Daoism, with the winner to become the national religion ranked above Confucianism. The debate drew cultivators, scholars and nobles from across the southeast and made the nation the region's main topic. *(Chapter 389)*. Scholars who had fled south were furious that Confucianism had been left out. In the end Tang Li named Confucianism the nation's fundamental teaching and left Buddhism and Daoism to settle second and third place. The little-known White Cloud Temple became a royal Daoist temple, and a young monk from White Water Temple began preaching in the streets.

## Government

The emperor gives out royal titles without lands. Kings and county kings live in residences in the capital that they may use but do not own, and they lose them along with the title. Five chief military governor offices, in the north, south, east, west and center, handle grain transport, salt and iron, and other vital affairs. The four outer governors earned their posts through military service. The central office has always been held by Wei Liang, who keeps his power hidden and is the one figure the clan's chief guest elder, Zhou Lingzhi, truly fears. *(Chapter 396)*

Unlike most courts on the continent, the Imperial Tang Clan deals firmly with the immortals of the mountains and never raises up immortal forces without reason. The emperor sorts out disputes between the two worlds himself.

## Religion

The nation has more Buddhist monasteries than any other on the continent, where Buddhism is otherwise rare. Daoist immortals and eminent monks hold court-funded Liberation Rites of Water and Land and Heavenly Worship Ceremonies there, and the walls of the monasteries are covered in the writing of famous scholars and poets. White Water Temple, more than a thousand years old, has a spring famed for brewing tea, while the monks of North Mountain Temple are the most active at court. *(Chapter 378)*

## Relationship with the Great Li Empire

The Great Li Empire had planted a spy holding a second-rank Peace and Safety Tablet in the nation long before the debate, a sign that it meant to take the Azure Phoenix Nation. [Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md) came in person and met Wei Liang first. After hearing the empire's plans, Wei Liang agreed to cooperate. Great Li agents then tried to ruin the retired assistant minister Liu Jingting of the Lion Garden so that he could not unite the scholars who had fled south. *(Chapter 406)* The scheme eventually succeeded, and the migrant clans fell to quarreling over who should lead them. *(Chapter 414)*

Li Baozhen of the Green Ripple Pavilion chose the nation as the base for his career. Afterward he used its scholars, officials and martial artists as pieces in an experiment at the grassroots, while Wei Liang watched and set rules that even Li Baozhen had to follow. During this period the nation seemed to be entering a golden age. *(Chapter 535)* Later the Great Li court rated the nation's grain waterway project and moved Liu Qingfeng to a border prefecture, and it seemed ready to promote Wei Liang. Once a remote backwater, the nation had become a place rich in prospects for officials. *(Chapter 652)*

## Trivia

- Its blue sandalwood Xuan paper is sold on several continents and has made its emperors some of the richest rulers in the southeast.
- Cui Dongshan revealed the nation's martial fortune at a county martial sage temple. One drop of golden rain fell every fifteen minutes, which he judged better than several nations he had seen but far short of the old Lu Empire. *(Chapter 381)*
- An earth bull equal to a Dragon Gate Tier cultivator hid in its mountains for more than two hundred years before cultivators tried to kill it. *(Chapter 375)*
- Cui Dongshan told Chen Ping'an to try two dishes in the capital, Buddha's Temptation and bittern flapjack. Chen Ping'an bought Pei Qian a sparrowhawk kite, a local specialty.
- People there are known for being especially proud of their nation.
