**Gu Tao** is a yin god of the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md), the husband of Gu Can's mother and the father of [Gu Can](Gu_Can.md). He once lived in the small town of [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md) and never made it home, and his soul has been wandering as a yin god since. He secretly protected [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) and his companions on the road to White Emperor City without revealing who he was.

## Appearance

Gu Tao appears as a man whose face closely resembles [Gu Can](Gu_Can.md)'s. *(Chapter 426)* In his yin god form he is hazy and faint, and he can make himself entirely invisible or sharpen his appearance at will.

## Personality

Gu Tao is restrained and steady. He kept his identity hidden from Chen Ping'an for the entire journey, bound by his duty and unwilling to break the rules of his station even when he burned to reveal himself. When he finally did, he bowed deeply and apologized for leaving without a word. *(Chapter 426)* He is unafraid of punishment and speaks plainly even with a lance skewering him to the ground.

He is also quietly shrewd. The Embroidered Flower River God learned that Gu Tao and Chen Ping'an had outmaneuvered him without making a single obvious move. *(Chapter 435 (2))*

## Background

Gu Tao was a man from the small town who died before [Gu Can](Gu_Can.md) ever knew him, yet his soul never left. He became a yin god under [Old Man Yang](Old_Man_Yang.md)'s authority and was drawn into the service of the Great Li Empire. When Chen Ping'an left the town heading north, Gu Tao was already trailing behind him unseen.

## Plot

### Journey to White Emperor City

On the road north, Gu Tao followed Chen Ping'an's group from a distance, shielding them from dangers they never knew about. [Lin Shouyi](Lin_Shouyi.md) made contact with him first, and Gu Tao began passing the boy quiet warnings about approaching threats. *(Chapter 119 (1))* When enemies closed in near the Embroidered Flower River, he swam alongside the ship like a fish, keeping watch. *(Chapter 119 (2))*

At the inn on the long corridor, he rested a heavy hand on Chen Ping'an's shoulder to hold him back from running into a dangerous scene, then faded away once the moment passed. *(Chapter 112)* He never spoke his name and made sure Chen Ping'an could not look at him directly.

He was with them all the way to White Emperor City, standing in the corridor above when A'Liang's clash with the sky lit up the night, staring up at it in a daze. *(Chapter 112)*

### Reunion

Years later, when Chen Ping'an visited the Chu Clan's riverside residence in the Great Li Empire, he found that its new master was the very yin god who had walked with him all those years ago. Gu Tao revealed himself, told Chen Ping'an about Gu Can's fated opportunity, and thanked him for looking after Little Can back in the town. *(Chapter 426)*

The Embroidered Flower River God had forbidden him from leaving the residence, and for slipping away to find Chen Ping'an, Gu Tao was run through the stomach with a golden lance and pinned to the ground. He did not cry out. The river god chose to delay further punishment only because he still needed Gu Tao's help to repair the residence's water fortune. *(Chapter 426)*

The river god later discovered that Gu Tao and Chen Ping'an had quietly talked circles around him, and he was not pleased about it. *(Chapter 435 (2))*

### Later

By the time [Lin Shouyi](Lin_Shouyi.md) had advanced far in his cultivation, he and Gu Tao had grown into genuine friends and kept in regular contact. *(Chapter 476 (1))* Gu Tao also crossed paths with [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) again during a later visit, and the two parted on warm terms.

By the later volumes, Gu Tao had risen from yin god to full deity and held a proper divine post in the empire, though he still carried the wound the river god had put through him. *(Chapter 567 (3))*

## Abilities

- **Yin god body:** He can become invisible, drift through water and solid barriers and project his presence at a distance.
- **Deific form:** After becoming a proper deity of the Great Li Empire, his body hardened against spiritual attacks, though a river god's golden lance could still injure him severely.
- **Stealth and sensing:** He tracked Chen Ping'an's group over a long journey without being detected by cultivators far above his level.

## Relationships

- **[Gu Can](Gu_Can.md):** His son, whom he never met while alive. He watched over Gu Can from a distance and felt genuine joy hearing that the boy was thriving. *(Chapter 426)*
- **[Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md):** He protected Chen Ping'an on the road north out of gratitude for everything Chen Ping'an had done for Gu Can. He thinks of him as a genuinely good person. *(Chapter 426)*
- **[Lin Shouyi](Lin_Shouyi.md):** The first person to make contact with him on the road. By the later chapters they are close friends who keep in touch across great distances. *(Chapter 476 (1))*
- **[Old Man Yang](Old_Man_Yang.md):** His master and the one who set him on the path of a yin god. The full nature of what Old Man Yang asked of him remains unclear.
- **Embroidered Flower River God:** His superior in the empire's divine hierarchy, who holds Gu Tao to strict rules and is quick to punish him when he steps out of line.

## Trivia

- Chen Ping'an said "I never would have expected that Uncle Gu actually became a yin god" when he recognized him at the Chu residence.
- The river god almost destroyed his soul entirely at their confrontation, and only held back because the residence repair needed him.
- He obtained news about Bamboo Scroll Lake from Red Candle Town several times, though the Great Li Empire fed him only what it wanted him to know. *(Chapter 426)*
- Lin Shouyi's ability to communicate with him on the road north was one of the first signs of Lin Shouyi's unusual spiritual sensitivity.
