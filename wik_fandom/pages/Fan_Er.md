**Fan Er** is the heir of the Fan Clan of Old Dragon City and the martial arts disciple of [Zheng Dafeng](Zheng_Dafeng.md). He met [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md) at the Dust Medicinal Shop and became one of his closest friends, and he later put his own money behind Downtrodden Mountain.

## Appearance

Fan Er is a good-looking boy, and the women working in the Dust Medicinal Shop often comment on his looks. His clear eyes show every emotion he feels.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> By the time Chen Ping'an returns to Old Dragon City, Fan Er has grown into a handsome young man without losing his lively air.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

## Personality

Fan Er is cheerful, open and quick to make friends. He told Chen Ping'an about his life after knowing him only a few days. He is smart and well raised, and despite his clan's wealth he is aware of how ordinary people struggle.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> He prefers the women of the medicine shop, who treat him like any boy, to the clan's celestial maidens, who see him as the future clan leader.

He has a strong sense of right and wrong. When a woman from the Dust Medicinal Shop died after being defiled by a Fang Clan descendant, he protested to his father even though everyone told him it was a trivial matter, and he was confined to the ancestral hall for a month for it. Zheng Dafeng judges him to be sturdy on the outside but fragile on the inside, a result of his easy upbringing. Chen Ping'an envies how carefree he is and wishes he could live the same way.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

Fan Er has little ambition. He only studies martial arts because he lacks the aptitude to be a Qi refiner, and all he really wants is to reach the eighth tier so he can fly. When Zheng Dafeng offered to make him the strongest fourth tier martial artist, he said he would rather relax and enjoy life like his teacher.

## Background

Fan Er is the second child of the Fan Clan leader. His older half-sister, [Fan Junmao](Fan_Junmao.md), was born to a different mother. He admires her deeply and is fond of her mother, who praises him when he does well and calmly explains his mistakes, while his own mother spoils him. His name simply marks him as the second child, and he is a little embarrassed by it. The character for two is also slang for a fool, and Fan Junmao remarks that their parents named him well.

He is the future leader of the clan, and Zheng Dafeng notes that the role weighs heavily on him.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup> He is also set to inherit Osmanthus Island, the Fan Clan's intercontinental ship.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> Fan Er was a third tier martial artist when Chen Ping'an met him. Zheng Dafeng says his foundation is among the best on the continent and his aptitude matches Chen Ping'an's, but his sister calls his aptitude mediocre.

When Chen Ping'an left for [Stalactite Mountain](Stalactite_Mountain.md), Fan Er saw him off at Osmanthus Island. He tried to give Chen Ping'an the gold ingots he had saved from years of New Year money and handed over two flagons of osmanthus wine stolen from his father. He also gave him an osmanthus wood guest badge marked "friend of Fan Er", and they agreed to drink flower wine together one day.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup>

Two years later he greeted Chen Ping'an outside Old Dragon City as a fourth tier martial artist. He told him about the Fu Clan's marriage alliance with the Cloud Forest Jiang Clan and about Zheng Dafeng storming the Fang Clan. The Fan Clan kept him inside the manor during the conflict, where he dug dirt in the yard with a small pickaxe and saved more than ten sacks of it for emergencies.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> He saw Chen Ping'an off again when he left for the Azure Phoenix Nation.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

Later, Fan Er personally decided to lend [Downtrodden Mountain](Downtrodden_Mountain.md) three hundred grain rain coins for the [Lotus Root Blessed Land](Lotus_Flower_Blessed_Land.md), asking only for periodic interest.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> He slipped out of Old Dragon City in secret to join the talks, and Chen Ping'an finally gave him the piece of chinaware he had promised years before, fired at the dragon kiln where he once worked.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> The loan drew heavy criticism from the elders of his clan. Some of them argued that he was too rash to be trusted with Osmanthus Island and wanted an older relative to run it for a few years.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup>

## Trivia

- After hearing Chen Ping'an's stories of eating clay as a child, Fan Er dug up a pouch of clay and hid it under his bed. He later tasted some during his punishment and complained that Chen Ping'an had lied to him.
- He once tried to stage a "battle for the ages" with Chen Ping'an, only to be dragged off by Fan Junmao. He later let [Pei Qian](Pei_Qian.md) chase him around a courtyard and praised her "unfathomable powers".
- He commissioned a lifelike portrait of Sui Youbian for Zheng Dafeng, which got his teacher nearly cut down by her sword.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup>
- Lady Gui considers him the best match for her disciple Jin Su, but Jin Su dislikes him and prefers Sun Jiashu.
- He calls the elderly Golden Core Tier swordsman who drives his carriage Grandpa Ma.
