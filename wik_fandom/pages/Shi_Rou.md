## Appearance

Shi Rou is a beautiful skeletal ghost, a rack of bones from the neck down concealed beneath an exquisite, colorful dress with large sleeves that hide her bony arms, with only her face appearing lifelike and lovely. *(Chapter 384)* After gaining control of an unoccupied Immortal Tier immortal body that had once belonged to the Ascension Tier cultivator [Du Mao](Du_Mao.md), she took on that body’s appearance instead, which left her feeling self-conscious about performing dainty, feminine gestures in such an unfamiliar visage. *(Chapter 465)* While keeping shop she sometimes disguises herself to appear as an old man, though she has occasionally forgotten to also disguise her voice. *(Chapter 567)*

## Personality

She is sly and teasing by habit, quick to make thinly veiled jabs, but underneath that she is anxious, deferential, and still shaped by centuries of abuse, often reacting to new situations with instinctive fear before settling down. *(Chapter 384)* She genuinely cares for those around her despite her awkward, uncertain place in the world, secretly setting aside money for Pei Qian out of guilt over not earning enough for Downtrodden Mountain, even though Pei Qian always insists on doing things properly instead. *(Chapter 639)* She is self-conscious about small vanities, such as secretly buying makeup and hiding it so no one will tease her over it. *(Chapter 639)* She can be quietly protective of her dignity, refusing to curry favor with guest elders who try to win her over only when it is convenient for them. *(Chapter 639)*

## Background

Shi Rou was once a living woman of the Colorful Garment Nation on the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent, long since dead and bound as a ghost in service to a lecherous old cultivator who collected beautiful female ghosts and forced them to assist him in sinister, malicious deeds. Several of her fellow captive ghosts suffered horrific fates at his hands, their souls burned down like candle wicks through a cruel immortal technique as punishment, yet Shi Rou was the only one among them who ever dared to defy him. *(Chapter 384)* Eventually she came into Chen Ping’an’s possession, established through a contract that Aunt Gui and the Golden Core Tier sword cultivator Ma Zhi helped arrange while Chen Ping’an was traveling aboard Osmanthus Island. Wary of ghosts and yin entities after a bad experience with a vengeful female ghost in his youth, Chen Ping’an kept her sealed inside a yellow paper talisman for a long time before finally releasing her again. *(Chapter 384)*

When Chen Ping’an offered her an unoccupied body, essentially at the Immortal Tier, to inhabit so that she could finally walk freely in the world of the living, Shi Rou was overwhelmed and repeatedly kowtowed in gratitude, swearing lifelong servitude in return. With Cui Dongshan’s help, she succeeded in transforming the colored glaze golden body of the deceased Ascension Tier cultivator Du Mao into a blessed land for her own soul, though tension between the body and her soul took time to fully settle, and her own cultivation talent proved to be far less remarkable than the fated opportunity that brought her this body in the first place. *(Chapter 385)* *(Chapter 386)*

Shi Rou went on to serve as one of Chen Ping’an’s stewards on Downtrodden Mountain, following him and Wei Bo around while adjusting to a far more restrictive and rule-bound life than she was used to, and she grew wary of Cui Chan’s grandfather after witnessing the sheer force of his fist intent firsthand. *(Chapter 457)* She later ran a pastry shop on Dragon Riding Alley alongside a general goods shop, overseeing both while Pei Qian attended a private school nearby, and the two of them developed a close, sisterly bond, with Pei Qian visiting often under the pretense of checking the account books but really just to make sure Shi Rou wasn’t feeling left out. *(Chapter 464)* *(Chapter 465)*

She continued managing the pastry shop for years afterward, keeping Downtrodden Mountain’s books in order and quietly caring for the people who passed through her life there, including the once-renowned pure martial artist Zheng Dafeng, who came to help out around the shop in his old age. *(Chapter 527)* Chen Ping’an later gifted her the Snowflake Dao Robe, taken from a female ghost of Meticulous Skin City, as a token of his continued regard for her. *(Chapter 571)*

## Trivia

- She secretly buys makeup and hides it carefully so that Pei Qian will not find it and tease her. *(Chapter 639)*
- Despite her unassuming role, she is treated with notable generosity by the young mountain master of Downtrodden Mountain, something she has come to recognize only after many years of peace finally let her stop living in constant fear. *(Chapter 639)*
- She shares her current body with the lingering, unresolved remnants of its original owner, Du Mao, making for an occasionally strange sight when she performs delicate, ladylike gestures in his visage. *(Chapter 465)*
