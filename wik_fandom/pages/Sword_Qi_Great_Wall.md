# Sword Qi Great Wall

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_Qi_Great_Wall)

---
> **Spoiler Warning:** This article contains spoilers from the *Sword of Coming* (*Jian Lai* / *Unsheathed*) novel and donghua.

The **Sword Qi Great Wall** is a vast city wall on the edge of the [Savage World](Savage_World.md), reached from the [Majestic World](Majestic_World.md) through the gate on [Stalactite Mountain](Stalactite_Mountain.md). For ten thousand years its sword cultivators have held back the demon tribe, led by the old great sword immortal [Chen Qingdu](Chen_Qingdu.md). Its people are descended from convicts and refugees of the Majestic World. [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md) first came there to deliver a sword to [Ning Yao](Ning_Yao.md), and he later served as its Hidden Official.

## Geography

The Savage World lies south of the wall, and the city where the clans live lies north of it.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> The top of the wall carries a wide carriageway running from the southern edge to the northern edge.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> Three moons hang in the sky, a sight unknown in the Majestic World.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

Great sword immortals have carved huge characters into the southern face of the wall. The strokes are so large that cultivators live inside them, and some sword cultivators who keep out of the city's affairs make their homes there. A sage of the Confucian Sect and one of the Buddhist Sect guard the two ends of the wall, while a Daoist sage watches the sky.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

The wall is a land without Dao, like the heavens beyond heavens faced by the Heavenly World. Even Ascension Tier sword cultivators are not forced to ascend, and in Ning Yao's words, not even the Heavenly Dao cares about the place.

## History

> **Spoiler Warning:** This section covers the origins of the wall and the later war, including events the donghua has not reached.

Ten thousand years ago, humans rose to the top of the world and drove the demon tribe into barren lands. Late in that war, conflict broke out among the humans, and the sword cultivators were exiled as prisoners to the place that became the wall. They built it and stood guard over it, while the lands beyond became the Savage World.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup> When the Savage World was still young, three of these prisoner sword cultivators, Chen Qingdu, Long Jun and Guan Zhao, went to challenge the demons atop Holding Moon Mountain. After that battle the demon ancestor vanished, and the two sides have fought ever since.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

Since the wall was raised, Chen Qingdu has never left it, not even for the weddings or funerals of his own clan.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> In the most dangerous battle in its history, when close to half the throne holders of the Savage World joined forces, he was the only one left standing on the wall. A demon of the Savage World once complained that its side had never won a single battle against the wall in ten thousand years.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

Before Chen Ping'an's arrival, the two sides held a wager of thirteen duels. The Hidden Official went first, and her opponent surrendered without fighting. The wall nearly collapsed during the middle duels until A'Liang stepped in and carried it to victory.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup> In the war that followed, the Hidden Official Xiao Xun defected to the Savage World along with Luo Shan and Zhu An, badly wounding Zuo You on the way. Chen Ping'an was named the new Hidden Official, the youngest and weakest in the post's history.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup>

## Society

More than a hundred thousand sword cultivators have lived there generation after generation, cut off from the outside world. Except for the few who can travel to Stalactite Mountain, they keep to their ancestors' teachings and never set foot in the Majestic World.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> Liu Youzhou points out that the population is made up of convicts and refugees from the Majestic World, so their feelings toward the other side of the gate are not as warm as outsiders assume.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup>

The major clans have long histories. The Yan Clan acts much like an empire's Ministry of Revenue and controls nearly half of the resources moving through the wall.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup> The Hidden Official is an ancient title passed down for generations, responsible for governance and punishment. On the wall, Chen Qingdu is the highest principle of all.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup>

Outsiders who come to temper their swords must face three trials against local sword cultivators. Ning Yao explains that this is a mark of respect, and that daring to climb the wall and look out at the Savage World already proves someone a sword cultivator.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup> The wall also forbids its own cultivators below the Golden Core Tier from going to Stalactite Mountain to cultivate.

## Trivia

- The Dong Clan's founding ancestor once returned after more than two centuries away. He carried the head of a thirteenth tier great demon in a bamboo case and engraved the character "Dong" on the wall.<sup>[[16]](#ref-16)</sup>
- A'Liang carved a character on the wall that Chen Ping'an calls "badass".
- Yuan Zaohua's list of the wall's ten strongest sword immortals is Chen Qingdu, Dong Sangeng, A'Liang, the Hidden Official, Chen Xi, Qi Tingji, Zuo You, Nalan Shaowei, the old deaf guy and Lu Zhi.
- Chen Ping'an later learns from Chen Qingdu that the wall naturally suppresses the Great Dao of martial arts.
- The generation of young sword cultivators in Chen Ping'an's time is described as the most bountiful in ten thousand years, though another chapter says it has the second-largest number of innate sword talents in that span.
