## Appearance

Chen Sanqiu is an extremely handsome young man, widely regarded as one of the most good-looking young masters on the city wall. *(Chapter 572)* He carries two swords on his waist, an unsheathed blade called Cloud Pattern with the characters for “Cloud Pattern” engraved onto it in archaic text, and a sheathed sword called Scripture. *(Chapter 572)* He wears a white Dao robe passed down by the ancestors of the Chen Clan of Taixiang Street. *(Chapter 617)*

## Personality

He is good-humored, quick with a joke, and genuinely devoted to his friends, even when that means covering their bar tabs or dragging a drunk friend home night after night. *(Chapter 586)* He has carried an unrequited devotion to Dong Huafu’s older sister, Dong Bude, for years without ever being discouraged by her repeated rejection, and he is remembered as a surprisingly heavy drinker whenever his feelings get the better of him. *(Chapter 574)* Despite his status as the young master of a top-notch clan, he treats friends from far poorer backgrounds, such as Fan Dache and the wine shop’s workers, with genuine warmth rather than condescension. *(Chapter 590)* He trains hard out of a quiet determination to keep the gap between his own cultivation and Ning Yao’s from growing too wide, even though he never mentions this openly. *(Chapter 575)*

## Background

Chen Sanqiu is the young master of the Chen Clan on Taixiang Street at the Sword Qi Great Wall, one of a close group of young prodigies there that includes Ning Yao, Die Zhang, Yan Zhuo, and Dong Huafu. As a child he idolized the legendary swordsman A’Liang, tagging along after him in hopes of free wine and picking up plenty of bad habits in the process, though he learned little actual swordsmanship from him. Before A’Liang’s departure from the Sword Qi Great Wall, the two shared a drink together, and A’Liang’s parting words left such a deep impression on Chen Sanqiu that he got thoroughly drunk afterward cursing him for sharing them. *(Chapter 574)*

He has been in love with Dong Huafu’s older sister, Dong Bude, since childhood, but she has always turned him down, telling her brother privately that she disliked Chen Sanqiu simply because he was too handsome, a reason even her own brother found hard to accept as legitimate. Despite the rejection, Chen Sanqiu has remained popular with many other women, and the local wine shops have long been willing to extend him credit without hesitation. *(Chapter 574)*

His sword Cloud Pattern originally belonged to his close friend, Little Ququ, a young man who rose from an impoverished background and shared Chen Sanqiu’s ambition to travel south and earn military merit on the battlefield. After Little Ququ died in battle, Chen Sanqiu inherited the sword, carrying it without a scabbard for years until he eventually bought one from the sword market. *(Chapter 611)* *(Chapter 617)*

When Chen Ping’an arrived at the Sword Qi Great Wall and began challenging established young sword cultivators, Chen Sanqiu was skeptical of his chances at first but came to accept him as a friend once he saw his resolve, eventually becoming one of his regular drinking companions at Die Zhang’s wine shop. *(Chapter 575)* He helped Chen Ping’an and Die Zhang’s wine shop get off the ground by taking a cut of its profits in exchange for occasionally sending customers their way, and he personally helped Chen Ping’an acquire printed books and essay collections from the Mirage Market. *(Chapter 590)*

He played a key role in smoothing over a falling-out between their friend group and Fan Dache, a clan descendant whose drinking and heartbreak over a failed engagement nearly cost him his place among their circle, personally dragging him home and paying for the damages when things got out of hand. *(Chapter 586)* *(Chapter 587)*
On the battlefield south of the city wall, Chen Sanqiu fights alongside Ning Yao, Die Zhang, Dong Huafu, and Yan Zhuo, coordinating his flying sword with theirs to cover their weaknesses, and he has lost close friends and even his first guardian swordsman to the fighting there over the years. *(Chapter 574)* *(Chapter 617)*

## Trivia

- He once purchased an exquisite seal inscribed with “My heart is set on my beloved, I think of her and I long for her,” a piece he liked so much he bought it outright rather than simply appraising it. *(Chapter 590)*
- His old ancestor, Chen Xi, once appeared unannounced during his sword training to remark cryptically that it was about time for “the old monk who’s been nodding off on the city wall for many years to open his eyes.” *(Chapter 611)*
- He lent his original sword to his friend Fan Dache, who struggled for years to break through to the Golden Core Tier. *(Chapter 617)*
