**Cao Xi** is a terrestrial sword immortal of Southern Whirl Continent who was born on Clay Vase Alley in the small town of Jewel Small World. He is one of the two best-known cultivators ever to come out of the town, alongside Xie Shi, and is the most senior ancestor of the Cao Clan.

## Appearance

Cao Xi looks like a plump, wealthy man rather than an immortal, and strangers in the town take him for a rich merchant.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> He has a moustache and a white, chubby wrist. His bonded flying sword is wound around it like a green rope or bracelet.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

## Personality

Cao Xi is loud, crude and shameless, and proud of it. Across Southern Whirl Continent he is known as stingy and unkind, a man who would pluck the feathers off a passing goose. He mocks Xie Shi's reputation for righteousness and claims that upright people like him are the hardest to get along with.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> He loves striking deals and treats nearly everything as business.

Beneath the bluster, he still feels for his hometown. Standing alone in his old family house, he lamented that he had become an immortal while the warmth of the place had vanished. He also caught rain in a bowl to taste the water of home, then poured it out, grumbling that scholars lie about it tasting better than wine. He remembers the names of all his childhood classmates, though every one of them is long dead.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

## Background

Cao Xi grew up on Clay Vase Alley and was a troublesome child. He once urinated into the Iron Lock Well and then threw stones into it for a month. His life is recorded in the town's Local County Chronicle.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup> Like the other children of the town, he had bonded porcelain. He grew strong enough that, according to Song Changjing, the buyers of such porcelain would treat him as an honoured guest rather than a possession.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

He left the town and made his name on Southern Whirl Continent. His swordsmanship is not considered elite among terrestrial sword immortals, but his bonded sword ranks among the ten best weapons on that continent. The sword was refined from an entire river by an ancient deity, and it is regarded as a pseudo-celestial tool.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> He is also half the owner of the Sea Suppressing Pagoda.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

## Plot

Cao Xi returned to the town around the same time as Xie Shi. Xie Shi had come to act against the Great Li Empire, and Cao Xi sided with Great Li, partly on behalf of the Pure Confucian Chen Clan, with whom one of his descendants had become engaged. He urged Xu Ruo to help kill Xie Shi as a warning to others, but Xu Ruo refused to make that decision.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

While in the town, he harshly disciplined the fox spirit that served his descendant Cao Jun. He struck it with twenty wisps of sword qi for speaking carelessly.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> He also visited the kiln supervision office, where his descendant Cao Mao served, and advised on its feng shui.

Cao Xi then visited Old Man Yang to ask about his mother's soul. Old Man Yang struck a deal with him. In return for the souls of Cao Xi's parents, Cao Xi had to give his old flying sword to [Li Liu](Li_Liu.md), teach her his sword technique, and serve as her Dao guardian. He protested that a sword immortal who might reach the 12th Tier should not guard a child, but he agreed once he understood what she was.

Before leaving the town, he ordered Cao Jun to join the Great Li army and kill a 10th Tier cultivator or two within a decade.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> He went to Lion Peak as Li Liu's Dao guardian and gave her the short sword he had used for 300 years before refining his river sword.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup> He accompanied her through secret realms and old sect ruins while she gathered treasures.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup> He later returned to Southern Whirl Continent to keep watch over the Mighty Suppressing Pagoda, leaving Cao Jun in the Great Li army.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup>

## Trivia

- When Old Man Yang named Li Liu as his student, Cao Xi joked that people would one day remember him only as Li Liu's master.
- He challenged an 11th Tier swordsman to a duel, then set the date a hundred years away and called it strategy.
