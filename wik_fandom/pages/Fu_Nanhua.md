**Fu Nanhua** is the son of Fu Qi, lord of [Old Dragon City](Old_Dragon_City.md) at the southern tip of Eastern Treasured Vial Continent. He came to the small town in [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md) for a fated opportunity, nearly died at the hands of [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md), and went home to become the clear heir to his father's seat.

## Appearance

Fu Nanhua is a tall, strikingly handsome young man whose good looks hold up even in a grimy alley. *(Chapter 9)* He carries a natural regal air and, when he wants to, a warm and easy smile. In the town he always kept a green dragon-shaped jade pendant at his waist, the Old Dragon Rainbringer, until he gave it away to Song Jixin.

## Personality

Fu Nanhua is refined, smooth-spoken and calculating. He can bend down to speak eye to eye with a poor boy one moment and quietly plan an ally's murder the next. He decided to kill [Cai Jinjian](Cai_Jinjian.md) from the moment he entered the town, even while walking beside her as a friend. When she was struck down in front of him, he felt relief, and his speech about avenging her was aimed at the sages watching over the town. *(Chapter 21)*

He is proud and quick to anger when he feels slighted, but he usually keeps his temper in check when his future is at stake. He respects those he sees as equals or betters, such as Song Jixin, and looks down on everyone else. He tends to agonize over hard choices, pacing the room as he weighs them, and his father mocks him for it. Over time he learns to put the clan's interests ahead of his own grudges, which earns his father's approval.

## Background

### Old Dragon City

Fu Nanhua was born into the Fu Clan, the rulers of Old Dragon City. He was once just one of several children with a claim to the city lord's seat and was not seen as special, so it came as a surprise when his father chose him to go to Jewel Small World. His older siblings, Fu Donghai and Fu Chunhua, had spent a century handling the clan's dealings with other continents. *(Chapter 254 (3))*

### The small town

Fu Nanhua entered the town with Cai Jinjian and the two formed an alliance, though he planned to dispose of her. He was seeking Song Jixin, and when Chen Ping'an refused to tell him where Song Jixin lived, he took the boy as a nuisance. Song Jixin hinted to him that no one would care if the orphan from Clay Vase Alley died. *(Chapter 10)*

Fu Nanhua fell in love at first sight with a small Mandrill Teapot that Song Jixin owned. He offered the Old Dragon Rainbringer as a meeting gift and a pouch of Offering Coins, and when Song Jixin pushed back, he tapped the table in a way that caused Song Jixin agony. He held back from killing him for the sake of his future. Song Jixin's terms were three pouches of gold essence coins and a killing on his behalf. *(Chapter 17)* According to Cui Minghuang, Song Jixin could not decide on the target and switched it from Liu Xianyang to Chen Ping'an. *(Chapter 89)*

Then Chen Ping'an ambushed Cai Jinjian with a porcelain shard while she and Fu Nanhua were walking together. Fu Nanhua stalked the bleeding boy down the alley, sure of an easy kill, but Chen Ping'an tricked him into looking back and slammed into him. Chen Ping'an pinned him against the wall, choked him with one hand and pressed the shard into his stomach. Fu Nanhua offered to swear an oath, hoping to deceive the boy with a half-truth, and blacked out just as Qi Jingchun arrived. *(Chapter 22)*

Qi Jingchun stopped Chen Ping'an, told Fu Nanhua to leave the small world, and gave him an opportunity of some kind, without asking him to promise anything about Chen Ping'an. *(Chapter 253 (2))* On Old Man Yang's orders, Zheng Dafeng took Fu Nanhua back to Old Dragon City.

### Return home

The ordeal nearly broke his Dao heart. Fu Nanhua, once known for his wide circle of friends, withdrew and saw few people. Even so, his standing rose quickly: his master was close to a breakthrough, a marriage to the Cloud Forest Jiang Clan was arranged, and he came to be seen as the one most likely to inherit the Old Dragon Robe. *(Chapter 253 (1))*

When Cai Jinjian visited the city, he tried to win her over with a plan to raise both of them to power together, but she turned him down. His father then appeared and laid out two paths: kill Chen Ping'an to cut away the knot left in his heart, or let it go and use the Fu Clan's secret method of turning such knots into pearls in the mind. Fu Qi mocked him for being troubled by a dead man and a nobody, and the shaken Fu Nanhua broke down in tears.

Later, when Chen Ping'an came to Old Dragon City, Fu Nanhua announced that the boy had to die. His sister Fu Chunhua privately looked down on him as a waste who had reached only the sixth tier despite all the resources poured into him. Soon after, [Zhi Gui](Zhi_Gui.md) arrived at the Fu Clan wrapped in the Old Dragon Robe, called him a pathetic little ant for not daring to kill Chen Ping'an, and left him feeling close to madness. *(Chapter 254 (4))*

In the end he chose not to use the clan's power for a private feud. Fu Qi was pleased and told him that the Fu Clan's real strength was money, which had carried Fu Qi himself to the Nascent Tier. *(Chapter 255 (1))* Fu Nanhua stayed a sworn enemy of Chen Ping'an but did not move against him. *(Chapter 361 (1))*

### Young city lord

Fu Nanhua married the young woman from the Jiang Clan. On their wedding day the two talked plainly and agreed on the terms of their marriage, and he found her beautiful, far from the rumors. *(Chapter 364 (1))* During the clash between Fu Qi and Zheng Dafeng, he sent a quiet signal that swayed the Ding, Fang and Hou clans, and afterward he personally told them that the Parasol Leaf Sect had also allied with the Fu Clan. By Volume 9 he was certain to be the next lord of Old Dragon City and was on good terms with Song Jixin, who had been posted to the city as a feudal lord. *(Chapter 571 (1))*

## Trivia

- Rumors once claimed his bride was shockingly ugly and over thirty. In fact she was at least as pretty as Jin Su.
- He once fell for Jin Su of Osmanthus Island, as did Sun Jiashu, and she rejected him outright.
- He did not know that Zheng Dafeng was more than a simple gatekeeper of Jewel Small World.
- He was not among the clan members chosen to greet the Great Li guests, and he was thrilled to later meet the Mohist Xu Ruo in person.
- Chen Ping'an hoped Gu Can would never become like Fu Nanhua, who bullies those weaker than him.
