## Appearance

Yao Zhen is an old general who is described as being serious and unsmiling when in the army and at home. However, he becomes more relaxed and talkative when he interacts with Chen Ping'an. Despite his age, he is **healthy and strong**. 

## Personality

Yao Zhen is portrayed as a complex character with a blend of strictness and warmth. He is described as an old general who is **serious and unsmiling** when in the army and at home. However, his demeanor changes when he interacts with Chen Ping'an, becoming more **relaxed and talkative**. Despite his age, he is still physically robust, as he is described as **healthy and strong**. A recent disaster has left him **heartbroken**, leading to a change in his temperament. Yao Zhen is also incredibly **stubborn** and adheres strictly to hierarchical relationships, more so than even pedantic Confucian scholars. He is a subordinate of the Imperial Liu Clan. He is also very fond of Chen Ping'an, considering him a young benefactor and enjoying his company. He is also willing to reveal secrets about the Great Quan Empire to Chen Ping'an that he would not have shared before. Additionally, Yao Zhen demonstrates a sense of loyalty and protectiveness towards those he cares about and is willing to risk his life for Chen Ping'an. Although he is generally serious, he also has a sense of humor, as seen when he jokes with Chen Ping'an, and there are moments when he shows a "youthful shamelessness".

## Background

Yao Zhen is an old general who is a subordinate of the Imperial Liu Clan. He and his clan have lived in the **rough border regions for many generations**. The Yao Clan is a large military clan. They are known for their mounted troops. Yao Zhen is a capable military leader, and his family is very focused on military strategy and literary studies. Despite their martial background, they place a heavy emphasis on literary studies, which is necessary for setting up military formations and employing military strategies.

Yao Zhen's ancestors were from the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent. According to a fortune teller, one of their ancestors had the character "Zhi" in their name and that this character had a strong affinity with Yao Zhen's grandchildren's generation. Because of this, all the children of Yao Zhen's grandchildren's generation have "Zhi" in their names, including Yao Jinzhi, Yao Lingzhi, and Yao Xianzhi.

He is also described as being very familiar with the rules and schemes of officialdom. The Yao clan has a long history of making contributions and being rewarded by the imperial court, with some members dying in battle, leaving behind widows. Yao Zhen is very strict about the hierarchical relationships between rulers and officials. He is also very protective of his family. He has become heartbroken after a recent disaster that his clan experienced. Despite his serious nature, Yao Zhen is also capable of showing a sense of humor and "youthful shamelessness".
