## Appearance

Zhou Mili takes the form of a small girl dressed in black clothing. Chen Ping'an later gave her his Golden Sweet Wine robe to wear over her black outfit, turning her into what looked like a little girl in white, and she continued wearing this robe afterward. She has thin, slightly yellow eyebrows and stays notably short for her apparent age no matter how much she eats.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup><sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

## Personality

Zhou Mili is cheerful, naive and easily excitable, prone to boasting about being a "great water monster" even though she is tiny and timid in practice. She loves roasted seeds and rice and dislikes fish intensely. She is devoted to Pei Qian, whom she calls Sect Sister or Senior Sister, and takes her duties as Downtrodden Mountain's right guardian very seriously despite frequently misunderstanding instructions or getting things backward. She is easily moved to tears but just as quickly cheered up, and she has a habit of hiding her own troubles so as not to worry the people she cares about.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup><sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>

## Background

Zhou Mili was originally a water monster living in Mute Lake on the Northern Complete Reed Continent, watching cultivators and travelers pass by without ever being able to fit in among humans. Disguised as a little girl selling newspapers, she was captured and passed between several parties aboard an intercontinental ship before Chen Ping'an bought her freedom. During a later confrontation on the ship involving a hidden enemy named Gao Cheng of the Malicious Ghost Valley, Chen Ping'an publicly named her Zhou Mili and disguised her further to shield her from danger, promising to protect her.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup><sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

Chen Ping'an entrusted her to Sect Leader Zhu Quan of the Flax Robe Sect to escort her to the Ox Horn Mountain ferry station of the Dragon Spring Prefecture, telling her she could turn to Zhu Lian on Downtrodden Mountain if she ever needed help. She was brought to Downtrodden Mountain's Dragon Riding Alley and introduced to Pei Qian, who took her in on Chen Ping'an's instructions. She became Pei Qian's close companion, serving first as a temporary guardian of Dragon Riding Alley's pastry shop before Chen Ping'an formally approved her promotion to right-hand guardian of Downtrodden Mountain's ancestral hall at Pei Qian's recommendation.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup><sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

She continued living on Downtrodden Mountain alongside Pei Qian, Chen Nuanshu and Chen Ruchu, accompanying Pei Qian on her travels to the Lotus Root Blessed Land and later to the Sword Qi Great Wall. During one return trip to Downtrodden Mountain, she was confronted and beaten by an official of the Fine Wine River's water palace after refusing to be recruited, but concealed Downtrodden Mountain's name out of caution rather than invoking Chen Ping'an's protection.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup><sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>

## Trivia

- The name Mili literally translates to "a grain of rice."<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>
- She does not need to eat, being a water demon, but enjoys food anyway and keeps up the habit regardless.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>
- Cui Dongshan addressed her as his junior apprentice-sister, implying she holds some standing within Chen Ping'an's lineage alongside Pei Qian.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup>
