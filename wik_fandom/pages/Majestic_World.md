# Majestic World

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Majestic_World)

---
The Majestic World is a vast and complex place with diverse geography, demographics, cultures, politics, and economies. Here's a deep dive into what the sources reveal about it:
![Screenshot 2025-01-24 5.30.09 PM.png](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/sword-of-coming/images/f/ff/Screenshot_2025-01-24_5.30.09_PM.png/revision/latest?cb=20250124203106)

## Geography:

- The Majestic World has **nine continents**, including Eastern Treasured Vial Continent, Complete Reed Continent, Southern Whirl Continent, Pure White Continent, and Golden Armor Continent.
- **Middle Earth Divine Continent** is described as the "glowing gem" and is the central and most significant of these continents. Several major empires exist on this continent.
- The Majestic World contains **four lakes and five seas**.
- There are **ten large worlds and thirty-six small worlds** that are considered blessed paradises due to their high levels of spiritual energy. These worlds include Paradise Small World, Astral Winds Small World, Jewel Small World, and Island Small World.
- Small worlds are also referred to as mystical realms. These are often formed from ancient ruins or battlefields. They have a history that is complex and can be incomplete.
- **Blessed lands** are also part of the Majestic World. They are larger than small worlds and often produce many prodigious figures who are then taken by the nations or sects that own the land.

## Demographics:

- The Majestic World is populated by a variety of beings including humans, immortals, Buddhas, deities, demons, and ghosts.
- There are different groups such as **Qi refiners**, who cultivate spiritual energy, **pure martial artists** and **swordsmen**.
- There is a clear distinction between people from "the mountains" (immortals and cultivators) and people from "outside the mountains" (mortals).
- The population is distributed across the nine continents, with varying levels of cultivation and societal development.
- **Immigrants** from other worlds also exist in the Majestic World. These “otherworldly immortals” often have unique abilities and knowledge.
- There are also **demonic cultivators** who utilize sinister techniques and can cause chaos.

## Cultures:

- The Majestic World is influenced by several major schools of thought: **Confucianism, Buddhism, Daoism, and Militarianism**. The Mohists also exist with their own set of values.
- Different continents and regions have their own unique cultures, customs, and dialects.
- **Language** and dialect proficiency can be an indicator of one's social status.
- There is a significant emphasis on cultivation, with many people striving to reach higher levels of power and longevity.
- The **arts and sciences** exist in the Majestic World, including geomancy, divination, and literature.
- Many stories and legends have developed around the world, with popular figures and places.
- There are cultural practices such as worshipping deities, ancestors, and powerful figures.
- There are different schools of thought, including the School of Fiction, which explores different cultures and customs.

## Politics:

- The political landscape is characterized by a complex interplay between **empires, nations, and powerful sects**.
- The **Great Li Empire** is one of the major powers in the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent. It has expanded its territory significantly in recent years.
- There are also other powerful entities, like the Lu Clan, known for their knowledge and philosophy.
- The **imperial courts** are central to the political structure. The imperial court is organized into six ministries.
- The position of **city god** exists in the night realm, mirroring the roles of governors in the living world. City gods rule over the beings and entities in the realm of the night.
- **Political conflicts** between different factions and schools of thought are common.
- The leaders of the various worlds are said to be blessed with immense fortune.
- Some rulers focus on expansion and power, while others are more focused on their people's well-being.
- The **relationship between the imperial court and immortal forces in the mountains** also influences political dynamics.

## Economy:

- **Trade** is a significant part of the economy, with goods and treasures exchanged between different regions and worlds.
- The value of land is determined by the presence of spiritual energy and the potential for profit.
- There are **various industries and sectors**, including the creation of porcelain, geomancy, cultivation, and travel.
- Certain resources, such as spiritual energy, are highly valued.
- **Immortal treasures and spirit tools** have high economic value.
- The collection of merit is important for deities and political achievement for officials.
- **Intercontinental ships** transport people and goods between different continents and worlds.

This overview provides a glimpse into the multifaceted nature of the Majestic World, with its vast geography, diverse populations, rich cultures, complex politics, and thriving economies.
