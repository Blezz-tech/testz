# Downtrodden Mountain

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Downtrodden_Mountain)

---
**Downtrodden Mountain** is a mountain in the southwestern part of Dragon Spring Prefecture, formerly Jewel Small World, and the home of [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md). Bought almost by accident with the gold essence copper coins he received as compensation, it grows from a quiet mountain into the seat of an immortal mountain force. By the end of Volume 8 it has its own ancestral hall, a cluster of subsidiary mountains, a share of a ferry station, and a blessed land of tens of millions of people.

It is home to an unusual collection of people: martial artists from the Lotus Flower Blessed Land, a former water serpent and a former carp spirit, a great water monster, Chen Ping'an's disciple [Pei Qian](Pei_Qian.md), and, for a time, the tenth-tier martial artist [Cui Cheng](Cui_Cheng.md).

## Geography

Downtrodden Mountain lies at the southernmost end of the western mountain range of Dragon Spring Prefecture, among the tallest mountains of the former Jewel Small World. Its terrain is described as unique among the region's mountains and particularly eye-catching. Its height makes it an exceptional spot for moon-gazing. Nearby are Cloud Drape Mountain, seat of the Northern Mountain God [Wei Bo](Wei_Bo.md), the much larger Gray Veil Mountain, and Golden Stalk Mountain, where [Ruan Qiong](Ruan_Qiong.md) built a new forge. A deep cliff cave on the mountain, marked by great furrows from a huge body, is the lair of the black python Wei Bo brought from Go Table Mountain.

## History

### Purchase

After receiving pouches of gold essence copper coins, Chen Ping'an asks [Ruan Qiong](Ruan_Qiong.md) to help him buy five mountains for 300 years: Downtrodden Mountain, Treasured Scripture Mountain, Immortal Herb Mountain, Rainbow Cloud Mountain and True Jewel Mountain. He chooses Downtrodden Mountain, despite the negative connotations of its name, because a bird drops its droppings right onto its label on his map.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>

[Old Man Yang](Old_Man_Yang.md) is surprised by the choice, remarking that apart from Cloud Drape Mountain and Incense Mountain, Downtrodden Mountain is the most interesting mountain of the lot.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> A figure observing the purchase reveals that he deliberately helped Chen Ping'an choose it. Ruan Xiu also learns that the Great Li emperor has attached some of the best of thirty bonus artifacts to Divine Elegance Mountain and Downtrodden Mountain, and that the court is likely to appoint a mountain god to it.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> When Ruan Xiu later writes to Chen Ping'an, she reports that its spiritual energy is second only to Cloud Drape Mountain and her father's Lamp Bearer Mountain.

### Black Python and the Building

At Go Table Mountain, Chen Ping'an arranges for the black python, which has the potential to become a flood dragon, to settle on Downtrodden Mountain and guard its fortune. A'Liang notes that a place where a python transforms into a flood dragon quietly becomes a place of great fortune.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> Wei Bo agrees to escort the python there and build Chen Ping'an a building of courage bamboo. Asked to name it, A'Liang first suggests "A'Liang Is Very Badass Building", then "humbly" settles on **The Badass Building**.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

### Mountain God Temple

The Great Li Empire appoints three new formal mountain gods at once, for Cloud Drape Mountain, Incense Mountain and Downtrodden Mountain. Downtrodden Mountain's god is the former kiln supervision official Song Yuzhang, whom the emperor promoted after he "died of sickness". Unlike the other gods' merely gilded statues, the head of his statue was forged entirely from gold, as part of how the emperor punished both his brother and the empress.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

The county's most trusted official, Fu Yu, oversees the temple's construction on the summit, while the rest of the mountain remains open to woodsmen from the town. During construction, a mysterious man is seen building bamboo houses on the mountain at night.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup>

### Home of Chen Ping'an

When Chen Ping'an returns from Great Sui with the little boy in azure and the little girl in pink, Wei Bo, now the Northern Mountain God, shows them the finished bamboo building.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup> Wei Bo carefully explains to Song Yuzhang that their merits and failings are recorded by the imperial court. The relationship between the loyal Great Li official Song Yuzhang and Wei Bo gradually sours, and Song Yuzhang's control over the mountain is restricted by imperial order.

[Li Xisheng](Li_Xisheng.md) climbs the mountain to inscribe talismans on the walls of the bamboo building. As he works through the night, Wei Bo watches Downtrodden Mountain slowly sink, little by little.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup> These talismans give the building its mystical qualities, including allowing the half-mad Cui Cheng to stay lucid. Cui Cheng moves in and trains Chen Ping'an on its second floor, making it the site of his brutal martial arts sessions.

Behind the building, Wei Bo digs a small pond, fills it with spring water from the fallen Divine Water Nation, and plants Chen Ping'an's golden lotus seed there. The seed is a relic of the Daoist Lu Chen, and Wei Bo realises that the root of Downtrodden Mountain's fortune does not lie in the mountain itself.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> A little lotus spirit later lives in the pond. Lu Chen himself pays a visit to the mountain.

### Expansion

After his return from Bamboo Scroll Lake, Chen Ping'an begins turning Downtrodden Mountain into a true mountain force. With more people arriving, new lodgings are built around the mountain. Onlookers in Dragon Spring Prefecture start mockingly calling it "Suck-Up Mountain" for all the powerful figures who gather there.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> Treasured Scripture, Rainbow Cloud and Immortal Herb Mountains are leased to Ruan Qiong's Dragon Spring Sword Sect for three hundred years, leaving Chen Ping'an with Downtrodden Mountain and True Jewel Mountain.

At Forest Deer Academy, Chen Ping'an signs a contract with the Great Li Empire, sealed by the Ministry of Rites, Ruan Qiong and Wei Bo. It grants him six more mountains: Ox Horn Mountain (formerly the Cloth Wrapper Shop's ferry station), Cinnabar Mountain, Gray Veil Mountain, Ao Back Ridge, Exuberant Dawn Peak and Sword Worship Plateau.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup> This makes him the largest mountain owner in Dragon Spring Prefecture after Ruan Qiong.

### The Lotus Root Blessed Land

When the Lotus Flower Blessed Land is split into four, Downtrodden Mountain receives one part, renamed the **Lotus Root Blessed Land**. It is home to around forty million people and hovers between a low- and medium-grade blessed land. Downtrodden Mountain must effectively act as its "heavens", setting its rules, choosing sites for future mountain and water god shrines, and avoiding draining the pond to catch the fish. Raising it to a medium-grade land is expected to cost two to three thousand grain rain coins.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup> Its entrance is a parasol leaf umbrella, kept for Downtrodden Mountain by Wei Bo.

These costs leave the mountain short of funds. Zheng Dafeng introduces a small fee for visitors climbing the mountain, Wei Bo holds three Night Travel Banquets in quick succession to raise money, and Chen Ping'an sends home immortal coins and treasures from his travels on Northern Complete Reed Continent.

### Ancestral Hall

After Chen Ping'an's return home, Wei Bo selects the site for the ancestral hall. When the hall is completed, Chen Ping'an leads a quiet ceremony to hang portraits and offer incense, inviting only nearby guests: Wei Bo, the deputy mountain master of Forest Deer Academy, Ruan Qiong and his disciples Dong Gu and Xu Xiaoqiao, and Liu Chongrun of Ao Back Ridge.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup>

Three portraits hang inside, with the old scholar ([Scholarly Sage](Scholarly_Sage.md)) in the centre, flanked by [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md) and [Cui Cheng](Cui_Cheng.md). 

Chen Ping'an later announces the hall's founding to his old companions at Mountain Cliff Academy, inviting them to visit at any time and to build residences on its vacant mountains.

## Locations

- **The Building**: the two-storey courage bamboo building named by A'Liang, inscribed with Li Xisheng's talismans. Its second floor was Cui Cheng's training ground. A stone table stands on the cliff outside, and Chen Ping'an buries wine behind it.
- **Mountain God Temple**: on the summit, home of Song Yuzhang.
- **Lotus pond**: behind the bamboo building, filled with Divine Water Nation spring water and home to the golden lotus seed and the little lotus spirit.
- **Mountain gate**: at the foot of the mountain, kept by Zheng Dafeng, who returns to his old job of gatekeeper.
- **Residential area**: lodgings built for the mountain's growing population.
- **Ancestral Hall**: founded in Chapter 568.
- **Mountain path**: Chen Ping'an has long wanted to widen it and pave it with bluestone, and he collects thirty-six bricks from a Daoist temple to pave part of the mountain.

## Associated properties

- **True Jewel Mountain**: one of the original five mountains.
- **Treasured Scripture, Rainbow Cloud and Immortal Herb Mountains**: leased to the Dragon Spring Sword Sect for 300 years.
- **Gray Veil Mountain**, **Cinnabar Mountain**, **Exuberant Dawn Peak** and **Sword Worship Plateau**.
- **Ao Back Ridge**: leased to Liu Chongrun, who relocated the female cultivators of Bejeweled Hairpin Island there under a 300-year contract.
- **Ox Horn Mountain**: the immortal ferry station, held under Wei Bo's name, with profits split evenly between him and Chen Ping'an. Its Cloth Wrapper Shop branch helps fund the mountain.
- **Frothing Ink**: a dragon boat, recovered with Liu Chongrun's help and docked at Ox Horn Mountain.
- **Lotus Root Blessed Land**: Downtrodden Mountain's share of the former Lotus Flower Blessed Land.
- **[Dragon Riding Alley](Small_Town.md#dragon-riding-alley) shops**: the pastry shop run by Pei Qian, Shi Rou and later Chen Lingjun, and the Burclover shop where Lu Baixiang stays.

## Roles

- **Mountain Master:** Chen Ping'an
- **Senior Steward:** Zhu Lian, the "Old Cook"
- **Young Steward:** Chen Ruchu, who sometimes even manages Zhu Lian<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup>
- **Gatekeeper:** Zheng Dafeng
- **Right-hand Guardian:** Zhou Mili, promoted at Pei Qian's strong recommendation

## Trivia

- Cui Dongshan judged Downtrodden Mountain to be "about as good as it'll ever be", and suggested focusing on the other mountains.
- Chen Ping'an plans to hang a couplet in the bamboo building about leaving the mountains with a sword when matters arise.
- Zhou Mili complains that many of the mountain's residents have no chance of finding wives, naming Wei Bo, Zhu Lian, Zheng Dafeng, Lu Baixiang, Song Yuzhang and Yuan Lai.
