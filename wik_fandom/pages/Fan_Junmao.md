## Appearance

Fan Junmao is frequently called a **"young mistress,"** and her appearance is described as captivating and memorable. She is noted for her **beautiful eyes** that can appear as "bottomless, pitch-black wells". Her eyes can also be fervent and filled with awe. At other times, she is described as having a **cold look** in her eyes. She has a **fair complexion** and a **graceful figure**. She is also described as stunningly beautiful. Her attire is often mentioned, and she is known to wear a **green dress** that is described as spectacular. She also appears wearing a **black robe** and when she is on the sea of clouds she is dressed in white. She seems to prefer simple clothing that is not flashy or ostentatious, although upon closer inspection her clothes are refined and of high quality. Fan Junmao is also described as having an **authoritative** and **commanding** presence, which is in contrast to her youthful appearance. Even though she is young, she has a regal bearing. She has also been described as having a **disdainful sneer**.

## Personality

Fan Junmao's personality is a complex mix of confidence, authority, and a sharp, sometimes abrasive, wit. She is presented as someone who is **highly self-assured**, often displaying a **commanding presence** despite her youth. She is not afraid to speak her mind, and she often does so with a **disdainful sneer** or a sharp retort. She can be **impatient and easily irritated**, especially when dealing with those she considers less intelligent or capable than herself. Despite her seemingly harsh exterior, Fan Junmao is also shown to be capable of **great insight and strategic thinking**. She is a shrewd observer of character and is not easily fooled. While she often expresses a cynical view of the world, she is also capable of acts of generosity and kindness, albeit often disguised with a mocking tone. She possesses a **strong sense of pride** and does not hesitate to show it, but she is also capable of self-reflection and admitting when she is wrong. Fan Junmao is also portrayed as being **extremely intelligent** and is not one to shy away from challenges or difficult situations. She is seen to be fiercely independent and does not like to be controlled. She is also shown to be **loyal and protective** of those she cares about, despite her often brusque manner. She can be **impatient**, and she often speaks her mind directly and without hesitation. She is described as being someone who is difficult to understand. She is also shown to be **ambitious** and is not afraid to take risks. She has a deep understanding of power dynamics and often uses her wit and intelligence to manipulate situations to her advantage. She is also **secretive**, and she is not always willing to reveal her true intentions.

## Background

Fan Junmao is a character with a mysterious and complex background that is gradually revealed throughout the sources. She is from a poor family in Pine Song Nation, but a traveling senior sister from the Mirror Heart Hall saw potential in her and took her in as a disciple in place of her own master. This led to her becoming the most outstanding disciple of the current generation in the Mirror Heart Hall. **She traveled across the cultivation world by herself for several years**, which contributed to her unique perspective and formidable abilities. It is suggested that **she has ties to the old divine lord**, and that the Fan Clan is essentially under his control. Her rapid cultivation base progression has always been extremely strange, and there is clearly far more to her than meets the eye. She is also described as being someone who was originally a spirit, and she **abandoned her cultivation and grew up naturally** before eventually becoming Fan Wan'er. **Half of her soul had been missing**, which led to her appearing hesitant and sluggish as if she was being suppressed by a huge boulder. She is also described as having a close relationship with the old man in the Yang Family's medicine shop.

Fan Junmao is shown to be deeply knowledgeable about the workings of the cultivation world and is not easily fooled. She is also **highly influential within the Fan Clan**, as she is the one who calls the shots behind the scenes. She is also very familiar with various cultivation techniques, including the art of refining bonded items. She is a formidable force to be reckoned with, and despite her young age, she commands respect from many different people, including even powerful cultivators.
