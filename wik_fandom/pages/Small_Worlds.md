# Small Worlds

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Small_Worlds)

---
Small worlds are one of two categories of alternate realms that exist alongside the nine continents, the other being the ten large worlds. There are 36 small worlds in total, and Jewel Small World, the floating realm that was home to [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md)'s small town before it fell to the ground, is one of them.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>

## Definition and Nature

Qi refiners commonly call small worlds "mystical realms" to set them apart from the ten large worlds. Unlike large worlds, which run through heaven and earth with an abundance of spiritual energy and are considered true immortal abodes, small worlds tend to feel comparatively damaged and incomplete, since many of them were born from the wreckage of ancient ruins or old battlefields.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> [Ning Yao](Ning_Yao.md) describes the 36 small worlds as concealed, secret places, "like a shy woman hiding behind a fan."

## Size and Scope

Small worlds vary enormously in size. Jewel Small World is the smallest and most insignificant of the 36, measuring only around 500 kilometers across. At the other extreme, legend holds that a single lotus leaf inside the Dao Ancestor's Lotus Flower World, itself counted as one of the 36 small worlds, is larger than the entire capital city of the Great Li Empire.

## The 36 Small Worlds

Among the named small worlds, Paradise Small World is said to be the most beautiful and scenic, and Astral Winds Small World the most strange and perilous. Island Small World is a mystical realm holding a number of ancient islands that mysteriously vanished from the outside world, and it is unusual in that it sits inside the abdomen of a primordial beast known as the island-engulfing whale. A damaged realm called the Ascending True Immortal Mystical Realm, derived from one of the 72 blessed lands rather than from a small world directly, also measures around 500 kilometers across, rivaling Jewel Small World in size; the Five Weapon Sect that controls it charges entrance fees in much the same way outsiders once paid to enter Jewel Small World.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

### Jewel Small World

Jewel Small World was never particularly rich in spiritual energy or valuable medicinal herbs, unlike many other small worlds. Instead, it became known across [Eastern Treasured Vial Continent](Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent.md) for its "[bonded porcelain](Natal_Porcelain.md)" system, its treasures, and the unusually high number of gifted cultivators its small town produced each generation.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> Within the small world, Qi refiners were handicapped more than almost anyone else; even a minor lapse in concentration while using cultivation there could damage a Qi refiner's cultivation base, with more advanced cultivators suffering more for it.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

For most of its 3,000-year history, entry into Jewel Small World was restricted and came at a price, but this was set to change once its guardianship passed out of the hands of the Sages: the small world was due to be downgraded into an unrestricted blessed land that anyone could enter freely, without paying the old entrance fee.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> That transition never had the chance to run its natural course. Jewel Small World eventually fell out of the sky following the death of its final guardian Sage, Qi Jingchun, and the territory was subsequently annexed by the Great Li Empire and reorganized into what is now Dragon Spring Prefecture.

## Domains

A separate category from both large and small worlds is the domain, a small storage space, known to Buddhists as an "all-encompassing seed" and to Daoists as a "world up the sleeve." A domain can hold any number of objects but, unlike a true small world, cannot contain living beings. One example mentioned is a domain inside a jade bracelet, roughly the size of a single house.

## Significance and Cultivation

Small worlds are frequently tied to fated opportunities, drawing cultivators who hope to find rare treasures or accelerate their cultivation, though the risks inside can be just as great as the rewards. The Great Li Empire's White Jade Capital, a tower housing a dozen floors of flying swords powerful enough to deter any cultivator on the continent, was itself built using the wealth the empire's ruling Song Clan extracted over generations from managing Jewel Small World.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> Elsewhere, the Fictionists maintain a realm called the White Paper Blessed Land, which grows or shrinks depending on how many characters and stories its members have written into being.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>
