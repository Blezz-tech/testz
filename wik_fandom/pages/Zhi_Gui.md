**Zhi Gui** is [Song Jixin](Song_Jixin.md)'s maidservant on [Clay Vase Alley](Small_Town.md#clay-vase-alley). To the town she looks like a sweet, shy servant girl. In truth she is Wang Zhu, a jewel formed from the soul of the last true dragon in the world, who spent thousands of years trapped in the Jewel Small World.

## Appearance

Zhi Gui looks like a slender young girl with large, round eyes. Her face is only just losing its childhood softness, and she grows taller and lovelier with every year. *(Chapter 113)* In front of most people she wears a timid, wide-eyed expression that makes onlookers feel sorry for her. Every so often her true nature shows through, and golden pupils flash in her eyes before vanishing again. *(Chapter 10)* Later, when she lets her guard down, her eyes are golden with vertical pupils. 

## Personality

Zhi Gui shows the world a gentle, obedient face, but it is a mask. On her own or with people she does not fear, she is cold, proud and sharp-tongued. When Granny Ma made one remark too many at the well, the quiet little maid walked over and slapped the old woman twice, saying she had put up with her for long enough. *(Chapter 31)* She once hacked an old wooden figure in the house to pieces with a meat cleaver, and she stamps on Song Jixin's four-legged snake whenever it annoys her. *(Chapter 36)*

She carries a deep and lasting anger toward the cultivators and sages who kept her imprisoned. She refuses to bow her head to anyone who tries to lecture her, and she says openly that she does not care for scholars' sermons. She thinks mostly of herself. When [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) came begging for help to save [Liu Xianyang](Liu_Xianyang.md), she turned him away with a mocking smile, then burped and remarked that she had eaten a little too much. *(Chapter 47)*

She is not without feeling. She stays at Song Jixin's side through his worst days, and she fusses over the hen and chicks they left behind in the small town. She is also capable of real fear. As [Song Changjing](Song_Changjing.md)'s carriages passed through the gate out of the Jewel Small World, she curled up in a corner and sobbed that she could sense terrifying things beyond it that even he could not handle. *(Chapter 66)*

## Background

### The well

Zhi Gui's true name is Wang Zhu. The two characters together form the character for "pearl," the most precious part of a true dragon. *(Chapter 63 (1))* She is a jewel formed from the soul of the world's final true dragon. *(Chapter 191 (2))* For 3,000 years she lay sealed in the small town, beneath the Iron Lock Well. She describes endless sixty-year cycles of Buddhist chanting, Daoist talismans, righteous light and sword qi tearing at her, without a single day of rest. *(Chapter 15)*

The town's children never knew what lay in the well. When Cao Xi relieved himself into it as a boy, the iron chain clanked and the water surged up to his feet, and he fled in terror. Years later Zhi Gui told him she would have made him drink the well dry if he had been slower. Xie Shi, on the other hand, thanked her for saving his life twice.

### Clay Vase Alley

When she finally broke free, she was drawn to Chen Ping'an at first. Then she sensed the rich dragon qi in Song Jixin's house next door. Too weak to reach it, she collapsed in the snow outside Chen Ping'an's gate, and he saved her life. The accounts of what happened next differ. [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md) says she had no choice but to bind herself to a human, and that she repaid her rescuer by slowly devouring what little good fortune Chen Ping'an had. [Old Man Yang](Old_Man_Yang.md) says she refused to bind herself to a mortal like him and declared herself Song Jixin's maidservant instead, and Chen Ping'an unknowingly handed the greatest opportunity in the Jewel Small World to his neighbour. *(Chapter 86 (1))* Liu Xianyang always insisted that her real name was Wang Zhu and that "Zhi Gui" was a name Song Jixin picked up somewhere on a whim. The town's wealthier families took the name as proof that Song Jixin was the kiln official's son. *(Chapter 5)*

Before leaving the small town, Qi Jingchun confronted her in Clay Vase Alley. She mocked him, defended what she had done to Chen Ping'an as simple hunger, and refused to repent. He forced her to kowtow three times, to the world, to all living beings and to the Great Dao. Then he warned her that somewhere beyond the town there would always be someone more unreasonable than she was. She thanked him for his teaching, then spat on the ground as soon as he was gone.

### Leaving the Jewel Small World

Zhi Gui left for the capital with Song Jixin and Song Changjing, who warned her that her fate was now tied to his nephew's and that she should not push her luck. She followed Song Jixin to the White Jade Capital tower. Later she returned briefly to the small town with Cui Chan, who told her she was the true owner of that small world and that he was only there to watch over her.

On the trip to [Old Dragon City](Old_Dragon_City.md), she was the real focus of the visit, not Song Jixin. The Fu Clan treated her as an honoured guest, and Fu Qi told [Fu Nanhua](Fu_Nanhua.md) that her opinion had decided who would inherit the city. She climbed the Dragon Scaling Platform alone and broke through the sea of clouds above it. When she reached the top, blood ran from her golden eyes as she looked out over a world filled with the tombs of her kind. *(Chapter 255 (1))* Coming down, she spoke coldly to the Mohist swordsman Xu Ruo, who warned her not to go too far. She replied that this world was an ancient battlefield piled with more corpses than any mountain. *(Chapter 257 (2))*

### Later years

[Lu Chen](Lu_Chen.md) once arranged a promise with the Great Li emperor and planned to hand [Ma Kuxuan](Ma_Kuxuan.md) over to Zhi Gui. She thought little of it until Ma Kuxuan began rising rapidly at True Martial Mountain. When he came to see her with the Divine Edict Sect's Qi Zhen, she brushed off his long devotion and told him to stop boasting before he had actually become powerful. That night she ate a grain rain coin whole and scolded Song Jixin's horned snake for being so timid. *(Chapter 415 (1))*

She still wears a maidservant's clothes, only finer than before, and she stays at Song Jixin's side after he becomes feudal lord of Old Dragon City. Song Jixin feels her drifting further from him, and he senses that something is going on between her and the city's Fu Clan. *(Chapter 631 (6))*

## Trivia

- Both [Liu Xianyang](Liu_Xianyang.md) and [Ma Kuxuan](Ma_Kuxuan.md) were in love with her.
- Ma Kuxuan's [grandmother](Ma_Lanhua.md) called her a poisonous vixen and warned him to stay away from her.
- She once told Song Jixin that she would curtsy ten thousand times if it earned her a bag of coins each time.
- Gu Can likes to call her a "bitch" and once promised to find Chen Ping'an a roomful of girls as pretty as she is.
- Fu Qi lent her the Old Dragon Robe, which she wrapped herself in like a blanket.
- [Pei Qian](Pei_Qian.md), watching scenes from her master's childhood, declared that Song Jixin and Zhi Gui both deserved to die.
