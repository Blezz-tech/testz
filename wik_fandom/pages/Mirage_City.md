# Mirage City

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Mirage_City)

---
Mirage City is a complex and multifaceted location with significant political, cultural, and mystical importance. It is a place where the fate of the Great Quan Empire is decided, and where various powerful figures and factions compete for influence. The city is a hub of activity, with a unique blend of tradition and modernity, where both mundane and mystical elements intersect. The city is a place of beauty and grandeur but is also a location where conflicts and tensions are always lurking beneath the surface.

**General Characteristics:**

- Mirage City is the **capital city** of the Great Quan Empire.
- It is a **major political and cultural hub**, drawing in various influential people and factions.
- The city is known for its **complex social dynamics** with a mix of powerful officials, wealthy clans, scholars, and cultivators.
- It is described as a **prosperous city**, with a high level of activity and a wide range of people from different backgrounds.
- The city has a **scholarly temple and city god pavilion**.
- The city is so large that people who live there their entire lives may not have seen all of it.
- It is a place where the local inhabitants look down on people from other places, making it difficult for newcomers.

**Political and Imperial Significance:**

- Mirage City is the seat of the **Great Quan Empire's imperial court**, making it a center of political power.
- The city is home to the **emperor** and various **high-ranking officials**, including powerful ministers and generals.
- The city is a place where the **emperor's authority** is centralized, and decisions that impact the entire empire are made.
- There are a lot of officials and nobles in the city who are competing for power.
- The city is home to the **Green Roving Residence**, which is being promoted to a palace, a move that reflects the influence of a noble scholar.

**Key Figures and Factions:**

- **Duke Gao Shizhen** is a significant figure in the city, described as shrewd and selfless, with a close relationship with the emperor. He has a son, Gao Shuyi, who is known for acting wantonly.
- **The Yao Clan** is an influential military clan that has a strong presence in the city. They have connections with the imperial court, and their arrival in the city is met with great fanfare. Yao Zhen is a prominent member of this clan.
- **The Xu Clan** is a powerful military clan that has been established in Mirage City for generations.
- **A noble scholar** is residing in Mirage City, who is a key reason for the Great Quan Empire's prosperity.
- There are also **numerous other powerful officials, nobles, and cultivators** that reside in or frequent the city.
- There are also powerful figures such as **Du Hanling**, the Temple Master of the Golden Summit Daoist Temple, who are influential in the city.

**Social and Cultural Aspects:**

- The city is known for its **gardens and scenic spots**, some of which are described as unmatched in beauty.
- There are unique cultural practices, such as the construction of **clay statues of gods**, with different styles associated with various clans.
- There is a **strong emphasis on scholarly pursuits**, with a scholarly temple and influential scholars.
- The city is a place where **various people from different backgrounds come together**, creating a diverse cultural environment.
- There are many **wealthy people** in the city, but also many depraved gamblers.
- The city has its own unique traditions and customs that newcomers must become accustomed to.

**Conflicts and Tensions:**

- There is a sense of **political intrigue and power struggles** among the various factions in the city.
- There are those who seek to **undermine the authority of the emperor and the imperial court**, creating a tense atmosphere.
- There are various **conflicts and battles** taking place in and around the city, with powerful individuals and factions vying for control.
- The city is a place where **those with ill intentions may visit**, while those with good intentions might avoid.
- There is an awareness that the peace and prosperity of the city might be threatened.

**Mystical and Spiritual Elements:**

- The **mountain god** has a shrine nearby the city where people can go to enjoy the view.
- There is a **noble scholar** living in the city who appears to be connected to the rise of the Great Quan Empire.
- It is suggested that some places in the city, like the Green Roving Residence and the Burial River God Temple, have **spiritual significance**.
- It is a place where **geomancy** is considered important, and that the placement of some places in the city may have significance.

**Connections to the Wider World:**

- Mirage City is **connected to other regions and powers** through trade and diplomacy.
- The city has an immortal ferry station that allows travel to other regions.
- There are individuals from other regions who come to the city for various reasons, including those seeking fated opportunities, making it a place of diverse interactions.
