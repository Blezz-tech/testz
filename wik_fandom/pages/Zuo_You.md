**Zuo You** is the second disciple of the [Scholarly Sage](Scholarly_Sage.md), the senior brother of [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md), and, initiallty reluctantly, of [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md). A swordsman in Confucian robes who cares nothing for debate, he is widely regarded as having perhaps the finest swordsmanship under the heavens. Even the Ascension Tier [Du Mao](Du_Mao.md) praises him, and the swordsmen of [Northern Complete Reed Continent](Northern_Complete_Reed_Continent.md) revere him. He spent more than a century wandering the seas, far from the human world.

He refused both his teacher's request to protect Qi Jingchun and Qi Jingchun's request to be Chen Ping'an's Dao guardian. Still, he steps in when he sees his junior brother being bullied, and his sword strikes at the Flood Dragon Trench and against Du Mao are among the most memorable in the story.

## Appearance

Zuo You wears azure Confucian robes and carries a sword at his waist. He looks nothing like a scholar, with a temperament neither as elegant as Qi Jingchun's nor as friendly as A'Liang's.

## Personality

Zuo You is blunt, stubborn and short-tempered. He dislikes speaking reason, asking what the point of a sword would be if one always had to reason with others. As a senior brother, he believes that lecturing a junior brother needs no further justification. As a boy, he found his teacher's boasting and Qi Jingchun's rapid page-turning the two most annoying things in the world. He is awkward around women, and finds a woman's heart harder to understand than his master's teachings.

Despite this, he is fundamentally a good and principled person. He does not attack those who leave him alone, and he refuses to take lives without cause. During his entire assault on Parasol Leaf Sect, he does not kill a single person. The Scholarly Sage calls him the most balanced of his disciples, patient and focused, and secretly the one most afraid of loneliness, who never stopped thinking of his teacher and fellow disciples while wandering the seas. *(Chapter 450 (2))* He once told Chen Ping'an that cultivators can no longer really be considered human, but have become something else. *(Chapter 278 (1))*

## Background

Zuo You was the Scholarly Sage's second disciple. In their impoverished days in a small alley on the Middle Earth Divine Continent, the Scholarly Sage put him in charge of their finances, since he managed money far better than his teacher. He would sneak over the wall at night and bring back snacks for the young Qi Jingchun. He was a late bloomer who advocated walking the path of the Great Dao alone. He began learning the sword late, yet progressed so astonishingly quickly that the Scholarly Sage feared he might become the first fourteenth-tier sword cultivator in thousands of years. *(Chapter 402 (1))*

Once, when a sage wronged their teacher, Zuo You secretly beat the sage up, then admitted it and went to apologise to his teacher. The Scholarly Sage scolded him in public and took him to apologise, but privately complained that he had hit the sage too lightly.

After his teacher lost the battle between third and fourth, Zuo You stayed by his side, cheerful and unbothered, as he entered the Forest of Virtue. He then distanced himself from the mortal world and set off alone to sea. Over the following century, he shattered the sword hearts of many talented young swordsmen from every continent who came to challenge him, including Cao Jun of Southern Whirl Continent. *(Chapter 364 (3))* The swordsman Ji Yue of [Northern Complete Reed Continent](Northern_Complete_Reed_Continent.md) was the first from that continent to seek him out at sea, and could not withstand even a single strike. *(Chapter 516 (3))*

## Plot

### The Flood Dragon Trench

While Chen Ping'an is crossing the sea on Osmanthus Island, a swordsman flies towards the Flood Dragon Trench, destroying a sect's rain god statue along the way when he is told to detour. From three to four hundred kilometres away he flicks his wrist, and sword qi pours down from the heavens into the trench like waterfalls. The elderly golden flood dragon threatening Chen Ping'an is pierced through all three hundred of its acupoints. Zuo You tells Chen Ping'an that he refused Qi Jingchun's request to be his Dao guardian, just as he once refused to protect Qi Jingchun, but that it isn't right for others to bully someone so young. He then introduces himself as "Little Qi's senior brother... and your senior brother, too". *(Chapter 265)*

He warns Chen Ping'an to cultivate earnestly and not disappoint Little Qi, or he will come to cause trouble, and declares that however strong Chen Ping'an becomes, Zuo You can still deal with him with a single slash. When a Daoist disciple of the Dao Ancestor's second disciple appears as a 300-metre golden Dao Manifestation to challenge him, Zuo You mocks him and shatters the manifestation instantly with a column of sword qi wider than Osmanthus Island. He leaves Chen Ping'an the flood dragon's golden robe, its whiskers and its pill-like jewel. *(Chapter 266 (1))*

### Parasol Leaf Continent

On Parasol Leaf Continent, Zuo You slays a great demon fleeing from Planchette Writing Sect with a single slash simply because he is in a foul mood, depriving a Parasol Leaf Sect cultivator of the kill. *(Chapter 356 (2))* Chen Ping'an later tells Jiang Shangzhen that Zuo You admitted his sword intent is inferior to A'Liang's but his swordsmanship superior. He adds that Zuo You only accepted him as junior brother with extreme reluctance. *(Chapter 359 (2))*

When the Ascension Tier Du Mao of Parasol Leaf Sect attacks Chen Ping'an and Zheng Dafeng at Old Dragon City, partly hoping to lure Zuo You out, Zuo You does not come. Afterwards, the Scholarly Sage finds him and slaps him repeatedly for having vanished for so long and cared so little for everyone else. Zuo You takes the beating in silence, then asks his teacher what he should do, and flies off over the sea. *(Chapter 366 (2))*

He descends on Parasol Leaf Sect and calmly announces, as if he were a student come to learn, that his master told him to come and curse their ancestors in the most vulgar terms. *(Chapter 367 (3))* For days he methodically destroys the sect's mountain and river fortune, waiting for Du Mao to come out. He does not kill anyone. When his sword qi frightens a young woman, he scratches his head in embarrassment, and he releases the pressure on a stubborn young swordsman who glares at him. He warns the sect that Du Mao will lead them to ruin. *(Chapter 368 (2))* Zuo You believes there are only two swords at the pinnacle of the world: the one a Middle Earth scholar used to slice Yellow River Small World apart, and his own.

Out at sea, the Scholarly Sage furiously asks why he did not use the rift to leave the world. Zuo You lowers his head without answering. His teacher sighs and tells him to go to Stalactite Mountain and the Sword Qi Great Wall, as he has always wanted. Zuo You bows in farewell, walks away step by step rather than flying, and tells his teacher that he accepted a great disciple in their junior brother. *(Chapter 369 (3))*

### Reputation

On Northern Complete Reed Continent, the swordsmen revere Zuo You for never wasting words, for having a temper shorter even than theirs, and for treating an Ascension Tier cultivator and a mortal alike. Zhu Quan of Wood Garment Mountain greatly admires him. *(Chapter 513 (1))* 

## Abilities

- **Swordsmanship:** widely considered the best in the world. Zuo You himself says his sword intent is inferior to A'Liang's but his swordsmanship superior.
- **Sword qi:** able to pour sword qi into the Flood Dragon Trench from hundreds of kilometres away, shatter a 300-metre Dao Manifestation instantly, and cut an Ascension Tier manifestation in half with a single languid slash.
- **Control:** His sword qi can kill the flood dragons of an entire trench while leaving Chen Ping'an and the elderly flood dragon untouched, and he can destroy a sect's fortune without taking a single life.
- **Learning:** Although he disliked studying, he is still more learned than most academies' noble scholars, thanks to the books his teacher forced on him.

## Trivia

- Zuo You's name (左右) literally means "left and right".
- The Burial River water goddess, a fan of the Scholarly Sage's lineage, calls his name "incredibly domineering".
