# Great Li Empire

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Li_Empire)

---
The **Great Li Empire** is the largest mortal empire on [Eastern Treasured Vial Continent](Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent.md), ruling the entire northern stretch of the continent. Founded by the Imperial Song Clan after centuries as a vassal state of the Lu Empire, it grew from a small dominion of 70 counties into a continental power and, by the later volumes, the de facto ruler of the entire continent.

## Geography

The Great Li Empire occupies the northernmost region of Eastern Treasured Vial Continent. Its people were long looked down upon as northern barbarians by the older civilizations to the south. *(Chapter 39)* The empire's territory expanded dramatically over two to three centuries, eventually swallowing the Lu Empire and sweeping south all the way to Old Dragon City, the southernmost city on the continent. *(Chapter 550 (4))*

The capital city is home to over a million residents and serves as the seat of the emperor, the imperial court, and the Mountain and River Formation that protects the realm. *(Chapter 84 (2))* The empire is also home to [Mountain Cliff Academy](Mountain_Cliff_Academy.md), one of only two Confucian academies on the continent, which played a major role in raising the empire's reputation from a northern backwater to a civilized power. *(Chapter 82 (1))*

## History

The Song Clan's realm spent several hundred years as a vassal state under the Lu Empire, which suppressed and abused it for generations. The Great Li Empire broke free and declared itself a sovereign state in its own right, an event still celebrated in the history books. *(Chapter 550 (4))*

Over the following centuries the empire expanded steadily, and in the fifty years before the novel's start it doubled in size, growing from 70 counties and 800 cities to 140 counties and 1,500 cities. Imperial Preceptor Cui Chan, the "Embroidered Tiger", is credited with half that growth. *(Chapter 39)* The other half belongs to Song Changjing, the Monarch and the empire's supreme martial artist, who personally destroyed the Lu Empire in a single campaign, ending its long reign as the continent's dominant power. *(Chapter 550 (4))*

By the later volumes, the Great Li Empire's heavy cavalry had marched south through every remaining empire and vassal state on the continent. Emperor Song He presided over a realm whose territory had no precedent in the history of the Majestic World, with the seas effectively forming its borders. *(Chapter 555 (2))*

## Government

The empire is ruled by the Imperial Song Clan. Three figures hold the real power:
- The **emperor**, who commands the formal apparatus of court and controls the divine infrastructure of mountains and rivers.
- **Song Changjing** (the Monarch), the emperor's younger brother and the continent's preeminent martial artist, who leads the military and conquered the empire's enemies.
- **Cui Chan** (the imperial preceptor, nicknamed the "Embroidered Tiger"), who shapes strategy, trains the cavalry, and serves as the emperor's most trusted advisor. *(Chapter 187 (2))*

The empire's administrative hierarchy includes six ministries, with the Ministry of Rites and Ministry of Justice playing prominent roles in relations with divine powers and conquered territories. Six military governor positions were created in the later volumes to administer the newly conquered lands. *(Chapter 550 (4))*

The empire is also supported by six pillar clans, among them the Yuan Clan and the Cao Clan, who wield enormous influence in court. *(Chapter 13)*

## Emperors

- **Song Zhengchun** — the emperor at the time of the story's beginning. He brought Song Jixin (his son Song Mu) back to the capital after the fall of [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md), and later officially recognized Song Mu as his heir. *(Chapter 116)*
- **[Song He](Song_He.md)** — Song Zhengchun's younger son, who inherited the throne and became emperor by Volume 7. Under his reign the empire completed its conquest of the continent. *(Chapter 550 (4))*

## Military

The Great Li Empire is renowned on Eastern Treasured Vial Continent for the quality and discipline of its martial artists and cavalry. More martial artists of the Middle Five Tiers serve the empire than any other nation on the continent, with Song Changjing at their head. *(Chapter 75)* Imperial Preceptor Cui Chan's meticulous cultivation of the cavalry over decades gave it a discipline that set it apart from every other army in the Majestic World. *(Chapter 559 (4))*

The empire's intelligence apparatus spans seven agencies, three of which were controlled by a single senior official during the small town era. *(Chapter 62)*

## Religion and divine hierarchy

The empire maintains tight control over the gods within its borders. All legitimate mountain gods, river gods, and yin gods within its territory are formally appointed by the imperial court and answer to a hierarchy of senior river gods and the Ministry of Rites. Rogue gods and illegal temples were systematically destroyed as the armies marched south. *(Chapter 556 (2))*

The Northern Mountain serves as the formal home of the empire's mountain god hierarchy, with the great mountain god of Cloud Drape Mountain (later [Wei Bo](Wei_Bo.md)) as one of its most powerful members. *(Chapter 559 (1))*

## Relationship with Mountain Cliff Academy

[Mountain Cliff Academy](Mountain_Cliff_Academy.md), overseen by [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md) during his 60-year tenure in [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md), provided the empire with most of its educated officials and was instrumental in transforming it from a settlement of northern barbarians into a literate, civilized power. The academy's decline after Qi Jingchun's departure threatened the pipeline of talent the empire depended on. *(Chapter 82 (1))*

## Trivia

- The empire's territory, spanning 140 counties and 1,500 cities before the southern campaigns, held over 20 million families. *(Chapter 41)*
- The Great Sui Nation's royal Gao Clan and the Great Li Empire's Song Clan are hereditary enemies. *(Chapter 34)*
- Song Changjing submitted a memorial to the emperor consisting of a single sentence — "There are heroes outside of our Great Li Empire as well" — and the emperor laughed and approved it without revision. *(Chapter 93 (1))*
- Cui Chan wrote the couplets for [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md)'s county offices and lingered a long while over the names on a wall in the small town's temple. *(Chapter 85 (1))*
