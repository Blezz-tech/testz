# Parasol Leaf Continent

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Parasol_Leaf_Continent)

---
**Parasol Leaf Continent** is one of the nine continents of the [Majestic World](Majestic_World.md), lying southeast of the [Eastern Treasured Vial Continent](Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent.md). It is a large and inward-looking continent with few immortal forces, most of them very powerful. It is home to the [Lotus Flower Blessed Land](Lotus_Flower_Blessed_Land.md), where [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md) spent much of his early travels.

## Geography

The continent is far larger than the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent, and larger even than Circling Ascension Continent.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> The seas around it are rough, with strong winds and high waves. For that reason almost all of its ferry stations lie in the north.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> The Parasol Leaf Sect and the Jade Tablet Sect hold the two ends of the continent, and each is said to hold half of its fortune.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> The Jade Tablet Sect's southern lands are green and rich in spiritual energy.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

The central region holds the Planchette Writing Sect, [Peace and Tranquility Mountain](Sects.md#peace-and-tranquility-mountain) and Great Subduing Academy.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup> The Great Quan Empire is one of the stronger empires there and was rising to lead the region.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> Burial River was once one of the three great rivers of the continent that flowed into the sea.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> The continent has four Confucian academies, one each in the north and south, Great Subduing Academy in the centre, and the little-known Literary Profundity Academy.

## History

Chen Ping An arrived at the Planchette Writing Sect's ferry station on his way to find the Observing Dao Temple. Soon after, a great demon of the Upper Five Tiers hidden in the central region destroyed almost half of the Planchette Writing Sect. At the same moment, the well prison of Peace and Tranquility Mountain opened, and more than half of the demons held there for thousands of years escaped. The central region fell into chaos, and the academies and sects of the continent joined forces to hunt them down.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

Du Mao, an Ascension Tier cultivator of the [Parasol Leaf Sect](Sects.md#parasol-leaf-sect), nearly killed Chen Ping An in [Old Dragon City](Old_Dragon_City.md). In return, Chen Ping An's senior brother [Zuo You](Zuo_You.md) brought the Parasol Leaf Sect to the worst crisis in its history.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup> The sect fell from grace, and the [Jade Tablet Sect](Sects.md#jade-tablet-sect), second on the continent for thousands of years, rose to the top almost without effort.

[Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md) believed that once the [Sword Qi Great Wall](Sword_Qi_Great_Wall.md) fell, the demon tribe would strike Parasol Leaf Continent and that few of its Upper Five Tier cultivators would survive.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> In time great demons were indeed wreaking havoc there.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup>

## Cultivation world

Unlike the Eastern Treasured Vial Continent, which is crowded with small forces, Parasol Leaf Continent has few sects. Most of them are very strong, and the leading cultivators of its two great sects reached the Immortal Tier centuries ago. For a thousand years the Planchette Writing Sect was counted third after the two great sects, though Peace and Tranquility Mountain was said to be at least its equal. Its cultivators are known for their pride and for belittling the elites of other continents.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup>

## Trivia

- The continent is very conservative and does not welcome outsiders. An outside power like [Xie Shi](Xie_Shi.md) would have been beaten half to death there.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup>
- It is so reluctant to deal with other continents that it has never built a single intercontinental ship of its own.
- Its many dialects make learning the official dialect of its imperial courts the easiest way for an outsider to travel.
- One passage places the Parasol Leaf Sect in the south and the Jade Tablet Sect in the north, the reverse of the rest of the novel.
