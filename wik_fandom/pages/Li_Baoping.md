**Li Baoping** is the youngest child and only daughter of Li Hong, the leader of the Li Clan of [Fortune Street](Small_Town.md#fortune-street-and-peach-leaf-alley) in [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md). She is one of [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md)'s five direct disciples and travels to [Mountain Cliff Academy](Mountain_Cliff_Academy.md) in Great Sui Nation under the protection of Chen Ping'an, whom she calls her "junior uncle".

## Appearance

Li Baoping is a small girl with her hair in two braids and cheeks often flushed from running. She wears a red jacket as a matter of habit. Setting out for the academy, she carries a small basket on her back topped with a conical bamboo hat, with no fewer than seven or eight embroidered pouches hanging all over her body. Her bamboo bookcase, which is the earliest of the three that Chen Ping'an made, crafted from ordinary green bamboo, stays as verdant as the day it was made even years later.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup><sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>

## Personality

Li Baoping is bright, direct and relentlessly curious. She chatters freely, asks questions without end, and does not let an unsatisfying answer go. She has a strong sense of fairness and is quick to step in on behalf of anyone she thinks is being wronged. When Li Huai's classmate at Mountain Cliff Academy snatched a book from him, Li Baoping announced she would tear it in half and give each of them a piece — and when the other child agreed, she tossed the book to Li Huai and beat the thief up instead. An old scholar watching from a distance laughed heartily at her solution.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

She is also stubborn, competitive and not above gloating. Li Huai is her habitual sparring partner in both argument and mischief. None of the children at the private school could match her in a fight.

Her affection for Chen Ping'an is genuine. She calls him "junior uncle", repeats things he says, holds him to his own principles, and tells him bluntly when she thinks he is wrong. When he sends letters to the children at Mountain Cliff Academy, she convenes the whole group using a self-carved "alliance leader badge" before reading each letter aloud in order, saving his letter to her for last, and she has read it nine times through.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

## Background

Li Baoping is one of five children from the private school whom Qi Jingchun took as direct disciples, giving each of them the character Qi before he died. The others are Li Huai, Lin Shouyi, Shi Chunjia and Dong Shuijing. Cui Chan considered it remarkable that she, out of all of them, was Qi Jingchun's true direct disciple.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

She grew up in the Li Clan manor on Fortune Street. Her grandfather, the Li Clan patriarch, is a Nascent Tier earth immortal and a top-level guest elder of the Great Li Empire — a status the clan kept private for many years. He is openly devoted to her, adoring her more than both grandsons combined. Her father Li Hong loves her best of all his children. Her older brother Li Xisheng taught at the private school and watched over her from a distance, while Li Baozhen sent her letters from the capital that she treasured and read eagerly.

She and Chen Ping'an first properly encountered each other when the old locust tree in Clay Vase Alley was uprooted. She had gone to look at it instead of going to school, and he found her there. He gave her three of the locust leaves he had collected, and she kept them.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

She was close to Zhu Lu, the Li Clan maidservant who taught her about martial arts and whom she had once followed everywhere.

## Plot

### Journey to Mountain Cliff Academy

Li Baoping set out from [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md) with Chen Ping'an, accompanied by Zhu He and Zhu Lu, bound for Mountain Cliff Academy in Great Sui Nation. Ruan Xiu packed her basket with maps, books and travel money. She left in high spirits, jumping for joy when Chen Ping'an gave the word to depart.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>

On the road she and A'Liang got along easily, in contrast to almost everyone else he travelled with, and she gave him a thumbs up when others refused to engage with him. At Go Table Mountain she and the group survived the attack of the black and white pythons, and she witnessed Zhu Lu's panicked decision to free Wei Bo and Zhu He's inability to hold his daughter accountable.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup>

At Pillow Relay Station, Zhu Lu attempted to assassinate Chen Ping'an on Li Baozhen's secret instructions. Chen Ping'an told Li Baoping the truth, believing she needed to know what the people around her had done. She was deeply hurt.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

She eventually reached Mountain Cliff Academy and settled into life there, attending classes on her own, climbing trees and buildings, squatting by the lake to watch fish, and generally puzzling her classmates with her habits. In her own assessment, she was having a decent time.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

### At Mountain Cliff Academy

When Chen Ping'an sent letters to the group from afar, Li Baoping gathered everyone with her wooden alliance leader badge, read all letters aloud, and distributed them — except to Li Huai, who had not been written to, much to his humiliation.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

She and the other Mountain Cliff Academy students attended the Night Travel Banquet hosted by the Great Li Empire's Northern Mountain as a group excursion, led by Mao Xiaodong, where she was reunited with Jiu'er, a young girl she had befriended earlier and missed greatly.

When Chen Ping'an visited, Li Baoping met Pei Qian, his attendant. The two girls took to each other immediately. In a later meeting, Pei Qian launched herself at Li Baoping the moment she saw her, and Li Baoping patted her on the head and teased her about growing taller. Li Baoping, Pei Qian and Cui Dongshan ended up sitting together on a branch at the mountain summit, and Li Baoping felt it was a shame her junior uncle was not there to join them.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup>

## Trivia

- Li Baoping is said to have been born unable to sit still, always darting around — a quirk her family attributed fondly to her constitution.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup>
- She once built a pond in the Li Clan courtyard for Li Xisheng by collecting pebbles from Dragon Whisker Creek over many months. When he tried to help with the physical work, she refused.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup>
- The three bamboo bookcases Chen Ping'an made (for Li Baoping, Li Huai and Lin Shouyi) were crafted at different times. Li Baoping's was made first, from ordinary green bamboo. The other two were made after Go Table Mountain, using the courage bamboo Wei Bo had given them. All three remained as green as ever years later.
