**Gu Can** is a boy from [Clay Vase Alley](Small_Town.md#clay-vase-alley) in the small town of [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md), raised by his mother and looked after by his neighbor [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md), who thinks of him as a little brother. Taken away by [Liu Zhimao](Liu_Zhimao.md), he grew into the feared "little devil" of Bamboo Scroll Lake before Chen Ping'an came to set him right.

## Appearance

As a child in the town, Gu Can is small and scrawny, always with two lines of snot under his nose and a pair of big eyes like black grapes. *(Chapter 7)* He weighed under 40 kilograms but carried an aptitude so heavy that was very heavy. *(Chapter 8)*

At Bamboo Scroll Lake he wears a dark green official python robe and has a habit of tucking his hands into his opposite sleeves. A few years later he has shot up to the height of a young man from the north, and in his later travels he carries a folding fan of divine cloud bamboo.

## Personality

His mother did not always raise him well, and he learned early to be sharp, sly and watchful as a way of protecting himself. He holds grudges for years and loves to rant about digging up the ancestral graves of anyone who wronged him, such as the drunkard who once kicked him in the stomach and stomped on his head until Chen Ping'an carried him off for help. *(Chapter 93 (1))* He is also patient enough to sit in the blazing sun all day for a single eel.

With the two people he trusts, his mother and Chen Ping'an, he is a different child: clingy, quick to cry and eager to please. At Bamboo Scroll Lake that loyalty turned into a belief that anything was allowed so long as it kept his mother safe, and he honestly could not see why killing his enemies, and the people around them, was wrong. After losing Tan Xue and making amends under Chen Ping'an's rules, he became calmer, more measured and far harder to read. He still keeps a list of grudges, but he no longer kills over hurtful words. *(Chapter 534 (2))*

## Background

### Clay Vase Alley

Gu Can grew up without his father, and his mother, a sharp-tongued beauty who could hold her own against Granny Ma and the other alley women, raised him alone. She fed Chen Ping'an a bowl of rice when he had nothing, and in return Chen Ping'an stood up for the pair, carried water for them in droughts and treated Gu Can like a younger brother. Chen Ping'an also gave him a small black catfish, which Gu Can kept in his water vat.

A storyteller with a bottomless white bowl, who turned out to be Liu Zhimao, the River Severing True Lord of Lake Shujian, found that Gu Can had a great aptitude for cultivation and took him as a disciple on the spot. When Cai Jinjian of Dawn Cloud Mountain came to claim him, Liu Zhimao drove her off. The little catfish was in truth a flood dragon, and formed a close relationship with Gu Can. *(Chapter 18)* 

Before leaving, Gu Can whispered to Chen Ping'an about the treasures he had hidden at the bottom of his water vat. *(Chapter 25)* Among them was a fist manual, the Mountain Shaking Guide, which became the foundation of Chen Ping'an's fist techniques. Gu Can later refused to take it back. His father Gu Tao turned out to be a [Yin god](Yin_god.md) whose soul had never left. When he later met Chen Ping'an, he thanked him for looking after Little Can all those years. *(Chapter 134 (2))*

### Bamboo Scroll Lake

Liu Zhimao took Gu Can and his mother to Cyan Gorge Island at Bamboo Scroll Lake. When a senior brother lured Gu Can away so a guest elder could take his mother as a mistress, Gu Can let the flood dragon swallow the senior brother whole, and told his master he could either kill him and face the dragon or lose one of his many disciples. *(Chapter 206 (5))* From then on he rode the dragon through the lake, wiped out sects that sent assassins after him, and picked beautiful girls for his future harem. *(Chapter 304 (2))* Liu Zhimao named him the future master of Cyan Gorge Island.

Chen Ping'an arrived years later and slapped him twice in public in Pond Water City. Gu Can took it without a word and brought him home, and when Chen Ping'an asked about an assassin, Gu Can explained calmly that killing her alone would not deter anyone. *(Chapter 430 (1))* Over dinner he insisted he had done nothing wrong. Unable to kill him or walk away, Chen Ping'an broke his own second bonded item that night and stayed on the island to keep Gu Can from killing again. *(Chapter 432 (2))*

When the Upper Five Tiers cultivator Liu Laocheng came to Cyan Gorge Island, he beat Gu Can and the flood dragon to the brink of death, and only Chen Ping'an stepping in kept them alive. *(Chapter 437 (3))* Chen Ping'an later killed Tan Xue, the flood dragon, with his sword for the lives it had taken. Gu Can ran through the snow and knelt outside his door, crying like the snotty boy from Clay Vase Alley for the first time in years. *(Chapter 442 (3))*

### Making amends

Some time later, Gu Can asked Chen Ping'an to take his mother away from Bamboo Scroll Lake. Having learned the price of not speaking reason, he wanted to learn the price of speaking it. *(Chapter 455 (3))* His mother went back to Dragon Spring Prefecture, while Gu Can traveled the mountains around the lake with Zeng Ye, and his cultivation sped up once he committed to it, reaching the Abode Tier.

Chen Ping'an left him the Hall of the Underworld and a replica of the Colored Glaze Pagoda, which held the souls of people he had killed, along with two years to arrange a Heavenly Worship Ceremony and a Liberation Rite of Water and Land for them. It was both a penance and a test. *(Chapter 464 (1))* Gu Can kept his promise, struck deals with the angry spirits, and slowly earned respect around the lake.

Before going into seclusion, Liu Zhimao visited him, passed him the River Severing Sutra, and judged that he had finally learned nuance. Gu Can thanked his master for taking him out of Clay Vase Alley, but he never opened the sutra that night. Afterward he quietly built up wealth and influence, though he privately grieved that the Gu Can Chen Ping'an most wanted to see was gone for good.

## Trivia

- He is said to have received more ancestral blessing than almost anyone in the town's history, more even than Liu Xianyang.
- He collected snake gall pebbles but only ever took one home at a time.
- He never thought of Liu Xianyang as a friend, since Liu Xianyang loved to joke about his mother, but he envied how easily Liu Xianyang fit in anywhere.
- He, Liu Xianyang and Chen Ping'an once wrote their names at the very top of a wall in the town's small temple.
- Chen Ping'an paid for the couplets and door gods on Gu Can's home every year, though he was poorer than Gu Can's family.
- Out of the five fated opportunities of the small town, Gu Can was the first to lose his when Tan Xue died.
