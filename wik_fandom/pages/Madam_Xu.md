**Madam Xu** is the wife of the leader of the Xu Clan of Light Breeze City. She came to the small town in Jewel Small World with her son and schemed to take the Liu Clan's ancestral armor from [Liu Xianyang](Liu_Xianyang.md).

## Appearance

Madam Xu is a middle-aged woman. *(Chapter 2)* She is described as beautiful and full-figured. *(Chapter 7)* In town she carries herself with calm dignity. *(Chapter 35)* Years later she appears at Sun Scorch Mountain as an elegant woman in a palace dress. *(Chapter 533)*

## Personality

Madam Xu is shrewd and calculating, and is called the calculating mistress of Light Breeze City. She treats every dealing like business. She once explained to her son that favours given now and then earn gratitude, while favours given all the time breed resentment. She also told him that people in small places are not as foolish as they look. *(Chapter 34)*

With her son she is patient and indulgent. She never minds when he shouts at her, because a powerful figure had declared at his birth that he was fierce and highly gifted. *(Chapter 44)* With others she is gracious on the surface but quietly threatening. When Lu Zhengchun swore loyalty to her alone, she smiled and suggested he repeat those words in front of her husband, and he fell to his knees. She does not dismiss danger easily. Even when the Mountain-moving Ape scoffed at [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md), she stayed uneasy and believed he would dare to seek revenge.

## Background

Madam Xu is the Dao partner of the leader of the Xu Clan, who is later named as Xu Hun. *(Chapter 457)* She entered the small town with her son, a boy in a large red robe, at the same time as the [Mountain-Moving Ape](Mountain-Moving_Ape.md) and [Tao Zi](Tao_Zi.md) of Sun Scorch Mountain. She had paid a great price to come, and her standing in Light Breeze City, along with her hopes of one day running the city, depended on the trip paying off.

Her target was the Liu Clan's scaly armor. Working in secret, she let Sun Scorch Mountain know about the armor and sword scripture in Liu Xianyang's family. She also told them that his bonded porcelain had gone to their enemy, the Wind Lightning Field. She then let the Mountain-moving Ape act as her decoy. She used Lu Zhengchun as a go-between with Liu Xianyang. After the ape struck down Liu Xianyang, she obtained the armor and planned to give it to her husband to make up for his weak constitution. She took Lu Zhengchun into the [Xu Clan](Clans.md) as part of a larger deal with the fallen Lu Empire's imperial clan, who had taken refuge in Light Breeze City.

When she learned more of the situation, she went to the kiln supervision office and offered to return the armor as an apology. Chen Dui of the Yingyin Chen Clan declined, saying the Xu Clan could not have known Liu Xianyang's true background. *(Chapter 65)* Chen Ping An later counted her among the people who left a bad impression on him at first sight. *(Chapter 226)*

With the armor, her husband became a formidable Nascent Tier cultivator and purged rival branches of the clan. The Xu Clan sold its holdings in Dragon Spring Prefecture, including Cinnabar Mountain, at a loss. Cinnabar Mountain later passed to Chen Ping An. *(Chapter 528)* To make up for this, they married a daughter of the clan to an illegitimate son of the [Pillar Yuan Clan](Clans.md) of the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md). *(Chapter 571)*

Madam Xu spent years trying to arrange a marriage between her son and Tao Zi, without success. During a visit to Sun Scorch Mountain, she told the Mountain-moving Ape that Chen Ping An must die before he could become a threat. Liu Xianyang and Chen Ping An both hold a grudge against her clan over the armor. Liu Xianyang described Light Breeze City and Sun Scorch Mountain as places that chase only profit and not the Great Dao. *(Chapter 620)*

## Trivia

- She is mostly called "the woman" or "the lady" of Light Breeze City's Xu Clan.
- Lu Zhengchun addressed her as Mistress Xu when he pledged his loyalty.
- Ma Kuxuan's greatest regret was that she had been too lenient and failed to kill Liu Xianyang. *(Chapter 642)*
- Liu Chicheng described her and her husband as a perfect match. One devotes himself to cultivation while the other runs the clan and makes money. *(Chapter 651)*
- The Xu Clan she helps run is famous for Fox Skin Beauty Talismans and later became one of the sects-in-waiting under the Great Li Empire.
