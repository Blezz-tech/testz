## Appearance

Ruan Xiu is a young woman with a slender build and an appearance described as unforgettable by those who meet her. *(Chapter 420)* She typically wears green clothes and keeps her hair in a ponytail. Despite her slight frame, she has a famously large appetite, particularly for sweets and desserts.

When perceived through spiritual sight, she appears radically different from her unassuming exterior. [Pei Qian](Pei_Qian.md), who could see the true nature of people, once saw Ruan Xiu as a vast sea of bright red flames with the figure of a regal woman holding a radiant sun at its center. *(Chapter 460)*

## Personality

Ruan Xiu is quiet and easygoing on the surface. At Dragon Spring Sword Sect, she is amicable to everyone but genuinely close with no one, preferring to sit apart eating desserts while the other disciples train. She routinely shirks her duties at the sect, showing little interest in the formalities of her position despite being called "Senior Sister Ruan" by the other disciples.

Beneath this relaxed demeanor is a person of deep feeling who keeps her emotions private. When a young girl once asked her if she had someone she liked, she answered simply, "I do. He's always in my heart." *(Chapter 631)* She is not one for grand declarations or dramatic gestures, preferring to express care through quiet, practical actions, such as looking after Chen Ping'an's shop and even his hens while he was away on his travels. *(Chapter 180)*

She possesses a stubborn streak inherited from her father. In her youth, she went into town to help Chen Ping'an despite [Ruan Qiong](Ruan_Qiong.md) explicitly forbidding it. *(Chapter 63)* She is also direct when it matters. When a persistent admirer tried to win her affection with gifts and gestures, she refused him plainly without entertaining the attention. *(Chapter 94)*

## Background

Ruan Xiu is the daughter of [Ruan Qiong](Ruan_Qiong.md), the Militarian Sage and one of the greatest swordsmiths in the world. She originally grew up at Wind Snow Temple before her father relocated to the Jewel Small World. Ruan Qiong's decision to leave was partly motivated by a desire to protect his daughter, whose extraordinary innate talent made her a potential target for dangerous forces.

Ruan Xiu was born with a fire god constitution, making water her natural enemy, and she carries a fire dragon bracelet. She is considered a one-in-a-thousand-year talent. Her constitution grants her a unique way of perceiving the world: she can see the purity of a person's mind, their karmic fate, and their fortune, and the world itself appears to her in different colors depending on these qualities.

She first encountered Chen Ping'an on Azure Cow Ridge in the Jewel Small World. *(Chapter 40)* What drew her attention to him was the extraordinary purity of his mind, which she could perceive through her innate abilities. *(Chapter 46)* Over time, she grew close to him in her own reserved way, looking after his affairs while he traveled far from home.

When Chen Ping'an was preparing to leave on his journey, Ruan Xiu asked her father to let her help forge his sword, but Ruan Qiong refused. *(Chapter 198)* She later delivered the finished sword to Chen Ping'an at [Downtrodden Mountain](Downtrodden_Mountain.md), along with a bag of peach cakes, as a quiet farewell. *(Chapter 204)*

After Dragon Spring Sword Sect was established, Ruan Xiu took on the role of senior disciple in practice if not in formal title. She later traveled south alongside Great Li sticky rod officials and resolved matters at Bamboo Scroll Lake through her own power and ability, without needing to resort to fighting. *(Chapter 478)*

Years later, when she and Chen Ping'an met again at Downtrodden Mountain, she addressed the unspoken tension between them directly. She told him, "The way I like you is not the way that you think I like you," and they parted on clear terms as friends. Her father Ruan Qiong, who had been waiting at the foot of the mountain and claimed he was merely "taking a stroll," said nothing about the conversation.

In later years, Ruan Xiu traveled north to the former Central Mountain territory to negotiate on behalf of Dragon Spring Sword Sect, handling sect affairs with quiet competence despite her reputation for avoiding responsibilities. *(Chapter 641)*

## Trivia

- Ruan Xiu's enormous appetite is a running detail throughout the story. She is especially fond of desserts and peach cakes, and [Cui Dongshan](Cui_Dongshan.md) was once noted to be visibly nervous around her when she had sweets in hand. *(Chapter 455)*
- Despite telling Chen Ping'an that her feelings were not what he assumed, she later confirmed to a young girl that she does have someone she likes, saying he is "always in my heart."
- Her fire god constitution means she perceives the world in a fundamentally different way from ordinary cultivators, seeing people and their fates as colors and light.
- Before leaving on his journey, Chen Ping'an considered leaving Immortal Herb Mountain in Ruan Xiu's care if he could not return. *(Chapter 84)*
