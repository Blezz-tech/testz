### The Three Teachings

The Three Teachings—**Confucianism, Buddhism, and Daoism**—are the most influential philosophical and religious forces in Majestic World. They hold significant sway over the lives of ordinary people and the actions of powerful cultivators.

- **Confucianism**: This teaching emphasizes morality, social harmony, and respect for authority. Confucian schools and academies are scattered across Majestic World, serving as centers of learning and upholding the moral order. They play a crucial role in shaping the laws and governance of mortal empires. **Confucian sages** are the highest authorities within Confucianism, possessing immense knowledge and influence.
- **Buddhism**: Focused on achieving enlightenment and escaping the cycle of suffering, Buddhism is a prominent force with numerous temples and monasteries throughout Majestic World. **Eminent monks** and **Golden Arhats** are powerful figures within Buddhism, often possessing supernatural abilities and profound wisdom.
- **Daoism**: Focused on living in harmony with the Dao, the natural order of the universe, Daoism is known for its diverse practices and esoteric teachings. Daoist temples and blessed lands are scattered across Majestic World, often serving as havens for cultivators seeking to refine their Qi and achieve immortality. **Daoist sages** are among the most powerful figures in Majestic World, commanding vast knowledge of the Dao and wielding incredible mystical powers.

### The Militarians

Although not considered one of the Three Teachings, the **Militarians** constitute a powerful force in Majestic World. They are primarily focused on military strength and martial prowess, with many serving as generals, bodyguards, and military officers in mortal empires.

- **True Martial Mountain and Wind Snow Temple**: These are the two main ancestral forces of the Militarians. True Martial Mountain is known for its close ties with mortal empires, while Wind Snow Temple is more focused on its own development and exploring ancient battlefields.

### The Hundred Schools of Thought

Beyond the Three Teachings and the Militarians exist the **Hundred Schools of Thought**, representing diverse philosophical and intellectual traditions. They hold varying levels of influence and often specialize in specific areas of knowledge or practice.

- **Examples**: The sources mention Commercialism, followed by the five major clans of Old Dragon City, including the Fu Clan and Sun Clan [1]; the School of Naturalists, whose knowledge is said to make up half the teachings in that school, including the Middle Earth Divine Continent's Lu Clan [2]; Mohists, whose artisans are renowned for their craftsmanship [1]; and Fictionists, who can manipulate reality through storytelling [3].

### Major Clans and Sects

Scattered throughout Majestic World are **powerful clans and sects**, each with its own history, traditions, and goals. They often control vast resources and influence and play significant roles in shaping the political and social landscape.

- **Examples**: The Cloud Forest Jiang Clan, renowned for its wealth and influence [4]; the Fu Clan of Old Dragon City, the most powerful clan in the city [5]; Parasol Leaf Sect, a powerful sect that lends its name to an entire continent [6]; and Mount Longhu's Heavenly Master Residence, a prominent Daoist faction [7].

### The Demon Tribe

Originating from a different world called the **Savage World**, the **demon tribe** represents a formidable force often seen as antagonistic to the inhabitants of Majestic World. They are known for their powerful physiques, martial skills, and insatiable desire to conquer. Their ongoing conflict with Majestic World is a major source of tension and conflict.

### Otherworldly Immortals

A relatively new faction, **otherworldly immortals** are individuals who have somehow entered Majestic World from other realms, like the **Lotus Flower Blessed Land**. They often possess unique abilities and knowledge and can disrupt the existing power dynamics. Their origins and motivations are often shrouded in mystery, making them a source of both fascination and apprehension.
