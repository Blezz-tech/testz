**Song Yuzhang**, known in the [Small Town](Small_Town.md) as **Master Song**, was the kiln supervision official who raised [Song Jixin](Song_Jixin.md) on Clay Vase Alley. The town believed Song Jixin was his illegitimate son. After his death he became the mountain god of [Downtrodden Mountain](Downtrodden_Mountain.md).

## Appearance

When he returned to the small town as an official of the Ministry of Rites, his complexion had grown a little fairer than in his years there.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> 

## Personality

Song Yuzhang was a bookish, mild official who made himself well liked. Of all the kiln supervision officials sent to the town over the years, he was the most popular with ordinary people, though he was not much good at actually running the kilns.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> He adapted to circumstances without fuss. When the ministers he travelled with laughed at the townsfolk, he alone did not join in.

He was loyal to the Great Li Empire to the point of foolishness, and he knew it. Cui Chan thought his devotion was ridiculous but still could not help admiring him a little.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup> Even as a god he kept the habits of a careful official. He insisted on bowing to [Wei Bo](Wei_Bo.md) according to the rules, even when Wei Bo tried to wave it off.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>

His one great weakness was the boy he raised. He let the rumour that Song Jixin was his son spread unchecked, and he treated the prince with something very close to a father's love.

## Background

### Kiln supervision official

Song Yuzhang was sent to the small town to serve as kiln supervision official at the same time the infant Song Jixin was hidden there.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup> He held the post for fifteen years. Before he was recalled to the capital, he spent all his money funding the covered bridge outside town, and he left Song Jixin behind to be looked after by his successor.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> The bridge he built hid the Imperial Song Clan's greatest scandal, and he oversaw that bloody piece of clan history from beginning to end.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup>

The late emperor first meant to let him live quietly, placing him in the Ministry of Rites and later in some unimportant post. Song Yuzhang ruined that by acting as Song Jixin's father. He never denied the rumours in town, and he kept showing the boy affection even after he returned as a Ministry of Rites official. Song Jixin resented him, yet part of him wished the rumour were true. That fatherly love was the one line the emperor would not allow anyone to cross.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

### Death

Song Yuzhang knew too many of the Song Clan's secrets, and he had always expected to be killed someday.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup> When the emperor picked him to go back to the Jewel Small World, he understood it as the gift of a dignified death, one that would not happen in the capital or under a false charge. He accepted it calmly.

He returned to the town with a group of officials from the Ministry of Rites and stayed alone in a house on Dragon Riding Alley. One night Wang Yifu, a general from the fallen Lu Empire, appeared in his yard. Song Yuzhang greeted him as though he had been expecting him and asked to finish his drink first. He asked Wang Yifu to tell Song Jixin that all those years he had wanted a pair of couplets from him. Wang Yifu refused. Song Yuzhang then remembered reading as a young man about the great waves of the South Sea crashing against Old Dragon City, raised his bowl to them, and drank. Wang Yifu broke his neck and laid him gently on the table as if he had only passed out drunk.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> On the orders of Song Jixin's mother, his head was then cut off and sent to the capital in a wooden box.

### Mountain god

After his death the emperor made Song Yuzhang's heroic spirit the mountain god of Downtrodden Mountain. Whether this was a reward for a loyal servant or a final punishment is hard to say. His new colleagues mocked him as the "Gold Head Mountain God."

As a god he remained just as loyal. He met [Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md), a man he had admired since his student days, in his mountain temple, and was moved simply to talk with him about virtue.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> He grew increasingly at odds with [Wei Bo](Wei_Bo.md), his superior as god of the Northern Mountain, and he accepted the imperial court's limits on his powers over Downtrodden Mountain without complaint.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup> When Song Jixin later visited [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md) at Mountain Cliff Academy, he asked for help with Song Yuzhang's struggling temple.

[Cui Dongshan](Cui_Dongshan.md) once summoned him at the summit and called him a fool who had died for nothing. Song Yuzhang bowed respectfully but stood his ground. He told Cui Dongshan that he saw no reason a man could not seek his own moral fulfilment and serve the empire and its people at the same time.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup> Cui Dongshan later told Song Jixin that his grief over Song Yuzhang was laughable, and that the true mastermind behind the death was neither the man who killed him nor the mother who sent his head to the capital.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup>

## Trivia

- As a young man he loved reading travel chronicles, which is how he knew about the waves at Old Dragon City.
- The great Great Li emperor remarked that Song Yuzhang chose tacky names for people but put real thought into them.
- The green gourd Song Jixin treasured most in his chest of curios was something Song Yuzhang had left behind.
- When outsiders are away from Downtrodden Mountain, he likes to appear and listen to [Pei Qian](Pei_Qian.md)'s stories, and he shares small memories from his years as kiln supervision official.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup>
