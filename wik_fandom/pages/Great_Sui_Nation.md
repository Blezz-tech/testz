# Great Sui Nation

[← Wiki Index](../INDEX.md) · [Original Fandom Page](https://sword-of-coming.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Sui_Nation)

---
The **Great Sui Nation** is a powerful nation in the north of [Eastern Treasured Vial Continent](Eastern_Treasured_Vial_Continent.md), ruled for its entire 1,200-year history by the Gao Clan. It was the [Great Li Empire](Great_Li_Empire.md)'s strongest rival in the north and a nation that holds scholars in unusually high regard. After [Qi Jingchun](Qi_Jingchun.md)'s death it took in Mountain Cliff Academy, and [Chen Ping An](Chen_Ping_An.md) escorted [Li Baoping](Li_Baoping.md), [Li Huai](Li_Huai.md) and [Lin Shouyi](Lin_Shouyi.md) there on his first long journey.

## Geography

The nation lies south of the Great Li Empire. Travelers coming from the Great Li Empire first pass through its subsidiary, the Yellow Court Nation, a journey of about 600 kilometers.<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup> The Yellow Court Nation was one of the Great Sui Nation's twelve subsidiary nations.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> Past the Great Sui Pass on the border, the capital is still about 400 kilometers away.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

The capital is large and walled. The new Mountain Cliff Academy stands on Eastern Splendor Mountain, also called East Mountain, the most scenic mountain in the city. Iron Tree Mountain is the tallest mountain in the capital.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> The city is guarded by a great protective formation nurtured over several centuries. The imperial palace has its own formation, called the Dragon Wall, and secret passages link the palace to the offices of the Six Ministries and the academy.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

## History

The Gao Clan has ruled the nation since its founding, and Yiyang County is the emperor's home.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup> One of its founding fathers was the Confucian general Yuan Gaofeng, who gave up a scholar's career to found the nation with the first emperor of the Geyang Gao Clan. He was the first official in its history granted the posthumous title of Civil Justice, and he is now honored as a deity in the capital's Confucian temple.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> Working with the Lu Empire, the Gao Clan held back the rise of the Great Li Empire for several decades.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

A [prince of the Gao Clan](Gao_Zhen.md) entered the small town of [Jewel Small World](Small_Worlds.md), paying half of the emperor's private treasury, and bought the golden carp and a Dragon King Basket there. After Qi Jingchun's death, the Great Sui Nation took in the remaining teachers and students of Mountain Cliff Academy, with the Great Li emperor's approval.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup> When the academy's local students bullied the children from the small town, Li Huai's father Li Er stormed into the imperial palace. He fought the chief eunuch, who drew on the capital's dragon qi, until Mao Xiaodong brought the two sides to peace.

Facing the Great Li Empire's march south, the emperor chose not to fight. The Imperial Song Clan and the Imperial Gao Clan sealed an alliance with pacts signed on Cloud Drape Mountain and on East Mountain.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> The nation's vassals, including the Yellow Court Nation, switched their tribute to the Great Li Empire. More than a hundred officials resigned, a famous scholar threw himself off a cliff, and a Go master renowned across half the continent burned his favorite Go table.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> Gao Xuan went to the Great Li Empire as a hostage in place of his older brother.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup> An eleventh tier gatekeeper of the capital and the old flood dragon of the Yellow Court Nation went with him to serve as assistant mountain masters of Forest Deer Academy.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup>

The alliance gave the nation more than a century to recover, but both sides kept scheming. After the death of the Great Li emperor Song Zhengchun, many officials and border generals wanted to tear up the pact. Assassins targeted Mao Xiaodong and the students from the Great Li Empire around the Thousand Elders Banquet in the hope of starting a war.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup>

## Government

The nation is ruled from the capital by the emperor and the Six Ministries. Its imperial censors hold great power, and the title of heavenly official belongs to the head minister of the Ministry of Rites, rather than of the Ministry of Personnel as in the Great Li Empire. The head minister also serves as the academy's official mountain master. Twelve chief eunuchs guard the palace and kneel only to the emperor. The nation's only Unpolished Jade Tier cultivator is an old ancestor of the Gao Clan who lives as a storyteller and does not excel in battle.

## Culture

Scholars are held in very high regard. Women may study at any academy that accepts them before they marry, but may not sit the imperial examination. Almost everyone in the nation speaks the continent's official dialect, unlike in the Great Li Empire. The nation counts itself home to the best Go players on the continent, and Ning Yao remarks that a ninth dan Go official of the Great Li Empire is only equal to a seventh dan expert of the Great Sui Nation.

The Gao Clan's centuries of peace left a wealthy people who are happy to spend. Families in the imperial court hold the same standing their ancestors had two or three hundred years ago, which Cui Dongshan contrasts with the Great Li Empire's open court. He also judges its scholars as more theoretical and less practical than those of the Great Li Empire.

## Mountain Cliff Academy

The new Mountain Cliff Academy on Eastern Splendor Mountain is much larger than the old one. When the children from the small town arrived, the emperor met them at the foot of the mountain in his formal robes. Its first students were mostly drawn from wealthy and powerful clans of the nation. Mao Xiaodong serves as head deputy mountain master and runs the academy's teaching. Having moved, the academy lost its place among the 72 Confucian academies. Restoring it was later among the aims raised for the continent's future.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup>

## Trivia

- The prince who entered the small town calls himself Gao Zhen in Chapter 13, but the same prince is called Gao Xuan from Chapter 159 onward. The clan's home is called Yiyang County in early chapters and Geyang later.
- Cui Dongshan compares the nation's cavalry to guard dogs raised in a courtyard, while the Great Li Empire's border cavalry are wild dogs with sharp teeth.
- The capital's residents are known in the north for being arrogant and unwelcoming to outsiders.
- Some time after the children's arrival, the nation announced it had gained a powerful tenth tier martial artist. In the south of the continent this was dismissed as a lie and a diversion.
