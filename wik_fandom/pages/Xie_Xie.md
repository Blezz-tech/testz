**Xie Xie**, whose real name is **Xie Lingyue**, is a descendant of the Wind God Xie Clan of the fallen Lu Empire, the empire's foremost cultivating clan. She was once the youngest Qi refiner in the Lu Empire to reach the fifth tier. After the empire's fall, her cultivation was sealed with Dragon Restricting Nails, and she passed from the control of [Song Jixin](Song_Jixin.md)'s mother into the hands of the imperial preceptor [Cui Chan](Cui_Chan.md), who mockingly renamed her "Xie Xie", meaning "thank you".

She joins [Chen Ping'an](Chen_Ping_An.md)'s group on its journey to [Great Sui Nation](Great_Sui_Nation.md) together with [Yu Lu](Yu_Lu.md). At [Mountain Cliff Academy](Mountain_Cliff_Academy.md), she becomes Cui Dongshan's maidservant and disciple, enduring years of harsh treatment while quietly regaining her strength.

## Appearance

Xie Xie is a slender, dark-skinned girl with a stern expression and a naturally seductive voice. She carries a bamboo flute at her waist. Cui Chan describes her appearance as unremarkable.

## Personality

Xie Xie is proud, guarded and bitter about the fate of her clan and the Lu Empire. She hides her feelings behind a smile, and Chen Ping'an finds her unapproachable. She feels ashamed of cultivators being called "transcendent and carefree beings" when she herself is a prisoner. She is fiercely ambitious, and Cui Chan claims she is so jealous of Lin Shouyi's smooth cultivation path that she is close to going mad. She cares more about results than study, and Mao Xiaodong notes she is far too fixated on cultivation.

Beneath her cold exterior, she can be warm with those who don't threaten her. She gets along well with [Li Huai](Li_Huai.md), playing battle games with him and his toys, and later smiles openly when her old companions visit. She has a long-running, childish feud with [Li Baoping](Li_Baoping.md).

## Background

The Wind God Xie Clan was the Lu Empire's number one cultivating clan, and the only one in history whose members all chose to fight and die for an empire. Hundreds of its cultivators fell defending the Lu Clan. Xie Lingyue survived and, after the empire's fall, was imprisoned with Dragon Restricting Nails driven into her body to seal her cultivation. She was at first under the control of the Great Li Empire's imperial concubine, Song Jixin's mother, before falling into Cui Chan's hands, which the narrative calls jumping "out of the frying pan and into the fire".<sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>

## Plot

### Joining the Journey

Cui Chan brings her to his manor, where Yu Lu already works as his servant. She is hopeless at housework and breaks many dishes. When she refuses to give her name, Cui Chan names her "Xie Xie", saying she should thank him for it.<sup>[[2]](#ref-2)</sup> On the road, she mocks Yu Lu, asking whether he still thinks he is the revered prince of the Lu Empire.<sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>

She joins Chen Ping'an's group with Yu Lu and Cui Chan. She easily crushes Lin Shouyi at Go and becomes friends with Li Huai, but she and Li Baoping dislike each other intensely.<sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup> Li Baoping accuses her of snoring so loudly she can be heard from far away. Xie Xie denies it, then retorts that she is a cultivator, someone people like Li Baoping revere as immortals.<sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>

Cui Chan secretly asks her to put in a good word for him with Chen Ping'an. She wanted his ownerless flying sword, but he only offers a Fish Bug Flute from the Lu Empire's secret imperial collection, a token of the alliance between a sect and the Lu Empire's first emperor. She tells Chen Ping'an all this frankly, admitting she is fond of beautiful things.<sup>[[6]](#ref-6)</sup>

At Cui Chan's urging, she and Yu Lu reveal their identities. When Yu Lu speaks calmly of the past, she explodes in tearful rage, declaring her true name, Xie Lingyue, and her clan's sacrifice for the "foolish and incompetent" royal family. Yu Lu coldly replies that she should die or assassinate Cui Chan if she truly has her ancestors' courage. Chen Ping'an tells them that grievances can be set aside without being forgotten.<sup>[[7]](#ref-7)</sup> Cui Chan later remarks on her jealousy of Lin Shouyi.<sup>[[8]](#ref-8)</sup>

### Mountain Cliff Academy

When Lin Shouyi is beaten by the son of the late general Pan Maozhen, Xie Xie does not visit him. Instead, she seeks out the Pan boy the same day and beats him until he bleeds from every orifice.<sup>[[9]](#ref-9)</sup> When Li Huai is bullied, though, she says she can do nothing against the Confucian prodigy Li Changying. Cui Chan, displeased, slaps her across the face.

Cui Chan, now Cui Dongshan, removes half of the Dragon Restricting Nails from her body, and she quickly recovers to the fifth tier, light surging across her body.<sup>[[10]](#ref-10)</sup> She becomes his disciple and maidservant, receiving a pile of treasures that only immortals could dream of, and becomes famous in the Great Sui capital under her real name.<sup>[[11]](#ref-11)</sup> In return, she endures years of harsh treatment: kneeling at his door, serving tea, and being slapped across the room for setting down a Go stone too hard. Chen Ping'an, as Cui Dongshan's teacher, does not interfere, just as Cui Cheng never interfered with Pei Qian's training. It is later revealed that she and Yu Lu were pawns deliberately chosen by Cui Chan and the Great Li empress.<sup>[[12]](#ref-12)</sup>

### The Assassination Attempt

When Chen Ping'an returns, she sternly asks why he has come, but lets him in, and later invites Lin Shouyi to cultivate in Cui Dongshan's courtyard, which has some of the best feng shui in the academy. During the assassination attempt, she locks Li Baoping, Li Huai and Pei Qian in the study and uses Cui Dongshan's secret technique to turn the courtyard into a miniature small world. As its core, she holds firm against a Nascent Tier swordsman's flying sword until she coughs blood and collapses unconscious.<sup>[[13]](#ref-13)</sup>

Cui Dongshan praises her for making him look good. He is especially pleased that she let Pei Qian and Li Huai play with his precious Vibrant Cloud Stones, since stinginess would have reflected badly on him. For the first time, he gives her a genuine smile and offers her a reward of her choice.<sup>[[14]](#ref-14)</sup> The final Dragon Restricting Nail is removed, and Cui Dongshan suggests giving her the Li Fire flying sword taken from the assassin.

### Later Years

Xie Xie continues to live in Cui Dongshan's old residence, cultivating wholeheartedly. With all the Dragon Restricting Nails removed, she progresses rapidly but hides it, and she leads the small-town students in cultivation.<sup>[[15]](#ref-15)</sup> When Chen Ping'an and the others gather at her residence, she smiles at the visitors, glares at him for nearly taking off his boots, and is the most astonished of all at the news of Downtrodden Mountain's ancestral hall. Chen Ping'an invites her and Lin Shouyi to cultivate in the mountains of Dragon Spring Prefecture.

## Abilities

- **Qi refining:** She was once the youngest fifth-tier Qi refiner in the Lu Empire. Her cultivation was sealed by Dragon Restricting Nails and restored once they were removed.
- **Formations:** She can form a miniature small world using Cui Dongshan's secret technique.
- **Go:** She is an exceptional player who easily beats Lin Shouyi.
- **Music:** She plays the bamboo flute and the guqin.

## Possessions

- **Fish Bug Flute**: a bamboo flute from the Lu Empire's secret imperial collection, promised by Cui Chan.
- **Treasures from Cui Dongshan**: a pile of immortal treasures received as his disciple.
- **Li Fire flying sword** (possibly): the assassin's sword, which Cui Dongshan suggested giving to her.

## Trivia

- 谢谢 (xiè xie) means "thank you". Cui Chan chose the name to mock her.
- Cui Dongshan once joked that she wanted to become Chen Ping'an's wife, to her silent mortification.
- In Cui Dongshan's farewell performance, she plays the guqin across a lake from Yu Lu's flute, "like an immortal couple".
